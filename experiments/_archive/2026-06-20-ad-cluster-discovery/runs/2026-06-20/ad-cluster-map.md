# Telehealth / Compounded-Rx Ad-Cluster Map

What's actually running, right now, in paid ads across compounded GLP-1, TRT/HRT, ED, and peptides — clustered into 10 verified messaging angles over a 275-ad corpus (189 valid creatives after junk/template drop), captured 2026-06-20.

## 30-second skim

The market is **led by price, not transformation.** The single largest angle by a wide margin is flat-rate price transparency — explicitly anchored against brand-name Ozempic/Wegovy/Ro/Hims — and it's overwhelmingly a Meta phenomenon. After that, the field fragments by vertical: convenience/access cuts across all of them, TRT runs an identity-restoration play, ED splits into two opposed registers (performance/speed vs. destigmatization), and a small but distinct "food-noise" angle sells *silence* rather than weight loss.

Ranked by prevalence (# advertisers, then # creatives):

| # | Angle | Creatives | Advertisers | Channel |
|---|-------|----------:|------------:|---------|
| 1 | **Price transparency / flat rate** | 66 | 18 | meta-heavy |
| 2 | **Convenience / online to doorstep** | 20 | 11 | meta + google |
| 3 | **Male vitality / identity restoration** (TRT) | 17 | 9 | meta only |
| 4 | **Education / myth-busting** | 10 | 9 | meta only |
| 5 | **Social proof / results** | 10 | 7 | meta-heavy |
| 6 | **ED performance / speed / formulation** | 15 | 5 | meta only |
| 7 | **Food-noise / appetite suppression** | 18 | 4 | meta only |
| 8 | **Safety / clinical credibility** | 9 | 3 | meta + google |
| 9 | **Affiliate / review roundup** (structural) | 10 | 2 | meta only |
| 10 | **ED destigmatization / discreet access** | 5 | 2 | meta only |

Price + convenience together account for the plurality of advertiser activity. Google ads skew almost entirely toward access-removal and safety-credential copy (search intent rewards it); the emotional/identity/education angles are a Meta-native game.

## Angle cards

### 1. Price transparency / flat rate — 66 creatives / 18 advertisers · meta:62, google:4
Lead hook is the price: flat monthly rate regardless of dose, no hidden fees, no bait-and-switch — often anchored explicitly against brand-name competitors at 80-89% more. The dominant pattern in the compounded-semaglutide cohort.
- *"Ro charges $400. Hims charges $380. Mochi runs $350. We deliver compounded tirzepatide for $144.97/month. Same active ingredient. Different price tag — and yes, you read that right."* — **Rx Pros Wellness Online** (meta)
- *"Why pay $1,000+ for brand-name GLP-1 when you can get the same active ingredient for 89% less? … ❌ No hidden fees ❌ No subscriptions ❌ No membership fee … Plans only $89/month"* — **Dad Club Co.** (meta)
- *"GLP-1 for $88/mo? YES PLEASE 🙏🔥 Most companies lure you in with a low price… then hit you with a 'membership fee' on page two 😒 Not us. … No bait-and-switch. No membership fees. No price increases ever."* — **Wellmedr** (meta)

### 2. Convenience / online to doorstep — 20 creatives / 11 advertisers · meta:11, google:9
Primary value prop is zero clinic visits — quiz/assessment scripts, meds shipped to door, no insurance. Used across TRT, HRT, GLP-1, peptides. Google-overrepresented vs. its corpus share because search-intent copy favors access-removal language.
- *"Testosterone Replacement Therapy, delivers straight to your doorstep with FountainTRT's at-home TRT treatment. ⛲ … ❌ No insurance needed"* — **FountainTRT** (meta)
- *"TRT - First Month Only $129 — Henry Meds — Getting TRT testosterone replacement therapy is simple and easy with Henry."* — **ADONIS HEALTH INC.** (google)
- *"Tirzepatide GLP-1 + GIP | Overnight Delivery | Home Injection Kit"* — **GOOD LIFE MEDS LLC** (google)

### 3. Male vitality / identity restoration (TRT) — 17 creatives / 9 advertisers · meta:17
TRT positioned as reclaiming masculine identity — energy, confidence, drive — framed as "feeling like yourself again," with symptom checklists as self-ID hooks. Separate from the weight-loss emotional narrative: different audience, different transformation arc.
- *"Testosterone therapy isn't just about hormones— It's about getting your energy back. Your drive. Your confidence. Your life. We've seen it change marriages, boost careers, and help men feel like themselves again"* — **Unlimited Health and Wellness** (meta)
- *"FEEL OFF LATELY? THIS MIGHT BE WHY! Many men brush off the signs of low testosterone, but it could be the reason life feels flat."* — **SlimCare Solutions** (meta)
- *"Avoid settling for fatigue. … TRT is about re-establishing e[nergy]…"* — **Rock Body Clinic** (meta)

### 4. Education / myth-busting — 10 creatives / 9 advertisers · meta:10
Leads with mechanism, myth correction, or counter-narrative rather than a direct sell. A push-signal pattern — betting an informed reader converts better. Distinct from safety/credibility: goal is to reframe the reader's mental model, not validate a regulatory standing.
- *"NO… GLP-1 cannot make users drop 2 dress sizes instantly. The science behind GLP-1's ability to mute food noise is real… But it's NOT magic. YES… there will most likely be side effects"* — **Blue Haven RX** (meta)
- *"GLP-1 receptor agonists … mimic a natural gut hormone … In 68-week clinical trials, adults with obesity lost ≈ 15% of their starting weight—about 35 lb for someone at 230 lb"* — **TMates** (meta)
- *"TRT isn't your only option! In this overview, we break down enclomiphene—an alternative approach … that helps your body naturally support testosterone production, rather than replacing it."* — **Temple Care & Wellness** (meta)

### 5. Social proof / results — 10 creatives / 7 advertisers · meta:9, google:1
Proof-led: large user counts, testimonial lab-result stories, before/afters, star ratings. Credibility via observable mass outcomes rather than institutional authority.
- *"429,576+ men didn't switch to Mars Men because of a slick ad. They switched because their bloodwork changed."* — **Mars Men** (meta)
- *"Discover why over 300,000 men have already made the switch to Rugiet Ready to treat ED."* — **Rugiet** (meta)
- *"I lost 35 pounds in 8 months with the help of GLP-1s… And with Fridays, it isn't just medication! It's real support."* — **Review Therapy** (meta, for Fridays)

### 6. ED performance / speed / formulation — 15 creatives / 5 advertisers · meta:15
ED ads leading with novelty of delivery format and onset speed — sublingual liquid, "10 minutes", "36 hours", 4-in-1 compounded formula. Racing on efficacy and format, not shame reduction. Strategically the opposite of the destigmatization cluster.
- *"One squirt under your tongue. Rock hard in 10 minutes. No pills. No pharmacy. No empty stomach rules."* — **Modern Men's Health** (meta) [identical copy also run by **Sophia Thorne**]
- *"4 ingredients. 1 bottle. Hard in 10 minutes. Ready for 36 hours. See if you qualify below 👇"* — **Things Men Should Know** (meta)
- *"A better sex life just dropped: Meet the new 3-in-1 Pill by Hims. … Same active ingredients as Viagra® and Cialis®"* — **hims** (meta)

### 7. Food-noise / appetite suppression — 18 creatives / 4 advertisers · meta:18
Leads with the felt experience of constant food preoccupation; positions GLP-1 as quieting cravings. The benefit is *silence*, not weight loss per se. High creative count concentrated in few advertisers (one running near-identical copy at volume).
- *"Tired of constantly thinking about food? TrimRx helps you control cravings and stop grazing all day."* — **Beyond the Scale** (meta)
- *"Can't stop thinking about food? You're not alone. … Helps quiet food noise … Silence the noise."* — **Easy Access Health** (meta)

### 8. Safety / clinical credibility — 9 creatives / 3 advertisers · meta:6, google:3
FDA-approval (brand-name Wegovy), board-certified providers, celebrity endorsement (Serena Williams for Ro) as the primary credibility hook. Used by the larger platforms that can absorb the brand-name cost premium — positions against compounded competitors on legitimacy.
- *"Get Wegovy® with Hims … ✅ FDA-approved GLP-1 pill and pens available … ✅ 100% online"* — **hims** (meta) [parallel **Hers** variant for women]
- *"Ro x Serena Williams — Ongoing reqd membership & med cost vary. See Rx warning."* — **Roman Health Ventures Inc.** (google)
- *"FDA Approved GLP-1 Medication"* — **Roman Health Ventures Inc.** (google)

### 9. Affiliate / review roundup — 10 creatives / 2 advertisers · meta:10
Non-product advertisers running comparison/roundup ads as third-party arbiters, routing to affiliate links. A structural category, not a persuasion angle — these advertisers have nothing of their own to sell.
- *"Shopping around for ED treatments, but not sure which one to pick? Here's the one that comes out on top..."* — **Online Rx Reviews** (meta)
- *"Best ED Treatment 2026 — Paid Content from WebMD's Sponsor: Compare top online ED treatment providers"* — **WebMD DNA Brand Studio** (meta)

### 10. ED destigmatization / discreet access — 5 creatives / 2 advertisers · meta:5
ED ads leading with normalization ("you're not alone", sex-therapist voice) and discreet delivery as the barrier-removal hook. Shame reduction and access, not formulation novelty — the strategic opposite of the performance/speed cluster.
- *"You're too young for ED... or are you? … While I'm not a Hims customer, I am a sexual health expert"* — **hims** (meta, creator voice)
- *"As a s//x therapist, I hear a *lot* of myths about ED. … @hims is here to help!"* — **hims** (meta, creator voice)
- *"Effective ED treatment – 100% online with discreet delivery, no in-person visit needed."* — **Motivated** (meta)

## Channel divergence (store brands on both Meta and Google)

Only **two** store-keyed brands cleared the bar of appearing on both channels in this sample: **hims** and **Good Life Meds**. Both run *materially different angles per channel.*

**hims** (10 Meta creatives / 3 Google):
- *Meta:* a portfolio play — GLP-1 safety/credibility ("Get Wegovy® with Hims… FDA-approved"), ED performance ("3-in-1 Pill"), and ED destigmatization via creator/sex-therapist voices. Emotional + editorial register.
- *Google:* pure keyword-intent search ads on an entirely different product surface — **hair loss** ("Men's Hair Loss Products", "Finasteride (Generic for Propecia)") and **mental health** ("Take a Free Anxiety Assessment"). Not a single weight-loss or ED creative in the Google set.
- **Read:** Meta is hims' brand/emotional and new-product channel; Google is its bottom-of-funnel, generic-drug, capture-the-search-query channel. Different products, different funnel stage, different register.

**Good Life Meds** (1 Meta / 3 Google):
- *Meta:* price + results ("$100 off your first month of Tirzepatide… U.S. Made. Licensed pharmacy. Real results. No hidden fees").
- *Google:* logistics/keyword copy ("Overnight Delivery | Home Injection Kit", "Sermorelin At Home Solution").
- **Read:** same divergence shape — persuasion/price on Meta, access-and-delivery keywords on Google.

**Everyone else is single-channel in this sample.** Ro, Found, Ivy RX, Effecty appear Google-only; Shed, Fridays, FountainTRT, Rugiet appear Meta-only. **The Google sample is too thin (26 records, capped at 3/domain) to generalize per-brand channel strategy beyond hims** — treat the hims finding as the one well-supported case and Good Life Meds as suggestive.

## Caveats

- **Push-signal, not demand.** This is what advertisers chose to *say*, not what consumers want or what converts. Prevalence = ad spend/volume, not effectiveness.
- **Not a census.** Meta = first-page results per keyword; Google = a capped sample (≤3 creatives/domain, 18 seed domains, 26 total records). Coverage is a snapshot of 5 keyword queries on one day (2026-06-20), not the full market.
- **"Not found" ≠ "not running."** An advertiser absent from a cluster, or a brand absent from a channel, may simply be outside this sample's reach — especially on the thin Google side. Absence here is *not observed*, not *not advertising*.
- **Junk dropped.** 86 of 275 records were template/empty creatives (`{{product.brand}}` tokens, blank copy) and excluded from all counts; 189 valid creatives remain. Cluster creative-counts overlap slightly (e.g. hims appears across 4 angles), so they sum above the valid total by design.
- **Keying is lenient and manual.** Legal-entity names (LLC/Inc.) were aliased by hand to brand domains; a 21% store-match rate reflects that the corpus is mostly long-tail advertisers the store was never meant to hold, not a store gap.
