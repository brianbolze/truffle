### How does Enclomiphene + Testosterone Cream work?

This protocol combines two complementary approaches: Enclomiphene supports your body's natural testosterone production and helps maintain LH/FSH levels, while testosterone cream provides direct testosterone support through daily topical application. Together, they deliver stronger testosterone results than Enclomiphene alone, while helping preserve fertility markers and testicular function.

### How do I take Enclomiphene + Testosterone Cream?

Enclomiphene is taken as a daily pill. Testosterone cream is applied once daily to areas with thin skin—such as the inner arms, wrists, lateral abdomen, inner thighs, or scrotum. Your Maximus clinician will confirm your exact dosing and application instructions.

### When will I notice results from Enclomiphene + Testosterone Cream?

Testosterone cream absorbs quickly, with levels peaking within 2 hours after application. Enclomiphene's effects on testosterone and fertility support are typically seen within 28 days. Most men notice improvements in energy, mood, and libido within several weeks, with full benefits building over 2–3 months as dosing is optimized.

### Will Enclomiphene + Testosterone Cream affect fertility?

Unlike traditional TRT, this protocol is designed to support fertility while raising testosterone levels. The Enclomiphene component helps maintain LH and FSH levels, which are critical for sperm production and testicular function. Responses vary and aren't guaranteed, so ongoing lab monitoring is important—especially if fertility is a priority.

### What are the potential side effects of Enclomiphene + Testosterone Cream?

Side effects vary by person. Testosterone cream may cause acne or oily skin, fluid retention, mood or libido changes, increased hematocrit/hemoglobin, or irritation at the application site. Enclomiphene side effects are typically infrequent but can include headache, nausea, dizziness, or hot flashes. Regarding hair loss: testosterone can convert to DHT, which may accelerate male-pattern hair loss in men who are genetically predisposed—discuss options like dutasteride with your clinician if this is a concern.

### Is there any risk of transference to children or women?

Yes, transference of testosterone cream is possible. Wash your hands thoroughly after application and cover the area with clothing. For extra protection, you can wash the application site 2 hours after applying—any earlier may interfere with absorption.

### Is Enclomiphene + Testosterone Cream safe for my liver?

Yes. Testosterone cream is absorbed through the lymphatic system, bypassing liver circulation and avoiding the hepatotoxic effects associated with some oral formulations. Your clinician will monitor liver enzymes as part of routine lab work.

### Do I need lab work for Enclomiphene + Testosterone Cream?

Yes. Lab testing is required to confirm eligibility, personalize dosing, and monitor both effectiveness and safety over time. If you have bloodwork from the last 6 months that includes Total Testosterone, SHBG (or free testosterone), LH, PSA, and Hematocrit, you can upload your results during sign-up.

### I'm currently on TRT, clomiphene, or enclomiphene—can I switch to this protocol?

Often, yes. Let your Maximus clinician know what you're currently taking and don't stop or change medications on your own. Your clinician will guide a safe transition plan and may time labs accordingly.
