## Growth Hormone (GHRH) peptides _vs._ synthetic growth hormone.

Synthetic growth hormone floods your system with a constant, unregulated dose. It bypasses your body’s feedback mechanisms, which is why it carries greater risks of insulin resistance, joint pain, and soft issue overgrowth.

Growth Hormone peptides take a fundamentally different approach. Secretagogues like Ser-morelin and Tesa-morelin stimulate your pituitary to produce more GH on its own terms. Your body’s negative feedback loop stays intact—meaning production self-regulates. You get the benefits of elevated GH without overriding your natural safety systems.

|  | Ser-morelin | Tesa-morelin | Synthetic HGH |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| GH Source | Your body’s natural GH | Your body’s natural GH (stronger stimulation) | External hormone replacement |
| Release Pattern | Natural, pulsatile rhythm | Natural, pulsatile rhythm | Constant ‘bleed' |
| Feedback Loops | Preserved | Preserved | Suppressed |
| Fat Loss | Moderate | Strong (visceral fat) | Strong |
| Muscle Support | Supports lean mass | Greater lean mass support | Strong |
| Sleep | Improves deep sleep | Improves deep sleep | Variable |
| Side Effect Profile | Lowest risk | Moderate (e.g. water retention) | Highest |

Side effects

Contact your provider immediately if severe symptoms occur. Regular monitoring may be needed to track your response to treatment.

Common side effects may include:

- Injection site reactions (redness, pain, swelling)
- Joint pain or stiffness
- Headache
- Dizziness

Serious side effects may include:

- Fluid retention or swelling
- Allergic reactions (rash, itching, swelling)
- High blood sugar or diabetes

### What are growth hormone peptides and how do they work?

Growth hormone peptides (also called secretagogues) are compounds that signal your pituitary gland to produce more of its own growth hormone naturally. Unlike exogenous growth hormone, which bypasses your body’s feedback systems, the GH peptides offered by Maximus work with your biology—stimulating natural GH pulsing while maintaining the safety mechanisms your body relies on.

### How long until I see results?

Most patients notice improved sleep quality within the first 2–4 weeks. Nail growth is typically the earliest visible sign of elevated GH, often appearing within a month. ### Are there side effects?

Growth hormone peptides have a favorable side effect profile compared to exogenous GH. Some patients experience mild injection-site reactions, temporary water retention, or tingling in the first few weeks—these typically resolve as your body adjusts. Because peptides work through your body’s natural feedback loops, the risk of serious side effects is significantly lower than with synthetic growth hormone.
