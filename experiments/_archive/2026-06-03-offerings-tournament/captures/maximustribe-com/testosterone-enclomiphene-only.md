## Enclomiphene  FAQs

[Contact Support](https://support.maximustribe.com/en/)

### How does the Enclomiphene protocol work?

Enclomiphene helps your body increase its own testosterone production over time, rather than replacing it. This can raise testosterone while supporting testicular function and fertility markers. Extensive scientific research and clinical practice have vetted the oral medications used in the Enclomiphene Protocol. You can learn more about our research behind Enclomiphene Protocol [here](https://www.maximustribe.com/white-paper/enclomiphene).

### When will I start noticing results?

Some people notice changes within a few weeks. More complete effects often build over time, with many people seeing fuller results around 6–8 weeks.

### How do I take Enclomiphene?

It’s taken as an oral pill once daily, as prescribed. Your clinician will confirm the right dose and timing based on your health history and lab results.

### What are the potential side effects of Enclomiphene?

Side effects vary by person. In clinical studies, reported side effects were generally infrequent and can include headache, nausea, dizziness, hot flashes, or mild GI symptoms.

On hair loss: some men worry about DHT-related hair loss with testosterone therapies. Enclomiphene works differently than traditional TRT and may be a better fit for men concerned about fertility and testicular function; your clinician can also discuss options if you’re specifically worried about hair loss.

### Is Enclomiphene as effective as TRT?

It depends on your baseline hormones and what’s driving your low testosterone. Enclomiphene is a safer alternative to TRT, and can be a strong option for men who want to increase testosterone while supporting fertility markers—whereas traditional TRT is a direct replacement approach. Your clinician will help match the best option to your labs and goals.

### Do I need lab work before starting Enclomiphene?

Yes. Lab work helps confirm your baseline hormone markers and allows your clinician to personalize your protocol and monitor progress safely.
