### Cold sores

Pricing for [valacyclovir](https://ro.co/medications/valacyclovir/) (generic Valtrex), an antiviral drug FDA-approved to treat oral herpes outbreaks (also known as cold sores).

[Valacyclovir](https://ro.co/medications/valacyclovir/) is not approved for suppressive therapy of oral herpes, but doctors have the discretion to prescribe it for daily use if they believe that it is an appropriate course of treatment for a particular patient. This is considered off-label treatment.

![Valacyclovir pills in Ro packages](https://imgctf--assets.ro.co/jj2wf7627pjc/7e1gMvQWiFEXuZShH3j4gm/4b7eba220cabb8d8234e4747cf8d88c2/Valacyclovir_lg-1.jpg)

Valacyclovir isn’t safe for some people and others may need a lower than normal dose to use valacyclovir safely. Tell your doctor if you have kidney disease or if your immune system isn’t normal because of a bone marrow or kidney transplant or HIV. ### Genital herpes

Pricing for [valacyclovir](https://ro.co/medications/valacyclovir/) (generic Valtrex), an antiviral drug FDA-approved to treat, prevent, and suppress herpes outbreaks.

[Valacyclovir](https://ro.co/medications/valacyclovir/) has a safety profile that is similar to placebo, even when used for long periods. If taken on a daily basis (suppressive therapy), it may reduce a person’s number of outbreaks by 70%–80% and reduce the risk of transmitting herpes to a partner by about 50%.
