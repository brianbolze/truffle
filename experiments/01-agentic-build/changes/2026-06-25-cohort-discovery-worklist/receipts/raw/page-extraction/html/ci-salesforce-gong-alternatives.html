You just wrapped up a successful sales call. But before you can enjoy your win, you’re rushing to complete the next steps — updating your CRM, summarizing meeting notes, and sending follow-up emails — all before your next meeting.

Sound familiar? For salespeople, time spent on busywork means less time actually selling. That’s why conversation intelligence software has become crucial for sales teams looking to reclaim that lost time.

By automating the collection and analysis of your customer interactions, tools like Gong promise to capture calls, surface insights, and help coach reps faster. But Gong isn’t the only option. Let's review conversation intelligence software alternatives and how they help sales teams better understand customers’ needs and improve sales performance.

10 best Gong alternatives and competitors for sales teams

Choosing the right conversation intelligence software can improve sales team productivity by automating tedious tasks, such as taking call notes or summarizing meetings. By automating these tasks with conversation intelligence software, you can quickly understand customers’ needs and provide personalized engagement.

There are conversation intelligence software options similar to Gong . This list is curated from G2, and all software has a minimum rating of 4 out of 5 stars, ensuring you’re getting the best contenders based on real user ratings and feedback.

1. Agentforce Sales

Agentforce Sales (formerly Sales Cloud) is an AI-powered CRM that offers a complete view of your business on one unified platform. It brings AI agents and human reps together at every step of the process. Agentforce Sales gives sellers real-time guidance and conversation insights so they can close deals faster. And, Salesforce offers a full suite of capabilities, including territory planning, incentive compensation management, partner relationship management, and more.

Key features:

  • Conversation intelligence: Agentforce Sales automatically records, transcribes, and summarizes voice and video calls with AI. It surfaces key signals like risks, objections, and next steps so your team can take action.
  • Sales signals: AI analyzes conversations and surfaces critical signals in calls before it’s too late to act. You can instantly see risks and impacted opportunities to refine your strategy and close deals faster.
  • Call summaries: Get up to speed fast with AI-generated call summaries. You can see what was discussed at a glance and jump to relevant transcript snippets for key details like objections, pricing, and decision-maker mentions.
  • Sales coaching and insights: Get custom insights from every call, powered by AI, and delivered right on the call record for easy access. Let sellers see key takeaways and top concerns without reading the entire transcript, with the exact context they need to move a deal forward.

2. Chorus by ZoomInfo

Chorus by ZoomInfo integrates with the ZoomInfo data and intelligence platform or your CRM to sync sales information. Chorus records and transcribes customer interactions across phone, video, and email so sales teams and others can search, share, and analyze data collected from conversations. The mobile app lets sellers record and listen to sales calls on the go. G2 reviewers say Chorus is easy to integrate and simplifies drafting follow-up emails after a conversation.

Key features:

  • Speech-to-text: AI-driven transcription captures same-room speaker separation to identify multiple speakers and provide accuracy in determining who says what.
  • Coaching materials: Chorus provides recommended high-scoring sales calls to curate a library of playlists for reps to learn from their peers.
  • Video record: Record live pitches and track conversation metrics, including talk-to-listen ratios, filler words, competitor mentions, objection handling, and deal progression.
  • Live forecasting: Use predictive analytics for live customer data to measure deal momentum for accounts and opportunities.

3. Salesloft

Best known for sales engagement, Salesloft is a revenue management platform with a suite of capabilities, including deal management and sales forecasting. It offers an AI-powered workflow automation and process management tool. Its conversation intelligence features surface insights and deal summaries. G2 reviewers find the conversation insights useful for producing transcriptions that are easy to refer back to, allowing them to focus on the customer conversation.

Key features:

  • Customer scoring: AI agents rate rep performance on sales calls by analyzing transcripts and surface insights that help identify sales blockers or deal risks.
  • Speech-to-text: AI agents transcribe and summarize sellers’ conversations, then analyze and link them to relevant deals.
  • Sentiment analysis: Captured call transcriptions include AI sentiment analysis for insights into emotions, tone, and interactions.
  • Coaching card: Sales managers can coach new hires, surface insights, and provide real-time feedback on seller activities.

4. Fireflies.ai

Fireflies.ai has an AI voice assistant that helps during meetings by transcribing, summarizing notes, and taking action. It integrates with many leading web-conferencing platforms to record meetings and supports more than 20 languages. Meeting notes, transcripts, and recordings are automatically synced to your CRM or collaboration apps. G2 users cite Fireflies.ai as a time-saver that helps them stay organized.

Key features:

  • Proactive assistance: It offers an in-meeting companion for coaching guidance, such as handling objections or refining pitch messaging.
  • Action item extraction: The AI chatbot automatically generates sales follow-up emails or answers questions based on meeting transcripts.
  • Summary generation: AI notetaking software generates detailed meeting overviews, outlines, notes, keywords, and action items.
  • Speech-to-text transcription: The mobile app records, transcribes, and summarizes in-person sales calls directly from a phone.

5. Jiminny

Jiminny captures real-time feedback and analyzes insights from conversations across video, voice, and email. It also supports offline conversations by recording in-person meetings via its mobile app and desktop upload feature. The conversation intelligence tool integrates with your CRM, dialer, and productivity platforms. G2 users appreciate Jiminny’s design and functionality.

Key features:

  • Speech-to-text: For global teams, Jiminny automatically detects and transcribes conversations across more than 60 languages.
  • Call analysis: Analyze recorded sales calls with custom keywords and searchable themes to capture renewal threats or objections tied to competitor offerings.
  • Sales coaching: The AI chatbot feature lets sellers get real-time answers about calls, deals, and pipelines.
  • Coaching materials: Export and share call recordings to a CRM and create a library of playlists that highlight best practices and winning techniques.

6. Avoma

As an AI-first growth acceleration platform designed for customer-facing teams at start-ups and scale-ups, Avoma combines AI meeting assistance with conversational intelligence and revenue intelligence. The all-in-one platform automates note-taking, scheduling, coaching, and forecasting for sales teams. G2 users like Avoma’s intuitive feature set and say it’s easy to integrate with their video call platforms.

Key features:

  • Call analysis: An AI-powered feature helps sellers gain insights from advanced phrases beyond exact keyword matching, such as those related to deal risks or churn signals.
  • Coaching: An AI assistant provides real-time, contextual guidance and suggestions to help respond to customer objections or complex questions.
  • Note-taking: The augmented notes feature lets you take your own notes and use AI to bookmark or highlight text live in the transcript.
  • Multi-language and accent support: Supports transcribing meetings in dozens of languages and dialects to make conversations more accessible.

7. Fathom

Fathom records, transcribes, and summarizes meetings on popular video conferencing platforms. Fathom’s AI note-taking handles multiple speakers and accents, and you can create custom dictionaries, so it learns your company’s language and industry acronyms. G2 reviews cite Fathom’s transcription accuracy and concise summaries.

Key features:

  • Speech-to-text transcription: Transcriptions handle multiple speakers and accents, and create custom dictionaries based on a company's language.
  • Call analysis: The AI search capability surfaces objections from sales calls and sends alerts via email or Slack when new matches appear.
  • Smart summaries: Customizable AI summaries use a built-in template that lets you tell the AI what to capture, such as decisions or next steps.
  • Coaching: For Fathom Business Plan users, AI scorecards provide AI-generated performance insights, and behavioral metrics show average call performance data.

8. Outreach Kaia

Outreach is a revenue workflow platform that features Kaia, a conversation intelligence assistant. Like Gong and similar competitors, it can record and transcribe sales calls and meetings, generate summaries, and sync data with a CRM system. Kaia provides AI-powered assistance by surfacing information and guidance for reps in real time during calls and analyzing recorded conversations to help teams identify trends and improve performance.

Key features:

  • Conversation analyses: Outreach intelligence features identify discussion topics and other buyer signals to help reps assess deal health and spot trends.
  • Summarization capabilities: Meeting summaries and transcripts are auto-generated instantly, and AI can provide a roundup of action items and follow-up questions.
  • AI assistance: During a live meeting, Kaia can provide in-context prompts for reps, supplying them with battlecards or suggesting next steps.
  • Coaching: AI can analyze conversations to help managers identify coachable moments, track rep behaviors, and enable performance comparisons.

9. Clari Copilot

Clari is a revenue orchestration platform that includes a conversation intelligence solution called Clari Copilot. The tool was designed to link sales interactions with revenue outcomes. In addition to recording and transcribing, Clari Copilot identifies buyer signals during sales calls and automatically syncs the data with CRM, pipeline, and forecast workflows. Sales teams can then use aggregated information to better understand why deals progress or stall.

Key features:

  • Buyer signal capture: AI capabilities detect key moments in conversations that indicate intent, sentiment, objections, competitor mentions, and next steps.
  • Revenue forecasting: Conversation insights are connected to pipeline and revenue workflows to help teams assess deal health and support forecast accuracy.
  • AI assistance: Clari Copilot provides insights, information, and prompts based on the context of a live sales call so the rep can adjust their responses or next steps.
  • Enablement: Managers can use “playbooks” and “gametapes” to support consistent execution and highlight successful examples from calls.

10. Otter.ai

Otter.ai is an AI meeting agent that automatically records, transcribes, and summarizes sales meetings. Otter works for in-person meetings, or users can import and transcribe pre-recorded audio and video files. OtterPilot for Sales integrates with Salesforce to help automate call reporting. G2 users say Otter enhances productivity during meetings by capturing notes and generating summaries.

Key features:

  • Autonomous task execution: An AI assistant answers sellers’ questions about meetings and generates follow-up emails and case studies.
  • Action item extraction: AI automatically extracts action items from recordings and assigns them to sales team members with links back to the exact moment in the conversations.
  • Sentiment and tone analysis: Uses AI to analyze sales meeting transcriptions for tone, emotion, tension, or enthusiasm.
  • Smart summaries: Create a pre-call summary of a customer’s latest exchanges, deal value, and key decision-makers gathered from your CRM and past sales calls.
