Longevity Clinics: Programs, Costs & Evidence in 2026

By Niko Hems, M.Sc.Published on 20 April 20269 min read

The market for preventive health checkups is growing, with a projected annual growth rate of 8.4% through 2030 (Research and Markets, 2024). Within this trend, longevity clinics are gaining significant traction. They promise not only to detect diseases early but to make the aging process itself measurable. Yet, the term "longevity clinic" remains vague, and the services offered are often opaque.

What exactly does a longevity clinic provide? How does it differ from a standard health checkup or a wellness retreat? What are the leading providers, and what are the costs involved?

This article provides a comprehensive overview of longevity clinics, covering the scientific foundations, provider comparisons, program structures, costs, and insurance considerations.

What Are Longevity Clinics?

A longevity clinic is a medical facility focused on extending a person's healthy lifespan, or "healthspan." The goal is not simply to maximize lifespan but to increase the number of years lived in good physical and cognitive health.

Traditional preventive care looks for already established diseases. Longevity clinics intervene earlier. They aim to measure biological aging processes at the cellular and molecular levels to delay the onset of age-related conditions like cardiovascular disease, neurodegeneration, cancer, and type 2 diabetes.

The difference from a standard checkup is substantial. A typical government-sponsored health screening might include a basic blood panel with fasting glucose and total cholesterol, a urine sample, and a blood pressure reading. Advanced imaging, functional diagnostics, and specialized biomarkers are not included. The entire appointment often lasts just 15 minutes.

The Science of Longevity: What Do These Clinics Actually Measure?

Modern longevity research is guided by the "Hallmarks of Aging," nine key biological processes that drive aging (López-Otín et al., Cell 2013). These include genomic instability, telomere attrition, epigenetic alterations, and chronic inflammation.

High-quality longevity diagnostics measure indicators of these processes, viewing the body as an interconnected system. Key metrics include:

  • Cardiovascular Markers: More precise values like ApoB (number of atherogenic particles), Lp(a) (a genetic risk factor), hs-CRP (an inflammatory marker), and NT-proBNP (a marker of cardiac stress) provide a more accurate picture of cardiovascular risk than standard cholesterol alone.
  • Metabolic Markers: The HOMA-IR index can detect insulin resistance years before fasting glucose levels rise. An oral glucose tolerance test (OGTT) shows how the body responds to sugar.
  • Inflammaging: Chronic, low-grade inflammation promotes age-related diseases. Markers like hs-CRP and cytokines such as Interleukin-6 (IL-6, available in Evolve® and up) make this state measurable.
  • Functional Metrics: Maximal oxygen uptake (VO₂max) is considered one of the strongest single predictors of all-cause mortality and is measured via cardiopulmonary exercise testing (CPET) (Mandsager et al., JAMA 2018).
  • Epigenetic Markers: Biological age is determined through DNA methylation patterns. Algorithms known as "epigenetic clocks" estimate whether the body is aging faster or slower than its chronological age would suggest.

This comprehensive dataset allows for the identification of risk constellations and the development of preventive strategies long before symptoms arise.

Lanserhof focuses on a holistic experience: regeneration, detoxification, and mental recovery in a luxurious setting. The multi-day stay is central to the concept, with diagnostics being one component. It is ideal for those seeking a restorative break while also receiving medical insights.

YEARS operates differently. The visit lasts one day. The goal is not on-site relaxation but the creation of a deep, scientifically-grounded dataset for a long-term health strategy. This model is suited for individuals who want precise findings and a physician-validated plan without investing several days.

Both approaches have their merits. The choice depends on whether your primary goal is relaxation or in-depth diagnostics.

Longevity Clinic Costs and Insurance Coverage

Costs vary significantly depending on the depth of the program:

  • Individual Tests (€200 – €500): Epigenetic age tests or specialized blood panels available online, often without physician interpretation.
  • Outpatient Checkups (€1,900 – €8,000): Comprehensive diagnostic days with broad biomarker panels, imaging, and functional tests, as offered by Preventicum or YEARS (Core® and Evolve®).
  • Deep-Dive Analyses (€8,000 – €20,000): Programs incorporating omics technologies and whole-body MRI, such as the YEARS Ultimate® program.
  • Inpatient Retreats (€7,000 – €50,000+): Multi-day stays including accommodation, meals, and therapeutic offerings, like those at Lanserhof.

The Role of Private Health Insurance

Public or state health insurance systems typically do not cover the costs of comprehensive longevity checkups. For those with private insurance, reimbursement depends on the specific plan and the clinic's billing method.

YEARS bills according to the official German medical fee schedule (GOÄ). Every test—whether ultrasound, ECG, or lab value—is itemized as an individual medical service on the invoice. Since comprehensive diagnostics often uncover medically relevant findings (e.g., a vitamin D deficiency, early insulin resistance, or elevated liver enzymes), this provides a medical justification for the tests performed. This can improve the chances of reimbursement, though it is not guaranteed. It is advisable to speak with your insurance provider or contact YEARS for a consultation before booking.

Key Technologies in Longevity Medicine

Epigenetic Clocks: Measuring Biological Age

Chronological age counts years. Biological age describes the actual state of your body at a cellular level. The most precise method for determining biological age is the analysis of DNA methylation patterns.

These patterns change predictably throughout life. Algorithms like the Horvath clock, the Hannum clock, or the phenotype-based PhenoAge read these patterns to calculate a biological age. If it is lower than your chronological age, your body is aging more slowly than average. Modern longevity clinics use these analyses to evaluate interventions and better assess personal risk for age-related diseases.

VO₂max: The Underrated Longevity Marker

Maximal oxygen uptake (VO₂max) measures the performance of the cardiovascular system under stress. A study of over 120,000 participants showed that individuals with elite cardiorespiratory fitness had an almost 80% lower mortality risk compared to those in the lowest fitness category (Mandsager et al., JAMA 2018).

This makes VO₂max a fundamental health marker, not just a metric for athletes. It is measured via cardiopulmonary exercise testing (CPET) on a stationary bike or treadmill. Clients who complete this test as part of the YEARS Core® program receive a baseline value that they can tangibly improve through targeted training.

Inflammation in Aging (Inflammaging)

Chronic, low-grade inflammation drives the aging process. Known as "inflammaging," this process promotes atherosclerosis, neurodegeneration, and cancer (Franceschi et al., Ann. N. Y. Acad. Sci. 2000).

Longevity clinics specifically measure inflammatory markers like hs-CRP and Interleukin-6 (IL-6). An elevated hs-CRP level can indicate cardiovascular risk, even if cholesterol levels are normal. This is clinically relevant: in the JUPITER trial, patients with normal LDL but elevated hs-CRP had a significantly higher risk of heart attack (Ridker et al., NEJM 2008).

Omics: Genomics, Epigenomics, and Microbiomics

Omics technologies are part of the most comprehensive longevity programs and currently represent the deepest available layer of personalized medicine.

  • Genomics: Whole-genome sequencing identifies inherited genetic predispositions for diseases like colon cancer, familial hypercholesterolemia, or breast cancer. Pharmacogenetics also reveals how the body may react to certain medications.
  • Epigenomics: This field analyzes DNA methylation patterns, providing a dynamic picture of how lifestyle and environment influence gene activity.
  • Microbiomics: The composition of gut bacteria affects metabolism, the immune system, and mental health. This analysis is part of YEARS Ultimate® and is not included in Evolve®.

How to Choose the Right Longevity Clinic for You

Longevity clinics offer a proactive form of healthcare that goes far beyond traditional checkups. The choice of provider depends on your specific priorities.

For those who want to receive a maximum amount of data in a single day, the outpatient diagnostic model at YEARS in Berlin offers a suitable structure. For those who also want to step away from daily life and combine diagnostics with a multi-day retreat, Lanserhof may be a better fit. For those seeking a solid, established option without travel, Preventicum in Essen is another consideration.

In any case, it is wise to discuss the specific program details, the scientific basis of the methods, and potential insurance reimbursement before making a commitment.
