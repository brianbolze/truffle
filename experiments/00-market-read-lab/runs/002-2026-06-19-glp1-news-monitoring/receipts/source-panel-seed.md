# Source Panel Seed

Use this as a starting point, not a mandatory checklist. Keep the panel tiny.

## Baseline To Stress-Test

- Run 000 `read.md`: GLP-1 pricing visibility / offer-structure read.
- Run 000 `run-notes.md`: branded-drug ambiguity, compounding volatility, and freshness
  caveats.

## Candidate Source Types

- FDA / official regulatory pages about semaglutide and tirzepatide compounding and
  shortage status.
- Manufacturer or official cash-pay/access pages for branded GLP-1 options
  (Wegovy, Zepbound, Saxenda if relevant).
- One or two recent industry/news sources only if they identify a change that could
  invalidate the stored pricing/access read.

## Guardrails

- Do not make a broad GLP-1 news digest.
- Prefer official sources over commentary when they answer the question.
- Capture which source types would need recurring monitoring, not just what changed.
- Treat this as a system-test for a minimal News / Monitoring routine.
