# Consumer Review

Question: **Where did Truffle create reader value, and where did it fall short?**

## Verdict

- **Valuable?** Partly — strong on the factory floor (the 7-brand map is clean and citation-graded), weaker as a reader instrument (the headline finding was largely predictable; the generalization claim is honest but thin at n=7).
- **Why:** A luxury/retail analyst already knows Rolex/Patek/AP/Lange don't publish prices — that posture is industry common knowledge. Truffle confirmed it from captured pages, but confirming the obvious ≠ surfacing the non-obvious. The genuine value is narrower: (a) the **Cartier category-grain split** (watches gated, jewelry/fragrance priced) is a real, well-documented observation; (b) the gate-type × gate-grain vocabulary is a useful analytical distinction, correctly held as recur-watch rather than over-built; (c) the **re-confirmation that the structured price-visibility token is dead off telehealth** (0/7, third vertical) is a real calibration signal for engine builders, not a reader insight.
- **Where Truffle added value:** Cartier's intra-brand split documented with specific captured price ranges — a usable downstream ingredient. Absence language clean throughout: "not published by convention" vs "not captured" — the store held without distorting the market posture.
- **Where Truffle added little or fell short:** Four of seven claims (luxury maisons publish no price) reduce to "luxury brand has no e-commerce" — a finding analysts bring before opening Truffle. "Swatch/Casio publish full e-commerce prices" is equally non-novel. The gate-type × gate-grain vocab is introduced then shelved — reads more like naming something than finding it.
- **What the consumer can do now:** Use the Cartier category-grain finding as a cited ingredient; use the 7-brand map as a denominator check; a downstream agent can assert "no watch price on Rolex/Patek/AP/Lange site" citing C1–C4 without re-browsing. Real, if modest.
- **What made it safer / better than generic Claude + web search:** Clean provenance — every claim traces to a specific `profile.md` path at a 2026-05-31 capture. Generic Claude would likely invent specific price ranges (C5) or assert staleness-unknown facts; Truffle's labeled absence is the safety advantage.
- **Biggest limit:** Cohort of 7 is thin for a generalization claim (4 in the same tier, same posture). The Cartier straddler is the most generalization-worthy point, but one straddler inside a hand-drawn 7-brand cohort doesn't prove gate-grain at scale. The read concedes this (W1 is recur-watch, not a build).
- **Human follow-up needed:** Validate the gate-type × gate-grain vocab against ≥1 more vertical (Finance/VC, per next-run advice) before using it for a real read. For actual luxury price intelligence, the read correctly flags dealer/grey-market sources (Chrono24, MSRP sheets) as out of scope — a human must source those.
