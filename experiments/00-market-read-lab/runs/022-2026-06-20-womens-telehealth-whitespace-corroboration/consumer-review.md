# Consumer Review

Question: **Was the read itself valuable enough for a human or agent to trust, reuse, or act on?**

## Verdict

- **Valuable? Yes.**
- **Why:** The run converted a store-only *hypothesis* (run 020's "15 men vs 5 women"
  audience asymmetry is probably a selection-bias artifact) into a **confirmed, dated,
  cited finding** — exactly what the prior store-only run said it could not do. The
  load-bearing deliverable is the **store reconciliation**: 8 of 9 cross-source-recurrence
  menopause brands are absent from the store, and the store's 5 women-leaning brands are
  nearly disjoint *by category* from the market's menopause set (store = 3/5
  GLP-1/weight-loss-framed; market-missing = menopause/HRT specialists). That two-lane
  surprise deepens the insight past a simple count gap.
- **What the consumer can do now:** Act on a concrete, tiered capture-candidate worklist —
  tier-1 (cross-recurrence, not in store): Midi Health, Winona, Evernow, Gennev, Stella,
  HerMD, Allara; tier-2 (strong single-source + SERP-confirmed): Alloy, Elektra Health,
  Pandia Health — ready for `/research-company` without further research.
- **What made it safer / better than generic Claude + web search:** Generic search can list
  menopause brands and would name most of tier-1. The Truffle-specific value is the *store
  reconciliation* — claims about a **specific, versioned artifact** (the 54-pack telehealth
  store): which brands are captured, that Wisp is captured `all-genders` not women-anchored,
  and that the captured women-leaning set is weight-loss-framed while the missing segment is
  menopause/HRT-framed. Plus the cross-source-recurrence discipline (run-012 method, a
  2-listicle intersection with affiliate-confound caveats, graded + dated receipt) and the
  accumulated triage memory (MRL-001/002/009 Evidence Logs) a fresh session can't reproduce.
- **Biggest limit:** Two affiliate/SEO listicles are a **coverage radar, not a census**;
  the 9-brand head is strong but a third source could shift it ±1–2. And the read is
  **membership-only** — it confirms the segment exists and is uncaptured, makes **no** size,
  share, or demand claim. "Store-absent" = real + uncaptured, not large.
- **Human follow-up needed:** The high-value next step (a `/research-company` capture of
  tier-1) needs approval (Firecrawl spend) — not autonomous. Also the 3rd-bounded-live-run
  checkpoint glance (011/012/022).
