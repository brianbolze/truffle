# Consumer Review

Question: **Where did Truffle create reader value, and where did it fall short?**

Start with the produced read as a roadmap instrument: would a real Truffle consumer be
glad it exists, and what did it teach about Truffle's value frontier? If Truffle added
no value, say so plainly. A no-value-found run can be complete when it cleanly maps why.

Truffle is the farm, not the chef: it should provide trustworthy company ingredients for human or downstream-system judgment. Use the diagnostics below after the verdict, not as boxes to fill.

## Verdict

- **Valuable? partly.** High roadmap value, low direct reader value.
- **Why:** This is an internal schema audit, not a market answer. It produced a clean,
  honest calibration result — the universal taxonomy is *not* telehealth-overfit for
  operating companies; the one structural break is capital allocators — but yields
  nothing a Strategist / Pantry / First Contact consumer can act on. As the lab's first
  read outside its design vertical (overdue after 26 telehealth-internal runs), it earns
  its slot via engine learning, not a deliverable.
- **Where Truffle added value:** the engine builder / schema steward. The
  capital-allocator finding (S1/S2/G1) is backlog-ready — 7 firms, 4 encodings, the lone
  store-wide `business_model: Other` — all meeting the TAXONOMIES.md "promote when the
  gap recurs" threshold. The denominator caveat (G2, profiled N=126) is a clean MRL-001
  addendum. The positive calibration (O1) earns a line in any "what the engine can do"
  write-up.
- **Where Truffle added little or fell short:** zero direct consumer value — no company
  intelligence, no market shape, nothing for Scott-Witt-style 5-second handoff.
- **What the consumer can do now:** the Pantry can assert "the taxonomy generalizes
  cross-vertical for sellers" *with receipts* — useful for any future query that groups
  by `offering_category` outside telehealth.
- **What made it safer / better than generic Claude + web search:** the answer is a
  receipted audit of the *actual captured store* (every count a full store-wide
  enumeration), not a plausible-sounding claim about taxonomy design. Generic Claude
  couldn't see the 4-way encoding split because it can't read the frontmatter.
- **Biggest limit:** the read is intrinsic — it audits encoding *consistency*, not
  correctness of individual calls, and says nothing about market completeness. It also
  can't know whether the allocator break is the *only* structural edge or the first
  found; a larger non-telehealth capture pass (more industrials, nonprofit `.org`
  entities) would test that.
- **Human follow-up needed:** the in-scope decision — whether to tighten the
  Investor/Holding gating rule — is a human call (a triage candidate, below), not a
  Loop-2 action.
