## Lenses

**Steward** — The system is honest. Provenance comments on most edges, explicit STRAIN flags on inferred ones, "undisclosed-or-uncaptured" language on bare empties, and "floor / not a census" framing throughout. The one slip risk — that `parent: []` could be read as "confirmed independent" — was surfaced explicitly as G2, and the read avoided it. The qualtrics↔delighted unreciprocated edge (C4) was faithfully recorded rather than smoothed over; Truffle captured what the sites disclose, and the reconciliation test exposed a real discrepancy. The system's State boundary was not crossed: no ownership concentration fact was claimed, and the "lead, not measured rate" framing on the 4-cluster finding is correct given a doubly-bounded denominator.

**Dev Agent** — No toil to remove via a primitive. The two-grep + per-target existence join (F1) is real friction but one sighting — do not build a helper. The `query-time-grouping-enough` bifurcation (groupable ≠ joinable ≠ claimable) is the sharpest articulation of that heuristic yet and is worth folding into QUERYING.md language if and when MRL-002 graduates — but that's a recipe edit, not a build. The one actionable signal: the absence-discipline gap on `parent: []` (G2) is fixable by a one-line convention note and exemplars in the capture playbook, not a schema change or new field. If MRL-008 eventually graduates with a flavor-aware rule, G2 belongs in the State-field sub-section.

**Founder** — This run landed a genuine first: the first time the lab probed the clean joinable axis end-to-end and found it behaves identically to the messy prose axis. That's a compounding insight — every relation flavor (pharmacy/prose, clinical/prose, corporate/structured) hits the same wall. The "one fully-reconciled edge in the whole store" finding (lifemd↔rexmd) is the best possible illustration of the gap and the cheapest possible evidence — it came from a grep, zero spend. The capture-candidate list (Thirty Madison, Niagen Bioscience, LifeMD siblings) is the right "what would unlock this" proposal and was correctly framed as propose-don't-execute.
