# Consumer Review

Question: **Where did Truffle create reader value, and where did it fall short?**

## Verdict

- **Valuable? Partly.** The durable value is **builder-facing**, not a novel market fact.
- **Why:** A strategist learns "hims and ro are actively spending on Google; henrymeds and
  lifemd have gone quiet; remedymeds/eden are invisible on this surface" — but the two
  active spenders are exactly the brands an industry-adjacent reader already knew were
  well-resourced. The headline deliverable is the **source-family proof** (ads_transparency
  is cheap, reliable, domain-keyed) + the **push≠demand discipline**, not "who's winning."
- **Where Truffle added value:** (1) confirmed a new push/resourcing source family the store
  cannot see today (1/130, 0 GLP-1); (2) a discriminating 3-state split (active/quiet/zero)
  even at n=6; (3) the most thorough false-confidence inventory of any lab run, each trap
  tied to a specific receipt row; (4) the tenure≠recency counterexample (lifemd: longest
  tenure, ~309d quiet).
- **Where Truffle added little / fell short:** the market fact is thin and familiar; n=6 of
  ~25 with two unresolved zeros blocks any cohort-level claim; single-point/single-region so
  the most interesting question — is a quiet brand's silence a *strategic retreat* or a
  *budget squeeze*? — is unanswerable, and the read names no cheap path to it.
- **What the consumer can do now:** use ads-presence as a **budget-motion filter** to layer
  onto demand-side signals (Trends/SERP from prior runs) — not as a standalone competitive read.
- **Safer than generic Claude + web search?** Yes — primary SerpAPI captures with exact
  first/last-shown dates and advertiser legal names, not a hallucinated "they advertise a lot."
- **Biggest limit:** single-region, single-point, two unresolved zeros, no demand-side
  counterpart; the signal is real but too thin + too familiar to update conviction.
- **Human follow-up needed:** none required; a 2nd ads read (or Meta/Apify) would resolve the
  zeros and is a resourcing call, not a design one.

## Lens check

- **Strategist:** lands plainly but doesn't update conviction — the active spenders are
  already-known names; most useful combined with demand signal, not standalone.
- **Pantry / downstream:** highest value here — the source-family validation (6 live
  captures, 0 schema_drift, domain-keyed) lets a downstream system adopt ads_transparency as
  a standard signal capture with concrete backing.
- **First Contact:** little — the value is meta (tool/builder validation); the market fact is
  already context for anyone in the GLP-1 space.
