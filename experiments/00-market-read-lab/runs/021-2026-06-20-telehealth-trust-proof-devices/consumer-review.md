Do not cite the named exemplar sets for C3 (pharmacy accreditation positives) without checking: defymedical, brellohealth, maximustribe, and ro-co are wrong; use eden-health, struthealth, hydramed, gethealthspan, mylifeforce, innerbalance instead. The brief-ready line ("LegitScript is the floor; a named medical bench and real pharmacy accreditation are where a brand can still look more legitimate than the pack") is safe to reuse once the LegitScript framing shifts from "~75%" to "~61%."

The read draws on 56 owned-page captures with capture dates, not SERP snippets — so "not shown" has a specific evidentiary meaning (not found on captured pages) that web search cannot replicate. That's the root cause of the C2/C3/C4 miscounts — and the evidence verifier's independent re-count surfaced a likely polarity error in LegitScript classification (~9 "not shown" domains counted as positive). The pattern direction is right; the integers need a corrective pass before any read cites precise counts.
- **Human follow-up needed:** Correct C2 (LegitScript ~33/54 shown, ~61%); correct C3 named positives (remove defymedical, brellohealth, maximustribe, ro-co; keep eden-health, struthealth, hydramed, gethealthspan, mylifeforce, innerbalance); correct C4 (~38 shown / ~16 not-shown); remove struthealth from C5 money-back named set.
