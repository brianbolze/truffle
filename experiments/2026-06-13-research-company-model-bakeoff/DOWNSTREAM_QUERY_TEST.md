# Downstream query test

Read only one candidate artifact set at a time. Do not open the packet until after answering.

Answer:

1. What does this company sell?
2. Who is the target customer?
3. Are prices published, partial, or gated?
4. What is the business model?
5. What are the strongest site-attested proof points?
6. What should a downstream consumer not assume?
7. If this is telehealth, what are the cohort-specific cuts?
8. What one follow-up capture would most improve the artifact?

Then audit each answer against the packet and label:

- `supported`
- `partially supported`
- `unsupported`
- `not answerable from candidate`

The best artifact is the one that lets a cold consumer answer accurately with the least raw-evidence rescue.
