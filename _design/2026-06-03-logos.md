# Design: the `logos:{}` set — multi-ratio brand marks

> **What this is.** The opt-in module that captures a company's brand marks at the ratios a consumer
> actually renders — a square **logomark**, a rectangular **wordmark**, and a wide **og** cover — each with
> its measurements attached. This doc is the *reasoning*; the contract you'd *obey* is the proposed `logos:{}`
> block in [`SCHEMA.md`](../SCHEMA.md) + the logos recipe in [`firecrawl-capture.md`](../skills/research-company/firecrawl-capture.md).
> Companion to the [frame](2026-05-29-frame.md) and the [offerings module](2026-06-03-offerings.md) (same module shape).
> **Status: PROPOSED — pending sign-off. Nothing in the live contract yet.**

## Why it exists

`logo_url` today is **favicon-grade for most brands** — a 16–32px icon standing in for a company whose real
identity is a wordmark (across the telehealth cohort: hims, doordash-style, casio… all favicon-fallback). Useless
on a slide. The motivating consumer is **real, not hypothetical**:

> **"Put every key competitor's logo on a competitive-analysis slide and as the cover of its Notion 'Organizations' row."**

That needs two ratios a favicon can't serve — a clean square **logomark** for a grid tile/avatar, a **wordmark**
for a comparison row — plus a wide **og** image for the Notion cover. It's **durable State** (a brand's marks are
what it *is*, from its own pages), **opt-in per cohort** (most consumers never render a logo), and **near-free over
the baseline** — it reuses the homepage payload and full-page screenshot the capture already takes, adding only the
agent's eyes for the wordmark. That's why it's a module, not a heavier `profile.md`.

It is **lighter than `offerings.md`**: no per-company roster file and no standalone contract — it's three frontmatter
entries + a recipe + a few committed asset files. So the "contract" lives as a `SCHEMA.md` field section, not a `LOGOS.md`.

## The shape (proposed)

`logo_url` stays (back-compat) and, on new captures, **canonicalizes to the wordmark** — the one mark that's almost
always good. The new `logos:{}` adds the typed set, each entry carrying **its own measurements as facts, not gates**:

```yaml
# Visual identity — logo_url now points at the WORDMARK on new captures (the reliable mark), not the favicon.
logo_url: assets/wordmark.svg          # the one logo for "just give me the logo"

# Logos (2.5, opt-in module). Each entry records what was found + its measurements; the CONSUMER applies the bar.
logos:
  wordmark: { src: assets/wordmark.svg, w: 232, h: 65 }                                       # ~rectangle, mark+name — the primary slot, ~always present
  logomark: { src: "https://www.google.com/s2/favicons?domain=hims.com&sz=256", px: 180, transparent: false }  # square — best-effort
  og:       { src: "https://www.hims.com/.../og.png", w: 1200, h: 630 }                        # wide cover (Notion) — best-effort
```

- **`src`** is a stable URL where one exists (google-s2 logomark, og:image, a hostable `logo_url`) — regenerable, no file.
  Where there's no URL (an inline-SVG wordmark), it's a **committed text file** under `store/<domain>/assets/` (SVG is text — commits clean, scales infinitely).
- **A slot is omitted only on true absence** (no `og:image` declared at all). A *small/weak* mark is **recorded with its
  measurement** (`logomark: {px: 32}`), never silently dropped — the consumer decides if 32px is enough.

## Key decisions

The settled calls (each with its *why* + the probe that settled it; full evidence in the [probe FINDINGS](../experiments/2026-06-03-logo-probe/FINDINGS.md)).

- **Additive `logos:{}`, never replace `logo_url` — 2.5 MINOR, grandfathered.** *(2.4 was taken by the parallel `Run profile` line.)* Replacing it is a MAJOR re-stamp of 60+
  profiles for zero gain; additive means old profiles read empty = "predates the module," no backfill.
- **`logo_url` canonicalizes to the wordmark on new captures.** The single "just give me the logo" should be the mark
  that's almost always usable — Probe 3: **wordmark 16/16 deck-ready vs logomark 13/16**. Old `logo_url`s stay (grandfathered).
- **Measurements are recorded facts; the consumer applies the bar — we never pre-drop.** A Notion cover tolerates a
  smaller og than a full-bleed slide; a hard ≥128px filter would silently lose a mark a tiny-icon consumer could use.
  Record `px`/`w`/`h`/`transparent`; omit only on absence. *(This is Probe 3's "gates" reframed — strength came from
  not over-deciding at capture time.)*
- **The wordmark needs the agent's eyes; logomark + og are deterministic.** A blind image scan can't tell a brand mark
  from a press wall — Probe 2's naive scan grabbed **NYT, Afterpay, Airbnb, a Zendesk asset**. So the agent picks and
  confirms the wordmark, and judges `transparent` on a checkerboard tile (which `hasAlpha` can't — Probe 3: 5 marks
  report alpha-yes yet carry an opaque box). `sips` reads the dimensions. "AI at ingestion," not a scrape.
- **Source chains, measured not trusted.** *logomark* = the larger **measured** of Google `s2/favicons?sz=256` and the
  apple-touch-icon — Probe 1: `sz=256` silently returns 32px for ro/gogeviti/marek; Probe 3: a *measured*-256 from S2 is
  true native detail, so **reading the bytes is a sufficient quality gate**. *wordmark* = the hostable `logo_url` (7/16)
  else extract the inline `<svg>` → text / crop the header (Probe 2: 16/16 recoverable *with* the agent). *og* = a
  *declared* `og:image` verified at **≥600px actual** width — Probe 3: the declared meta size lies (marek declares 600, serves 30).
- **No-URL assets commit as text SVG under `store/<domain>/assets/`; stable-URL assets store just the URL.** An inline-SVG
  wordmark is the only copy, and as text it sidesteps the no-binaries-in-git rule. *⚠ Flagged, not reopening: per your #3,
  a rare raster crop (no URL, not text — 1/16 in the probe) goes to a gitignored sidecar, which makes it **local-only /
  unrecoverable on a fresh clone**. If that ever bites, the fix is to commit the tiny PNG.*
- **Opt-in module, asked in the step-2.5 pre-flight batch — like `offerings.md`.** Most captures don't render logos; the
  express on-ramp carries a `logos` ask to skip the question. Destination: `logos:{}` in `profile.md` + files in `assets/`.

## Principles — why it stays light

- **Record observations, not verdicts.** `px`/`transparent`/`og.w` are measured facts; "is this mark good enough for *my*
  slide" is the consumer's call. Same anti-hallucination grain as offerings' verbatim price.
- **Vision for semantics, `sips` for dimensions.** The thing a script can't judge (is this the brand's real mark; is the
  background baked) the agent already does by looking; the thing vision shouldn't guess (exact px) the script measures.
  **No Python image dep** — `fc.py` stays stdlib-only; `sips` shells out, rsvg/ImageMagick are recipe-time tools.
- **Pure State.** A brand's marks are what it *is*. Which competitors matter / how they rank is Judgment — it stays in the
  venture. The og→Notion-cover push is a downstream sync; the store only *holds* the og.
- **No cross-company key.** A logo is keyed within its company; there's no canonical "the logo" across brands — the same
  move that lets the engine skip entity-resolution.
- **Anti-Doro.** No image ML, no perceptual hashing, no served logo API. `curl` + `sips` + the agent's eyes, plus
  rsvg-convert/ImageMagick at recipe time for SVG-rasterize and header-crop.

## What we refused, and why

<details>
<summary>The richer designs probed, then deliberately cut</summary>

- **Replace `logo_url` with `logos:{}`** — a MAJOR re-stamp of the whole corpus for no read it doesn't already serve; additive grandfathers cleanly.
- **Pass/fail gates** — a hard ≥128px (or transparent-only) filter *silently* drops a mark a smaller-context consumer could use. Record the measurement; let the bar live at use-time.
- **Auto-removing baked backgrounds** (corner flood-fill) — notoriously mangles logos. Flag `transparent: false`; a human glances.
- **A deterministic "is this the right / on-brand logo" check** — that's semantic, it's the vision step's job. Doing it with pixels is exactly how Probe 2 v2 grabbed the press wall.
- **A sharpness score in v1** — needs PIL/numpy and only catches non-S2 upscale-blur, a minor case now that S2's 256s proved genuinely crisp. Deferred until a dep earns itself.
- **og as anything but best-effort** — the least reliable slot (11/16, five distinct failure modes). Never a required field; gated on a real ≥600px image.
- **A standalone `LOGOS.md` contract + per-company roster file** — over-built for three frontmatter entries; logos live in `SCHEMA.md` + the recipe.

</details>

## Decision log

<details>
<summary>How the design got here — a three-probe arc (all 2026-06-03)</summary>

- **Probe 1 — logomark resolution.** Measured actual px of google-s2-256 vs apple-touch across 16 telehealth competitors:
  **13/16 clear a 128px bar**, but only via `max(measured px of both)` — `sz=256` is a request, not a promise (returns 32px for three brands).
- **Probe 2 — wordmark fill.** **7/16 have a hostable wordmark URL; 9/16 ship it as inline `<svg>`** (→ extraction). v2's naive
  "widest image" scan grabbed press/payment logos (NYT/Afterpay/Airbnb/Zendesk) → the lesson: trust only the vetted `logo_url`
  + on-domain JSON-LD; a blind scan can't tell a brand mark from a wall.
- **Probe 3 — pixel acceptance (16-brand Claude workflow).** Fan-out materialize+vision-judge, then an independent synthesizer:
  **logomark 13/16 · wordmark 16/16 · og 11/16 · union 16/16** (every brand has ≥1 deck-ready ratio; the 3 logomark-fails are
  all covered by a hostable wordmark). A measured-256 from S2 is true native detail; extraction yields a usable wordmark for all
  16 *with the agent*; the independent pass flipped zero verdicts. Evidence + eyeball-able [contact sheet](../experiments/2026-06-03-logo-probe/contact-sheet.html).

</details>
