# Strategic Pillars / Themes
Pillars are the roadmap tag legend. Use them to classify every Truffle initiative so the roadmap shows where investment is concentrated — or starved.

- **Coverage** — breadth of the captured universe: more companies, cohorts, relations, and discovery.
- **Depth** — richness and trust on a single company: profiles, SKUs, visual identity, provenance, and capture QA.
- **Freshness** — the time axis: signals, monitoring, history, changelog, and refresh.
- **Access** — raw read-back without opinion added: query paths, MCP, exports, and other ways to retrieve the store.
- **Synthesis** — the read-time opinion layer: briefs, lenses, convergence, maps, presets, and other interpreted outputs.

Pairs with [User Personas](./personas.md): pillars = *what value* a bet creates; personas = *whose shoes* you stand in to feel it.
