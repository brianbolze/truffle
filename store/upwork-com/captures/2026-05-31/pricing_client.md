## Frequently asked questions

What is Upwork’s client service fee and how is it calculated?

For the [Basic](https://www.upwork.com/talent-marketplace) plan, when you hire on Upwork, you pay a 3% or 5% Marketplace fee on all payments that you make to freelancers for fixed-price and hourly projects, Project Catalog projects, bonuses, and Direct Contracts, and BYO contracts.

For the [Business Plus](https://www.upwork.com/business-plus) plan, when you hire on Upwork, you pay a 8% or 10% Business Plus fee on all payments that you make to freelancers for fixed-price and hourly projects, Project Catalog projects, and bonuses.

The discounted Marketplace fee of 3% and the discounted Business Plus fee of 8% are available for eligible clients in the U.S. who pay with a checking account. Check to see if you’re eligible [here](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211068088).

When do I pay a client service fee?

For the Basic plan and Business Plus plan, you only pay a client service fee when you make a payment and your default [billing method](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/211067988) will be charged automatically for the balance due.

For the Business Plus plan (with Net 30 terms), payment is due 30 days from the end of the invoiced month. Note: Monthly invoicing with payment terms is only available by application to US-based companies.

Can I change my plan later on?

Any client with Account Admin access can upgrade their account at any time. There are no penalties or restrictions for upgrading. Any client with Admin Full access can request to downgrade their Business Plus plan to the Basic plan. Business Plus runs on a 30-day membership cycle, and downgrades take effect at the end of your membership cycle. You can manage your membership plan for your Upwork account under [Settings/membership/change\_ plan](https://www.upwork.com/nx/plans/client/change-plan).

Are there any other fees?

Both the Basic and Business Plus plans do not include upfront costs to join.

In addition, you also pay a one-time contract initiation fee for each new Marketplace and Project Catalog contract.

For Business Plus, there is no contract initiation fee, with the exception of fixed-price contracts of $100 USD or less.

Business Plus clients can use direct contracts to invite and pay freelancers sourced outside of Upwork. Each active direct contract is billed at $49/month per active contract.

What are Contract Initiation Fees?

Contract Initiation Fees are one-time fees charged to clients when starting a new Marketplace or Project Catalog contract with a freelancer or agency on Upwork. The fee ranges from $0.99 to $14.99 per contract. For hourly contracts, the fee is charged at the time of the first invoice. For fixed-price contracts, the fee is charged when you fund your first milestone. Contract Initiation Fees are non-refundable and are not assessed as part of the Marketplace Fee.

[See details](https://support.upwork.com/hc/en-us/articles/26106318334611-What-is-the-Contract-Initiation-Fee-on-Upwork)

For Business Plus, a one-time Contract Initiation Fee of up to $4.99 USD applies to new Service Contracts under $100 USD.

How do I get 30-day payment terms?

Business Plus clients in the U.S. may apply for monthly invoicing with 30-day payment terms through our third-party partner. The application process requires submitting business details and supporting documentation.

Based on your business profile, your application may result in:

• Immediate approval to activate 30-day payments

• A request for additional information, or

• A decline if the service cannot be provided for your business.

How does Upwork make money?

Upwork earns revenue through service fees on both sides of the marketplace. Clients pay a service fee on contracts — 5% on Basic and 10% on Business Plus. Talent pays a service fee ranging from 0% to 15% per contract.
