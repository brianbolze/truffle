### Hayden Brown

President & CEO

For more than two decades, Upwork has been a pioneer of a better way to work. We've enabled businesses and professionals to thrive through major shifts – from migrating to the cloud, capturing the potential of mobile, to creating new value through social media. No matter how needs and skills evolve, our purpose remains the same: To create opportunity in every era of work.

Today, we stand at a new frontier. AI is transforming what is possible for companies and careers alike. Once again, Upwork is the place where businesses and talent come to meet this moment. Our platform empowers everyone – from Fortune 100 enterprises to ambitious startups – to access the human and AI expertise they need to move fast, solve problems, and scale. Facilitated by our mindful AI work agent, Uma™, our AI-powered operating system supports every step of turning aspirations into reality.

Upwork is where clients and talent achieve things that they never imagined possible. Whether you're here to grow your business, advance your craft, or lead your industry into this new era, we are glad you have found Upwork.

Let's build what's next – together.

Hayden Brown

President and CEO

Hayden Brown is president and CEO of [Upwork](https://www.upwork.com/), the world’s human and AI-powered work marketplace, where Fortune 100 companies through small businesses and highly skilled independent professionals from 180 countries connect and work together. Hayden joined Upwork in 2011 as a product manager and held roles running Upwork’s marketplace business, as chief product officer, and as chief marketing officer before being named CEO in January 2020.

‍

Hayden is driven by her passion for delivering on Upwork’s purpose to create opportunity in every era of work. Under her leadership, Upwork has vastly expanded its product portfolio, and today, she is animated about leveraging cutting-edge technology, including AI, to make work easier and more delightful for businesses and professionals alike. Most recently, she oversaw the introduction of Uma, Upwork's Mindful AI, a powerful AI work companion designed to help businesses and freelancers every step of the way across the Upwork experience.

‍

During her time with the company, Upwork has grown from $10 million to more than $750 million in annual revenue, and from $100 million to more than $4 billion in annual client spend. Upwork’s workforce, now numbering approximately 2000 team members, has always been made up of ~75% freelancers working remotely from around the world on the Upwork platform.

‍

After receiving an AB in politics at Princeton, Hayden started her career at McKinsey and Company as a business analyst, where she fell in love with tech. She joined Microsoft’s online strategy team and later the company’s corporate strategy group, before starting the corporate development function at LivePerson, and eventually joining Upwork (then oDesk). She has been named to Forbes’ Future of Work 50 and SIA’s Staffing 100 North America lists, and was at the helm when Upwork was named one of TIME’s Most Influential Companies as well as one of Fast Company’s Most Innovative Companies.
