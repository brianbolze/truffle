What is a Home Sleep Test (HST)?

A diagnostic home sleep test is a way to objectively measure how well you sleep using special medical-grade devices, like Happy Ring, at home. These devices measure things like breathing quality, sleep quality, heart function, and more while you sleep. Doctors use the information from the test to find out if you have any sleep problems, like sleep apnea.

The Happy Ring is an FDA-cleared Class II medical device, available with a prescription from a Happy Sleep physician. Home sleep testing services utilize a combination of clearances under K240236 and K210034.

How long do I have to test?

Each Happy Sleep Test membership includes up to 3 nights of testing over a 5 night period. Your test period begins on the day your Happy Ring is delivered.After that you may either return your Happy Ring using the included shipping label or keep it for ongoing sleep monitoring. If you choose to keep Happy Ring, you’ll be automatically enrolled in our annual membership plan. The Happy Sleep Membership allows you to track your sleep every night so you can monitor your progress and continue to meet with your doctor to adjust your care plan.

Is Happy Sleep covered by medical insurance?

Happy Sleep is in network with most health insurance plans (Cigna, Aetna, United, Emblem, Blue Cross, Medicare, and more). Telemedicine visits, sleep testing and oral appliance treatments are covered by most medical insurance for patients who are diagnosed with sleep apnea, and are symptomatic. Coverage does vary according to insurance plans. Your financial responsibility includes your co-pay, deductible, and co-insurance. When you have been prescribed sleep testing and treatment by our staff, we contact your health plan for preauthorization and determine any out-of-pocket expenses. If you don’t have insurance, check out our Pricing page.

Why test for multiple nights?

Two or more nights of home sleep testing increases your success rate and reduces the need for retesting. This ensures our board-certified sleep physicians have sufficient data to interpret your results accurately and provide the best recommendations and care. Since sleep patterns can vary significantly from night to night, continuous monitoring isessential for optimizing your sleep.
