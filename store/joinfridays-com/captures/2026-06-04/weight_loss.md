### How does the Fridays weight loss program work?

Fridays offers clinically backed GLP-1 treatments that help regulate appetite, improve metabolism, and support sustainable weight loss. The process is simple—no in-person visits, no hassle—just effective treatment delivered directly to you.

### What are GLP-1 medications?

GLP-1 medications work by regulating appetite to reduce food intake. Studies have shown that they can lead to weight reductions of up to 15% in people who take them for a year or more, in combination with healthy lifestyle habits\*.

Weight loss is not solely dependent on willpower, as there are biological factors that affect metabolism, hunger, and weight that are beyond your control. GLP-1 medications promote a prolonged sense of fullness, help control cravings, and improve the body's ability to process sugars more efficiently (source). For this reason, these medications are often prescribed for managing type 2 diabetes or obesity.

In chronic weight management, GLP-1 medications are sometimes used in conjunction with a low-calorie diet and increased physical activity. They are also used in patients with diabetes to assist with blood glucose control.

During your initial consultation, your provider will assess your health needs to determine if this type of medication is suitable for you. They will also address any questions you may have about GLP-1 medications.\*Note: It's always important to consult with your healthcare provider for medical advice and personalized treatment options.

### Do I need to follow a strict diet or exercise plan?

Although GLP-1 medications help with weight loss on their own, healthy eating and physical activity can maximize your results. Fridays offers guidance to help you develop sustainable habits alongside your treatment.

### How long will it take for me to see results?

Results may vary, but most people notice a reduction in cravings and appetite within the first few weeks, and visible weight loss usually begins after one or two months of consistent use.

These results are derived from two separate patient cohorts: (1) individuals with initial visits on or after March 1, 2024 and baseline weights exceeding 175 lbs, who recorded an average weight loss of 9.75 lbs within the first month of enrollment; and (2) individuals meeting the same initial criteria with documented weight measurements at 12 months, who demonstrated an average body weight reduction of 15.28% as of April 2025.

‍

Fridays supports patients through a combination of medical care, behavioral coaching, and access to GLP-1-based medications when appropriate. Individual results may vary. The GLP-1-based medications prescribed vary depending on the medical judgment of the provider. The weight loss outcomes presented above are based on an internal review of member data collected beginning March 1, 2024, and span a range of prescribed medications. These outcomes are not tied to a single formulation or brand, nor are they intended to suggest results for any specific medication.

‍

Weight loss is influenced by a variety of factors, including genetics, diet, physical activity, and metabolism. This analysis reflects real-world outcomes observed in our patient population and is intended solely for informational and educational use in advertising materials. Fridays Health encourages individuals to consult with a qualified medical professional before starting any weight care program.
