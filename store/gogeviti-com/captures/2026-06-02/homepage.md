## We've got answers

How is Geviti different from my regular doctor?

Your doctor checks around 14 markers once a year and has 15 minutes with you. Geviti tests 100+ biomarkers, then pairs you with a dedicated Longevity Specialist who builds a personalized protocol — supplements, prescriptions, and ongoing adjustments — all included in your membership. You're not just getting data. You're getting a team.

What does the 100+ biomarker panel actually test?

Hormones (testosterone, estrogen, thyroid), metabolic markers (insulin, HbA1c, lipids), inflammation (CRP, homocysteine), vitamins and minerals, liver and kidney function — and more. Your results come with a personalized protocol, not just a color-coded dashboard.

How soon will I notice a difference?

78% of members see clinically meaningful improvement within 6 months. Your first protocol ships within days of your lab results, and your care team adjusts it regularly based on how you're responding.

Does insurance cover this?

We accept HSA and FSA payments on all plans. Traditional insurance doesn't cover proactive optimization — but most members find Geviti costs less than the separate labs, supplements, and specialist visits they were already paying for.

Is there a long-term commitment?

No. You can cancel anytime — no contracts, no cancellation fees. Your health data and records stay accessible to you even after you cancel.

Is Geviti available in my state?

Geviti is available in 47 states. At-home blood draws are available in most major metro areas through our network of CLIA-certified partner labs.

Do I need my own doctor to get prescriptions?

No. Your Geviti care team includes licensed providers who can prescribe as part of your protocol — peptides, HRT, GLP-1s, and more. Everything is handled within the platform. No separate doctor visits needed.

What happens to my health data?

It's yours. All data is encrypted at rest and in transit, stored in HIPAA-compliant infrastructure, and never sold to third parties. You control your information.
