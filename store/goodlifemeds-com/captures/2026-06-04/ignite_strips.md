### How should I store Ignite Strips?

Keep them in a cool, dry place and inside their packaging until use to protect potency.

### How often can I take it?

Use one strip per day unless your provider gives different instructions.

### Can I take Ignite Strips with alcohol or food?

You can take them with or without food, but avoid eating or drinking until the strip fully dissolves, and limit heavy alcohol use.

Yes— when prescribed by your provider, they’re designed to be safe, effective, and easy to use.
