### How fast do the strips work?

Most people begin to feel effects within 30–90 minutes, though timing can vary by individual.  Because the strips dissolve orally, they offer quick absorption compared to traditional formats.

### How do they work?

PT-141 (Bremelanotide) activates receptors in the brain related to arousal, desire, and sexual response.  It supports improved blood flow, sensitivity, and overall responsiveness, helping enhance the experience of intimacy.  The oral dissolvable strip allows for direct absorption, helping the formula take effect efficiently.

### How do you take them?

Place one strip under your tongue and allow it to fully dissolve.  Avoid eating or drinking for a few minutes to support optimal absorption.  Use as needed, typically 30–90 minutes before intimacy, unless otherwise directed by your clinician.

## Frequently asked questions

### Are Bliss Strips discreet to carry or use?

Yes — each strip is individually sealed and pocket-sized, making them easy to carry and use privately whenever needed.

### Do Bliss Strips have side effects?

Most women tolerate them well. Mild, temporary effects such as flushing or warmth may occur in some individuals. Your provider will review any potential risks before prescribing.

### Do Bliss Strips affect hormones?

No — Bliss Strips are _not_ hormone-based. They work by supporting pathways linked to intimacy and arousal without altering estrogen or testosterone levels.

### Can Bliss Strips support low desire or reduced arousal?

Bliss Strips may help increase arousal cues, sensitivity, and readiness, which can support women experiencing reduced desire related to stress, mood, or hormonal shifts.

### Are Bliss Strips safe for women?

Yes — Bliss Strips are formulated for adult women and reviewed by a licensed provider before they’re prescribed to ensure they are safe and appropriate for your health profile.
