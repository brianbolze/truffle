### What is Microdose GLP-1?

Microdose GLP-1 is a gentler way to begin or maintain GLP-1-based support for wellness and weight management. Rather than a single large weekly dose, this approach uses smaller, twice-weekly injections of a compounded GLP-1 medication (such as semaglutide or tirzepatide), designed for individuals who prefer steady, lower-intensity dosing.

### How It Works

GLP-1 medications work by helping regulate appetite, slowing gastric emptying, and supporting more consistent metabolic signaling. The microdose method enhances this effect by distributing the dose across the week, which may:

- Promote better appetite control
- Support a more balanced metabolic rhythm
- Minimize sharp fluctuations or discomfort during dose adjustment

## Frequently asked questions

### What happens if I’m not approved for GLP-1 microdosing?

If GLP-1 microdosing isn’t clinically appropriate, your provider will review alternative options and help guide you toward a plan that better fits your health goals.

### Can I increase my dose over time?

Yes, your provider may adjust your dose over time based on how you respond and tolerate treatment. Any changes should always be made under clinical guidance.

### How long does it take to notice results?

Some people notice appetite or craving changes within the first few weeks, while others may experience more gradual effects. Results vary based on individual response, dosing, and lifestyle factors.

### Are side effects common with GLP-1 microdosing?

Side effects may still occur, but many patients report fewer or milder effects compared to higher-dose GLP-1 therapy. Common side effects can include mild nausea or digestive discomfort, especially when starting.

### How is microdosing different from standard GLP-1 therapy?

Standard GLP-1 therapy is designed for significant weight loss and often involves higher doses. Microdosing focuses on lower doses for a more gradual, lifestyle-integrated approach with fewer side effects for some patients.

### What are the potential benefits of GLP-1 microdosing?

Potential benefits may include improved appetite awareness, reduced cravings, better portion control, and metabolic support—without the intensity often associated with higher doses.

### How is GLP-1 microdose administered and dosed?

GLP-1 microdosing is administered via a subcutaneous injection twice weekly, following a clinician-directed dosing plan tailored to your needs.

### How does GLP-1 microdosing work?

GLP-1 medications work by helping regulate appetite, slowing gastric emptying, and supporting blood sugar balance. Microdosing delivers these effects at a lower intensity to support consistency and tolerability.

### Who is GLP-1 microdosing best for?

Microdosing may be a good fit for individuals looking for subtle appetite support, those sensitive to higher GLP-1 doses, or people interested in metabolic support without aggressive weight loss protocols.

### What is GLP-1 microdosing?

GLP-1 microdosing uses a lower, carefully calibrated dose of a GLP-1 medication to support appetite regulation and metabolic health with a more gradual, gentle approach than standard dosing.
