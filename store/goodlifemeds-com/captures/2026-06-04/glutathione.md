## Frequently asked questions

### What are the anti-aging effects of glutathione?

Glutathione’s antioxidant properties help combat oxidative stress, which contributes to aging. It supports cellular health and may help reduce signs of aging.

‍

### Where do we source our Glutathione injections?

Good Life works with state-licensed compounding pharmacies who produce Glutathione. Compounding is regulated by both FDA and state boards of pharmacy. A licensed compounding pharmacy must comply with both federal and state laws, which include administrative requirements and quality standards.

### Can glutathione injections be taken alongside other medications?

Consult your doctor before combining glutathione injections with other medications to ensure safety and avoid potential interactions.

### What about mental health benefits?

Glutathione’s role in reducing inflammation and oxidative stress may positively impact mental health. It’s being studied for its potential in conditions like chronic fatigue syndrome and brain inflammation.

### Do glutathione injections improve athletic performance?

Some athletes use glutathione injections to enhance recovery, reduce muscle fatigue, and support overall performance. However, individual responses may vary.

### How long does it take to see results from glutathione injections?

Results vary, but some individuals notice improvements in skin tone, energy levels, and overall well-being within a few weeks of starting glutathione injections.

‍

### What is glutathione?

Glutathione is a powerful antioxidant naturally produced by the body. It consists of three amino acids: cysteine, glutamic acid, and glycine. Glutathione plays a crucial role in protecting cells from oxidative stress and free radicals.

### What role does glutathione play in skin health?

Glutathione injections are recognized for promoting skin health. They can reduce hyperpigmentation, sunspots, wrinkles, acne, and melasma by eliminating toxins and free radicals from the body. Regular injections are essential for effectiveness.

‍

### How does glutathione benefit overall health?

Glutathione provides a broad array of health benefits, including:

• Breaking down certain free radicals.

• Blocking excess inflammation.

• Supporting DNA production and repair.

• Enhancing the immune system.

• Transporting mercury out of the brain.
