This test is performed every 3 to 6 months. It confirms that the medication has plus or minus 10% of the appropriate concentration of the active ingredient.

### Sterility

Passed

This test ensures the medication is free from any contaminants, including bacteria or other pathogens. Every batch is tested and must meet the requirements of USP 797.1.

### pH

Passed

This test assesses the acid/base balance to ensure minimal irritation upon injection.

Passed

This test, along with sterility testing, ensures the medication is free of harmful toxins. Every batch is tested for bacterial endotoxins, which may not exceed the threshold limits defined in USP 85.

### Who is a good candidate for Slim Shot® injections?

Slim Shot® is ideal for adults looking to boost energy, support fat metabolism, and enhance weight-loss results—especially those who struggle with low B12 levels, slow metabolism, or stubborn fat despite diet and exercise.

### What benefits can I expect from Slim Shot®?

You may experience improved energy, better fat utilization, enhanced metabolic support, and overall increased daily vitality.

### Can Slim Shot® be used alongside GLP-1 medications like Tirzepatide?

Yes—Slim Shot® is often paired with GLP-1 treatments to support energy, fat burning, and metabolic efficiency. Your provider will confirm if the combination is appropriate.

### How long does it take to see results from Slim Shot®?

Many people notice increased energy within days and metabolic benefits within a few weeks when combined with healthy lifestyle habits.

### How often should I take Slim Shot® injections?

Most treatment plans involve weekly injections, but your provider will recommend the exact schedule based on your goals and health history.

### What is Slim Shot® and how does it help with weight loss?

Slim Shot® combines Lipo-MIC, Vitamin B12, and L-Carnitine to support fat metabolism, improve energy levels, and help your body convert stored fat into usable fuel.
