### How do you take Cialis?

Follow your provider's instructions and medical advice when taking Cialis®. Good Life offers a once-daily 5mg tablet dose of Cialis®. Typically, this dosage is taken at the same time each day, regardless of sexual activity frequency. It becomes fully effective within 4-5 days, with peak concentration achieved in as little as 30 minutes. Cialis® can be taken with or without food, making it convenient to incorporate into your routine.

### What is cialis?

Cialis® is an FDA-approved prescription medication for erectile dysfunction that helps men achieve and maintain harder erections. The generic version of this drug is called tadalafil. Similar to Viagra® and Levitra®, Cialis® is a phosphodiesterase-5 (PDE5) inhibitor that enhances blood flow to specific areas of the body, including the penis. With daily use, Cialis® ensures you're ready for sexual activity whenever the mood strikes.

### Side effects

Common side effects of Cialis® include headaches, flushing, upset stomach, abnormal vision, stuffy nose, chest pain (angina), back pain, muscle aches, indigestion, hearing loss, and rash. If you experience persistent side effects, consult your healthcare professional. A prolonged erection lasting more than 4 hours may indicate a serious condition called priapism. Other rare but serious side effects include low blood pressure, irregular heartbeat, heart attack, heart failure, allergic reactions, or sudden vision loss (NAION). If any serious side effects occur, seek medical attention immediately.

## Frequently asked questions

### Does Cialis® have any food or drug interactions?

Like many other medicines, Cialis® should not be taken in combination with nitroglycerin/nitrates for heart problems or hypertension (high blood pressure), riociguat, alpha-blockers, grapefruit juice, sildenafil, vardenafil, Adcirca®, ritonavir, doxazosin, or antifungal medicines.

Additionally, the following drugs can increase the effects of Cialis® by altering the drug's metabolism: ketoconazole, erythromycin, itraconazole, rifampin, and clarithromycin.

‍

### What won't Cialis® do?

Cialis® will not protect you against sexually transmitted diseases, provide sexual stimulation, make your penis bigger, treat premature ejaculation, or cure other diseases than for which it was prescribed (eg, peyronie’s disease).

‍

### Does Cialis® treat anything other than ED?

Yes. Cialis® is approved to aid the following medical issues: pulmonary arterial hypertension (if taken without nitrates) and enlarged prostate (benign prostatic hyperplasia or BPH).

‍

### How to take Cialis®?

Follow the instructions and medical advice of your provider when taking Cialis®. Hims offers access to once-a-day Cialis® in a 5mg tablet dose. Typically, this dosage of Cialis® is taken once a day at the same time, no matter how often you have sex. It only takes 4-5 days for daily Cialis® to become fully effective and you can expect peak concentration in as little as 30 minutes. Cialis® can be taken with or without food, so taking it around the same mealtime is an option.

### What are common side effects of Cialis®?

Common side effects of Cialis® include headaches, flushing, upset stomach, abnormal vision, stuffy nose, angina (chest pain), back pain, muscle aches, indigestion, hearing loss, and rash. If you have persistent side effects, talk to your health care professional. If you have a prolonged erection that lasts longer than 4 hours, you may have a serious medical condition called priapism. Other rare serious side effects are low blood pressure, irregular heartbeat, heart attack, heart failure, allergic reaction, or NAION (sudden vision loss). If any serious side effects occur, seek medical attention immediately. See safety information page for full details.

### How long does Cialis® last?

The effects of Cialis® can last up to 36 hours, but the highest amount of the drug can typically be found in the bloodstream between 30 minutes and six hours after taking Cialis®. However, because Cialis® remains in the body for longer than other ED medications, it is important not to take Cialis® more than once every 24 hours.

### How does Cialis® work?

**Inhibits PDE5**

Cialis® is an oral ED medication that works by suppressing an enzyme in the body called PDE5.

**Increases blook flow**

When PDE5 is suppressed, blood is able to flow more freely through the body—including to the (you guessed it) penis.

**Gets you hard**

More blood flow to the penis means harder, more satisfying erections once you’re sexually stimulated.

### Why Cialis®?

**The no-planning pill**

Once-a-day Cialis® stays in your system for longer than other ED medications so you can be ready for sex any time.

### How long does it take for Cialis® to work?

When taken as-needed and at a higher dose, Cialis® typically starts working within 30-60 minutes, so you will need to take it about an hour before sex to give it time to kick in. However, when Cialis® is taken daily and at a lower dose, it may take a few days to build up in your system. After four or five days of daily use, you may find that you are able to have successful sex at your leisure.
