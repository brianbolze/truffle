### What is the Wegovy® pill and how does it work?

Wegovy® is a GLP-1 receptor agonist medication that helps regulate appetite and support weight management. It works by mimicking a natural hormone in your body that signals fullness, slows digestion, and helps reduce overall calorie intake.

When taken as prescribed under clinician supervision, it may help support sustainable weight loss as part of a comprehensive plan that includes nutrition and lifestyle guidance.

Wegovy® is not a stimulant and does not work by increasing metabolism — it supports appetite regulation and satiety signaling.

### How is the pill different from injectable GLP-1 medications?

The pill form offers an alternative for individuals who prefer not to use injections.

While both oral and injectable GLP-1 medications work through similar hormonal pathways, differences may include:

- Method of delivery (oral vs. injection)
- Dosing schedule
- Absorption considerations
- Cost and accessibility

Your clinician will help determine which option is appropriate based on your medical history, preferences, and treatment goals.

### Who is a good candidate for the Wegovy pill?

Wegovy® may be appropriate for adults who:

- Have a BMI in the overweight or obesity range
- Have weight-related health concerns
- Have struggled with traditional diet and exercise alone
- Are looking for medical support as part of a structured weight management plan

It is not appropriate for everyone. Certain medical conditions, medications, or pregnancy status may affect eligibility. A licensed clinician will review your medical history to determine if treatment is safe for you.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long does it take for the Wegovy pill to start working?

People respond to medication, including Wegovy®, differently, so there is no set time when you can expect to see results with Wegovy®. Ask your healthcare professional about what might be realistic for you.

‍

### What should I do if I miss a day of taking the Wegovy pill?

If you miss a daily dose of Wegovy® pill, skip that day’s dose and get back on track by taking the scheduled dose the next day.

### How do I take the Wegovy Pill?

Take your Wegovy® pill on an empty stomach in the morning with water (up to 4 ounces). Wait at least 30 minutes after taking before having any food, drinking beverages, or taking other oral medications. It is also important that the pill be swallowed whole.

### How much weight did adults in the wegovy pill medical study lose?

In a medical study, adults living with obesity, or with overweight and at least one weight-related medical problem, who took Wegovy® pill, along with a reduced-calorie diet and increased physical activity, lost on average about 33 lb, which was about 14% of their starting body weight. In comparison, adults who took placebo lost on average about 6 lb, or about 2.4% of their starting body weight.

‍

The average starting weights were 235 lb and 231 lb for the Wegovy® and placebo groups, respectively.

### What are some common mistakes people make when trying to lose weight?

Some common challenges include:

- Severely restricting calories, which can slow metabolism
- Relying on short-term diet trends
- Ignoring sleep and stress management
- Not addressing underlying metabolic factors
- Expecting rapid results without long-term strategy

### Is diet and exercise enough?

Weight loss is complex and influenced by biology, hormones, metabolism, sleep, stress, and lifestyle, not just willpower.

For some individuals, diet and exercise alone may not lead to sustainable results due to hormonal signaling that affects hunger and fullness.

GLP-1 medications work by supporting appetite regulation and satiety signaling, which may help address some of the biological drivers of weight gain. They are intended to complement but not replace healthy lifestyle habits.

### What are the most common Wegovy® side effects?

Like all GLP-1 medications, Wegovy® may cause side effects — especially when starting treatment or increasing dosage.

Common side effects may include:

- Nausea
- Upset stomach
- Reduced appetite
- Constipation or diarrhea
- Fatigue

Many side effects are mild and improve as your body adjusts. Your provider will typically start at a lower dose and gradually increase it to help minimize discomfort.

If you experience persistent or severe symptoms, your care team should be contacted promptly.
