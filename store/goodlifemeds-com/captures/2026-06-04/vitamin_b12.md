## Frequently asked questions

### How long do I need to take vitamin B12 shots?

Duration depends on the cause of deficiency. Some people require injections until levels normalize, while others may need long-term supplementation.

‍

### Where are our B12 injections sourced from?

Good Life works with state-licensed compounding pharmacies who produce B12. Compounding is regulated by both FDA and state boards of pharmacy. A licensed compounding pharmacy must comply with both federal and state laws, which include administrative requirements and quality standards.

### Can I get enough B12 from food alone?

B12 is mainly found in animal products (meat, fish, dairy). If you’re vegetarian or vegan, consider B12 supplements or fortified foods.

‍

### Are there any side effects of vitamin B12 injections?

Side effects are rare but may include pain at the injection site, mild allergic reactions, or skin irritation. Always follow your healthcare provider’s recommendations.

### Can vitamin B12 injections boost energy or aid weight loss?

While B12 shots can improve energy levels in deficient individuals, there’s no evidence to support weight loss claims. They’re primarily used for health reasons, not as a weight loss solution.

### How do I know if I need vitamin B12 shots?

Routine blood work often doesn’t check B12 levels, so deficiencies can go undetected. Consult your healthcare provider if you experience symptoms or have risk factors (e.g., vegetarian diet, certain medical conditions).

‍

### What symptoms can vitamin B12 injections improve?

B12 injections can alleviate symptoms such as blurred vision, depression, difficulty walking, irritability, and fatigue. Left untreated, B12 deficiency can lead to irreversible neurological damage.

‍

### Who should consider vitamin B12 injections?

Individuals with low B12 levels or a deficiency may benefit from these injections. Common symptoms include fatigue, weakness, memory issues, and numbness or tingling.

‍

### How are vitamin B12 injections given?

B12 shots are typically injected into the muscle or subcutaneously (deeply under the skin), often in the upper arm or thigh. The frequency of injections varies based on individual needs.

‍

### What are vitamin B12 shots?

Vitamin B12 shots, also known as cobalamin injections, are administered to raise B12 levels in individuals with deficiencies. B12 is essential for red blood cell production, DNA synthesis, brain function, and energy levels.
