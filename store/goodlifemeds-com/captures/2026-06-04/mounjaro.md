### How does Mounjaro work?

Mounjaro is a dual GIP/GLP-1 receptor agonist, which means it activates two naturally occurring hormones that help your body:

‍

- Feel full sooner and stay full longer
- Slow digestion to support steady appetite control
- Improve insulin sensitivity and blood sugar regulation

‍

Together, these effects help reduce hunger, improve metabolic balance, and support sustained weight loss when paired with healthy lifestyle habits.

‍

### What are the common side effects?

Most side effects are mild to moderate and tend to improve as your body adjusts. The most common include:

‍

- Nausea
- Upset stomach or indigestion
- Diarrhea or constipation
- Decreased appetite
- Fatigue
- Injection-site reactions (mild redness or discomfort)

‍

Less common but more serious side effects can occur, so it’s important to talk to a healthcare provider about your medical history and any symptoms you experience while using Mounjaro.

## Frequently asked questions

### How is Mounjaro different from Ozempic or Wegovy?

Mounjaro activates **two hormones (GIP + GLP-1)**, whereas Ozempic/Wegovy activate only GLP-1. This dual action may offer enhanced appetite control and metabolic support for some people.

### Do I need to follow a specific diet while on Mounjaro?

No strict diet is required, but most people see the best results when combining Mounjaro with balanced meals, smaller portions, adequate protein, hydration, and light physical activity.

### What happens if I miss a dose of Mounjaro?

If it’s been **4 days or less**, you can take the missed dose when you remember. If more than 4 days have passed, skip the missed injection and resume your regular schedule.

### Does Mounjaro help control blood sugar?

Yes. Mounjaro is FDA-approved for type 2 diabetes because it supports healthy blood sugar levels by improving insulin sensitivity and reducing post-meal glucose spikes.

### How long does it take to see results with Mounjaro?

Most people begin noticing reduced appetite within the first 1–2 weeks. Visible weight-loss results typically appear around **4–8 weeks**, with continued progress over several months depending on dosage and lifestyle.
