## Frequently   Asked   Questions

###### What should I expect from my primary care doctor?

![](https://lifemd.com/assets/public/common/images/caret-down.svg)

From your primary care doctor, you'll have a trusted expert you can rely upon when you have a health issue–an urgent matter, general questions, and when you seek out a specialist or hospital care.

###### Can my doctor prescribe medications?

![](https://lifemd.com/assets/public/common/images/caret-down.svg)

We make it easy to request and renew prescriptions. Your doctor can prescribe your necessary prescriptions and our nationwide network can bring your medications right to your doorstep.

###### How does LifeMD handle labs, imaging, bloodwork, and other tests?

![](https://lifemd.com/assets/public/common/images/caret-down.svg)

Through our partnerships, we can send a nurse directly to your house to perform the necessary labs and/or bloodwork. Or, if you prefer, we can send you directly to Quest Diagnostics, LabCorp, or any other convenient facility.

###### Does this replace insurance?

![](https://lifemd.com/assets/public/common/images/caret-down.svg)

No. You should keep your insurance in case of a medical emergency. Your doctor can refer you to specialists if/when needed.

###### Does LifeMD offer STI/STD testing?

![](https://lifemd.com/assets/public/common/images/caret-down.svg)

Yes. If your doctor believes that a lab test would be beneficial for you, they can order a lab test for you. After receiving the lab order, you'll visit a lab location, have your lab test(s) run, and the results should be sent to you from LifeMD within 3-5 business days.
