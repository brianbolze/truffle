## Frequently Asked Questions

###### How fast can I get a consultation?

![](https://lifemd.com/assets/public/lifemd-plus/images/caret-down.svg)

Video responses within 60 minutes, messages within 4 hours, 24/7.

###### How does the LifeMD+ membership work?

![](https://lifemd.com/assets/public/lifemd-plus/images/caret-down.svg)

A LifeMD+ membership unlocks access to a comprehensive suite of healthcare benefits designed to simplify and enhance your health journey. With 24/7 access to healthcare providers — along with same-day prescription refills, exclusive pricing on wellness resources, and convenient lab testing options — LifeMD+ makes it easier than ever to prioritize your health.

LifeMD is a monthly subscription program. Your credit card will be charged $19 at the beginning of each billing cycle, and the subscription will automatically renew unless you choose to cancel. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time through your account settings in the LifeMD patient portal.

Please see the next question for more details on costs associated with care.

###### What is the cost of care with LifeMD+?

![](https://lifemd.com/assets/public/lifemd-plus/images/caret-down.svg)

While the LifeMD+ membership comes with a range of valuable benefits, additional charges may apply for consultations, prescriptions, and/or lab work based on your needs and insurance coverage. Here's a summary of what to expect:

\- Video visits with licensed healthcare providers, available within an hour°, are $50 (cash), or the cost of your co-pay\* if you use your insurance.

\- Annual and specialty lab testing through Quest Diagnostics or Labcorp is the responsibility of the patient but is often covered by insurance.\*

°Video-based (synchronous) visits will be available within one hour of request. Certain conditions may be treated via message-based (asynchronous) care. Message based treatments will receive responses from providers within 4 hours.

\*Costs through insurance vary based on your coverage and are determined by your insurance provider.

###### Can I get an appointment on weekends or holidays with LifeMD+?

![](https://lifemd.com/assets/public/lifemd-plus/images/caret-down.svg)

Yes! LifeMD+ offers 24/7 access to licensed healthcare providers, including weekends and holidays. Appointments are available within an hour°, ensuring you can receive care when you need it most.

°Video-based (synchronous) visits will be available within one hour of request. Certain conditions may be treated via message-based (asynchronous) care. Message based treatments will receive responses from providers within 4 hours.

###### Are lab tests available through LifeMD+?

![](https://lifemd.com/assets/public/lifemd-plus/images/caret-down.svg)

Yes. LifeMD+ members benefit from annual and specialty lab testing through Labcorp or Quest Diagnostics to help you track and manage your health markers. Your LifeMD care team can send a lab order directly to your LifeMD patient portal.

Lab orders required or requested by a LifeMD-affiliated provider as part of a specialty program (e.g., weight management) are at no cost to you (if completed at a Quest Diagnostics or Labcorp lab.) For labs not covered by LifeMD, options standardly include utilizing insurance or paying out-of-pocket (discounted rates may be available).

Please keep in mind that due to state regulations, LifeMD cannot cover lab service costs for patients residing in NY, NJ, and RI; these patients (or their insurance) will be billed by the lab directly.

###### What does LifeMD+ include, and what costs extra?

![](https://lifemd.com/assets/public/lifemd-plus/images/caret-down.svg)

Membership is $19/month with access to video visits ($50 cash or your copay). Labs can be covered by insurance; message consults are $20.

###### Do I need insurance?

![](https://lifemd.com/assets/public/lifemd-plus/images/caret-down.svg)

Insurance is optional; you can pay cash or use insurance for eligible services.

###### How do labs and tests work?

![](https://lifemd.com/assets/public/lifemd-plus/images/caret-down.svg)

At-home testing through Quest/Labcorp, with some state restrictions.

###### Can LifeMD prescribe medications?

![](https://lifemd.com/assets/public/lifemd-plus/images/caret-down.svg)

Yes, with same-day renewals, but no controlled substances.
