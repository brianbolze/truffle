## Frequently Asked Questions

###### How does the LifeMD+ membership work?

![](https://lifemd.com/assets/public/common/images/caret-down.svg)

A LifeMD+ membership unlocks access to a comprehensive suite of healthcare benefits designed to
simplify and enhance your health journey. With 24/7 access to healthcare providers — along with
same-day prescription refills, exclusive pricing on wellness resources, and convenient lab testing
options — LifeMD+ makes it easier than ever to prioritize your health.

LifeMD is a monthly subscription program. Your credit card will be charged $19 at the beginning of
each billing cycle, and the subscription will automatically renew unless you choose to cancel. You can
manage or cancel your subscription at any time through your account settings in the LifeMD patient
portal.

Please see the next question for more details on costs associated with care.

###### What is the cost of care with LifeMD+?

![](https://lifemd.com/assets/public/common/images/caret-down.svg)

While the LifeMD+ membership comes with a range of valuable benefits, additional charges may apply
for consultations, prescriptions, and/or lab work based on your needs and insurance coverage. Here's a
summary of what to expect:

- Video visits with licensed healthcare providers, available within an hour°, are $50 (cash), or the
cost of your co-pay\* if you use your insurance.
- Annual and specialty lab testing through Quest Diagnostics or Labcorp is the responsibility of the
patient but is often covered by insurance.\*

°Video-based (synchronous) visits will be available within one hour of request.
Certain conditions may be treated via message-based (asynchronous) care. Message based treatments will
receive responses from providers within 4 hours.

\*Costs through insurance vary based on your coverage and are determined by your
insurance provider.

###### Can I get an appointment on weekends or holidays with LifeMD+?

![](https://lifemd.com/assets/public/common/images/caret-down.svg)

Yes! LifeMD+ offers 24/7 access to licensed healthcare providers, including weekends and holidays.
Appointments are available within an hour°, ensuring you can receive care when you need it most.

°Video-based (synchronous) visits will be available within one hour of request.
Certain conditions may be treated via message-based (asynchronous) care. Message based treatments will
receive responses from providers within 4 hours.

###### Are lab tests available through LifeMD+?

![](https://lifemd.com/assets/public/common/images/caret-down.svg)

Yes. LifeMD+ members benefit from annual and specialty lab testing through Labcorp or Quest
Diagnostics to help you track and manage your health markers. Your LifeMD care team can send a lab
order directly to your LifeMD patient portal.

Lab orders required or requested by a LifeMD-affiliated provider as part of a specialty program
(e.g., weight management) are at no cost to you (if completed at a Quest Diagnostics or Labcorp lab.)
For labs not covered by LifeMD, options standardly include utilizing insurance or paying out-of-pocket
(discounted rates may be available).

Please keep in mind that due to state regulations, LifeMD cannot cover lab service
costs for patients residing in NY, NJ, and RI; these patients (or their insurance) will be billed by
the lab directly.

###### What is the LifeMD Weight Management Program?

![](https://lifemd.com/assets/public/common/images/caret-down.svg)

The LifeMD Weight Management Program is a 360° approach that combines groundbreaking medications,
advanced lab testing, and the expertise of leading clinicians to create a highly effective approach to
weight loss. We’ll take your unique metabolic profile and lifestyle habits into account to help ensure
you lose the extra weight — one of the most important things you’ll ever do for your health.

Licensed healthcare providers can prescribe a wide range of medications to help you reach your
weight-loss goals — from GLP-1s to additional options such as Triple Therapy (an oral treatment plan
that combines metformin, bupropion, and topiramate).

Triple Therapy is included in the cost of the program. Medication costs for GLP-1 medications are not
included unless stated at the time of purchase. When appropriate, our insurance benefits check service
will work with you and your insurer to determine coverage for brand-name prescription medications. You
can also explore discounted cash-pay pricing for several brand-name medications.

At LifeMD, our philosophy revolves around fostering long-term patient-provider partnerships,
supported by leading technology, a multi-person care team, and the appropriate medication — all
converging to pave the way for achievable weight loss and lasting wellness.

###### What happens once I enroll in the LifeMD Weight Management Program?

![](https://lifemd.com/assets/public/common/images/caret-down.svg)

Once you enroll in the LifeMD Weight Management Program, we'll welcome you to the program, review
your medical history, and collect any additional information needed to ensure a solid start. We'll
also help coordinate the collection of lab work, if needed.

With your specific weight loss goals and metabolic profile front and center, and if appropriate, your
provider will prescribe you a treatment plan you can stick with — and that works. Your clinician may
determine you're a candidate for weight loss medication, such as GLP-1 medication or Triple Therapy
medication. The weight loss medications prescribed through LifeMD can help you safely and effectively
drop weight (alongside healthy eating and exercise habits, of course).

###### Is the LifeMD Weight Management Program completely online?

![](https://lifemd.com/assets/public/common/images/caret-down.svg)

Yes! Everything, from your initial visit to support from clinicians and ongoing check-ins, can be
done right from your computer or smartphone. Lab work may be required.

###### What is the LifeMD Mental Health Program?

![](https://lifemd.com/assets/public/common/images/caret-down.svg)

The LifeMD Mental Health Program is a flexible, virtual care service designed to support your mental
well-being through personalized, judgment-free treatment. Depending on your needs and medical history,
you may qualify for a treatment plan that includes medication prescribed to be taken daily or only as
needed. Medication is included in the cost of your monthly membership.

If medication is prescribed, your provider will guide you on how to take it safely. You’ll receive
regular check-ins and renewal prompts, and you can stay connected with your LifeMD care team through
secure messaging in the patient portal.

###### What types of mental health conditions does LifeMD treat?

![](https://lifemd.com/assets/public/common/images/caret-down.svg)

LifeMD offers two distinct care plans, depending on the type and frequency of your symptoms — and the
kind of support you’re looking for:

- **Social or performance anxiety** is supported with prescription medication taken only as
needed. This approach is designed to help you manage high-stress moments — such as public speaking,
interviews, or social events — without the need for daily medication. The medication is included in
the cost of your monthly membership.
- **General anxiety or depression** is supported with a daily prescription medication. This plan
provides steady, ongoing support for your mental well-being. The medication is included in the cost
of your monthly membership.

###### What is the LifeMD Women's Health Program?

![](https://lifemd.com/assets/public/common/images/caret-down.svg)

The LifeMD Women’s Health Program is a personalized, evidence-based approach to hormone replacement
therapy (HRT) and lifestyle support for women navigating perimenopause or menopause. The program is
designed to help relieve symptoms such as hot flashes, night sweats, brain fog, mood changes, reduced
libido, and more.

Your care will include prescription HRT (when appropriate), secure messaging with your LifeMD care
team, regular check-ins with a licensed healthcare provider to assess your progress, and access to
tools that support healthy aging — all from the convenience of your phone or computer.

###### What medications may be prescribed through the LifeMD Women's Health Program?

![](https://lifemd.com/assets/public/common/images/caret-down.svg)

Medications that may be considered are estradiol (patch, insert, or gel), micronized progesterone,
and thyroid replacement if necessary. The hormones prescribed through LifeMD are bioidentical, meaning
they have the same chemical and molecular structure as the hormones naturally produced by your body.
Customized programs may include different strengths, routes, and dosing schedules for medications.
Your provider will work with you to determine the most appropriate option(s) based on your symptoms,
preferences, and medical history.
