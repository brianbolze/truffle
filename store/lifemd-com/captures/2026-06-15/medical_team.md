## Frequently Asked Questions

###### How do I qualify for a virtual consultation with a LifeMD-affiliated provider?

![](https://lifemd.com/assets/public/medical-team/images/caret-down.svg)

To get started with LifeMD, you'll answer some questions about your symptoms and health history before meeting with a LifeMD-affiliated provider. This information helps the LifeMD-affiliated provider determine the type of care you may need. Depending on your condition, you can request to be seen by the next available provider - within just one hour - or schedule a virtual consultation at a later time.

###### Can I meet with a doctor online?

![](https://lifemd.com/assets/public/medical-team/images/caret-down.svg)

Yes, you can meet with a doctor online. The LifeMD medical team is a network of board-certified medical professionals with expertise across a wide range of health specialties. Each provider offers a wide range of services – including diagnosis, medication prescriptions, refills, and more!

###### Does online medical care work?

![](https://lifemd.com/assets/public/medical-team/images/caret-down.svg)

[Telehealth](https://lifemd.com/what-is-telehealth) is an excellent option for non-emergency medical care. LifeMD-affiliated medical professionals can help you manage health conditions and suggest a treatment plan for a wide range of medical concerns. A healthcare provider can also order lab work if needed. LifeMD-affiliated providers are accessible 24/7 in all 50 states.

###### What can a telehealth provider prescribe?

![](https://lifemd.com/assets/public/medical-team/images/caret-down.svg)

Telehealth doctors have the ability to prescribe most medications, as long as it’s medically appropriate. LifeMD-affiliated providers can prescribe a wide range of medications including antibiotics, allergy medications, pain relievers, and more. However, telehealth doctors cannot prescribe any medication that is classified as a controlled substance.

###### Do you provide doctor’s notes and sick notes?

![](https://lifemd.com/assets/public/medical-team/images/caret-down.svg)

During a consultation, LifeMD-affiliated clinicians can only provide a note stating the date when a patient was seen and that a treatment plan was established.

###### Does LifeMD offer discounts on medication?

![](https://lifemd.com/assets/public/medical-team/images/caret-down.svg)

Yes! The LifeMD prescription discount card is accepted at over 60,000 pharmacies to save up to 92% on many medications.

In addition to the physical card mailed to members, the prescription discount card can also be found in the LifeMD app. Just present the physical or digital card to your pharmacist when you pick up your prescription.

###### Is the data I share with LifeMD private?

![](https://lifemd.com/assets/public/medical-team/images/caret-down.svg)

At LifeMD, one of our main priorities is to protect your personal information and privacy. LifeMD is a HIPAA compliant platform. We will use your data solely to improve our services. You can learn more about our [privacy policy](https://lifemd.com/privacy)

###### If needed, can I receive a doctor’s referral through LifeMD

![](https://lifemd.com/assets/public/medical-team/images/caret-down.svg)

A LifeMD-affiliated clinician can provide referrals for labwork, imaging tests, and specialists when needed.

###### How often can I meet with my LifeMD healthcare provider?

![](https://lifemd.com/assets/public/medical-team/images/caret-down.svg)

You can schedule a virtual meeting with a LifeMD-affiliated clinician from the comfort of your home at any time.
