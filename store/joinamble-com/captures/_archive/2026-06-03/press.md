### FAQ

- **I know someone who'd love Amble and would like to refer them. How do I start?**

To start the referral process, simply share your unique referral link via email or social media.
- **What is a successful referral?**

In order for a referral to count, the person you've referred must be brand new to Amble when they sign up. A doctor must successfully complete their medical evaluation for an eligible treatment.
- **Are all treatments eligible?**

No. At this time, referred friends must sign up and be approved for a Weight Loss by Amble program to count as a successful referral. The list of eligible programs may change at any time.
- **How do I know how many of my referrals have signed up?**

Once your referrals have signed up using your unique link, you'll see them show up in the "My Referrals" section.
- **If one of my referral signs up, when and how do I get my reward?**

We manually approve all rewards. Once your reward is approved, you will receive an email from us notifying you with more details.
- **My referral forgot to use my unique referral link to sign up. Is there a way I can still receive credit?**

Please contact our team to assist you.
