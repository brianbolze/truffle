### How to take

Sermorelin is typically injected subcutaneously (under the skin), usually into abdominal fat, once daily—preferably in the evening. Your provider may adjust the dose based on your goals and response.

### Potential side effects

Common side effects may include mild irritation or redness at the injection site, headaches, flushing, dizziness, or temporary sleep disturbances. Some users experience increased hunger or vivid dreams. Rarely, Sermorelin can cause nausea, water retention, or tingling sensations.

Most side effects are mild and tend to resolve as your body adjusts.
