## Same Treatment Different Starting Line

Right now, roughly 1 in 8 U.S. adults have taken a GLP-1 medication, driving meaningful, sustained weight loss and massive metabolic improvements1. Yet, if you cannot easily cover the out-of-pocket costs, you have likely been left watching from the outside.

The reality is straightforward: access to GLP-1 therapy in the U.S. has not been evenly distributed.

For many Americans, the barrier hasn’t been a lack of awareness or a willingness to improve their health, it’s been cost. It’s the single parent managing work, childcare, and a rising cost of living, without the financial flexibility to prioritize their own well-being. It’s the patient forced to delay care year after year, watching their condition progress while highly effective treatments remain just out of reach.

## Prioritizing Family and Those Most In Need

**Eligibility Criteria**

To provide support where it is needed most, patient eligibility and specific discount levels are determined entirely by an independent, third-party verification partner based on verified financial need. Whether it is a single parent managing household expenses on their own, or a dual-income family trying to keep up with the rising cost of living, the program is built to provide proportional relief. Prospective patients can apply online through a secure, third-party portal to verify their eligibility and find out if they qualify.

**A Direct, Transparent Model**

**‍** Amble Health operates strictly as a direct-pay platform offering GLP1 weight loss program to remove the delays, denials, and red tape associated with traditional healthcare. Because the Amble Cares Program is designed specifically to provide affordable, out-of-pocket access without the need for insurance approvals, program pricing cannot be combined with outside coverage. Participants cannot submit claims for any products purchased through Amble to Medicare, Medicaid, commercial insurers, or FSA/HSA accounts. Patients participate exclusively on a direct-pay basis, ensuring prices stay low and care stays accessible.

### FAQ

- **I know someone who'd love Amble and would like to refer them. How do I start?**

To start the referral process, simply share your unique referral link via email or social media.
- **What is a successful referral?**

In order for a referral to count, the person you've referred must be brand new to Amble when they sign up. A doctor must successfully complete their medical evaluation for an eligible treatment.
- **Are all treatments eligible?**

No. At this time, referred friends must sign up and be approved for a Weight Loss by Amble program to count as a successful referral. The list of eligible programs may change at any time.
- **How do I know how many of my referrals have signed up?**

Once your referrals have signed up using your unique link, you'll see them show up in the "My Referrals" section.
- **If one of my referral signs up, when and how do I get my reward?**

We manually approve all rewards. Once your reward is approved, you will receive an email from us notifying you with more details.
- **My referral forgot to use my unique referral link to sign up. Is there a way I can still receive credit?**

Please contact our team to assist you.
