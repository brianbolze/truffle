### What is NAD+ and why is it important?

Nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide (NAD) is the energy currency of your cells. It powers everything from muscle movement to brain function.

Your body makes NAD+ naturally. But production slows down with age.

This creates a cellular energy problem. Your cells can’t repair themselves properly. Energy production drops.

The result? You feel older than you should.

Restoring NAD+ levels helps your cells function like they did when you were younger.

NAD+ is your body’s master energy molecule. It powers over 500 different cellular functions.

- **Energy Crashes:** Cells can’t make energy efficiently. You feel drained even after rest.
