ORAL NMN Supplements

The best way to raise NAD+ levels is oral supplements conatining Nicotinamide Mononucleotide (NMN), the most potent NAD+ precursors that cells use to produce NAD+. Our patented Vitality NAD+ Booster is by far the most effective product to optimize NAD+ levels and revitalize cellular function.

IV Infusion or Subcutaneous Injection

IV infusion of NAD+ can temporarily increase NAD+ levels in the plasma (the liquid portion of the blood) but is degraded after 24 hours. NAD+ is too big to get inside the cells and therefore cannot increase NAD+ levels inside the cells. It is not the right method for optimizing cellular health. Subcutaneous NAD+ injection has limited effecacy unless it is injected at high dose and every day. Test your levels to make sure that you are getting adequate dosages.

Inhibit enzymes that consume NAD

NAD is broken down by enzymes like CD38, Sirtuins, and PARP. CD38, which rises with inflammation and aging, is a major NAD degrader. Supplements like quercetin and apigenin can inhibit CD38 activity in test tubes. While CD38 inhibition may boost circulating NAD levels, its effect on intracellular NAD is neglegible.

Boost enzymes that make NAD

NAMPT is a key enzyme for NAD production in our cells. Supplements, diet, sleep, and exercise can influence NAD-synthesizing enzymes and NAD levels, but alone, their impact on NAD+ levels is minimum and insufficient. A combination of NAD precursors like NMN and a healthy lifestyle is the best approach to optimize NAD levels.
