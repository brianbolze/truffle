# Advancing health, performance, and longevity through science

## We use the power of leading-edge science to save and improve lives around the world

Jinfiniti is a scientist led, data driven company that is dedicated to empowering clinicians and individuals to optimize their health, performance and longevity. Jinfiniti does this by developing and  analyzing unique and actionable biomarkers. Therefore, Jinfiniti has the unique ability to develop clinically proven interventions that will correct the discovered sub-health issues for the prevention of age-related diseases and improve efficacy of treatment.

## From scientific innovation to global health transformation — a little history behind our enterprise

The company was founded by a team of scientists with highly successful academic careers with a desire to bring powerful and positive change to the health and longevity field by developing a number of innovative biomarkers that are not available anywhere else in the world. Their work quickly attracted a team of highly experienced and very passionate entrepreneurs, who now serve as senior executives. Together we can now offer testing, personalized interventions and evaluation of treatment outcomes to clinicians and individuals around the world.

All tests are conducted at Jinfiniti’s high-complexity CLIA laboratory (ID #: 11D2264938) in Augusta, Georgia, managed by skilled clinicians and scientists. This specialized lab can perform routine clinical tests but focuses on exclusive Laboratory Developed Tests (LDTs) unavailable anywhere else.

## Management Team

![](https://www.jinfiniti.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/JinxiongShe2.jpg)

Dr. Jin-Xiong She

PhD, Founder, CEO & Chief Scientific Officer

Dr. Jin-Xiong She received his college education in China and earned his PhD in France. After a brief postdoctoral stint at the University of Florida, he advanced rapidly to Full and Endowed Professor, Division Chief of Experimental Pathology, and Director of Research at UF’s Diabetes Center. In 2002, he became the Georgia Research Alliance Eminent Scholar in Genomic Medicine at the Medical College of Georgia, founding the Center for Biotechnology and Genomic Medicine and directing multiple core research facilities.

Dr. She has authored over 400 peer-reviewed papers (H-index 76, 19,000+ citations), secured over $100 million in research grants, and mentored numerous students and faculty. Ranked among Georgia’s top 20 medical researchers, he has served as President of both the Chinese Geneticists in America and the ADA council on Immunology, Transplantation, and Immunogenetics. A serial entrepreneur, he founded or co-founded multiple companies in the US and China, and currently leads Jinfiniti Precision Medicine to optimize healthspan through biomarker testing and precision nutraceuticals. More details can be found on his [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/jin-xiong-she-phd-311b461a7/) and [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=heGp6m4AAAAJ&hl=en) profiles.
