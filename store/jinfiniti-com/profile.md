- **Lab positioning:** About page says tests are run in a high-complexity CLIA lab in Augusta, Georgia, and identifies CLIA ID 11D2264938.

## How It Works / Model

- **Ecommerce path:** Consumers buy tests, supplements, bundles, or subscriptions through product pages and cart flows.

- **Subscription layer:** Some NAD products are sold both as one-time ecommerce products and as recurring optimization membership subscriptions.
