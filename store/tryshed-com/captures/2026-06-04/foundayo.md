FAQ

### What is Foundayo® and how does it work?

Foundayo® (orforglipron) is a once-daily oral GLP-1 receptor agonist, FDA-approved for chronic weight management in adults with obesity or overweight. It works by mimicking a natural hormone that helps regulate appetite and food intake, signaling the brain to reduce hunger, which may help make it easier to eat less without feeling deprived, when combined with diet and exercise.

FAQ

### What kind of results can I expect on Foundayo®?

In the ATTAIN-1 clinical trial, participants taking the highest dose who completed treatment lost an average of 12.4% of their body weight (27.3 pounds) over 72 weeks, compared to 0.9% with placebo. Results vary by individual and depend on factors like adherence, diet, exercise, and overall health. Source: Foundayo Prescribing Information. Lilly USA, LLC, 2026.

FAQ

### How is Foundayo® different from injectable GLP-1s?

Foundayo® is a once-daily pill. Injectable GLP-1s are typically administered as a weekly subcutaneous injection. Both belong to the same class of medication, but Foundayo® requires no needle, no injection site rotation, and no weekly shot schedule.

FAQ

### How do I take Foundayo®?

Take one tablet by mouth once daily, as prescribed by your provider. Swallow it whole—do not break, crush, or chew. Your provider will guide your dosing schedule.

## Questions? We’ve got answers.

### What is Foundayo® and how does it work?

Foundayo® (orforglipron) is a once-daily oral GLP-1 receptor agonist, FDA-approved for chronic weight management in adults with obesity or overweight. It works by mimicking a natural hormone that helps regulate appetite and food intake, signaling the brain to reduce hunger, which may help make it easier to eat less without feeling deprived, when combined with diet and exercise.

### What kind of results can I expect on Foundayo®?

In the ATTAIN-1 clinical trial, participants taking the highest dose who completed treatment lost an average of 12.4% of their body weight (27.3 pounds) over 72 weeks, compared to 0.9% with placebo. Results vary by individual and depend on factors like adherence, diet, exercise, and overall health. Source: Foundayo Prescribing Information. Lilly USA, LLC, 2026.

### How is Foundayo® different from injectable GLP-1s?

Foundayo® is a once-daily pill. Injectable GLP-1s are typically administered as a weekly subcutaneous injection. Both belong to the same class of medication, but Foundayo® requires no needle, no injection site rotation, and no weekly shot schedule.

### How do I take Foundayo®?

Take one tablet by mouth once daily, as prescribed by your provider. Swallow it whole—do not break, crush, or chew. Your provider will guide your dosing schedule.

### Are there any side effects of Foundayo®?

The most common side effects may include nausea, constipation, diarrhea, vomiting, indigestion, stomach pain, headache, and fatigue. These side effects are typically mild to moderate and may decrease over time. Your provider can help you manage side effects throughout your treatment. See full Prescribing Information at foundayo.lilly.com. Foundayo may reduce the effectiveness of oral birth control pills, and providers may recommend alternative contraception for 30 days after starting and after each dose increase. Foundayo has a Boxed Warning for possible thyroid tumors, including thyroid cancer. Do not use if you or a family member has a history of medullary thyroid carcinoma. See full prescribing information See full prescribing information at foundayo.lilly.com.

### Is Foundayo® right for everyone?

Foundayo® is not appropriate for everyone. It is indicated for adults with obesity or overweight with at least one weight-related condition. A licensed provider will review your health history and goals to determine whether it may be a safe and suitable option for you.

### Does Foundayo® need to be refrigerated?

No. Unlike injectables and some other treatments, Foundayo® does not need to be refrigerated, making it easy to use at home or take with you on the go.
