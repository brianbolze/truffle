## Questions about GLP-1 injections? We’ve got answers.

### Can I use GLP-1s if I have diabetes?

It depends on your specific diagnosis, medications, and overall health history. Some GLP-1 medications are commonly used to help manage type 2 diabetes, while others are prescribed specifically for weight management. If you have diabetes, your provider will review your blood sugar levels, current medications, and medical history to determine whether a GLP-1 is appropriate for you — and which option makes the most sense. Our licensed providers evaluate each patient individually before prescribing.

### Do I need to follow a strict diet?

No strict or extreme diet is required. GLP-1 medications work by helping regulate appetite and improve feelings of fullness. Most patients naturally eat smaller portions and feel more in control of cravings. That said, long-term success is best supported by balanced, sustainable nutrition habits. We encourage focusing on:

- Protein intake
- Fiber-rich foods
- Hydration
- Strength training

The goal isn’t restriction — it’s building habits you can maintain.

### Is there a risk of muscle loss?

Rapid weight loss of any kind can lead to some muscle loss if protein intake and strength training aren’t prioritized. That’s why we emphasize:

- Adequate protein
- Resistance training
- Gradual, medically guided dosing

When used responsibly and paired with proper nutrition and exercise, GLP-1 treatment can support fat loss while helping preserve lean muscle mass. Our providers monitor progress and adjust plans as needed to support healthy, sustainable results.
