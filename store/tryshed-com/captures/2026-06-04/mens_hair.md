## Questions about men’s hair treatments? We’ve got answers.

### How does minoxidil help with hair loss?

Minoxidil is believed to work by improving blood flow to the scalp, which may help support hair follicles and promote the hair growth cycle.

### How does finasteride help with hair loss?

Finasteride helps reduce levels of dihydrotestosterone (DHT), a hormone linked to hair follicle shrinkage and hair thinning.

### Are there any side effects?

Some men may experience scalp irritation, such as redness, dryness, or itching. In rare cases, allergic reactions or unwanted systemic effects may occur. Speak with your healthcare provider if you experience side effects.
