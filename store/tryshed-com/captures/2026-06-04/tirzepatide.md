### How does compounded tirzepatide work?

It works by mimicking natural hormones (GLP-1 and GIP) that help control appetite, slow digestion, and support blood sugar—helping you feel fuller, reduce cravings, and lose weight with healthy lifestyle changes.

### How is compounded tirzepatide administered?

Compounded Tirzepatide injections are taken once a week. Your medication is shipped directly to your door, and we’ll provide clear instructions on how to self-inject. Your provider will also be available to guide and support you through the process.

### What are the common side effects?

Possible side effects may include nausea, vomiting, constipation, diarrhea, indigestion or stomach discomfort, bloating, reduced appetite, delayed gastric emptying (feeling unusually full or slow digestion), headache, fatigue, and injection site reactions. Serious but rare side effects may include pancreatitis, gallbladder problems, changes in kidney function, severe abdominal pain, jaundice, changes in urination, or swelling.
