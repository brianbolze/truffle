## We built the care we always wished existed

Ivím began with a simple truth: people deserve better than a healthcare system that treats them like a transaction.

* * *

Two brothers saw the same problem from opposite sides of the table.

One saw it in the operating room, watching preventable conditions turn into life-threatening emergencies. The other lived it as a patient, navigating a system that felt rushed, fragmented, and impersonal.

Both wondered the same thing: why doesn’t care actually feel like care?

So they built the alternative they always wished existed.

**Real doctors. Real protocols. Real support over time.**

Not just a form and a prescription. Not an algorithm pretending to care. A true clinical relationship. The kind that actually changes things. They coded the first version themselves. Took support calls late into the night. Turned down a nine-figure acquisition because the buyer didn’t share the vision. And five years later, that vision is still the same:

_Care that feels like someone is genuinely in it with you._
