## Understanding individualized compounded GLP-1 medications

## What makes GLP-1 ID different? +

GLP-1 ID combines customized care, flexible medication options, and ongoing support to deliver better results than one-size-fits-all approaches.

## Are GLP-1 medications safe? +

Yes, when prescribed by a licensed provider and monitored appropriately.

## What are the most common side effects of compounded GLP-1 medications? +

The most common side effects of compounded semaglutide and compounded tirzepatide are similar to their brand-name counterparts and are typically gastrointestinal in nature, including:

- Nausea
- Vomiting
- Diarrhea
- Constipation
- Bloating or gas
- Decreased appetite or early satiety


These side effects are usually mild to moderate and tend to improve over time, especially with gradual dose escalation under provider supervision.

## What if I prefer branded medications? +

You still have full access to Zepbound® and Wegovy® if you choose through our traditional GLP-1 program.

## How do I know if I’m eligible? +

Our intake questionnaire and consultation with a provider determine if you medically qualify.

## Can I cancel anytime? +

Our GLP-1 ID programs are structured based on the duration you select at checkout. Most patients begin with the Jumpstart option, which is a 4-month commitment.

You can choose from 1, 2, 4, or 6-month programs, and your access to care, medication, and support will remain active for the full duration you select.

Because GLP-1 medications are prescribed and shipped based on your selected program length, cancellation is not available once your program has started.
