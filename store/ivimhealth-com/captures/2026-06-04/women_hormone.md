## FAQ

## What is the Women's Hormone Optimization Program and How Does It Work? +

[Ivím Health](https://www.ivimhealth.com/) is proud to begin offering the Women’s Hormone Optimization Program, a individualized medical program designed to optimize declining hormone levels, which may help support well-being during perimenopause and [menopause](https://health.umms.org/2025/03/13/hormone-therapy-understanding-the-benefits-and-risks/).

Optimization refers to the use of medications, like estrogen and progesterone, to restore balance, which may support mood, energy, metabolism, bone health, and sexual wellness.

At [Ivím Health](https://www.ivimhealth.com/), the Hormone Optimization Program is tailored using individualized hormone therapies delivered via oral capsules or patches. DHEA is offered only as an optional sexual wellness add-on and not part of the core hormone optimization program.

## What is the difference between HRT and Ivim’s Hormone Optimization Program? +

Hormone Optimization Program is Ivim Health’s comprehensive approach to treating the symptoms associated with the menopause transition including medical therapy, supplement integration and lifestyle changes.

Hormone replacement therapy is the use of estrogen and progesterone products to medically treat the decline in hormones that occurs with age.

## What’s included in the Ivím Health Hormone Optimization Program? +

The Hormone Optimization Program includes:

· All prescribed Hormone Optimization Program medications (estradiol and/or progesterone) for hormone replacement therapy

· Access to longevity and sexual wellness add-ons (e.g., DHEA, PT-141)\*

· Optional lab kits for hormone monitoring

· Unlimited virtual consultations with licensed providers

· Access to the Ivím Health app for managing your medications and tracking progress

· Exclusive online Ivim patient community

· 20% off all supplements and wellness products

_\*Add-ons available if prescribed and billed in addition to your Hormone Optimization Program_

## What medications are included in Ivím’s Women’s Hormone Optimization Program? +

Included hormone medications may vary based on your individualized plan and include:

**· Estradiol/Estriol (oral or patch)** – May help support bone health, cognitive function, and symptom relief.

**· Progesterone (oral capsule)** – May help improve sleep, anxiety, and protect the uterine lining.

**· DHEA (vaginal cream)** – _Available only as an optional sexual wellness add-on_ to support libido and vaginal health.

## What are the potential risks and side effects of hormone replacement therapy? +

The medications used in the Hormone Optimization Program are generally well tolerated under medical supervision, but may cause:

· Breast tenderness

· Mood swings or irritability

· Nausea or bloating

· Acne or oily skin

· Menstrual changes (for perimenopausal women)

Your [Ivím provider](https://www.ivimhealth.com/about-us/) will tailor your hormone plan and adjust it as needed through regular follow-ups included in your Ivim Membership

Yes— your [Ivím Health](https://www.ivimhealth.com/) provider may also recommend pairing high-quality supplements with your routine. Some options may include:

## Is the Hormone Optimization Program safe if I have a history of cancer or cardiovascular issues? +

Your [Ivím Health provider](https://www.ivimhealth.com/) will review your complete medical history to determine whether hormone optimization is appropriate. For those with a [history of hormone-sensitive cancers](https://www.mdanderson.org/cancerwise/does-hormone-replacement-therapy-increase-cancer-risk.h00-159699123.html) or cardiovascular conditions, **non-hormonal or alternative therapies may be considered** to support your symptoms safely.

## What forms of hormone replacement therapy does Ivím Health offer? +

[Ivím Health](https://www.ivimhealth.com/) offers multiple administration methods to suit individual needs:

**· Oral capsules** – A convenient, daily option for both estrogen and progesterone.

**· Transdermal estradiol patches** – Applied 1–2 times per week, offering steadier hormone delivery and reduced clotting risk.

**· Vaginal creams (DHEA only)** – Available as a sexual wellness add-on to support local vaginal health.

Your provider will help determine the best format for your goals and comfort level.

## Do I need to commit to a certain length of time? +

Yes. The Hormone Optimization Program requires a **minimum four-month commitment** to allow for treatment efficacy, medication titration, and symptom monitoring. This approach supports long-term results and better patient outcomes.

## How do Ivím’s lab kits work and are they required? +

Labs are not required to start the Hormone Optimization Program, but they’re strongly recommended. If your provider orders labs:

· You’ll receive an at-home hormone testing kit with instructions included

· Labs test for key markers like estradiol, progesterone, DHEA-S, FSH, LH, SHBG, and more

· Results are reviewed during follow-ups to optimize dosing

## Can I access sexual wellness treatments through this program? +

Yes. [Ivím Health](https://www.ivimhealth.com/) offers sexual wellness treatments as optional add-ons, including:

**· [PT-141](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12851303/) (Bremelanotide)** troches which may be helpful for low libido

**· [DHEA](https://www.mayoclinic.org/drugs-supplements-dhea/art-20364199) vaginal cream** that may potentially decrease vaginal dryness and increase sexual satisfaction

**· Apomorphine/Oxytocin** troches that may enhance sexual climax and bonding

These are prescribed based on individual needs and goals.

## Is DHEA part of the standard Hormone Optimization Program? +

No. DHEA is **not included** in the standard Hormone Optimization Program but is available **as an optional add-on** for sexual wellness. It’s most commonly used as a vaginal cream to improve libido, tissue health, and satisfaction during intercourse.

## How often will I meet with a provider? +

You’ll have **unlimited virtual access** to your provider. Many patients meet with their provider:

· At the start of the program

· For follow-ups after 1–2 months

· Quarterly or as needed to adjust medications or discuss symptoms

Chat support is also available through the Ivím mobile app.

## What are the benefits of using the patch over capsules? +

Transdermal patches are a popular option for patients who prefer:

· More stable hormone levels

· Reduced risk of blood clots (compared to oral estrogen)

· Less frequent dosing (changed only 1–2 times per week)

Your provider will help you choose the best delivery method based on your health profile.
