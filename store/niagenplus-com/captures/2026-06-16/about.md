# Health begins within

NAD+ levels in tissues decline by up to 65% between the ages of 30 and 70. Sleep, nutrition, and recovery strategies can only accomplish so much when the cellular systems underlying them are depleted. Niagen Plus addresses the cellular foundation, delivering pharmaceutical-grade Niagen® through IV and injectable formats that bypass digestion for enhanced bioavailability.

![](https://www.niagenplus.com/cdn/shop/files/close_up_niagen_iv_bag_split_block.jpg?v=1776969826&width=1002)

## Published clinical data from a real-world setting

A peer-reviewed, retrospective pilot study compared commercially administered NAD+ IV and Niagen™ NR IV across four consecutive infusions. Niagen IV averaged 37 minutes per session versus 97 minutes for NAD+ IV, with markedly fewer adverse experiences and no clinically significant changes in blood chemistry or inflammatory markers at 30 days post-infusion.

_Reyna et al., 2026. Frontiers in Aging._

[Read the Peer-Reviewed Study](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12907335/)

![](https://www.niagenplus.com/cdn/shop/files/woman_getting_niagen_iv_split_block.jpg?v=1776969826&width=1002)

## The first controlled clinical study on Niagen™ IV

In a randomized, double-blind, placebo-controlled pilot study, a single 500mg infusion of Niagen™ IV produced a more than 20% peak increase in blood NAD+ levels at 3 hours post-infusion, with 75% less infusion time compared to NAD+ IV and fewer adverse experiences reported.

_Hawkins et al., 2024. Preprint, medRxiv._

[Read the Preprint Study](https://www.medrxiv.org/content/10.1101/2024.06.06.24308565v1)
