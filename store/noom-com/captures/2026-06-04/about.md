## Noom uses the latest in proven behavioral science to empower people to take control of their health for good.

Through a **combination of** **psychology**, **technology**, **medication**, and **coaching**, our platform has helped millions meet their personal health and wellness goals.

While **weight management** is still our core focus, we have expanded our behavior change platform to help people with additional health conditions such as **diabetes** and **menopause**—on our continued journey to build a healthier world for everyone, everywhere.

## How does Noom work?

Psychology

N

![Noom weight loss app screen showing a mini-habit for portion control: “Use a smaller plate, bowl, or serving dish” to help reduce calorie intake. Includes a healthy salad image, habit tracking progress, and encourages mindful eating using behavioral psychology strategies. Ideal companion for GLP-1 users focused on sustainable weight loss.](https://www.noom.com/tachyon/2025/04/s3-psychology-desktop-3.5x-1.png?w=1080)

Psychology

Our products are developed using evidence-based approaches and scientifically proven principles, such as cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT), to help people build sustainable habits that last a lifetime. Noom makes it easy, fun, and rewarding to make health a habit.

Technology

L

![Noom app screen showing body composition metrics for a user getting started with their weight loss journey. Displays body fat percentage (35%), waist-to-hip ratio (.99), current weight (216 lbs), and metabolic rate (1,400 calories). Highlights areas for improvement and supports behavior change using personalized health data.](https://www.noom.com/tachyon/2025/04/s3-technology-desktop-3.5x.png?w=1080)

Technology

We’re constantly innovating and fine-tuning our products to ensure our members have access to the most effective healthcare tools in the market—like AI food logging, Welli, our AI-powered personal health assistant, and an AI Body Scan to provide crucial personalized insights, all designed to maximize results.

Medication

L

![Illustration of a Noom weight-loss care experience featuring a message from Dr. Nancy Lynn on a smartphone screen, with images of a semaglutide injector pen, a vial, and a Noom-branded pill.](https://www.noom.com/tachyon/2025/07/s3-medication-desktop-3.5x%402x-scaled.webp?w=1080)

Medication

Noom provides access to a wide-range of medications for weight management and conditions such as menopause—with more to come. Paired with our proven lifestyle change program, these medications ensure that members have the comprehensive care they need to reach their health goals.

Coaching

L

![Noom app image featuring a supportive coach message helping a user navigate a weight-loss plateau. Demonstrates Noom’s psychology-based approach with personalized coaching to drive sustainable behavior change.](https://www.noom.com/uploads/2025/10/s3-coaching-desktop-scaled.png)

Coaching

Noom members are matched with our team of coaches who help guide them along their health journeys and provide the personalized support needed to achieve their goals.

“I was determined to find a fix for a system that had become so focused on sick care instead of health care. Most everyone knows they should eat healthier, exercise more, be less stressed, and get better sleep, but most of us don’t know how. Noom gives you the how—and the why—that empowers you to take control of your health.”

**—SAEJU JEONG, CO-FOUNDER**

Through extensive research that began in 2008, Saeju and co-founder Artem Petakov discovered that helping people achieve a healthy weight was a critical starting point for creating transformative long-term health outcomes.

Now, after helping millions of people lose weight and lead healthier lives, Noom continues to evolve our learnings and apply them to today’s largest health problems. Noom’s highly personalized behavior change platform plus cutting-edge technology and medications are powerful tools for addressing a broad range of chronic and non-chronic conditions—helping more people everywhere live better longer.
