## Frequently Asked Questions

_2_ _3_

### How does microdosing a GLP-1 work?

Noom’s Microdose Program uses lower doses of medication designed by our Chief Medical Officer to lose weight, reduce side effects, preserve muscle mass, and support sustainable weight management.

_2_ _3_

### How is Noom Microdose GLP-1Rx Program different from Noom’s other GLP-1 programs?

The Microdose Program utilizes lower doses of medication. It comes with the same comprehensive Noom program and clinical care and support as our other Med programs.

_2_ _3_

### Will I still lose weight on a smaller dose?

It is absolutely possible to reach your weight-loss goals with a smaller dose. For example, members using Microdose GLP-1Rx lose up to 17 pounds in 60 days2. The key is finding your optimal dose, not the maximum dose.

_2_ _3_

### What kind of support comes with Noom Microdose GLP-1Rx Program?

You’ll have access to our clinical team, receive dose optimization guidance, coaching support, and a community of members on similar journeys.

_2_ _3_

### How long does it take to see results?

Most members begin losing weight within the first month. The gradual approach is designed to drive sustainable results that are easier to maintain long-term.
