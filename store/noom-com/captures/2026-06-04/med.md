## Frequently Asked Questions

_2_ _3_

### What exactly is Noom Med and how does it work?

Noom Med pairs prescription-weight-loss medications with Noom’s behavior change psychology and personalized clinical support. Members work with licensed clinicians who prescribe and manage medications when clinically appropriate, while Noom’s digital tools and coaching help sustain long-term habits that support lasting weight loss.

_2_ _3_

### What weight loss medications does Noom Med offer?

Noom Med can provide access to GLP-1 medications such as [Wegovy®](https://www.noom.com/med/wegovy/), [Zepbound®](https://www.noom.com/med/zepbound/), [Ozempic®](https://www.noom.com/med/ozempic/), and others when appropriate, as well as select oral weight-loss medications like [metformin](https://www.noom.com/med/metformin/), or Wegovy® pill for eligible individuals. Prescribed medications require a clinical evaluation.

_2_ _3_

### How much does weight loss medication cost with Noom?

Costs vary depending on your treatment plan and medication.

- The Noom Microdose GLP-1Rx Program provides direct access to [GLP-1s starting at $99](https://www.noom.com/med/glp1-microdose/), regardless of insurance, when prescribed.
- Noom Med also includes access to brand-name medications like [Ozempic®](https://www.noom.com/med/ozempic/) and [Zepbound®](https://www.noom.com/med/zepbound/) starting at $69 plus any out-of-pocket cost for the medication itself depending on insurance coverage.

All medication plans include full access to the Noom app, with psychology-based lessons, tracking tools, and support designed to help you build lasting habits and maintain long-term results.

_2_ _3_

### Do I need insurance to get weight-loss medications from Noom?

No — you do not need insurance. Noom Med supports both members with and without insurance.

If you have insurance, we will work to help maximize your coverage for medication and clinician services.

If you do not have insurance, you can still access clinicians and medications through affordable cash-pay options.

_2_ _3_

### How much weight can I expect to lose with Noom Med?

Results vary based on your medication, health history, and consistency with the program. However, **Noom Med users lose 48% more weight in 6 months compared to those taking GLP-1 medications without Noom**\*, highlighting the added impact of combining medication with psychology-based behavior change support.

_\*Following clinical trial information is for branded medications only: In 68- and 72-week clinical trials studying Wegovy® (2.4 mg) and Zepbound® (15 mg) in patients without diabetes and with BMI ≥ 30, or BMI ≥ 27 with a weight-related condition, the average weight loss was 15% and 20%, respectively, when paired with diet and exercise changes (compared to 2.4% and 3.1%, respectively, with diet and exercise alone). The FDA does not review any compounded medication for safety or efficacy._

_2_ _3_

### How do I get started with Noom Med?

1. Complete an [online health assessment](https://www.noom.com/lp/clinical?route=clinical) to tell us about your goals and medical history.
2. Connect with an expert clinician who will determine whether weight-loss medication is appropriate.
3. If prescribed, have your weight-loss medication coordinated through your pharmacy or shipped to you.
4. Use the Noom app for tools, coaching, and ongoing behavior support to help you stay on track.
