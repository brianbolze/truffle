Prediabetes is a costly population health problem, with one in three American adults affected by prediabetes and 80% unaware of their condition4. With Noom’s CDC-recognized Diabetes Prevention Program that has earned Full Plus Recognition, organizations can offer the **highest-quality, evidence-based diabetes prevention**, reducing the incidence of this costly chronic disease.

**Build up healthier habits, drive down the risk of diabetes.**

- Techniques that teach people the “why” behind their habits and how to change them.
- Daily lessons tailored to each person’s goals.
- One-on-one support from DPP lifestyle coaches who are trained in diabetes prevention.
- Comprehensive monitoring of members’ routine to help deepen their awareness of the areas to focus on.
- Tech-enabled experience that automates food logging, tracks movement, and integrates seamlessly with people’s devices.
- Awarded Full Plus Recognition by the CDC.

### Diabetes management is no small feat. That’s where Noom comes in.

People with type 2 diabetes don’t always have the easiest time managing it. When 47% have uncontrolled A1C levels5, 89% are overweight or suffer from obesity6, and 76% struggle with consistent physical activity7–the numbers speak for themselves. Noom’s psychology-driven Diabetes Management Program helps those suffering from type 2 diabetes manage their A1C levels, assess risk factors, and build sustainable healthy habits, guiding them to a tailored lifestyle change that aids in long-term glycemic control.

**Diabetes management made simple.**

- Personalized condition coaching that provides guidance on clinical protocols.
- Blood glucose management that guides people towards healthier habits.
- Tailored learning path to help understand and manage common challenges they may face.
- Macro monitoring with tracking tools and detailed meal analysis.
- Diabetes-specific community circle that offers a supportive environment to members.
- One-on-one support and personalized clinical care with diabetes experts.\*
- Medication management when clinically appropriate.\*

\*Available late 2025.

### Noom works for the clinical treatment of obesity.

Breakthrough medications, like GLP-1s, have revolutionized obesity care. While these medications have proven effective, they can be costly and much less effective in sustaining weight loss on their own. With Noom Med, our powerful obesity care solution, we match biology with psychology to deliver better outcomes at a lower overall cost for long-term success.

**A sustainable approach to obesity treatment.**

- One-on-one care from a specialized obesity clinician.
- Integrated lab testing to assess metabolic health.
- Evaluation of hidden biological factors causing weight gain.
- Prescribed medication when clinically necessary.
- SmartRX pharmacy carve-out solution fulfills GLP-1s medications at the lowest net cost without the complexity of rebates, with the flexibility to set custom copays separate from other covered medications.
- GLP-1 Companion provides tailored lifestyle support for those taking a GLP-1 or other anti-obesity medication.

[Learn More](https://www.noom.com/health/glp1companion/)

### Noom is revolutionizing the affordability and responsible use of GLP-1s.

SmartRx is a unique cost carve-out solution for employers that offers transparent, discounted pricing on GLP-1 medications for weight loss.
