## Frequently Asked Questions

What is a GLP-1 Companion?

A GLP-1 Companion is a digital health experience that supports people taking GLP-1 weight loss medications by helping them navigate treatment more easily and turn medication-driven weight loss into lasting lifestyle change.

What kind of support does Noom’s GLP-1 Companion provide?

Noom’s GLP-1 Companion offers smart medication guidance, side-effect support, habit coaching, personalized nutrition guidance, muscle-focused movement, body composition insights, and everyday tools to support consistency and long-term success on GLP-1 medication.

Do people tend to lose more weight when they use a GLP-1 Companion?

Real-world Noom data shows that members who engage more deeply with Noom’s GLP-1 Companion experience greater weight loss and stay on medication longer—highlighting how GLP-1 digital health support can help people get more from their treatment.

Can the GLP-1 Companion be added to an existing Noom plan?

Yes. The GLP-1 Companion can be added to an existing Noom plan, allowing eligible members to access GLP-1 digital health support without starting over.

How much does Noom’s GLP-1 Companion cost?

Noom’s GLP-1 Companion is included at no additional cost with a Noom plan for eligible members taking GLP-1 medication.
