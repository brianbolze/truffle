Features

Quality Guard screens, scores, and replaces responses across every study.

![](https://framerusercontent.com/images/Buhoukd7IiUcEKOJxCYxNmY0.png?width=1392&height=1280)

Real-Time Detection

Quality Guard analyzes voice patterns, response depth, and behaviors like tab-switching and screen reading. It screens for contradictions, copy-pasted answers, repeat respondents, and other fraud. Every flagged interview is backed by human review.

Built-In Quality Scoring

Each response is scored across signals including informativeness, response depth, engagement, follow-up quality, repetitiveness, and confusion. Responses that don't meet the bar are removed and replaced.
