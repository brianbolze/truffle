Analyze and quantify your data

Segment respondents, compare groups, and run significance testing in natural language. Every chart and number traces back to the underlying responses, so you can verify what you're seeing before you act on it.

Research Agent works the way researchers do: explore broadly, dig into what matters, then pull it together. Ask follow-up questions, refine your segments, and layer in web context all within Listen.
