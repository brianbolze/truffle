## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can BENADRYL® products make me drowsy?

The diphenhydramine in BENADRYL® Allergy can cause drowsiness.

### Can I use BENADRYL® past the expiration date?

If your BENADRYL® has expired, please discard it properly and purchase a new package. [Visit the FDA](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/safe-disposal-medicines/disposal-unused-medicines-what-you-should-know) website for more information.

### How much BENADRYL® can I take?

When taking any over-the-counter medicine, always read the drug facts label carefully and use only as directed. Follow this [dosing guide](https://www.benadryl.com/benadryl-dosing-guide) to learn more about how much to take and how often to use BENADRYL® products.

### Can I use BENADRYL® products if I have high blood pressure?

If you take prescription medication or are being treated for any medical condition, check with your doctor or pharmacist before using any over-the-counter medicines.

Ask a doctor before use if you have:

- Heart disease

- High blood pressure
