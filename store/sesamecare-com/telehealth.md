## Credibility & access
- **Health-merchant credibility:** LegitScript-certified (footer seal); Better Business Bureau accredited (footer); named clinicians and leadership (team page, provider listings); provider vetting claims active licensure, years of practice, education, training, and specializations as listing conditions. Pharmacy accreditations not shown.
- **Controlled-substance Rx:** non-scheduled only through virtual visits — captured pages repeatedly say Sesame providers cannot prescribe controlled substances, including stimulants, opioids, benzodiazepines, narcotics, testosterone, and controlled weight-loss drugs such as phentermine/Qsymia.
- **Labs:** optional/provider-ordered. General FAQ says lab orders are submitted by Sesame providers when clinically appropriate. Weight-loss subscription may include Quest lab work with state exceptions; menopause subscription includes basic labs if necessary and routes orders to Quest, LabCorp, Clinical Labs of Hawaii, or patient-paid local labs depending on state.
- **Payment & commitment:** visits are direct-pay; Sesame says it does not bill health insurance for visits. Payment options include card payments and HSA/FSA debit cards; prescription/refill and subscription pages state HSA/FSA eligibility or reimbursement. Medication coverage may still use insurance at the pharmacy, and weight-loss providers may help with medication prior authorization. Commitment is mixed: no membership needed for ordinary visits; Sesame Plus is optional at "$10.99 per month or $99 per year"; weight loss, Mental Health Rx, and menopause are subscription programs with self-cancellation/refund rules.
