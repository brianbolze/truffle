Am I a good candidate for Sesame’s Mental Health Rx service?Material Add Icon

Sesame’s Mental Health Rx subscription is an affordable, convenient and clinically comprehensive option for individuals ages 18+ who may need or who are currently using psychiatric medications to manage conditions like anxiety disorders, depression, post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD), insomnia, performance anxiety, or who are experiencing symptoms such as constant worry, stress, burnout or trouble sleeping.

This service might be right for you if:

- You are struggling with your mental health and unsure where to begin.
- You paused medication use, but feel like you need medication support again.
- You've lost access to your current mental health prescriber.
- You are experiencing long wait times for care.
- Your current mental health care costs are too high.

Sesame’s Mental Health Rx subscription is not intended for the treatment of bipolar disorder, borderline personality disorder (BDP), obsessive-compulsive disorder (OCD), eating disorders, schizoaffective disorder or schizophrenia. Note that all prescriptions are at the discretion of your Sesame healthcare provider.

What is included in my Mental Health Rx subscription?Material Add Icon

Your Mental Health Rx monthly subscription price will include the following:

- An initial video evaluation with a provider of your choice, along with follow-up video visits to monitor medication progress and side effects as needed
- 28 days of unlimited open messaging with your provider
- A prescription and/or refill for mental health medication(s), if appropriate.
- Medication management (adjustments to medication dosage as needed)

Please note that **medication is not included** in the price of your Mental Health Rx subscription. However, generic mental health medications are covered by most major insurers, and you will also receive a prescription savings card from Sesame that can help reduce the cost of medication by up to 80%, whether you’re insured or not.

Providers on Sesame set their own prices, which means that some providers may offer the Mental Health Rx subscription at a higher price.

Can I get prescription mental health medication online?Material Add Icon

Yes. If deemed clinically appropriate, a licensed professional on Sesame can prescribe medication and other treatments for mental health conditions like anxiety, depression, PTSD, insomnia, and performance anxiety. Any prescriptions will be sent to a pharmacy of your choice for pickup.

Commonly prescribed mental health medications include anti-anxiety medications, antidepressants, mood stabilizers, and antipsychotics. Examples include serotonin and norepinephrine reuptake inhibitors (SNRIs), selective serotonin reuptake inhibitors (SSRIs) and tricyclic antidepressants (TCAs).

Can I obtain refills for multiple prescriptions? Material Add Icon
Yes, you can get refills for more than one prescription with Mental Health Rx! Please note that patients are responsible for covering the costs of all prescribed medications.
Do psychiatrists on Sesame prescribe controlled substances?Material Add Icon
No, providers on Sesame will not prescribe medications that are listed as controlled substances. This includes stimulant ADHD medication (Adderall, Vyvanse), benzodiazepines (Ativan, Klonopin, Valium, Xanax), and opioids.
Does Mental Health Rx include talk therapy or psychiatry?Material Add Icon

No, Mental Health Rx is a medication management program designed to make it easier and more affordable to refill medications for anxiety disorders, depression, and more.

However, you can book a broad range of online psychiatry services and talk therapy services on Sesame, including:

- [Online therapist sessions](https://sesamecare.com/specialty/therapist)
- [Online psychiatric visits](https://sesamecare.com/specialty/online-psychiatrists)
- [Online couples therapy](https://sesamecare.com/service/online-couples-therapy-session)

In addition, licensed mental health care providers on Sesame can address and treat mental health conditions such as:

- [Anxiety](https://sesamecare.com/complaint/anxiety)
- [Depression](https://sesamecare.com/complaint/depression)
- [Post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD)](https://sesamecare.com/complaint/ptsd-treatment)
- [Bipolar disorder](https://sesamecare.com/complaint/bipolar-disorder)
- [Obsessive compulsive disorder (OCD)](https://sesamecare.com/complaint/obsessive-compulsive-disorder)
- [Adult attention-deficit/hyperactivity disorder (ADHD)](https://sesamecare.com/complaint/adhd)
- [Schizophrenia](https://sesamecare.com/complaint/schizophrenia)

This is not a complete list of psychiatric services offered by providers on Sesame. Use our search bar to look for the specific type of psychiatric care or treatment and book an appointment at your convenience. Most care providers will request an initial consultation to discuss your symptoms or concerns. After a consult, providers on Sesame can refer you to a specialist, refer you to therapy, and/or schedule follow-up appointments for further care.

Do you accept health insurance? Material Add Icon

No! Sesame is available to everyone regardless of insurance status, and we do not bill health insurance in order to keep prices affordable for all. This means you don’t need to worry about surprise bills, specialist referrals or insurance coverage to access high-quality care.

Are any of the services included in my subscription eligible for HSA or FSA reimbursement? Material Add Icon
Yes, if you have a Health Savings Account (HSA) or Flexible Spending Account (FSA) through your health insurance plan, most of the services within your subscription may be eligible for reimbursement. If you reach out to us at support@sesamecare.com, we will provide an itemized bill that you can submit to your HSA or FSA.
What is the refund policy? Material Add Icon

Sesame’s commitment to providing a convenient, high-quality mental health medication management service is matched by our dedication to fair and transparent customer service practices. Our refund policy for subscriptions and medications is outlined below:

**Subscription refunds**

- If you sign up for Sesame's Mental Health Rx subscription and cancel at least 3 hours prior to your initial visit start time, you will receive a full refund. No refunds will be issued for the first month if the initial visit has occurred.
- You can self-cancel your subscription at any point. To avoid future charges, cancellation must occur before the next billing cycle starts. Fees paid for previous months are not refundable.

**Requesting a refund**

To request a refund, please contact Sesame’s Customer Success Team at support@sesamecare.com. Provide your name, contact information, and details of the service for which you are requesting a refund. Our team will review your request and guide you through the refund process based on the terms above.

Please note that this refund policy is subject to change, and we advise our patients to review it regularly. Your continued use of our services following any changes indicates your acceptance of such changes. Sesame’s full cancellation policy can be found [here](https://sesamecare.com/faq) and within our [Terms of Service](https://sesamecare.com/terms-of-service).

Important safety information Material Add Icon

Sesame’s Mental Health Rx service is not meant to serve as a mental health crisis intervention service or as psychiatric care.

If you or someone you know is experiencing a mental health crisis or emergency, such as suicidal thoughts, psychosis, severe distress, or a risk of harm to oneself or others, seek immediate help by contacting a local emergency number, visiting the nearest emergency room, or calling the [National Suicide Prevention Lifeline](https://988lifeline.org/) at 988.
