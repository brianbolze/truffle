Am I a good candidate for weight loss medication?Material Add Icon

Great question! Ideal candidates for weight loss medication include those who have a body mass index (BMI) of 30 or higher, OR a BMI of 27 or higher with weight-related health conditions like Type 2 diabetes, high blood pressure or high cholesterol.

Most weight loss plans involve a class of medications called GLP-1 agonists, which can be used to treat overweight and obesity; however, GLP-1 medications are not suitable for everyone and should be avoided by:

- Those with a history of pancreatitis
- Those with a personal or family history of medullary thyroid cancer (MTC)
- Those with multiple endocrine neoplasia syndrome Type 2 (MEN2)
- Those with Type 1 diabetes

A medical provider on Sesame can help determine if GLP-1s or an alternative medication is appropriate based on your individual health needs and circumstances.

What prescription drugs are available for weight loss?Material Add Icon

There are a range of prescription drugs that can aid in weight loss. Some are FDA-approved specifically for weight loss, while others are diabetes medications that are prescribed off-label. These include:

**FDA-approved weight loss medications include:**

- [Wegovy (semaglutide)](https://sesamecare.com/medication/wegovy)
- [Zepbound (tirzepatide)](https://sesamecare.com/medication/zepbound)
- [Saxenda (liraglutide)](https://sesamecare.com/medication/saxenda)
- [Contrave (naltrexone/bupropion ER)](https://sesamecare.com/medication/contrave)
- [Orlistat (Xenical)](https://sesamecare.com/medication/orlistat)

**Diabetes medications that may cause weight loss include:**

- [Ozempic (semaglutide)](https://sesamecare.com/medication/ozempic)
- [Mounjaro (tirzepatide)](https://sesamecare.com/medication/mounjaro)
- [Rybelsus (oral semaglutide)](https://sesamecare.com/medication/rybelsus)
- [Victoza (liraglutide)](https://sesamecare.com/medication/victoza)
- [Metformin (Glucophage)](https://sesamecare.com/medication/metformin)

There are also several weight loss medications that are classified as controlled substances due to their potential for abuse, including Qsymia (phentermine/topiramate ER) and phentermine. Please note that providers on Sesame do not prescribe controlled weight loss medications.

What are the benefits of losing weight?Material Add Icon

Reaching a healthy weight can lead to noticeable physical changes, but its impact goes far beyond appearance. Losing weight can also lower your risk of developing certain health conditions (and improve existing ones). These benefits can improve your quality of life — starting from within.

**Here are some key benefits of weight loss:**

- **Reduced risk of chronic diseases:** Losing weight can help prevent or manage conditions like type 2 diabetes, liver and kidney failure, cancer, certain types of arthritis, high blood pressure, obstructive sleep apnea and heart disease.
- **Better heart health**: Weight loss can improve cholesterol levels and lower your risk of stroke.
- **Improved mobility**: Losing weight makes it easier to move around and reduces strain on your joints, especially your knees.
- **Better sleep**: Losing weight can help reduce obstructive sleep apnea symptoms and improve overall sleep quality.
- **Enhanced mood and self-esteem**: Achieving weight loss goals can boost your confidence and may help improve symptoms of depression and stress.
- **Increased energy and physical fitness**: Reaching a healthy weight can boost your energy levels throughout the day and improve physical fitness. Consistent exercise and strength training are especially important for those on GLP-1 medications, as they may put some people at risk of losing muscle mass. This is why your clinician will recommend adequate protein intake and specific strength training exercises.
- **Improved sexual function and libido**: Weight loss can improve erectile dysfunction and sex drive (libido), particularly for those with certain health conditions like polycystic ovarian syndrome (PCOS) and obesity
- **Healthier blood sugar levels**: Weight loss can improve your body's insulin sensitivity, which helps regulate blood sugar levels.

Even moderate weight loss can make a big difference to your health. If you’re in need of clinical obesity guidance, your Success by Sesame provider will support you every step of the way on your weight loss journey.

Important safety information Material Add Icon

Certain GLP-1 therapies such as semaglutide (the active ingredient in Ozempic, Rybelsus, and Wegovy), liraglutide (the active ingredient in Saxenda and Victoza), and tirzepatide (the active ingredient in Mounjaro and Zepbound) may increase the likelihood of developing thyroid gland tumors, including MTC, a type of thyroid cancer. This conclusion is based on studies where laboratory animals given semaglutide, liraglutide, tirzepatide and other GLP-1 medications developed tumors. However, it is not yet confirmed if these medications pose the same risk to humans. You can find additional information about the risks of FDA-approved medications that contain semaglutide [here](https://www.novomedlink.com/semaglutide/medicines.html).

It’s important to inform your doctor if you or any family member has ever had MTC or MEN. If this is the case, your doctor will likely advise against taking GLP-1 medications.

If you are prescribed a GLP-1 medication and exhibit any of the following symptoms, you should contact your provider immediately:

- A lump or swelling in the neck

- Hoarseness

- Difficulty swallowing

- Shortness of breath

- Severe abdominal pain


Before taking GLP-1 medications, you should also discuss the following with your provider:

**Allergies**: Inform your doctor and pharmacist of any allergies you have to semaglutide (including under brand names Ozempic, Rybelsus, Wegovy) or similar medications such as tirzepatide (Mounjaro, Zepbound), dulaglutide (Trulicity), exenatide (Bydureon BCise, Byetta), liraglutide (Saxenda, Victoza, or in Xultophy) and lixisenatide (Adlyxin, or in Soliqua).

**Personal and family medical history:** Be sure to disclose if you have a history of medical conditions like pancreatitis, diabetic retinopathy, gallbladder disease, kidney disease, or thyroid cancer. Inform your doctor about recent episodes of diarrhea, nausea, vomiting or any inability to consume liquids, which could lead to dehydration.

**Current medications and supplements:** Provide a comprehensive list of all medicines and supplements you are currently taking, including prescription and nonprescription medicines, vitamins, nutritional supplements, and herbal products. This is crucial as GLP-1 medications can alter how your body absorbs oral medications.

**Pregnancy and breastfeeding:** Inform your doctor if you are pregnant, breastfeeding, or planning to become pregnant soon.

**Surgical procedures:** In the event of surgery, including dental procedures, inform the medical or dental professional that you are on GLP-1 medication.

It's essential to thoroughly discuss these risks with your healthcare provider and consider your personal and family medical history when deciding whether to use these medications. Your doctor can provide guidance on the best course of action based on your specific health situation.
