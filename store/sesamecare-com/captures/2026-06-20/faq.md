## About Sesame

What is Sesame?Material Add Icon

Sesame is the first-ever healthcare marketplace that allows patients to book directly with licensed doctors and providers at affordable, upfront cash prices — no long wait times, no hidden fees, and no insurance needed. We call it "half-price healthcare."

How? We've stripped away all of the things that mark up the price of your care, like third-party billing fees and insurance middlemen. Instead, patients can book online and in-person care across 36+ specialties, including dermatology & mental health, within 1-2 hours at the touch of a button.

Why is Sesame different?Material Add Icon
Sesame is the first direct-to-patient care marketplace that connects patients with real, quality doctors.
Who is Sesame for?Material Add Icon
Sesame is made for everyone - regardless of your insurance status. On Sesame, you can find, book, and pay doctors directly at unbeatable cash prices. Many of our customers either do not have insurance coverage or use Sesame to save money by paying doctors directly.
Do you accept health insurance? Material Add Icon

No! Sesame is available to everyone regardless of insurance status, and we do not bill health insurance in order to keep prices affordable for all. This means you don’t need to worry about surprise bills, specialist referrals or insurance coverage to access high-quality care.

How does booking care on Sesame work?Material Add Icon

Sesame makes it easy to find and pay for care directly, allowing you to book an in-person or telehealth appointment in just a few minutes. To get started, follow these steps:

- Search for telehealth or in-person appointments by specialty, condition, or provider
- Choose a provider at a price and time that works for you
- Pay for your appointment online, upfront
- Complete a brief medical history form before your appointment

At your scheduled time, you’ll either join your telehealth appointment or visit the provider in person, depending on the type of care selected.

Who are the doctors on Sesame?Material Add Icon
All clinicians on Sesame (doctors, dentists, nurse practitioners, etc.) are independent medical professionals.
What is Sesame’s cancellation policy?Material Add Icon

Sesame offers full refunds for appointments canceled within the following timeframes:

- **Virtual appointments**: at least 3 hours before the scheduled time
- **In-person appointments**: at least 24 hours before the scheduled time
- **All other appointments (e.g., labs, imaging, or those without a set date/time)**: within 10 days of purchase, once the provider confirms the appointment did not take place
- **Subscriptions**: You may cancel your subscription at any time up to forty-eight (48) hours before the applicable renewal processing date.

**To cancel via web:**

- Navigate to sesamecare.com
- Click on **Account** (top right)
- Click on **Visits** or **Programs**
- Select the program you’re enrolled in or visit time and date
- Scroll down and click **Cancel Appointment** or **Cancel Program**

**To cancel your subscription via the mobile app:**

- Open the app and go to the **Home** tab
- Select **View Program Details** under **Treatment Progress**
- Navigate to the **Program Information** tab
- Scroll to **Subscription Details**
- Tap **Manage Subscription**
- Log in if prompted and select **Cancel Program**

**To cancel a visit via the mobile app:**

- Click the **Visits** tab
- Select your visit
- Scroll to **Visit Details**
- Tap **Cancel This Visit**

Alternatively, you can request cancellation by emailing support@sesamecare.com or calling +1 (877) 947-6411.

If you cancel your subscription, you will continue to have access to benefits through the end of your current billing period. Subscriptions will not renew after cancellation, and prorated refunds are not provided.

**Refund Exceptions**
We are unable to offer refunds for:

- Patient no-shows
- Requests within 24 hours of in-person appointments or 3 hours of virtual appointments
- Requests for specific medications or paperwork that are not accepted
- Surgical procedures with confirmed scheduling
- Labs not completed within 7 days of purchase

Am I eligible to use Sesame?Material Add Icon
Yes, Sesame is open to everyone, regardless of insurance coverage. That means people with insurance, without insurance and anything in-between can use Sesame.
Is my payment information safe? Material Add Icon
Sesame uses [Stripe](https://stripe.com/), the most trusted, secure third-party payment platform in the world. Stripe keeps your payment information for all future purchases on their secure servers; no one else receives access to your payment information.

## About telehealth appointments

What is a telehealth appointment and how does it work?Material Add Icon

Telehealth appointments are medical visits that allow you to speak to a doctor or health care provider online via video call. These visits allow you to access care at home or on-the-go, often at a better price.

Doctors on Sesame provide high-quality care over video, including everything from treating the common cold to crafting a treatment plan to fight depression, and so much more. To get the most out of your visit, make sure you are in a well-lit space with a solid WiFi connection.

How do I access my telehealth appointment? Material Add Icon

After you book a video appointment, you will receive both an initial confirmation and a reminder via email and text.

There are three ways in which a video visit can be accessed:

1. Select the **Join virtual appointment** button in your booking confirmation email. If you have an existing account or created one at purchase, log in at sesamecare.com, navigate to **My appointments**, then select **Join virtual appointment**.
2. Click the link in the text message sent to you 15 minutes prior to your scheduled appointment time.
3. Log into your Sesame account through the mobile app or web browser, and join your visit from there.

If you're using a web browser, we suggest using Google Chrome, Firefox, or Safari to connect to your visit.

What should I do if I didn’t receive my invite?Material Add Icon

If you didn’t receive your visit invite, you can still access your appointment details directly through your account.

**On web:**

- Log in to your account
- Click your profile icon in the top right corner
- Select **Visits** from the dropdown menu
- You will see both upcoming and past visits there

**On mobile app:**

- Open the app
- Tap **More**
- Select **Visits**
- You will be able to view your upcoming and past visits from there


If you still can’t locate your visit after following these steps, please contact Sesame Customer Support for further assistance.

What should I do if my doctor doesn’t join the appointment?Material Add Icon

If your provider is unable to attend the visit, you’ll have the option to either reschedule by selecting a different provider or you can choose another time with the same provider.

Alternatively, you will be given the option to request a full refund if that's what you'd prefer.

If you need assistance with scheduling, please contact Sesame Support by emailing support@sesamecare.com, or by calling +1 (877) 947-6411.

How will I know if the appointment I’m booking is via video?Material Add Icon
The service name and details will clarify if the appointment you’re booking is via video.
What should I do if I need lab work?Material Add Icon

All lab orders are submitted by Sesame providers. If you need a lab order, you'll first need to book a visit with a Sesame provider of your choosing.

To book an appointment with a provider in your state, you can use the link here. Please confirm your location in the top right corner of the page.

Please note that providers may issue lab orders when clinically appropriate. Since this is determined after an assessment, lab orders are not guaranteed with the purchase of an appointment.

Can my provider prescribe medication?Material Add Icon

Yes, physicians on Sesame can prescribe a wide range of medications, which can be useful for infections, allergies, and other acute ailments.
Please note that they will not prescribe certain drugs, such as narcotics or medications that have been designated controlled substances through telehealth.

Otherwise, many of the prescriptions available in an office setting or urgent care can be prescribed if your clinician deems it appropriate.

Can I receive a work note or other paperwork with my visit?Material Add Icon

Providers are able to issue notes, referrals, lab orders, or other paperwork when deemed appropriate. Providers are not able to assist with worker's compensation, FMLA, disability, or insurance paperwork. In-person care is sometimes required to obtain paperwork from a provider.

Inclusions and exclusions can be viewed prior to booking in the service details.

Work note or other paperwork requests must be shared before or at the time of service. Sesame is unable to facilitate paperwork requests after completion of the visit.

Sesame is unable to provide paperwork specific to requirements for filing insurance claims.

Can I obtain controlled substances or narcotics on Sesame?Material Add Icon
Sesame providers are unable to prescribe controlled substances, including narcotics, through virtual visits. These prescriptions may be prescribed in an office setting and require in person care, if deemed clinically appropriate.
How can I prepare for my telehealth appointment?Material Add Icon

After booking your telehealth appointment on Sesame, you will receive a link to test the phone, tablet, or computer where you will speak with your provider.

Sesame will send you a unique and secure webpage for you to access your telehealth appointment directly from your web browser or mobile app. In the rare case that you run into any technical issues, our friendly support team is available to walk you through any challenges.

## About cost, pricing, and offerings

What are my payment options?Material Add Icon

On Sesame you can pay using VISA, MasterCard, American Express, and Discover. You cannot pay by check, money orders, or cash.

If you have a health savings account (HSA) or flexible spending account (FSA), these accounts often include debit cards you can use for purchases. These cards can also be used on Sesame.

Do I have to pay online or can I pay at the doctor’s office?Material Add Icon
All appointments booked on Sesame must be paid for online.
How much does each appointment cost?Material Add Icon
Prices are set individually by each provider.
Do I need to be a member to use Sesame?Material Add Icon
No, you do not need to be a member to take advantage of Sesame’s unbeatable prices. Services are priced individually and available to everyone at affordable direct-pay prices. However, if you want to save up to 25% on all services and get the most out of Sesame, you can join our monthly membership Sesame Plus. Learn more about Sesame memberships [here](https://sesamecare.com/join/membership).
Can I use insurance to pay for my appointment?Material Add Icon

Sesame services are paid directly, separate from health insurance.

If your insurance includes a health savings account (HSA) or flexible spending account (FSA), you can use your funds from these accounts to pay for care on Sesame. Some of these plans include a debit card you can use to pay upfront, and others require you to submit your receipt after your purchase. Your booking confirmation will serve as your receipt. Sesame does not provide any additional paperwork.

Providers are unable to assist with insurance claims, including prescriptions.

What if I need to reschedule my appointment?Material Add Icon

You can reschedule your appointment if your plans change:

- **Virtual appointments**: Must be rescheduled at least 3 hours before the scheduled time.
- **In-person appointments**: Must be rescheduled at least 24 hours before the scheduled time.

If you reschedule within the allowed timeframe, the amount you paid will be issued as a credit to your account, which you can apply toward your next booking.

Reschedule requests made outside of these timeframes may not be eligible for credit.

Can I get a prescription refilled?Material Add Icon
Yes, you can book an online or in-person prescription refill appointment in which a provider will determine the clinical appropriateness and length of refill with each prescription. Sesame does not guarantee prescription orders or refills. You may book an online prescription refill appointment [here](https://sesamecare.com/service/online-prescription-refill-visit).
How can I send my Sesame prescription to another pharmacy?Material Add Icon

Sending your prescription to a different pharmacy is simple! Once your Sesame provider submits your prescription, you’ll receive a text message with a link to view your prescription details.

If you’d like to change the pharmacy, simply click the link in the text message and update the pharmacy information directly.

Can I use any pharmacy when getting a refill?Material Add Icon
Yes. Once your provider submits your refill prescription, you’ll receive a text message with a link that allows you to select any U.S. pharmacy of your choice. Simply follow the link and choose the pharmacy that is most convenient for you.
Do I have to pay for a follow-up appointment?Material Add Icon
Unless stated otherwise in your appointment, if you need a follow-up appointment, you will need to book and pay for that separately.
Can I pay for my visit with an FSA or HSA?Material Add Icon
If your insurance includes a health savings account (HSA) or flexible spending account (FSA), you can use your funds from these accounts to pay for care on Sesame. Some of these plans include a debit card you can use to pay upfront, and others require you to submit your receipt after your purchase.

## About provider reviews

Does Sesame moderate provider reviews?Material Add Icon
Yes, we moderate reviews to ensure they are informational, helpful, and trustworthy, while not sharing any Protected Health Information about a patient.
Are all reviews verified?Material Add Icon
Yes, all provider reviews are verified to guarantee that reviews are only left by real patients. All reviews on Sesame are directly from patients who have already had an appointment with a provider.
How do I leave a review after my appointment?Material Add Icon
After your appointment, you will receive an email from us asking for feedback. Follow the link in the email to review your provider. After you submit your feedback, our team will review it to ensure no personal information is shared. Approved reviews are published in a few days.
Are all provider reviews posted?Material Add Icon

We make an effort to publish all provider reviews. However, if your review includes any of the following information, we may choose to withhold the review:

1. **Personal Information**: Reviews with personally identifiable information will not be shared. All Protected Health Information (PHI) will be removed before posting the review.
2. **Profanity**: Reviews with offensive content or profanity will not be posted.
3. **Accuracy of diagnosis/ treatment plan**: While we encourage you to describe your experience with the provider, we don’t publish claims about the accuracy of a provider’s treatment or diagnosis.

I submitted a review. Why hasn’t it been posted yet? Material Add Icon
We have a team that moderates all reviews to ensure your privacy is protected and guarantee that all reviews are helpful and accurate reflections of our providers. That way, other patients can confidently select a provider that will best match their needs. This process can take a few days. We appreciate your patience.
Does Sesame post only positive reviews?Material Add Icon
No. We make an effort to publish all provider reviews - positive or negative - provided that they meet Sesame's standards. Reviews allow patients to confidently select a provider who best matches their needs, and we believe that authentic customer feedback empowers people to make informed decisions about their care.
