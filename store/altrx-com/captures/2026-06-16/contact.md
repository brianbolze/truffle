What states do you service?

We service all 50 states.

What happens after I'm qualified?

Once you're approved, a licensed clinician reviews your intake, writes the prescription if appropriate, and your medication is shipped to your door (the full flow typically takes ~2–3 weeks). We'll be along to help if you have any questions. Email us anytime at help@altrx.com

Do I need insurance?

No! Insurance is not required. Most GLP-1 programs (including altRx) are set up as straightforward self-pay care. We do offer Buy Now Pay Later plans, as well.

Is GLP-1 safe for me?

GLP-1s are FDA-approved for weight management in appropriate patients, but they are not right for everyone — your clinician screens your history and risks (including key contraindications like certain thyroid cancers/MEN2).

What if I stop or pause?

If you stop with your GLP-1 program, appetite and weight regain are common, so many people either taper, maintain, or restart with a clinician-led plan and lifestyle support.

When do I see results?

Many people notice appetite changes early, with more visible weight loss typically building over the first 4–12 weeks and continuing over months with consistency.

Are there hidden fees?

Never! With altRx we work hard to offer transparent, affordable pricing. Our pricing includes the consult, prescription process, and shipping/delivery and will always be stated on our pricing pages.

How old do I have to be to participate in the altRx program?

altRx requires users to be 18+ to use the service/site.

What happens if my BMI falls below 25?

Because GLP-1s for weight management are indicated for obesity or overweight with a related condition, your clinician will reassess and may adjust dosing, shift to maintenance, or pause/discontinue depending on your health goals and risk factors.
