## What is ED?

Erectile dysfunction (ED) is defined as the inability to get or maintain an erection sufficient for sex. Having erection trouble from time to time isn’t necessarily a cause for concern. However, when ED becomes persistent, it may result in performance anxiety, an unsatisfactory sex life, low self-esteem, stress, and even relationship problems.

- Trouble getting an erection
- Trouble keeping an erection
- Reduced sexual desire
