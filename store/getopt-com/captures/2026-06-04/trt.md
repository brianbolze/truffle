### Low energy

Men with low T often feel tired and lacking the energy to get through the day, even if they have good sleep patterns and low stress.

### Low libido

Men with low testosterone may experience decreased sex drive or erectile dysfunction.

### Weight gain

Without adequate testosterone, the body may struggle to maintain lean muscle mass while accumulating more fat.

### Sleep issues

Low T can disrupt the body’s natural sleep-wake cycle, making it difficult to fall asleep or stay asleep throughout the night.

## TRT questions

What does TRT do?

Testosterone replacement therapy (TRT), otherwise known as testosterone replacement treatment or hormone replacement therapy for men, restores testosterone levels to the optimal range of young adults. The goal of hormone therapy for men is to alleviate symptoms of low testosterone and regain the positive effects of restored testosterone levels.

Is a testosterone deficiency easy to treat?

In most cases, testosterone deficiency can be easily treated with TRT under the supervision of a medical professional. However, if the root cause of low T is congenital or genetic, specialized treatment may be necessary.

Does testosterone decrease with age?

Testosterone levels typically decline with age, but not all men experience it. Other factors can cause low testosterone symptoms, so consulting a healthcare professional to identify the underlying cause is important.

How do I know if I have low T?

If you’re experiencing common symptoms of low T, like lack of sex drive, fatigue, decreased muscle mass or strength, increased body fat, and mood changes, you may have low T. However, other factors can also cause these symptoms. Thus, speaking with a healthcare professional is important to determine if low T is the cause.

Are there TRT side effects?

Despite some possible side effects, our physicians typically view TRT as a safe and beneficial treatment option for men. Some potential side effects include acne, alopecia, reduced fertility, and erythrocytosis.

How much does TRT cost?

The cost of testosterone replacement therapy (TRT) can vary depending on the treatment type, dosage, administration frequency, and the healthcare provider. Opt Health offers TRT to clients who qualify as part of our Optimization and Longevity plans, starting at $245 per month plus a $195 initial lab fee.

Are there non-medical uses of TRT?

While testosterone replacement therapy (TRT) is primarily used to treat medical conditions such as low testosterone levels, TRT has some non-medical uses. For example, because of its ability to improve cognitive function, energy levels, and sexual function, TRT has also been marketed as an anti-aging treatment.

How is TRT used medically?

TRT is used medically to treat men with low testosterone levels. TRT can be administered through injections, patches, gels, or pills, depending on individual needs.
