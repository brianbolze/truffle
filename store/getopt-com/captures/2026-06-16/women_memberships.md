## The core elements

Our memberships are built on a unified foundation designed to optimize your health, no matter your starting point, unique symptoms, or areas of improvement. From initial lab reviews to personalized guidance and adjustments, here’s how these essential components will shape your journey to optimal health at any age.

01


### Initial comprehensive lab testing

Begin with a deep dive into your health – a pro-athlete level blood panel covering over 55 biomarkers, including hormones, inflammation, cardiovascular risk, and more. It's the first step in understanding the full picture of your well-being.

WHY IT MATTERS?

This extensive testing lays the groundwork for personalized health optimization, ensuring our recommendations are perfectly aligned with what your body truly needs.


02


### Expert consultation & tailored treatment plan

Sit down with an Opt Health physician for a comprehensive review of your lab results. This 1:1 consultation is where science meets personalization; together, you'll chart a course for your health, setting clear, achievable goals.

WHY IT MATTERS?

Direct, expert guidance transforms insights into action. Your treatment plan is not just about addressing needs but elevating your health potential to its maximum .


03


### Your health, delivered

Receive quarterly shipments directly to your door, containing all prescribed supplements, medications, and peptides. Plus, enjoy the flexibility of our à la carte options for any additional needs.

WHY IT MATTERS?

Convenience meets customization. Everything you need, when you need it, ensuring your path to optimal health is as seamless as possible.


04


### Continuous personalization & expert consultations

Regularly, every 3-4 months, we reassess your health with a comprehensive blood panel, followed by a focused consultation with your Opt Health physician to adjust your plan as needed.

WHY IT MATTERS?

Through consistent evaluations and personalized adjustments, we ensure your health plan evolves with you, keeping your goals within reach.


05


### App-enabled health management & communication

Utilize our app to track progress, access health data, and communicate with your care team, making your health journey both manageable and transparent.

WHY IT MATTERS

Our app puts control of your health journey at your fingertips, ensuring easy access to support and information, anytime, anywhere.

## Have any questions?

For quick insights and support on your health journey, explore our carefully curated FAQs.

Do I have to become an Opt Health member to receive treatment?

Yes, our memberships ensure continuity of care so we can measure and monitor your progress and provide ongoing treatment recommendations.

Do you accept insurance?

Currently, we do not work directly with any insurance companies. In certain circumstances, your health insurance provider may reimburse you for Opt Health services. We do accept HSA/FSA.

Can I pay with my HSA/FSA account?

We do accept HSA/FSA. However, every plan is different so please check with your provider.

How do I see my physician?

Our consultations are conducted via video through our HIPAA-compliant telemedicine platform.

Can I join if I am already receiving care for other medical conditions?

Absolutely, all medical conditions and current medications will be reviewed and discussed during your initial consultation.

How will I receive supplements and prescriptions?

We ship all supplements and prescriptions from our pharmacy directly and discreetly to your home.

How often can I speak with my Opt physician?

Depending on your plan, you will have 2-4 consultations per year after your initial consultation. However, our care team is available 7 days a week for questions through the app.

Does your app integrate with my existing fitness tracker?

Yes, you can connect most fitness trackers, including Apple Watches, Garmin, and FitBits, to the Opt Health app.

How often is my Opt Performance Score updated?

Your Opt Performance Score is updated on a bi-weekly basis. Our system monitors data from wearables, check-in's, and your quarterly labs to update this score.
