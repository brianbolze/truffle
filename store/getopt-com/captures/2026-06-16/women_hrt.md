### Menstrual irregularities

Menstrual cycles are affected by hormones and any imbalances can lead to a variety of issues.

### Menopause-related changes

Hot flashes, vaginal dryness, and other concerns are the direct result of the hormonal changes of menopause.

### Reduced bone density

Hormones play a major role in bone health, making it essential to maintain optimal hormone levels.

### Skin health concerns

Hormone imbalances can result in skin changes such as acne, facial hair, oily skin, and others.
