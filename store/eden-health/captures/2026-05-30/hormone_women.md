## Lab tested for quality & potency

Our pharmacies perform third party testing through FDA and DEA registered labs to run quality control checks for every compounded lot prescribed for Eden patients. They test four key characteristics that are associated with quality and safety.

Potency Test

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab714_Checkmark%20Icon.svg)

Passed

This test confirms that the compound has the appropriate concentration of the active ingredient. It is performed every 3 - 6 months, and confirms that the medication is plus or minus 10% of the desired concentration.

Purity Test

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab714_Checkmark%20Icon.svg)

Passed

This test indicates how much of the sample is the target compound versus other materials. A high purity (typically expressed as a percentage, e.g., 99.5%) means the sample contains very few impurities, making it more suitable for sensitive applications, such as pharmaceutical formulations.

Non-GMO Test

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab714_Checkmark%20Icon.svg)

Passed

Per the allergen declaration for this medication formulation, this product does not contain any GMO material.

Heavy Metal Test

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab714_Checkmark%20Icon.svg)

Passed

A heavy metal test for pharmacy-grade products detects and quantifies toxic metals like zinc, copper and arsenic to ensure they are within safe limits set by regulatory bodies.

## Learn more about My Custom Hormone Kit

I feel off, but my doctor says I’m fine. Could it still be hormones?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/hormone-kit-for-women#)

Yes. Hormonal shifts during perimenopause and menopause can cause subtle but life-disrupting symptoms like brain fog, sleep issues, and mood swings, even if lab results appear normal. Eden’s providers understand these changes and offer personalized HRT plans that may help support balance and wellbeing.

Isn't HRT risky?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/hormone-kit-for-women#)

"I’ve heard scary things.” Hormone therapy has evolved. When prescribed appropriately, it can be safe and effective for many women. Eden uses FDA-approved options and pharmacist-compounded therapies, always with licensed medical oversight. We prioritize your safety, your goals, and informed decision-making every step of the way.

Will this actually help me feel like myself again?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/hormone-kit-for-women#)

Many women report that properly guided HRT helps restore clarity, comfort, and confidence. While every journey is different, Eden’s approach is grounded in science, compassion, and personalization because midlife deserves more than silence or one-size-fits-all care.

How do I know if I need hormone therapy?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/hormone-kit-for-women#)

If you're experiencing symptoms like hot flashes, disrupted sleep, brain fog, mood swings, or vaginal dryness, your hormones could be shifting. Many women feel "off" long before menopause officially begins. A telehealth consult with an Eden provider can help assess whether HRT is right for you.

What symptoms can HRT help with?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/hormone-kit-for-women#)

Eden’s HRT plans may help support:

- Sleep quality

- Mood stability

- Mental clarity

- Intimacy and vaginal comfort

- Energy and thermoregulation


These benefits vary by person and depend on your hormone levels, delivery format, and consistency of use.

Is HRT only for menopause?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/hormone-kit-for-women#)

Not at all. Many women begin experiencing hormonal shifts during perimenopause — often starting in their late 30s or 40s. Eden supports women in both perimenopause and menopause with personalized therapy that adapts to your life stage and symptoms.

Will HRT help with intimacy or low libido?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/hormone-kit-for-women#)

Yes, hormonal changes can affect libido, arousal, and vaginal comfort. Eden’s HRT protocols may help support intimacy by restoring balance, improving vaginal tissue health, and increasing emotional connection through better sleep and mood.

Is hormone therapy safe?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/hormone-kit-for-women#)

When prescribed appropriately, HRT can be safe and effective for many women. Eden uses FDA-approved products (like estradiol cream and patch) and compounded therapies (like oral progesterone), all under licensed provider supervision. We customize each plan to your health history and goals.

How long does it take to feel a difference?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/hormone-kit-for-women#)

Some women notice improvements in sleep, energy, or mood within 2–4 weeks. Vaginal comfort and intimacy may improve within the first month. Deeper changes, like cognitive clarity or body composition support, may take 1–2 months or longer with consistent use.

Do I need progesterone if I don’t have a uterus?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/hormone-kit-for-women#)

If you’ve had a hysterectomy, you may not need progesterone for endometrial protection. However, some women still benefit from progesterone’s calming, sleep-supporting effects. Your provider will help determine whether it belongs in your plan.

What’s the difference between bioidentical and synthetic hormones?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/hormone-kit-for-women#)

Bioidentical hormones are chemically identical to the hormones your body naturally produces. Eden offers FDA-approved options and compounded bioidentical hormones tailored to your needs. All therapies are prescribed based on your goals, symptoms, and safety profile.

How do I get started with Eden’s HRT program?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/hormone-kit-for-women#)

It starts with a telehealth consultation. You’ll complete a detailed intake form covering symptoms, health history, and goals. A licensed provider will review it and design a personalized plan, which may include vaginal cream, a transdermal patch, or compounded progesterone capsules. Medications are discreetly delivered to your door.
