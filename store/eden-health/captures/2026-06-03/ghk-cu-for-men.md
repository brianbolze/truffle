## Lab tested for quality & potency

Our pharmacies perform third party testing through FDA and DEA registered labs to run quality control checks for every compounded lot prescribed for Eden patients. They test four key characteristics that are associated with quality and safety.

Potency Test

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab714_Checkmark%20Icon.svg)

Passed

This test confirms that the compound has the appropriate concentration of the active ingredient. It is performed every 3 - 6 months, and confirms that the medication is plus or minus 10% of the desired concentration.

Purity Test

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab714_Checkmark%20Icon.svg)

Passed

This test indicates how much of the sample is the target compound versus other materials. A high purity (typically expressed as a percentage, e.g., 99.5%) means the sample contains very few impurities, making it more suitable for sensitive applications, such as pharmaceutical formulations.

Non-GMO Test

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab714_Checkmark%20Icon.svg)

Passed

Per the allergen declaration for this medication formulation, this product does not contain any GMO material.

Heavy Metal Test

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab714_Checkmark%20Icon.svg)

Passed

A heavy metal test for pharmacy-grade products detects and quantifies toxic metals like zinc, copper and arsenic to ensure they are within safe limits set by regulatory bodies.

## Learn more about GHK‑Cu Foam for Men

What is GHK-Cu, and how does it support men’s hair?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/ghk-cu-for-men#)

GHK-Cu is a naturally occurring copper peptide studied in cosmetics for its potential to improve visible skin firmness and scalp tone.

Is GHK-Cu Hair Foam FDA-approved for hair regrowth?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/ghk-cu-for-men#)

No. GHK-Cu Foam is a cosmetic product. It is not FDA-approved to treat, cure, or prevent hair loss.

How quickly can results be noticed from GHK-Cu Foam?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/ghk-cu-for-men#)

Some users report improved scalp condition and hair appearance over 3–6 months of consistent use. Individual results may vary.

Are there any side effects?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/ghk-cu-for-men#)

Side effects are rare but may include temporary scalp sensitivity or minor irritation. Discontinue use and speak with your provider if needed.

Can GHK-Cu Foam be used with other hair treatments?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/ghk-cu-for-men#)

Yes. Under provider supervision, this foam may complement other topicals or supplements safely.

Is GHK-Cu Foam safe during pregnancy or breastfeeding?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/ghk-cu-for-men#)

Although topical peptides are generally well-tolerated, consult your clinician for individualized guidance.

What if I stop using GHK-Cu Foam?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/ghk-cu-for-men#)

As with most cosmetics, visible benefits are maintained with continued use. Stopping may result in a return to baseline scalp appearance over time.

How does GHK-Cu Foam compare to Minoxidil?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/ghk-cu-for-men#)

Minoxidil is a drug approved to stimulate hair regrowth. GHK-Cu Foam is a cosmetic peptide product designed to enhance the look and feel of your scalp and support healthy hair appearance.
