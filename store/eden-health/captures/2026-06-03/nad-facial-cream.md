What is NAD+ and why does it matter for your skin?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/nad-facial-cream#)

NAD+ (nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide) is a vital coenzyme found in every skin cell. It fuels cellular energy production, DNA repair, and collagen synthesis; key functions for maintaining youthful, healthy-looking skin.

But NAD+ levels naturally decline with age, often by over 50%, reducing your skin’s ability to regenerate, retain moisture, and defend against environmental damage.

Replenishing NAD+ at the surface helps restore your skin’s natural resilience and glow.

What are the potential benefits of NAD+ Face Cream?

By delivering NAD+ directly to the skin, this cream helps support hydration, texture, elasticity, and visible tone. Many users report smoother skin, fewer fine lines, and an overall more refreshed appearance with regular use.

It's an effortless addition to your skincare routine that works from the cellular level outward

## Lab tested for quality & potency

Our pharmacies perform third party testing through FDA and DEA registered labs to run quality control checks for every compounded lot prescribed for Eden patients. They test four key characteristics that are associated with quality and safety.

Potency Test

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab714_Checkmark%20Icon.svg)

Passed

This test confirms that the compound has the appropriate concentration of the active ingredient. It is performed every 3 - 6 months, and confirms that the medication is plus or minus 10% of the desired concentration.

Purity Test

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab714_Checkmark%20Icon.svg)

Passed

This test indicates how much of the sample is the target compound versus other materials. A high purity (typically expressed as a percentage, e.g., 99.5%) means the sample contains very few impurities, making it more suitable for sensitive applications, such as pharmaceutical formulations.

Non-GMO Test

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab714_Checkmark%20Icon.svg)

Passed

Per the allergen declaration for this medication formulation, this product does not contain any GMO material.

Heavy Metal Test

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab714_Checkmark%20Icon.svg)

Passed

A heavy metal test for pharmacy-grade products detects and quantifies toxic metals like zinc, copper and arsenic to ensure they are within safe limits set by regulatory bodies.

## Learn more about NAD+ Face Cream

What is NAD+ and why does it matter for your skin?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/nad-facial-cream#)

NAD+ (nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide) is a vital coenzyme found in every skin cell. It fuels cellular energy production, DNA repair, and collagen synthesis; key functions for maintaining youthful, healthy-looking skin.

But NAD+ levels naturally decline with age, often by over 50%, reducing your skin’s ability to regenerate, retain moisture, and defend against environmental damage.

Replenishing NAD+ at the surface helps restore your skin’s natural resilience and glow.

What are the potential benefits of NAD+ Face Cream?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/nad-facial-cream#)

By delivering NAD+ directly to the skin, this cream helps support hydration, texture, elasticity, and visible tone. Many users report smoother skin, fewer fine lines, and an overall more refreshed appearance with regular use.

It's an effortless addition to your skincare routine that works from the cellular level outward

How is topical NAD+ different from pills or sprays?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/nad-facial-cream#)

Oral NAD+ supplements support the whole body but lose potency during digestion. Sprays may offer fast but non-targeted effects.

Eden’s NAD+ Face Cream focuses NAD+ delivery exactly where it’s needed most—your skin—providing targeted support for surface regeneration, firmness, and barrier function.

No pills, no needles; just high-performance topical care.

Are there any side effects with NAD+ Face Cream?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/nad-facial-cream#)

Most users tolerate NAD+ Face Cream well. Occasionally, new users may notice mild redness, tingling, or dryness as their skin adjusts; especially when starting a new regimen. These effects are usually short-lived. We always recommend starting with a patch test and consulting your Eden provider if you have any skin sensitivities or concerns.

Who should avoid using NAD+ Face Cream?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/nad-facial-cream#)

While NAD+ Face Cream is suitable for most skin types, it may not be appropriate for those with certain skin conditions, sensitivities, or allergies. If you’re pregnant, breastfeeding, or managing a skin disorder, your Eden provider will assess your history and help determine if NAD+ is the right choice for your routine.

Can NAD+ Face Cream interact with other skincare products?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/nad-facial-cream#)

Yes, NAD+ Face Cream can usually be used alongside other skincare products. That said, layering with potent actives like retinoids, AHAs, or vitamin C may require spacing or adjustment to avoid irritation. During your Eden consultation, your provider will review your full skincare routine and guide you in combining products safely and effectively.
