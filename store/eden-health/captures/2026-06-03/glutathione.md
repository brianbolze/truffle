## Lab tested for quality & potency

Our pharmacies perform third party testing through FDA and DEA registered labs to run quality control checks for every compounded lot prescribed for Eden patients. They test four key characteristics that are associated with quality and safety.

Potency Test

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab714_Checkmark%20Icon.svg)

Passed

This test is performed every 3 to 6 months. It confirms that the medication has plus or minus 10% of the appropriate concentration of the active ingredient.

Sterility Test

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab714_Checkmark%20Icon.svg)

Passed

This test ensures the medication is free from any contaminants, including bacteria or other pathogens. Every batch is tested and must meet the requirements of [USP 797.1](https://www.usp.org/compounding/general-chapter-797).

Endotoxicity

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab714_Checkmark%20Icon.svg)

Passed

This test, along with sterility testing, ensures the medication is free of harmful toxins. Every batch is tested for bacterial endotoxins, which may not exceed the threshold limits defined in [USP 85](https://www.usp.org/harmonization-standards/pdg/general-methods/bacterial-endotoxins).

pH Test

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab714_Checkmark%20Icon.svg)

Passed

This test assesses the acid/base balance to ensure minimal irritation upon injection.

## Learn more about Glutathione

What is Compounded Glutathione (GSH), and why is it important?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/glutathione#)

Glutathione is a naturally occurring antioxidant involved in cellular processes, including detoxification and maintaining redox balance. Compounded GSH allows for personalized dosing under medical supervision.

Is compounded GSH FDA-approved?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/glutathione#)

Compounded GSH is not FDA-approved for specific conditions and is prescribed off-label by clinicians based on individual patient needs.

What are the benefits of increasing glutathione (GSH) levels?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/glutathione#)

Boosting your body’s glutathione levels can support a wide range of health functions, from improving energy production and sharpening mental clarity to enhancing immune response and speeding recovery from physical or environmental stress. Because glutathione is central to detoxification and cellular repair, many users report feeling more resilient, focused, and energized over time. While individual results vary and depend on consistent use, increasing GSH is often a key part of long-term strategies for healthier aging, better metabolic function, and overall wellness.

What makes compounded GSH different from regular supplements?
