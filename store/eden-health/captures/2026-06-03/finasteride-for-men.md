What is Finasteride, and how does it support hair health?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/finasteride-for-men#)

Finasteride is FDA-approved for the treatment of male-pattern baldness. It works by inhibiting the enzyme 5-alpha-reductase, reducing conversion of testosterone into DHT, which contributes to hair loss in genetically predisposed men.

Is Finasteride FDA-approved for hair loss treatment?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/finasteride-for-men#)

Yes. 1mg oral Finasteride is FDA-approved for the treatment of male pattern hair loss in men.

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/finasteride-for-men#)

Finasteride is FDA-approved for the treatment of male-pattern baldness. It works by inhibiting the enzyme 5-alpha-reductase, reducing conversion of testosterone into DHT, which contributes to hair loss in genetically predisposed men.

Is Finasteride FDA-approved for hair loss treatment?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/finasteride-for-men#)

Yes. 1mg oral Finasteride is FDA-approved for the treatment of male pattern hair loss in men.

What dosage is typically prescribed?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/finasteride-for-men#)

1mg daily is the standard dose for hair loss. This dosage was studied and found effective in long-term clinical trials.

How soon will I see results with Finasteride?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/finasteride-for-men#)

Results vary, but clinical studies report improvements in hair count and appearance within 3–6 months, with continued gains over 12 months.

What are common side effects of Finasteride?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/finasteride-for-men#)

Side effects may include decreased libido, erectile dysfunction, or mood changes. Most men tolerate Finasteride well, but all medications carry risks. The FDA recently issued guidance regarding **unapproved topical forms**, not the oral formulation used here.

Can Finasteride be combined with other treatments?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/finasteride-for-men#)

Yes. Finasteride is often used in combination with topical Minoxidil or supplements under medical supervision to enhance results.

Is Finasteride safe for women?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/finasteride-for-men#)

No. Finasteride is not intended for women and is contraindicated in pregnancy due to risk of fetal harm.

What happens if I stop using Finasteride?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/finasteride-for-men#)

Hair loss typically resumes within several months after stopping. Continued use is necessary to maintain results.

How does Finasteride compare to Minoxidil?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/finasteride-for-men#)

Finasteride targets internal hormonal causes by reducing DHT. Minoxidil acts topically to improve blood flow and follicle stimulation. They are often used together for complementary support.

How do I obtain Finasteride tablets?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/finasteride-for-men#)

Finasteride is available by prescription only. Eden providers will assess your eligibility through an online evaluation.
