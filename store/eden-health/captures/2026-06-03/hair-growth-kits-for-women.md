What are Minoxidil and GHK-Cu, and how might they support hair health?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/hair-growth-kits-for-women#)

Minoxidil tablets, when prescribed off-label, may help support the appearance of hair thickness by affecting hair follicle activity. GHK-Cu is a cosmetic peptide shown in some studies to support skin and scalp renewal.

Are these treatments FDA-approved for women’s hair growth?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/hair-growth-kits-for-women#)

No. Oral Minoxidil is FDA-approved for hypertension but is prescribed off-label for hair concerns at lower doses. GHK-Cu is a cosmetic product and not FDA-approved for treating hair loss.

## Lab tested for quality & potency

Our pharmacies perform third party testing through FDA and DEA registered labs to run quality control checks for every compounded lot prescribed for Eden patients. They test four key characteristics that are associated with quality and safety.

Potency Test

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab714_Checkmark%20Icon.svg)

Passed

This test confirms that the compound has the appropriate concentration of the active ingredient. It is performed every 3 - 6 months, and confirms that the medication is plus or minus 10% of the desired concentration.

Purity Test

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab714_Checkmark%20Icon.svg)

Passed

This test indicates how much of the sample is the target compound versus other materials. A high purity (typically expressed as a percentage, e.g., 99.5%) means the sample contains very few impurities, making it more suitable for sensitive applications, such as pharmaceutical formulations.

Non-GMO Test

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab714_Checkmark%20Icon.svg)

Passed

Per the allergen declaration for this medication formulation, this product does not contain any GMO material.

Heavy Metal Test

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab714_Checkmark%20Icon.svg)

Passed

A heavy metal test for pharmacy-grade products detects and quantifies toxic metals like zinc, copper and arsenic to ensure they are within safe limits set by regulatory bodies.

## Learn more about My Custom Hair Growth Kit for Women

What are Minoxidil and GHK-Cu, and how might they support hair health?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/hair-growth-kits-for-women#)

Minoxidil tablets, when prescribed off-label, may help support the appearance of hair thickness by affecting hair follicle activity. GHK-Cu is a cosmetic peptide shown in some studies to support skin and scalp renewal.

Are these treatments FDA-approved for women’s hair growth?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/hair-growth-kits-for-women#)

No. Oral Minoxidil is FDA-approved for hypertension but is prescribed off-label for hair concerns at lower doses. GHK-Cu is a cosmetic product and not FDA-approved for treating hair loss.

How long before I might see changes?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/hair-growth-kits-for-women#)

Some individuals report changes in hair appearance after several months of consistent use. Individual results vary and no outcomes are guaranteed.

Are there risks or side effects?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/hair-growth-kits-for-women#)

Side effects of oral Minoxidil may include dizziness, increased hair growth in unintended areas, or changes in heart rate. GHK-Cu may cause minor scalp irritation. Always consult your provider.

Can these be used with other treatments?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/hair-growth-kits-for-women#)

Yes. Your provider may recommend combining these with other topicals or nutraceuticals, depending on your hair goals.

Is this safe during pregnancy or breastfeeding?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/hair-growth-kits-for-women#)

No. Neither oral Minoxidil nor GHK-Cu is recommended during pregnancy or breastfeeding.

What happens if I stop?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/hair-growth-kits-for-women#)

Hair thinning may gradually resume after stopping. These treatments are most effective with continued use.

How is this different from topical Minoxidil?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/hair-growth-kits-for-women#)

Oral Minoxidil works systemically and is prescribed off-label. GHK-Cu supports scalp hydration and visible texture improvement. Your provider will help determine if this combination is right for you.

How do I get started?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/hair-growth-kits-for-women#)

The kit requires a prescription. Eden’s providers will evaluate your needs and build a personalized plan.
