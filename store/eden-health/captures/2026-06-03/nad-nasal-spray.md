What is NAD+ and why does it matter?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/nad-nasal-spray#)

NAD+ (nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide) is a vital coenzyme found in every cell, responsible for converting food into energy, supporting brain function, and repairing DNA. As you age, NAD+ levels drop, often by more than 50%, leading to fatigue, brain fog, and slower cellular repair. Replenishing NAD+ helps restore energy, clarity, and resilience.

What are the potential benefits of NAD+ Nasal Spray?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/nad-nasal-spray#)

By delivering NAD+ directly through the nasal membrane, this spray offers fast, non-invasive support for mental sharpness, metabolic health, and aging defenses. Many Eden customers report feeling more alert, focused, and energized within days, a simple way to support long-term vitality.

How is nasal spray different from pills or IVs?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/nad-nasal-spray#)

Oral NAD+ supplements often lose potency during digestion. IVs work, but require time and needles. Eden’s nasal spray skips both, offering high bioavailability and quick absorption, without the hassle.

## Lab tested for quality & potency

Our pharmacies perform third party testing through FDA and DEA registered labs to run quality control checks for every compounded lot prescribed for Eden patients. They test four key characteristics that are associated with quality and safety.

Potency Test

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab714_Checkmark%20Icon.svg)

Passed

This test confirms that the compound has the appropriate concentration of the active ingredient. It is performed every 3 - 6 months, and confirms that the medication is plus or minus 10% of the desired concentration.

Purity Test

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab714_Checkmark%20Icon.svg)

Passed

This test indicates how much of the sample is the target compound versus other materials. A high purity (typically expressed as a percentage, e.g., 99.5%) means the sample contains very few impurities, making it more suitable for sensitive applications, such as pharmaceutical formulations.

Non-GMO Test

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab714_Checkmark%20Icon.svg)

Passed

Per the allergen declaration for this medication formulation, this product does not contain any GMO material.

Heavy Metal Test

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab714_Checkmark%20Icon.svg)

Passed

A heavy metal test for pharmacy-grade products detects and quantifies toxic metals like zinc, copper and arsenic to ensure they are within safe limits set by regulatory bodies.

## Learn more about NAD+ Nasal Spray

What is NAD+ and why does it matter?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/nad-nasal-spray#)

NAD+ (nicotinamide adenine dinucleotide) is a vital coenzyme found in every cell, responsible for converting food into energy, supporting brain function, and repairing DNA. As you age, NAD+ levels drop, often by more than 50%, leading to fatigue, brain fog, and slower cellular repair. Replenishing NAD+ helps restore energy, clarity, and resilience.

What are the potential benefits of NAD+ Nasal Spray?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/nad-nasal-spray#)

By delivering NAD+ directly through the nasal membrane, this spray offers fast, non-invasive support for mental sharpness, metabolic health, and aging defenses. Many Eden customers report feeling more alert, focused, and energized within days, a simple way to support long-term vitality.

How is nasal spray different from pills or IVs?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/nad-nasal-spray#)

Oral NAD+ supplements often lose potency during digestion. IVs work, but require time and needles. Eden’s nasal spray skips both, offering high bioavailability and quick absorption, without the hassle.
