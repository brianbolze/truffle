What is GHK-Cu, and how does it support hair health?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/ghk-cu-for-women#)

GHK-Cu is a powerful copper peptide naturally produced in the body. In cosmetic studies, it’s been shown to help improve scalp health, hydration, and visible firmness, making it a popular choice for those looking to support fuller-looking hair and healthier skin at the root.

Is GHK-Cu Hair Foam FDA-approved for hair growth?

## Lab tested for quality & potency

Our pharmacies perform third party testing through FDA and DEA registered labs to run quality control checks for every compounded lot prescribed for Eden patients. They test four key characteristics that are associated with quality and safety.

Potency Test

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab714_Checkmark%20Icon.svg)

Passed

This test confirms that the compound has the appropriate concentration of the active ingredient. It is performed every 3 - 6 months, and confirms that the medication is plus or minus 10% of the desired concentration.

Purity Test

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab714_Checkmark%20Icon.svg)

Passed

This test indicates how much of the sample is the target compound versus other materials. A high purity (typically expressed as a percentage, e.g., 99.5%) means the sample contains very few impurities, making it more suitable for sensitive applications, such as pharmaceutical formulations.

Non-GMO Test

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab714_Checkmark%20Icon.svg)

Passed

Per the allergen declaration for this medication formulation, this product does not contain any GMO material.

Heavy Metal Test

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab714_Checkmark%20Icon.svg)

Passed

A heavy metal test for pharmacy-grade products detects and quantifies toxic metals like zinc, copper and arsenic to ensure they are within safe limits set by regulatory bodies.

## Learn more about GHK‑Cu Foam for Women

What is GHK-Cu, and how does it support hair health?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/ghk-cu-for-women#)

GHK-Cu is a powerful copper peptide naturally produced in the body. In cosmetic studies, it’s been shown to help improve scalp health, hydration, and visible firmness, making it a popular choice for those looking to support fuller-looking hair and healthier skin at the root.

Is GHK-Cu Hair Foam FDA-approved for hair growth?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/ghk-cu-for-women#)

GHK-Cu is classified as a cosmetic ingredient, not a drug. So while it’s not FDA-approved to treat or prevent hair loss, it’s been widely studied for its ability to support healthier-looking hair and a visibly rejuvenated scalp.

How quickly can I see results from GHK-Cu Foam?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/ghk-cu-for-women#)

Some users report visible improvements in the appearance of their scalp and hair density over several months of consistent useResults vary.

Are there any side effects?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/ghk-cu-for-women#)

Mild side effects may include temporary scalp sensitivity. Stop use and consult your provider if irritation occurs.

Can I combine GHK-Cu Foam with other hair treatments?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/ghk-cu-for-women#)

Yes. Under medical supervision, this foam may be used alongside other topical or oral therapies.

Is it safe during pregnancy or breastfeeding?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/ghk-cu-for-women#)

Always consult your provider. While GHK-Cu is a cosmetic ingredient, no formal safety data exists for use during pregnancy or breastfeeding.

What if I stop using GHK-Cu Foam?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/ghk-cu-for-women#)

The cosmetic benefits are only maintained with regular use. Discontinuation may lead to a gradual return to baseline scalp appearance.
