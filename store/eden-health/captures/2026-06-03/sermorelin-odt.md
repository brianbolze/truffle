How is Sermorelin ODT different from injections?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/sermorelin-odt#)

Sermorelin ODT is a needle-free tablet that dissolves under the tongue. It offers a convenient alternative to injections, with a different absorption process that may affect how quickly some people notice effects. Your provider can help you decide which form is right for you.

How do I take Sermorelin ODT?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/sermorelin-odt#)

Take your tablet at night, ideally 30 minutes before bed. Place it under your tongue and let it fully dissolve for at least 1 to 2 minutes without chewing or swallowing. Avoid eating, drinking, or brushing your teeth for 30 minutes afterward to support proper absorption through the mouth. This allows the medication to bypass the digestive system and absorb more directly into your bloodstream.

## Lab tested for quality & potency

Our pharmacies perform third party testing through FDA and DEA registered labs to run quality control checks for every compounded lot prescribed for Eden patients. They test four key characteristics that are associated with quality and safety.

Potency Test

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab714_Checkmark%20Icon.svg)

Passed

This test confirms that the compound has the appropriate concentration of the active ingredient. It is performed every 3 - 6 months, and confirms that the medication is plus or minus 10% of the desired concentration.

Purity Test

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab714_Checkmark%20Icon.svg)

Passed

This test indicates how much of the sample is the target compound versus other materials. A high purity (typically expressed as a percentage, e.g., 99.5%) means the sample contains very few impurities, making it more suitable for sensitive applications, such as pharmaceutical formulations.

Non-GMO Test

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab714_Checkmark%20Icon.svg)

Passed

Per the allergen declaration for this medication formulation, this product does not contain any GMO material.

Heavy Metal Test

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab714_Checkmark%20Icon.svg)

Passed

A heavy metal test for pharmacy-grade products detects and quantifies toxic metals like zinc, copper and arsenic to ensure they are within safe limits set by regulatory bodies.

## Learn more about Sermorelin ODT

How is Sermorelin ODT different from injections?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/sermorelin-odt#)

Sermorelin ODT is a needle-free tablet that dissolves under the tongue. It offers a convenient alternative to injections, with a different absorption process that may affect how quickly some people notice effects. Your provider can help you decide which form is right for you.

How do I take Sermorelin ODT?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/sermorelin-odt#)

Take your tablet at night, ideally 30 minutes before bed. Place it under your tongue and let it fully dissolve for at least 1 to 2 minutes without chewing or swallowing. Avoid eating, drinking, or brushing your teeth for 30 minutes afterward to support proper absorption through the mouth. This allows the medication to bypass the digestive system and absorb more directly into your bloodstream.

What are the common side effects of Sermorelin ODT?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/sermorelin-odt#)

Some individuals may experience mild side effects such as headache, flushing, dizziness, nausea, or changes in taste. These effects are typically temporary. If anything feels off or persists, you can reach out to your Eden provider for support.

Are there any serious side effects?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/sermorelin-odt#)

Serious side effects are rare but may include severe allergic reactions, changes in blood pressure, or increased intracranial pressure. Seek immediate medical attention if these occur.

Can Sermorelin interact with other medications?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/sermorelin-odt#)

Yes, Sermorelin can interact with glucocorticoids, thyroid hormones, and other medications that affect growth hormone levels. Always inform your healthcare provider of all medications you are taking.

Who should not use Sermorelin?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/sermorelin-odt#)

Individuals with a known allergy to Sermorelin or any of its components, pregnant or breastfeeding women, and those with active cancer should not use sermorelin. Patients with underactive thyroid/hypothyroidism should use caution as this condition can interfere with the effects of Sermorelin.

Are there long-term risks associated with Sermorelin ODT?

[![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab050/6a0ccac3db38d1a0339ab6fc_Plus.svg)](https://www.eden.health/treatment/sermorelin-odt#)

While Sermorelin is generally considered safe and well-tolerated, long-term use of Sermorelin ODT can potentially lead to certain complications. These may include:

- ‍Carpal tunnel syndrome: Long-term elevation of growth hormone levels may lead to symptoms associated with carpal tunnel syndrome, such as numbness or tingling in the hands and fingers.
- ‍Edema: Fluid retention, which can cause swelling in the extremities, is another possible side effect of sustained growth hormone stimulation.‍
- Insulin resistance: Chronic use of sermorelin may affect glucose metabolism, potentially leading to insulin resistance or type 2 diabetes.
- ‍Increased risk of cancer: There is ongoing debate and research about the potential risk of cancer associated with prolonged growth hormone therapy, as elevated levels of growth hormone can stimulate the growth of certain types of cells.
