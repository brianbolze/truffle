How will I be charged?

DoorDash for Business customers have the choice of paying via monthly invoices or by credit card. Companies only pay for what employees order.

Using Business Profiles, your team can easily separate work and personal orders at checkout for easy expensing. With a designated payment method, automatic business receipts, and integrations with leading expense providers like Concur, Emburse, Expensify, and Ramp, your employees can streamline their expenses while staying compliant.

Receipts are automatically sent to your chosen expense provider, making reimbursement faster and reporting more accurate.

What restrictions can I place on budgets?

By default, DoorDash for Business budgets apply to restaurant orders. You may also choose to allow or restrict budget use for these categories:

- Convenience

- Grocery

- Alcohol\*


\*We are not able to invoice for alcohol.

Can I set budgets to be available only on certain days or at certain times?

Yes, budgets can easily be set to apply to certain days or times, or restricted to deliveries at specific locations, such as offices.

Do budgets apply to pickup orders?

Yes, budgets will apply to delivery or pickup orders by default.

Will I be charged for unused budgets?

No, you will not be charged for unused budgets.
