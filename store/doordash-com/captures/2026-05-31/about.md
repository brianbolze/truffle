## Frequently Asked Questions

What is DoorDash?

DoorDash is an on-demand delivery platform that connects customers with local restaurants, grocery stores, and retailers. You can order food, groceries, alcohol, and convenience items directly from the DoorDash [website](https://www.doordash.com/) or app.

How does DoorDash work?

Customers place an order through the DoorDash app or website. A Dasher (independent delivery driver) picks up the order from the restaurant or store and delivers it. Orders can be tracked in real time.

How do I contact DoorDash customer service?

You can reach customer support by visiting the [DoorDash Help Center](https://help.doordash.com/consumers/). Options include in-app chat, phone support (varies by region), and help articles.

What is DashMart?

[DashMart](https://www.doordash.com/en/dashmart) is DoorDash’s own convenience and grocery store. It offers snacks, groceries, household essentials, and more—all delivered quickly.

What is DashPass and how much does it cost?

[DashPass](https://doordash.com/dashpass) is a subscription service that gives members $0 delivery fees and reduced service fees on eligible orders. It costs $9.99/month (plus tax) with the monthly plan or $96/year ($8/month, plus tax) with the Annual Plan. With the Annual Plan, you save more upfront and with our monthly plan, it's a subscription without the commitment — you can cancel anytime. You can read more [DashPass FAQ](https://help.doordash.com/consumers/s/article/What-is-DashPass?language=en_US#WhatisDashPass) in our Help Center.

Does DoorDash have promo codes?

Yes! New users get a $0 delivery fee on their first order; terms and conditions apply. Existing users can find everyday deals and promotions in the app and via email notifications.

Does DoorDash have gift cards?

Yes, DoorDash gift cards can be purchased online [here](https://www.doordash.com/gift-cards/) and redeemed in the app.

Can I use SNAP/EBT on DoorDash?

Yes, you can use SNAP/EBT cards for grocery delivery in certain locations. Availability depends on participating grocery partners and your area. You can read more in our [Help Center](https://help.doordash.com/consumers/s/article/Use-Your-SNAP-Benefits-on-DoorDash) or find [participating locations near you](https://www.doordash.com/p/snap-ebt).

What does “DoubleDash” mean on DoorDash?

DoubleDash is a product feature that lets you add items from a second nearby store or restaurant to your original order for no additional delivery fee or order minimum. Terms and conditions apply.

How late does DoorDash deliver?

Delivery hours depend on restaurant and store availability. Many cities offer options open until late at night (2–3 a.m.), and in some areas, 24-hour delivery is available. Enter your address on the [DoorDash site](https://www.doordash.com/) or app to see options near you.

Can I order alcohol on DoorDash?

Yes! In eligible markets, DoorDash delivers beer, wine, and liquor. Customers must be 21+ and show valid ID. Find [alcohol delivery options near you](https://www.doordash.com/p/alcohol-delivery).
