## FAQs

### How do you source the ingredients used in your products?

AG1 product ingredients are sourced from quality partners for absorption, potency, and nutrient density. Each ingredient is chosen to meet strict standards for both sourcing and performance, and is thoroughly tested for quality and safety.

### How do you test ingredient quality and safety?

AG1 products are tested to confirm nutritional levels and are tested for contaminants, including pesticides, herbicides, and banned substances. All supplements from the AG1 brand are NSF Certified for Sport(R), including AG1 Next Gen, AGZ, Vitamin D3+K2, and AG Omega3– which entails rigorous third-party testing to help ensure the safety and accuracy of the label and amounts.

### How do you maintain consistency in ingredient quality?

Consistency is maintained through strict sourcing, controlled manufacturing processes, and testing of raw materials _and_ finished products. Each batch is held to the same quality standards and is tested throughout the processes of sourcing, manufacturing, and production.
