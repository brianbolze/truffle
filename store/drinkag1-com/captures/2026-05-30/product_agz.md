AGZ is designed to help you feel relaxed within the first night of use. Over time, establishing a regular wind-down routine with consistent sleep hygiene practices can help improve the overall quality of sleep.

We recommend drinking AGZ before bedtime each night to signal to your body that it’s time to begin winding down. For the best results:
