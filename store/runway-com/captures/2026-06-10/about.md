# We empower teams to build great companies by making business understandable and accessible to everyone

## Our story

01

2011

![A vintage-style illustration of a man standing in an office surrounded by stacks of papers, binders, and a phone, with spreadsheets and floating paper strips in the background.](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/683909604bbd11a23e6dd536/68cb19ee501b9cfd4357291a_4157773dc88865e5376f6878a524af67_about-01.webp)

## Why do we care so much?

Hi! I'm Siqi, one of the founders of Runway. I was 23 when I started running my first company: Serious Business. Every month, I got these spreadsheets in my inbox from a Harvard MBA on my team that I didn't really understand.

But that's okay—that's what “business people” are for right?

![A vintage-style illustration of a man standing in an office, facing a large shadowy monster emerging from spreadsheet-covered walls.](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/683909604bbd11a23e6dd536/68cb19ee9d7b688e6f0a9d15_7e3b925516817de53374d5c0d483f00b_about-02.webp)

## Finance has always made me feel insecure

Even as a 4-time CEO and 3-time founder, I've always been insecure about my understanding of the business side of things.I didn't think of myself as a “business person.”

I didn't even know what contribution margin meant until I was responsible for the metric at Postmates.

![A vintage-style illustration of a chaotic office with scattered papers and two people working under blankets, while a man in the center stands with his face in his hands.](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/683909604bbd11a23e6dd536/68cb19ee3d30689b594fcd6a_c8d53d690524b705a36e7855fd9c1aba_about-03.webp)

## Fast forward

Fast forward to March 2020, I was just appointed CEO of a company called Sandbox VR.

COVID hit, and we immediately had to scramble. Our CFO and I were scenario planning in Excel: what happens if COVID lasts 3 months? 6 months? 2 years? Long story short, we had to lay off most of the company and hibernate. And so that's what we did.

![A vintage-style illustration of two men sitting on a park bench, holding coffee cups and having a conversation, with trees and a city skyline in the background.](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/683909604bbd11a23e6dd536/68cb19eeb48a722d6ff4e636_a517f20a89bdeaf57ce11a6fcd1d1aaf_about-04.webp)

## Is this the best we can do?

About half an hour after I laid myself off, I asked our CFO Akash if there wasn't something better we would have used, because the experience of making copies of spreadsheets and finding bugs in them really sucked. And he told me it's pretty much spreadsheets or ugly enterprise software, that's it.

In the past decade or so, we've built amazing products for literally everything else a company does - from analytics to communication, from design to project management. Why hasn't dealing with financials in a company changed at all? In a world where Figma and Notion exist, that seems like an opportunity. And so we started Runway.

![A vintage-style illustration of a busy office meeting with a group of people gathered around, some sitting and others standing, with laptops and charts, and a city skyline visible through large windows in the background.](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/683909604bbd11a23e6dd536/68cb19ee57f41c02f6f8f8c2_27cacb78829d61cba6cb59251084c0b7_about-05.webp)

## Getting off the ground

And so we got started right in the middle of the pandemic. Luckily, we found some incredible people who believed in our mission, including Garry Tan (YC & Initialized), a16z, Elad Gil, Naval Ravikant, Dylan Field (founder of Figma), Eric Ries, Claire Hughes Johnson (COO of Stripe), Henry Ward (founder of Carta), Akshay Kothari (COO of Notion), Eugene Wei, Lenny Rachitsky, Nikita Bier, Scott Belsky, Soleio Cuervo, Balaji Srinivasan, and many others.

![A vintage-style illustration of a busy office with people working at desks filled with computer screens, surrounded by large charts and graphs displaying data on the walls.](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/683909604bbd11a23e6dd536/68cb19ee6d94e82e58ad1322_e7b8101f2e440038a20c0d648bf56efa_about-06.webp)

## Encountering turbulence

It wasn't all smooth sailing from there. It took us a long time to deeply understand the problems facing finance owners and business operators today, and invent new solutions to these problems that are powerful, flexible, and accessible.

To get off the ground, we had to rebuild a good chunk of Excel, invent new abstractions that connect your business intent with numbers for the first time, and integrate with hundreds of sources of data within enterprises.

![A vintage-style illustration of seven people sitting on a long park bench, chatting and holding drinks, with a city skyline in the background.](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/683909604bbd11a23e6dd536/68cb19eeda85536e6e9f5e82_aadfd36ca13c7d917de92042506d5e26_about-07.webp)

## Building a culture of shared values

Through a lot of pain and sweat, we've learned how to build successful products in a way that is unique to Runway, and we've crystallized these learnings into the Runway values.

### Our values

- Give a shit

- Create clarity

- Build trust

- Raise the bar


![A vintage-style illustration of an office with several people working at desks, with a city skyline and sunset visible through floor-to-ceiling windows.](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/683909604bbd11a23e6dd536/68cb19eee6ba82688ddf69bf_a81ffef4166e7adb682a2f6f87e3dae3_about-08.webp)

## Runway today

Now—four years later—we are at an inflection point. While there's so much more to do, it's clear that our mission to make business accessible and understandable to everyone is within reach.

Already, companies like AngelList, Superhuman, MyFitnessPal, Kick, Kit, SandboxVR and many more, large and small, have chosen Runway to help them run their businesses. More are joining them every day.

Customers are coming to us faster than we can onboard them. Finance leaders are moved by what we're building, and we can't build fast enough to keep up with demand.
