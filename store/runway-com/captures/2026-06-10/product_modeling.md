## Frequently asked questions

### What are drivers in Runway?

Drivers in Runway are key financial elements like cash flow or expenses, pivotal for strategic planning. They can be easily named, allowing for an intuitive understanding. You can use dimensions in Runway as properties to segment certain drivers, such as department, customer type, or location. This categorization deepens your analysis, facilitating more informed strategic planning and decision-making by highlighting different aspects of your business.

### What does implementation look like?

Implementation with Runway is designed to be a streamlined process, tailored to move as quickly as you need. Begin with our self-guided onboarding to easily get the core components of your model into Runway. Our goal is to make your transition as smooth and swift as possible, ensuring you can start leveraging Runway’s full potential without delay.

### What if Runway doesn't support my integrations?

Runway is built to support and adapt to your growing needs, accommodating a wide range of integrations, data warehouses, and API access. Whether you're looking to merge existing data or require bespoke integration solutions, we are fully equipped to support you at every step.

### How can you trace values in Runway?

In Runway, tracing values is designed to be user-friendly. A few clicks allow you to see how each value is calculated and trace it back to its origin in your data. This makes it easier to quickly identify and correct errors, enhancing clarity and understanding across your organization.

### How does Runway keep my data secure?

Runway is SOC 2 Type II compliant, which means we adhere to strict standards for data protection, handling, and security. This compliance requires regular audits to verify that our controls are effective over time. We're dedicated to keeping your data secure and protected against unauthorized access, breaches, and other risks.
