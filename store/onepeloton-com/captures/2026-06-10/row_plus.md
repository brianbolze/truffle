# FAQs

* * *

Is Peloton IQ available on all Peloton devices?
Arrow

Peloton IQ provides training and insights designed to personalize every workout. Peloton IQ is available on all Peloton devices, but its most advanced features (e.g. Movement Tracking) can only be experienced on the Cross Training Bike+, Tread+, and Row+. A Peloton All-Access Membership is required to access all Peloton content and applicable features on your Peloton hardware.

How much space will Row+ take up in my home?
Arrow

Row+ has a footprint of 8' x 2', and we recommend at least 24" of clearance on the sides and back of Row+ to take full advantage of Peloton content.

For storage, Row+ is vertically stowable with the Upright Wall Anchor to safely and quickly transition back to living space after your workout. The ceiling height minimum is 8' to ensure the Row+ can be safely stowed vertically.

Is there a way to have my old exercise equipment removed?
Arrow

After placing your order for new Peloton equipment, eligible customers can request haulaway service from Peloton’s trusted third-party provider, Taskrabbit delivery. A Member rate of $155 will be applied.

Eligible items for removal include any standalone piece of exercise equipment that can be safely removed from your home, like treadmills, stationary bikes, stair machines, and ellipticals. Equipment can be from Peloton or other brands.

Most old equipment can be hauled away with an average lead time of 5 days prior to your delivery arriving. Equipment removal services are available in most zip codes within the continental United States. Some restrictions apply. [Learn more](https://support.onepeloton.com/s/article/Peloton-Partnerships-Sell-or-Recycle-Old-Equipment-with-Peloton-Repowered-and-Haulaway?language=en_US).

Does Row+ fold up?
Arrow

Row+ comes with an Upright Wall Anchor to easily store it in an upright position and save space. [Learn more](https://support.onepeloton.com/s/article/9387598238868-Storing-Your-Peloton-Row-Upright?language=en_US) about storing Row+ upright.

What are the benefits of a rowing machine workout?
Arrow

There are huge [benefits to rowing](https://www.onepeloton.com/blog/7-benefits-of-a-rowing-machine-workout/). A rowing machine workout burns an abundant number of calories without stressing your joints. And, because it’s not a weight-bearing exercise, Row+ing doesn’t overwhelm your knees and ankles. Instead, you control your pace and movement, which is particularly helpful if you’re a beginner or recovering from an injury.

A rowing machine also offers a welcome variety of workouts—interval training, [endurance training](https://www.onepeloton.com/blog/endurance-training/), strength training—to help you shake things up. Because you can get so much done in a short, intense rowing workout, you’ll be able to achieve your goals quickly.

[Learn more](https://www.onepeloton.com/blog/guide-to-rowing-machine-workouts/) about the benefits and what to expect during a rowing class.

What if I don't want to take instructor-led classes?
Arrow

With Row+, you can explore stunning scenery with Scenic classes, do your own thing with the ‘Just Row’ feature, or tune out while you work out by streaming games, shows, and movies across streaming platforms with the Entertainment feature.

What’s included with an All-Access Membership and is it required for the Row+?
Arrow

With the All-Access Membership, you’ll get access to our entire [library of live and on-demand classes](https://www.onepeloton.com/classes), individual user profiles for everyone in your household, and free use of the [Peloton App](https://www.onepeloton.com/app) with the ability to take workouts from your phone, smartwatch, tablet, and TV. Age, height and weight restrictions apply.

Does Row+ come with a warranty?
Arrow

Yes, Row+ comes with a Limited Warranty, fully covering the touchscreen and other original components for up to 12 months, and the Row+ frame for up to 5 years. Replacement parts covered under warranty can only be shipped to the United States (restrictions may apply in remote areas, which may include Alaska, Hawaii, Puerto Rico, U.S Virgin Islands, and ferry-only accessible locations). Please note, the warranty is voided and service and repairs are not offered if the Row+ is moved outside of the United States. [See terms](https://support.onepeloton.com/s/article/Peloton-Hardware-Limited-Warranty-and-Protection-Plans?language=en_US).

Add a [Peloton Protection Plan](https://support.onepeloton.com/s/article/360034569232-Peloton-Protection-Plans) to get 48 months of total coverage and additional benefits like accidental damage coverage, surge protection, $0 deductibles.

Can I try the Row+ before I buy it?
Arrow

Yes, you can check out the Row+ at a local showroom or get a 30-Day Home Trial with your first-time Row+ purchase. [Locate a showroom](https://www.onepeloton.com/showrooms) near you and book an appointment to test out a Row+ in person.

With a 30-Day Home Trial, you’ll get 30 days of full body training to find the classes you’ll love once your equipment is delivered. If you’re not satisfied, contact us within 30 days to schedule your collection and we'll refund your purchase. Learn more about our [30-Day Home Trial](https://www.onepeloton.com/home-trial). Terms apply.

What do I need to do ahead of my Row+ being delivered?
Arrow

Your new Row+ will be delivered to your home and set up by our team of professionals. We'll place it in the perfect spot you've already picked out, finish assembly, connect it to power and Wi-Fi and walk you through the basics—getting you up and rowing in no time.

Note, our team will not install the Upright Wall Anchor. We strongly recommend hiring a third-party for this installation if this is a feature of the Row+ you wish to use.
