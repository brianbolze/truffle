# FAQs

* * *

What kind of classes do I get with App Membership?
Arrow

App One and App+ members have access to as many strength, meditation, yoga, stretching, boxing, HIIT cardio, Pilates, barre, dance cardio, and outdoor running and walking classes as they want per month.

Cardio equipment classes are also available for all App members, which includes workouts meant to be taken on any stationary bike, treadmill, or rower. App One members have limited access to these classes and can take three per month. App+ members have no limits on how many cardio equipment classes they can take each month.

Additionally, all App members have access to the Just Work Out feature, which allows you to track activities outside of a Peloton class on your Peloton App for iOS® and Android®. Plus, you can listen to music from third-party apps. With Peloton Gym, you’ll get step-by-step workout plans that you can use to easily build and stick to clear routines.

Is the Peloton App trial free?
Arrow

Upon starting a trial, as a new Peloton App Member, you have the option to choose between a free trial of App One Membership or App+ Membership. Once your trial period has ended, you’ll be automatically billed for App One or App+ Membership depending on which option you choose. To opt out, please cancel your trial at any time during the trial period. Trials are available to new Peloton paid members only.

There is no upfront cost to a trial, but Members are required to have a payment method on file. For information about how to begin your trial, [click here](https://support.onepeloton.com/s/article/360036580951-Peloton-App-Free-Trial?language=en_US).

Will my trial be for Peloton App One or App+ Membership?
Arrow

Upon starting a trial, as a new Peloton App customer, you have the option to choose between a trial to the App One Membership or the App+ Membership. Once the trial period has ended, you will be automatically billed for the App One or App+ Membership depending on which option you chose. To opt out, please cancel your trial at any time during the trial period. Trials are available to new Peloton paid Members only.There is no upfront cost to a trial, but Members are required to have a payment method on file.

How can I access classes on the App?
Arrow

Peloton workouts were designed to be taken at home, on the go, or at the gym. As such, Peloton programming is compatible with your mobile phone, tablet, TV, computer, smart watch, and works with many smart TV providers.

Can I use Bluetooth® enabled devices with the Peloton App?
Arrow

Yes, you can use compatible headphones, heart rate monitors, fitness trackers, and smart watches via Bluetooth® with a Peloton App+ Membership. Additionally, when using a third-party Bluetooth® FTMS enabled treadmill or rower, you can connect to your equipment to track your workout performance and goals.

How do I upgrade or downgrade my Peloton Membership?
Arrow

Once you have selected or purchased a Peloton App Membership using the Web, you will be able to easily upgrade or downgrade your Peloton App Membership from the Peloton website at any time.To view and compare all membership options, visit the [membership page](https://www.onepeloton.com/membership).

Upgrading Your App Membership

Please follow the steps below to upgrade your Peloton App Membership:

1. Login using your Peloton credentials at [onepeloton.com/mymembership](https://onepeloton.com/mymembership)
2. From the “My Memberships” page, select the “Upgrade” button next to your current Membership.
3. You will then be asked to choose the Membership and billing cycle (if applicable) you would like to upgrade to.
4. Once you have completed these steps, your Membership will be officially upgraded. You will receive an order confirmation and receipt via email. You have now successfully upgraded your Peloton App Membership.

Downgrading Your Peloton App Membership

If you would like to downgrade your Peloton App Membership, please follow the steps below. If you choose to downgrade, you will not receive a refund.

1. Login using your Peloton credentials at [onepeloton.com/mymembership](https://onepeloton.com/mymembership)
2. From the “My Memberships” page, select the “Manage” button next to your current membership.
3. After doing so, select the Peloton App Membership plan you wish to downgrade to.
4. You will then be asked to choose the cadence of your Membership billing cycle, if applicable (annual or monthly), and to confirm your payment method. You will be informed of what content will be available at your new Membership plan. Once you have completed these steps, your Peloton App Membership will be updated at the end of your current billing cycle. You will receive an order confirmation and receipt via email. You have now successfully updated your Peloton App Membership.

If you have any questions or concerns, please don’t hesitate to contact our Member Support Team by [clicking here](https://support.onepeloton.com/hc/en-us).

How many profiles do I get with an App Membership?
Arrow

Users with an App Membership get access to one profile.

I already have a Peloton Bike, Tread, or Row. Does App Membership work for me?
Arrow

No. If you have a piece of Peloton equipment, including a Peloton Bike, Bike+, Tread, or Row, you will need an All-Access Membership. You will not have full access to all Peloton content needed for your Peloton equipment with just an App Membership. To have access to all functionality of your Peloton equipment, you will need to maintain the correct level of membership. [Find out more about all membership offerings here.](https://www.onepeloton.com/membership)
