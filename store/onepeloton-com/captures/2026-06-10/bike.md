# FAQs

* * *

What are the differences between Cross Training Bike and Bike+?
Arrow

Bike+ has a bigger 23.8” swivel screen, and comes with Sonos-tuned speakers, a fan, phone tray, and an auto-resistance option which automatically changes resistance levels to match instructors’ cues. Bike+ also has a movement-tracking camera to track your reps and deliver form analysis. The hands-free control on Bike+ helps you start or pause a class, record your weight, and take control of your workout with your voice.

Is Peloton IQ available on all Peloton devices?
Arrow

Peloton IQ provides training and insights designed to personalize every workout. Peloton IQ is available on all Peloton devices, but its most advanced features (e.g. Movement Tracking) can only be experienced on the Cross Training Bike+, Tread+, and Row+. A Peloton All-Access Membership is required to access all Peloton content and applicable features on your Peloton hardware.

Do I need cycling shoes to use the Bike?
Arrow

Cycling shoes enhance your Peloton experience and are recommended. Peloton Bikes require Delta-compatible cleats. Peloton cycling shoes have a narrow to normal fit. If you fall between sizes or have a wider foot, we recommend sizing up.

How much space will the Bike take up in my home?
Arrow

Bike and Bike+ both have a compact 4' x 2' footprint to fit nicely in homes of all sizes.

The total dimensions of Bike are 54" L x 23" W x 61" H. We recommend at least 24” of space on all sides for storing and training. The ceiling height minimum is 8' to ensure all riders have adequate headroom.

See the full list of specs [here](https://www.onepeloton.com/bikes/compare).

Is there a way to have my old exercise equipment removed?
Arrow

After placing your order for new Peloton equipment, eligible customers can request haulaway service from Peloton’s trusted third-party provider, Taskrabbit delivery. A Member rate of $155 will be applied.

Eligible items for removal include any standalone piece of exercise equipment that can be safely removed from your home, like treadmills, stationary bikes, stair machines, and ellipticals. Equipment can be from Peloton or other brands.

Most old equipment can be hauled away with an average lead time of 5 days prior to your delivery arriving. Equipment removal services are available in most zip codes within the continental United States. Some restrictions apply. [Learn more](https://support.onepeloton.com/s/article/Peloton-Partnerships-Sell-or-Recycle-Old-Equipment-with-Peloton-Repowered-and-Haulaway?language=en_US).

What if I don't want to take instructor-led classes?
Arrow

You can explore stunning scenery with Scenic classes, or do your own thing with the Just Ride feature. You can also get transported to virtual worlds and play with Lanebreak, or tune out while you work out by streaming games, shows, and movies across streaming platforms with the Entertainment feature.

What’s included with the All-Access Membership and is it required for Bike?
Arrow

All-Access Membership gives you and your household access to our entire library of [live and on-demand classes](https://www.onepeloton.com/classes) including cycling, strength, bootcamp, outdoor running, yoga, stretching and more, all on the Bike and the [Peloton App](https://www.onepeloton.com/app).

You’ll also have access to guided, time-based and distance-based Scenic classes, training programs tailored to meet your goals, and regularly scheduled challenges. One Peloton All-Access Membership provides access to Peloton content and all features on one Bike, Tread, and Row+. Age, height and weight restrictions apply.

Does Cross Training Bike come with a warranty?
Arrow

The Limited Warranty offered by Peloton fully covers the Bike touchscreen and most original components for up to 12 months, the Bike frame is covered for 5 years. If a repair is needed during your coverage period, we will dispatch a skilled technician to your home to complete the repair. [See terms](https://support.onepeloton.com/s/article/Peloton-Hardware-Limited-Warranty-and-Protection-Plans?language=en_US).

You can also add a [Peloton Protection Plan](https://www.onepeloton.com/protection-plan) to get 48 months of total coverage and additional benefits like accidental damage coverage, surge protection, $0 deductibles.

Can I try the Bike before I buy it?
Arrow

Yes, you can check out the Bike at a local showroom or get a 30-Day Home Trial with your first-time Bike purchase. [Locate a showroom](https://www.onepeloton.com/showrooms) near you and book an appointment to test out a Bike in person.

With a 30-Day Home Trial, you’ll get 30 days of full body training to find the classes you’ll love once your equipment is delivered. If you’re not satisfied, contact us within 30 days to schedule your collection and we'll refund your purchase. Learn more about our [30-Day Home Trial](https://www.onepeloton.com/home-trial). Terms apply.

What do I need to do ahead of my Bike being delivered?
Arrow

To prepare for a quick and seamless delivery of your Bike, it’s recommended to clear the access route from the entrance of your delivery location to your room of choice as well as the final set up area for the Bike. In order to save time and to start working out right away once the delivery is complete, set up your Peloton user account prior to the delivery of your Bike. You can do this by visiting [members.onepeloton.com](http://members.onepeloton.com/) as soon as you’ve placed your order, logging in using the same email address you used to purchase your Bike. For more information about delivery and assembly, read more recommendations [here](https://support.onepeloton.com/s/article/Order-Experience-What-To-Expect-From-The-In-Home-Delivery-And-Set-Up-Of-Your-Peloton-Bike?language=en_US).
