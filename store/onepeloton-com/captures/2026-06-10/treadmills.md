# Transform the way you run with Peloton treadmills

Whatever your level or pace, unlock personalized training plans, expert coaching, and a community that supports you with Peloton. These interactive treadmills offer several ways to walk, run, or hike right from the comfort of your own home.

Climb to new heights with hiking classes featuring steeper inclines. Run a marathon course or along beautiful landscapes with Scenic Runs. Catch up on your favorite series while you walk with Entertainment options. Beat your high score in Lanebreak. Level up with personalized Pace Targets and programs to get you started with running to marathon training.

Enjoy all of this with encouragement from your friends on the Leaderboard and from motivating playlists and instructors. Choose the best treadmill to fit your space and your fitness goals.
