# FAQs

* * *

Will my 30-day trial be for Peloton App One or App+ Membership?
Arrow

Upon starting a trial, as a new Peloton App customer, you have the option to choose between a trial to the App One Membership or the App+ Membership. Once the trial period has ended, you will be automatically billed for the App One or App+ monthly Membership depending on which option you chose. To opt out, please cancel your trial at any time during the trial period. Trials are available to new Peloton paid Members only.

There is no upfront cost to a trial, but Members are required to have a payment method on file. For information about how to begin your 30-day trial, [click here](https://support.onepeloton.com/hc/en-us/articles/360036580951-How-Do-I-Begin-My-30-Day-Free-Trial-).

What do I get as a paid App One or App+ Member?
Arrow

**App One**

With the App One Membership, Members will have full visibility to the class library, but access to Bike/Bike+, Tread/Tread+, and Row classes will be limited.

The App One Membership allows access to the following:

• Strength, cardio, Pilates, yoga, outdoor walking and running, and stretching classes

• Activity tracking, live metrics, step-by-step gym programs

• Three (3) cardio equipment classes per month: Bike/Bike+, Tread/Tread+, and Row classes (excluding Lanebreak and Scenic classes, which are not offered on the Peloton App

With the App One Membership, you will have access to 3 cardio equipment classes. Each time you take a class, you will be notified in app, and a class is considered taken after 180 seconds. Failure to leave the class before 180 seconds will result in the class being considered taken for the month. Once a class is taken, it cannot be removed or replaced with another cardio equipment class. Please review our [Membership Terms](https://www.onepeloton.com/membership-terms).

• Peloton Programs and monthly Challenges

• AppleWatch® and WearOS by Google® stats

• Heart Rate Monitor connectivity

**App+**

With the App+ Membership, Members will have access to take as many classes as they want on the Peloton App platform, including Bike/Bike+, Tread/Tread+, and Row classes, Peloton App+ exclusive classes, Peloton Programs and monthly Challenges (excluding Lanebreak and Scenic classes, which are not offered on the Peloton App). Features include activity tracking, live metrics, AppleWatch® and WearOS Google® stats, Heart Rate Monitor connectivity, and more. App+ Members can also download the Peloton Strength+ app from the Apple App Store at no additional cost.

¹New App Members Only. Terms apply. Credit card required. After your free trial, App Membership is $12.99/mo for App One or $28.99/mo for App+. Cancel anytime before free trial ends.

²Credit card required. After your trial, membership is $9.99/mo. Cancel anytime before trial ends.
