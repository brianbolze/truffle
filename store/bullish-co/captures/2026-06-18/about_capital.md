# We seek founders in fierce pursuit of shaking up consumer markets.

#### Approach

### Stage

We invest in **early-stage** (Pre-Seed to Series A) companies. And have the proven ability to spot groundbreaking ideas before they’re groundbreaking.

### Investment

We invest anywhere from **$500,000 to $3,000,000** in people and companies that we find remarkable.

### Geography

We are solely focused on the **US consumer** market. But, our reach extends beyond the oversaturated hubs of NY and SF into every corner of the US.

### Industry

We’ve built careers on consumer behavior and our investments are focused on the **consumer market and B2C propositions.**

### Advantage

Money is the least interesting thing we bring to our investments. We have **expert marketers, strategists, and creatives** delivering outsized value to our portfolio companies.
