# Direct Aviation    Market Outlook

![Direct Aviation Market Outlook](https://electra-aero.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/9892fa15817bdf08a1530cdb4ccc41c24b304f04.png)

A new era of aviation has begun, and with it, access to a 35-million-passenger-trips-per-day market craving
an alternative to trips taken between 50–500 miles. In the U.S., that’s 1.6 trillion person-miles per year
traveled
uncomfortably by car or uneconomically through the hub-and-spoke network. Technology has limited aviation’s
ability to serve this travel-hungry market.

Until now.


Hybrid-electric power has created the powerful opportunity of Direct Aviation. To understand the impact that
this form of regional travel will bring to our industry, we created the Direct Aviation Market Outlook.


This report is a rigorous, evolving analysis of regional travel patterns and market opportunity across the
United States, the growing demand for a faster, more direct way to move, and the vision to make that
opportunity real.

## Aviation Has Entered  a New Era

Hybrid-electric propulsion has unlocked transformative capabilities that piston and turbine aircraft
could never achieve, creating an opportunity for more than 6,000 new U.S. commercial air routes.


This era will redefine how people and goods move throughout the regions, creating the kind of
economic impact that ripples through the nation and the world.


## Regional Mobility  Reimagined

Congestion and geographical barriers impose an enormous toll due to time wasted and productivity
lost.

This burden is most acute for the journeys that fall between slow car trips and long-haul
flights. This has created a regional mobility gap.

Electra has rigorously examined U.S. regional travel patterns to identify the level of demand for
a new model that will transform the way we travel.
