# Nautilus

## The Elegant Sports Watch

With its iconic design, the Nautilus has epitomized the elegant sports watch since its launch in 1976. Today, it boasts a splendid and incredibly rich collection of models, including a variety of complications such as perpetual calendar or flyback chronograph. Crafted in steel, rose gold or white gold, these timepieces accompany the most active lifestyles with unparalleled class and sophistication.

## Nautical-Inspired Shape

Inspired by the shape of a ship's porthole, the construction of the Nautilus pushed the art of case manufacture to a new pinnacle, while the use of new technology ensured it was impermeable to the elements. With its rounded octagonal bezel, ingenious porthole case construction, and horizontally embossed dial, the iconic design of the Nautilus is immediately recognizable.

## Sporty and Technical

While the Nautilus is mostly known for its iconic design, we have added many different Complications and Grand Complications to the original model over the years, enhancing its nautical and sporty spirit with technical sophistication. Today, the Nautilus collection includes functions such as Travel Time, Chronograph, Moon Phases, Annual and Perpetual Calendars.

## Gem-Set Watches

The gem-set Nautilus offer a vibrant palette with diamonds, blue sapphires, rubies, or emeralds, each stone carefully selected for its perfect cut, color intensity and clarity. Exquisite techniques such as the invisible setting or the snow-setting illustrate the mastery of Patek Philippe in the art of gemsetting.

## Crafting A Legacy
