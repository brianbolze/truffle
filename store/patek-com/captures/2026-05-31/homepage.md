## The last family-owned Genevan manufacture

Owned by the Stern family since 1932, our Manufacture benefits from full creative freedom, which allows us to design, develop and craft watches that connoisseurs consider to be the world's finest - as pledged by our founders Antoine Norbert de Patek and Jean Adrien Philippe.

A long-term vision

## Innovation and Tradition

We have been pursuing traditional Genevan watchmaking artistry without interruption since 1839, safeguarding rare handcrafts, such as enameling and _guillochage_ that would have otherwise disappeared. At the same time, we nurture a tradition of innovation, relying on the latest technologies to further push the boundaries of watchmaking.

[Advanced Research](https://www.patek.com/en/manufacture/a-tradition-of-innovation/advanced-research) [Rare Handcrafts](https://www.patek.com/en/manufacture/artisans-of-time/rare-handcrafts)
