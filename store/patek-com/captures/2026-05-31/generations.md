# Celebrating the Generations Campaign

It is over 25 years since Patek Philippe launched the “Generations” advertising campaign and line that has become iconic for its timeless appeal and longevity. Built from a universal and human truth, the campaign has evolved and grown to reflect the times and capture the emotion, personality and candid nature of a relationship between parent and child.
