The purpose of Patek Philippe International Customer Service is to guarantee a service of excellence all over the world. This includes our pledge to service, repair, or restore any Patek Philippe timepiece regardless of its age.

## A Close Relationship

Your relationship with us begins the day you purchase one of our timepieces. To maintain this bond over time, we have developed a global network of Authorized Retailers and Certified Service Centers dedicated to maintaining the quality and savoir-faire associated with our watches. We ensure that they all accept any watch for service and provide repair cost estimates.

## The Art of Restoration

Our vintage watches, typically over 45 years old, are entrusted to the skilled restoration team in Geneva. Their mission is to preserve each timepiece's original condition, both technically and aesthetically, using specialized skills and techniques that are rare today.
