## Wristwatches

Before the First World War and its wide adoption by soldiers, the wristwatch was regarded as a women's accessory. It has since established itself as the most practical and efficient way to wear a watch. With more than 150 references, spanning 10 major collections and equipped with one of our fifty in-house movements, our wristwatches accommodate all types of needs and watch enthusiasts.

## Pocket Watches

For decades, pocket watches were the main stage on which we demonstrated our skills. Today, as guardians of watchmaking artistry, we are one of the last manufactures that perpetuates the craft of making pocket watches as part of our current collection. Produced in small numbers, their aesthetic finesse remains a source of fascination and inspiration.

## The Dials

We have a very special relationship with dials. The Stern brothers, who acquired Patek Philippe in 1932, were owners of _Cadrans Stern Frères_ – considered “the” Swiss dial specialists. Today, crafting our dials remains a balance of techniques and crafts to create beauty with legibility. Our dials interpret the hundreds of components that lie beneath them, and make sense of our most complex complications.
