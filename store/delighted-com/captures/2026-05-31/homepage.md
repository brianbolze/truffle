## Our customers don't just send surveys. They manage experiences.

Customers use Delighted to make an outsized impact in record time with minimal resources. Here’s how:

![](https://delighted.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/icon_ask-1.svg)

Ask

Create customer and employee surveys in seconds. Our intuitive surveying platform bakes in all the best practices, including the most effective questions to ask.

![](https://delighted.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/icon_analyze-1.svg)

Analyze

Understand your survey results at a glance with built-in dashboards and reports. Drill a level deeper by tagging and segmenting your feedback for actionable insights.

![](https://delighted.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/10/icon_act-1.svg)

Act

Reduce churn and fuel growth by routing feedback directly to the people who can take action. Use email digests, two-click integrations, and more.
