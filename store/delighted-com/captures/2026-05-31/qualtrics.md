## Spot churn before it happens

[Qualtrics iQ](https://www.qualtrics.com/iq/) puts predictive intelligence and statistical analysis at your fingertips. Discover the key drivers of customer and employee happiness and identify at-risk customers based on behavior and feedback, no spreadsheets or PhD required.

XM Directory

## One system of record

The [XM Directory](https://www.qualtrics.com/xm-directory/) combines feedback data in one timeline view alongside demographics, customer lifetime value, service interactions, and more. Roll up feedback reporting for all lines of business into custom role-based and regional dashboards.

![qualtrics xm directory](https://delighted.com/wp-content/uploads/2023/11/qualtrics-6-2x.png)

## Seamless integrations. Immediate action.

Sync insights directly into your closed-loop systems. Qualtrics integrates with the same partners as Delighted, such as Slack, Zendesk and Salesforce, [plus hundreds more](https://www.qualtrics.com/marketplace/integrations/).

## Close the loop on feedback with automated workflows

Notify the people who can make a difference based on threshold alerts in changes of business metrics, offer promotions to increase loyalty, and create tickets for your support team – all automatically. [XM Automated Actions](https://www.qualtrics.com/platform/actions/) helps close experience gaps quickly and efficiently, recommending workflows for your company and industry.
