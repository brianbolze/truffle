### Frequently asked questions

How is Qualtrics different from other employee survey tools?

Most tools stop at data collection. Qualtrics captures the complete voice of your workforce—across surveys, pulse checks, [lifecycle moments](https://www.qualtrics.com/employee-experience/employee-lifecycle/), and passive listening channels—then uses AI to surface what it means and guide what to do next. Managers get personalized action recommendations. Executives get workforce intelligence tied to business outcomes. It's the difference between a report and a roadmap.

We already run an annual engagement survey. Why do we need more?

[Annual surveys](https://www.qualtrics.com/articles/employee-experience/employee-engagement-survey/) measure yesterday's problems. By the time results are compiled and shared, your best people may already be interviewing elsewhere. Qualtrics enables continuous listening—real-time pulse surveys, lifecycle feedback, and AI-powered theme detection—so you can act before issues become exits. Organizations with increased [employee listening](https://www.qualtrics.com/articles/employee-experience/employee-listening/) see up to +25 points higher engagement.

How does Qualtrics help managers actually act on feedback—not just read it?

The platform delivers personalized insights and recommended actions directly to each manager, based on their team's specific feedback. Rather than handing a leader a 40-page report, Qualtrics shows them where to focus and what to do differently tomorrow. When feedback isn't acted on, employees are 4x more likely to consider leaving—Qualtrics closes that gap.

Can Qualtrics connect employee experience data to business outcomes?

Yes—this is a core differentiator. Qualtrics links EX data to [customer experience](https://www.qualtrics.com/articles/customer-experience/customer-experience/) results and broader business performance metrics, helping you demonstrate the direct ROI of your people initiatives. The State of Iowa, for example, saw a 20% increase in trust in leadership within six months of launching their centralized EX program.

How does Qualtrics handle the volume of open-text feedback we collect?

AI automatically analyzes thousands of comments to surface trending themes, sentiment, and emotion—without requiring manual review. Comment summaries and Insights Explorer make it fast to identify what's [driving engagement or attrition](https://www.qualtrics.com/articles/employee-experience/employee-engagement-retention/), so your team spends time solving problems instead of finding them.

Does Qualtrics cover the full employee lifecycle, or just engagement surveys?

The platform covers the entire journey—from [candidate experience](https://www.qualtrics.com/employee-experience/candidate-experience/) and onboarding through [development](https://www.qualtrics.com/employee-experience/360-degree-feedback/), pulse listening, and exit. [Lifecycle intelligence](https://www.qualtrics.com/employee-experience/employee-lifecycle/) connects data across these moments so you can spot patterns before they become retention problems and understand which touchpoints drive the most impact on retention and productivity.

How does Qualtrics help with employee retention specifically?

[Retention Analytics](https://www.qualtrics.com/employee-experience/employee-retention/) lets you simulate flight-risk scenarios and model the business impact of attrition before it happens. You can connect engagement scores directly to attrition data, identify at-risk populations early, and fine-tune your retention strategy with confidence—rather than reacting after key talent has already left.
