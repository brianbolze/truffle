### Frequently asked questions

What makes Qualtrics different from other customer experience platforms?

Qualtrics goes beyond surveys and dashboards. The platform unifies every customer signal— [surveys](https://www.qualtrics.com/free-account/), chat, email, [digital behavior](https://www.qualtrics.com/customer-experience/digital-analytics/), real-time feedback—into 360-degree customer profiles, then applies AI to predict what happens next and automate what to do about it. The result is compounding intelligence that gets smarter with every interaction, rather than a static snapshot of what already happened.

We have customer data in multiple systems. Can Qualtrics bring it all together?

That's exactly what it's designed to do. Qualtrics unifies signals across every touchpoint into a single actionable view, eliminating the blind spots that come from siloed tools. Whether [feedback](https://www.qualtrics.com/articles/customer-experience/collecting-customer-feedback/) is coming from [contact center conversations](https://www.qualtrics.com/customer-experience/contact-center-analytics/), digital channels, or direct surveys, it all connects into one complete picture of the customer experience.

How does Qualtrics help teams act on feedback faster?

Instead of delivering static reports, the platform gives every team real-time, AI-powered action recommendations based on what customers are telling you right now. Automated workflows can trigger personalized recovery efforts, [close the loop with customers](https://www.qualtrics.com/articles/customer-experience/how-create-closed-loop-program/), and route the right feedback to the right experience owner—all without manual intervention. [ServiceNow](https://www.qualtrics.com/customers/servicenow/), for example, runs 31 action workflows that have generated over 10,000 automatic follow-up actions across their business.

Can Qualtrics monitor contact center interactions, not just survey responses?

Yes. Complete Interaction Intelligence allows you to [monitor 100% of contact center conversations](https://www.qualtrics.com/customer-experience/contact-center-analytics/)—every call, email, and chat—to understand what's driving satisfaction and dissatisfaction in real time. This closes a major gap for organizations that rely only on post-interaction surveys to understand what's happening on the front line.

How does Qualtrics help us retain customers and reduce churn?

The platform identifies at-risk customers earlier by connecting behavioral signals, feedback patterns, and engagement data into predictive churn models. Once a risk is detected, automated workflows personalize the recovery effort—so you're intervening before a customer leaves, not sending a win-back campaign after they're gone. With 32% of customers switching after just one poor experience, early intervention is [where retention is won or lost](https://www.qualtrics.com/articles/customer-experience/customer-retention/).

How does Qualtrics connect customer experience to revenue impact?

Every action the platform recommends is tied to measurable outcomes—retention rates, support cost reduction, [lifetime value growth](https://www.qualtrics.com/use-cases/customer-lifetime-value/). Automated workflows are built to close loops with [quantifiable ROI](https://www.qualtrics.com/articles/customer-experience/customer-experience-roi/), so you can demonstrate the direct business impact of your CX program to leadership, not just report on satisfaction scores.
