## **FAQs**

We’ve compiled answers to the most common inquiries about our stem cell and exosome products, from safety and usage to regulatory compliance and clinical applications.

Are STEM Nova Network biologics FDA‑approved?

No. Our 3D UCT‑MSC and exosome products are manufactured in FDA‑registered, ISO‑compliant facilities under cosmetic good‑manufacturing practices, but they are not FDA‑approved drugs or biologics. The U.S. Food and Drug Administration states that exosome products intended to treat diseases or conditions in humans require FDA approval and “there are currently no FDA‑approved exosome products”. We strictly market our products for research or topical cosmetic use and do not make medical claims.

What makes 3D‑cultured MSCs better than traditional 2D?

Three‑dimensional culture mimics the body’s natural environment and dramatically increases exosome production and potency. In a hollow‑fiber 3D bioreactor, MSCs produced up to 19.4‑fold more exosomes than in a standard 2D flask, and the supernatants were 15.5‑fold more concentrated. 3D culture also allows continuous harvesting of exosomes, requires far fewer plastic flasks and less labour, and can produce an equivalent yield in less than 30 minutes per day. These 3D‑derived exosomes show superior uptake by target cells and stronger effects .compared with 2D exosomes.

These 3D-derived exosomes demonstrate superior biological signaling profiles and cellular uptake characteristics compared with 2D exosomes, supporting the enhanced potency behind our 3DExo+™ platform

Can I inject these biologics?

Stem Nova biologics are manufactured under cGTP-compliant standards (21 CFR Part 1271) in an AATB-accredited, FDA-registered tissue establishment, with lot-specific Certificate of Analysis covering sterility, endotoxin, mycoplasma, and viability. They are labeled for topical and research use only. Any clinical application is at the sole discretion and professional responsibility of the licensed practitioner, in accordance with all applicable federal and state regulations.

Who are Stem Nova's products intended for?

Stem Nova biologics are supplied exclusively to licensed medical professionals. How products are used within a practitioner's clinical protocols is at their sole discretion and in accordance with applicable federal and state regulations. We do not advise on indications, applications, or patient protocols.

What does “xeno‑free ”mean?

“Xeno‑free” means that all components are derived from the same species (human) or are recombinant, so no animal‑derived ingredients such as fetal bovine serum are present. RegMedNet explains that xeno‑free media contain only proteins of human origin or recombinant quality. Using xeno‑free, serum‑free manufacturing reduces the risk of introducing foreign proteins or pathogens, making the product safer and more consistent for cosmetic or research use.
