#### Our story

Cloudflare started as a simple application to find the source of email spam. From there it grew into a service that protects websites from all manner of attacks, while simultaneously optimizing performance. Today, we're one connectivity cloud helping organizations of every size build a better Internet — for their employees, their customers, and themselves.

[Read our founding story](https://www.cloudflare.com/our-story/)

#### Our IPO

Cloudflare's global cloud platform delivers a wide range of network services to businesses of all sizes around the world — making them more secure while enhancing performance and reliability of their critical Internet properties. On **13 September 2019**, Cloudflare made its debut on the public market as it rang the bell at the New York Stock Exchange and became a publicly traded company **(NYSE: NET)**.
