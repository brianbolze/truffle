## Simplified safe design

Blue Energy uses mature light water reactors and the latest passive safety advancements to create a plant that is 100% walk-away safe. The reactor building is submerged in a pool that provides defense-in-depth, reducing nuclear-grade components and simplifying security.

![](https://blueenergy.co/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/20251213-BlueEnergy-Husseldesign-SafeDesign.png)

02

## Timeline certainty

The plant and turbine hall can be energized with gas in three years while the nuclear island is licensed and installed—giving offtakers timeline certainty.

![](https://blueenergy.co/wp-content/uploads/2026/03/20251213-BlueEnergy-Husseldesign-Timeline-certainty.png)
