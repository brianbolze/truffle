## How Finasteride Works

Finasteride blocks the conversion of testosterone to DHT — the hormone primarily responsible for male pattern baldness.

### Inhibits 5-alpha reductase

Blocks the enzyme that converts testosterone into dihydrotestosterone (DHT).

### Reduces scalp DHT

Lowers DHT levels by up to 70%, protecting hair follicles from miniaturization.

### Stops further loss

Prevents ongoing follicle shrinkage so existing hair remains thick and healthy.

### Promotes regrowth

Many men experience visible regrowth in thinning areas within 3–6 months of daily use.
