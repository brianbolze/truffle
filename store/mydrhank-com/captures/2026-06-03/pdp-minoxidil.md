## How Minoxidil Works

Minoxidil is a vasodilator that stimulates hair follicles by increasing blood flow and extending the growth phase of hair.

### Widens blood vessels

Opens potassium channels in the scalp's blood vessels, increasing nutrient and oxygen delivery to follicles.

### Extends growth phase

Prolongs the anagen (active growth) phase of the hair cycle so strands grow longer and thicker.

### Revives dormant follicles

Can reactivate miniaturized follicles that have stopped producing visible hair.

### Gradual, visible regrowth

With consistent daily use, most patients see noticeable improvement in 3–6 months.
