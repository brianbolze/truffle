## How the 3-in-1 Hair Combo Works

Three clinically supported ingredients work together to attack hair loss from multiple angles.

### Minoxidil stimulates growth

Increases blood flow to follicles and extends the active growth phase of your hair cycle.

### Finasteride blocks DHT

Prevents the hormone responsible for follicle miniaturization and pattern baldness.

### Biotin strengthens hair

Supports keratin production for stronger, more resilient hair strands.

### Follicle nutritional support

Hair is 95% keratin, and biotin is essential for keratin production. Low biotin levels are found in 38% of women experiencing hair loss, making it a critical foundational nutrient for the follicles that finasteride and minoxidil are actively working to restore.

### Synergistic results

Combining all three in one tablet delivers more comprehensive results than any single ingredient alone.
