## Comprehensive Hormone Panel For Men

Our hormone test for men evaluates all the major contributors of low testosterone. These include testosterone, estradiol, DHEA-S, Thyroid and PSA.

## TESTOSTERONE TEST

Testosterone is the most significant hormone to male sexual development, libido and reproductive function. It is also important to men’s health overall, contributing to lean muscle mass, bone strength, fat distribution, cognitive function and red blood cell production.

## ESTRADIOL TEST

Playing an important role in men’s health, estradiol is especially crucial to a healthy sex drive, erections and sperm production. This hormone needs to be in balance with testosterone in order for sexual function to work optimally.

## DHEA-S TEST

DHEA Sulfate, an adrenal gland hormone, helps produce other hormones including testosterone. Low levels of DHEA-S can lead to erectile problems and decreased sex drive. Supplementing with DHEA is often recommended as part of Male Excel’s hormone replacement therapy treatment plans.

## FREE T3 TEST

An underperforming thyroid represents one of the biggest threats to your testosterone. To see if your thyroid levels are holding you back, we’ll examine your Free T3, a hormone produced by the thyroid gland and one of the best indicators of thyroid hormone health.

## PSA TEST

Abnormal Prostate Specific Antigen (PSA) levels may indicate a prostate problem. We’ll test for PSA to make sure it’s in the normal range, so we can rule out related health conditions prior to beginning hormone replacement therapy.

## Accurately Measure Hormones with Blood Spot Testing

- Utilizes blood spots, instead of a blood draw
- Measures your body’s hormone production, providing a baseline marker
- Offers a total picture of hormone imbalance
- Enables you to compare future tests to your baseline, so you can see measurable progress in your optimization journey
- Measures Free T3 (triiodothyronine), a test which reveals thyroid imbalance. High thyroid levels of Free T3 hormones in the blood may be a sign of an overactive thyroid, and low levels could be a sign of an underactive thyroid
- Allows your Male Excel provider to use the information gathered from your male hormone test to understand your current hormone levels and to create a treatment plan that delivers results for your body
