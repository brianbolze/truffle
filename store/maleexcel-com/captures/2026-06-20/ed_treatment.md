## Frequently Asked Questions About ED Treatment

How Do I Get Started With ED Treatment?

At MaleExcel.com, you can receive treatment for erectile dysfunction in just a few easy steps. Our process requires you to complete a medical assessment online. Upon completion, a US-licensed medical provider will review it. If they determine ED medication is right for you, a licensed pharmacy will prepare and ship your discreetly-packaged medication to you.

How Does ED Medication Work?

When you get an erection, blood flows into your penis and is temporarily trapped there, creating pressure in the surrounding chambers that causes your penis to expand. When you have ED, this process is interrupted. Male Excel’s ED medication works by increasing blood flow to your penis, helping to improve the ability to get and maintain erections. Sexual stimulation is all that is required for ED medication to work. After sex, the erection should go away. In the rare event of an erection lasting more than 4 hours, seek immediate medical attention to avoid permanent damage.

Which ED Medications Do You Prescribe?

Male Excel prescribes Sildenafil (Viagra®) and Tadalafil (Cialis®), also known as oral PDE-5 inhibitors. Sildenafil was the first extensively researched PDE5 inhibitor, and it was FDA-approved in 1998 under the name Viagra® to treat ED in men. Tadalafil has been on the market since 2003 to treat ED. Male Excel offers the generic versions of these medications for a fraction of the cost of their name-brand equivalents.

What Is The Difference Between Cialis® And Daily Cialis®?

If you’d like to take Cialis® for erectile dysfunction, you’ve got two options: You can take a higher-dose Cialis® tablet on an as-needed basis (only when you want to have sex), or you can take a lower-dose Cialis® tablet every day (so you can have sex at any time).

Who Prescribes My ED Medications?

Male Excel providers are US-licensed healthcare professionals. Each provider is legally qualified to evaluate your health status and prescribe ED medications, like Generic Viagra®, Generic Cialis® and Generic Daily Cialis®. They are also qualified to prescribe these medications to you online, so you can avoid trips to in-person clinics or pharmacies.

Do I Need To Have A Physical Before Being Prescribed ED Medication?

For your safety, Male Excel currently only prescribes ED medication online to men who have physically seen a doctor in the past three years. As long as you have seen a doctor for any reason and gotten your vitals (blood pressure, etc.) measured within the past three years, our medical providers can prescribe ED medication to you.
