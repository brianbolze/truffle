## Frequently Asked Questions About Testosterone Replacement Therapy Cost

What can impact testosterone replacement therapy price?

Testosterone replacement therapy cost **depends on two simple factors:**

1) The TRT treatment option you select AND

2) The TRT dose our physician prescribes you.

How do you handle TRT prescription refills?

Before the end of your 60-day supply of TRT, we’ll send you a refill reminder via email or text. At that time, you can either continue with or adjust your treatment plan by clicking the link provided and following the online instructions.

Will my TRT cost increase?

To reach optimal testosterone levels, your medication dosage may need to be adjusted. Any change in dosage will change your TRT cost.

Is TRT covered by insurance?

Our approach to testosterone replacement therapy, which strives for optimal testosterone levels, is not covered by insurance. The problem with insurance is that, once your testosterone levels reach “normal”, your insurance carrier deems you to be cured. At that point, your carrier will no longer cover your treatment. The thing is, **most men still experience negative symptoms with normal testosterone levels.** This is the main reason why men come to Male Excel: to achieve optimal levels and feel their absolute best.

Why do you require Medical Membership?

Our aim is to generate optimal outcomes for you. To support that goal, we offer Medical Membership as part of your treatment plan. Medical Membership means you’ll have unlimited access to our Patient Care team for general questions about HRT, as well as unlimited access to medical providers for more specific health-related inquiries. We look forward to a successful relationship and ongoing patient care is a vital part of that.
Since testosterone is a Schedule III controlled substance, medical monitoring is required by law.
