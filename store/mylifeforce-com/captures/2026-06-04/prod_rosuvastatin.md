# Rosuvastatin

Rosuvastatin is part of the class of lipid-lowering medications known as “statins.” It works by reducing the amount of cholesterol the liver produces, which helps it remove cholesterol from the bloodstream. The net result is a lowering of key cardiovascular risk factors, including LDL, or “bad” cholesterol, as well as reducing the risk of developing atherosclerotic cardiovascular disease that leads to heart attacks and strokes.
