##### Step 1

#### Measure your baseline

We send a licensed phlebotomist to your home to conduct a blood draw measuring 50+ biomarkers. You complete an onboarding questionnaire, covering your health history, lifestyle, and goals for the future.

![Man having blood drawn by a nurse](https://www.mylifeforce.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fa.storyblok.com%2Ff%2F130005%2F1344x1344%2F298baf8508%2Flf24_hp_stickycolumn_v2.jpg&w=3840&q=75)

##### Step 2

#### Interpret your results.

We apply a proprietary, evidence-based algorithm to calculate your Lifescore — a single metric clarifying the overall state of your health. A Lifeforce clinician then further analyzes your data to identify your top priorities.

##### Step 3

#### 1:1 Clinician Consult

During your first 45 minute telehealth consultation, your board-certified clinician will discuss your priorities and help you understand your results and personalized recommendations based on your goals.

#### Start your Lifeforce journey
