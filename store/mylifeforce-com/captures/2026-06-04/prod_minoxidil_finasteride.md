#### Promotes Hair Growth

Minoxidil is a FDA-approved treatment to promote hair growth by prolonging the growth phase (anagen phase) of the hair cycle.

![LF24_Plan_Icon](https://a.storyblok.com/f/130005/40x40/4ed6b92403/lf24_plan_icon.svg)

#### Reduces Hair Shedding

Finasteride blocks conversion of testosterone to dihydrotestosterone (DHT), a hormone linked to hair loss, reducing hair follicle shrinkage and effectively slowing hair thinning.

![LF24_ClinicianGradeFormula_Icon](https://a.storyblok.com/f/130005/40x40/be47c60366/lf24_cliniciangradeformula_icon.svg)

#### Boosts Thickness

Finasteride slows follicle miniaturization to prevent further hair loss, while minoxidil prolongs the anagen (growth) phase and shortens the telogen (rest/shedding) phase to promote thicker, denser hair.
