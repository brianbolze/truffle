# Metabolic Panel

Metabolic health represents how cleanly and efficiently your body converts fuel into energy.

93% of Americans are metabolically unhealthy, resulting in an increased risk of chronic disease. By testing insulin, glycomark, adiponectin, and uric acid, we’ll take a deeper dive into your metabolic risk.

Changes in metabolic health are often the precursor to future longevity issues including diabetes, weight gain, heart attack risk, hormonal imbalance, and even mental health decline.

For example, signs of pre-diabetes usually show up 5-7 years before a diabetes diagnosis, creating ample time to intervene if proactively managed through advanced biomarkers.
