# Levothyroxine/Liothyronine (T4 + T3)

Levothyroxine/liothyronine therapy supports thyroid hormone optimization by providing both T4 and T3, the two hormones that regulate metabolism, energy production, brain function, and many essential processes throughout the body.

T4 (levothyroxine) acts as a reservoir hormone that the body can convert to T3 as needed, while T3 (liothyronine) is the active hormone used by cells to generate energy and drive metabolic activity. Together, these hormones help support balanced thyroid signaling and optimal metabolic function.
