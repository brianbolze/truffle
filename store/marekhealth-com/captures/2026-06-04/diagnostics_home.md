## Howitworks

Purchase your tests

Select the panels or tests you'd like and place your order. Once paid, you'll receive an email with your lab order form, next steps, and best practices for sample collection.

Provide your sample

Visit a Quest location near you to provide your sample. Use the Quest locator below to find a location near you.

Get your results

Our partnered facilities will process your lab work. Test results typically arrive within 14 business days, and you'll receive an email as soon as they arrive.
