​Do you offer supplementation to support cognitive health?

Yes, we provide personalized supplementation plans tailored to your specific needs. Based on your bloodwork and personal goals, our team will recommend the most effective supplements to improve cognitive function and support long-term brain health.

![Toggle Icon](https://images.marekhealth.com/website_assets/homepage/plus.webp)

What types of deficiencies can cause brain fog?

Brain fog can result from a variety of deficiencies or imbalances, including low testosterone, low thyroid function, vitamin B12 deficiency, low levels of omega-3s, and insufficient iron. Identifying and addressing these deficiencies through proper testing and supplementation can help clear brain fog.

![Toggle Icon](https://images.marekhealth.com/website_assets/homepage/plus.webp)

How can lifestyle changes help me think sharper?

Regular physical activity, a nutritious diet, adequate sleep, and stress management play critical roles in maintaining and improving cognitive function. These lifestyle changes, along with mental exercises, can sharpen your thinking and boost overall brain health.
