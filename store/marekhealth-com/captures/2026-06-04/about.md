We live in the real world where it’s harder for the average person to focus on lifestyle intervention when they have bigger priorities to worry about.

But ultimately we’re saying there’s no shame in leaning on high leverage therapeutics. While they often have some form of risk attached, it becomes a non-issue when you have expert provider insight to mitigate that risk.
