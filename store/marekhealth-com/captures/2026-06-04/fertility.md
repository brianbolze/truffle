​​Can HCG improve male fertility?

Yes, HCG can improve male fertility by stimulating the testes to produce more testosterone and support sperm production, particularly in cases of low testosterone or hypogonadism.

![Toggle Icon](https://images.marekhealth.com/website_assets/homepage/plus.webp)

​How is HCG used in female fertility treatments?

In women, HCG is often used as part of fertility treatments to trigger ovulation. It mimics the body's natural LH (luteinizing hormone) surge, which is essential for releasing an egg during ovulation.

![Toggle Icon](https://images.marekhealth.com/website_assets/homepage/plus.webp)

Can HCG help men who are on testosterone therapy maintain fertility?

Yes, HCG is often used alongside testosterone therapy to maintain or restore fertility. It helps prevent testicular shrinkage and supports sperm production, which can be compromised during testosterone replacement therapy.

![Toggle Icon](https://images.marekhealth.com/website_assets/homepage/plus.webp)

​What are the common side effects of using HCG for fertility?

Some possible side effects include headaches, bloating, and mood swings. In men, excessive testosterone production could lead to acne or increased aggression. In women, it may increase the risk of ovarian hyperstimulation syndrome (OHSS) if not monitored properly.

![Toggle Icon](https://images.marekhealth.com/website_assets/homepage/plus.webp)

​How long does it take for HCG to improve fertility?

The timeline for seeing results varies, depending on individual health conditions and the treatment plan. Some men may see an improvement in sperm count within a few months, while women may experience ovulation after just one treatment cycle. Regular monitoring is essential to assess effectiveness.
