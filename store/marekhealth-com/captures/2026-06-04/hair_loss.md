\*If you’re an American Citizen working overseas with an APO box, we may be able to ship select items to you, please inquire with your Health Coach.

![Toggle Icon](https://images.marekhealth.com/website_assets/homepage/plus.webp)

Will my hair loss restart if I stop taking Finasteride or Dutasteride?

Yes, discontinuing therapy usually leads to the return of hair loss, as DHT levels will rise again. Continued use is typically necessary to maintain the results.

![Toggle Icon](https://images.marekhealth.com/website_assets/homepage/plus.webp)

Can Finasteride/Dutasteride affect testosterone levels?

While Finasteride and Dutasteride reduce DHT, they do not lower overall testosterone levels. Some men may actually see a slight increase in circulating testosterone levels due to the inhibition of its conversion to DHT.

![Toggle Icon](https://images.marekhealth.com/website_assets/homepage/plus.webp)

Is it safe to use Finasteride/Dutasteride long-term?

Yes, many men use these medications safely for long periods. Ongoing monitoring with your healthcare provider can help ensure you remain healthy and minimize potential risks or side effects over time.

![Toggle Icon](https://images.marekhealth.com/website_assets/homepage/plus.webp)

Can Finasteride/Dutasteride be combined with other hair loss treatments?

For optimal results, these treatments can be combined with other options such as topical minoxidil, GHK-Cu, platelet-rich plasma (PRP) therapy, or hair transplant surgery. Our partnered medical providers at Marek Health will develop a personalized protocol tailored to your specific needs to ensure that you achieve the best results for your goals.
