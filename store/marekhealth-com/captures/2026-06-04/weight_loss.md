Will I Regain the Weight After Stopping Treatment with Semaglutide/Tirzepatide?

It's possible to regain weight after stopping Semaglutide or Tirzepatide, as seen in the STEP trials where many participants regained weight after discontinuing treatment. However, at Marek Health, we focus on sustainable long-term success. We don't just provide medication; we work with you to implement lifestyle strategies that support lasting results. By optimizing your nutrition, exercise, and other key health factors, we help you maintain the progress you've made. If discontinuation of therapy is your goal, we'll guide you toward achieving and sustaining your body composition goals even after stopping treatment.

![Toggle Icon](https://images.marekhealth.com/website_assets/homepage/plus.webp)

How Do Semaglutide and Tirzepatide Work?

Both Semaglutide and Tirzepatide mimic the action of natural gut hormones to:

- Increase insulin production

- Reduce glucagon synthesis

- Delay gastric emptying

- Suppress appetite


![Toggle Icon](https://images.marekhealth.com/website_assets/homepage/plus.webp)

What are the potential side effects?

Like any medication, Semaglutide and Tirzepatide may cause side effects, including:

- Gastrointestinal issues: nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, constipation, abdominal pain

- Injection site reactions

- Fatigue

- Headaches


![Toggle Icon](https://images.marekhealth.com/website_assets/homepage/plus.webp)
