What are the benefits of using topical rapamycin for skin health compared to oral rapamycin?

Topical rapamycin provides a focused solution for skin health by directly targeting the skin’s surface where cellular damage occurs. This localized approach helps reduce signs of aging, such as wrinkles and skin sagging, more quickly than oral rapamycin. While oral rapamycin works systemically to address overall aging, topical rapamycin is specifically designed to address aging at the skin level.

![Toggle Icon](https://images.marekhealth.com/website_assets/homepage/plus.webp)

Do I need a prescription to obtain topical rapamycin?

Yes, a prescription is required for topical rapamycin. A licensed physician will review your health history to determine whether it's suitable for you. Upon approval, they will develop a personalized treatment plan tailored to your unique health needs.

![Toggle Icon](https://images.marekhealth.com/website_assets/homepage/plus.webp)

Is topical rapamycin safe to use during pregnancy or while breastfeeding?

Topical rapamycin is not recommended for use during pregnancy, while planning to become pregnant, or while breastfeeding, as it can pose risks to fetal development.

![Toggle Icon](https://images.marekhealth.com/website_assets/homepage/plus.webp)

What are the potential side effects of using topical rapamycin?

The most common side effect experienced by first-time users is mild skin dryness, typically lasting less than a week. If you encounter discomfort, applying a gentle, chemical-free moisturizing cream before bed can help alleviate the dryness.
