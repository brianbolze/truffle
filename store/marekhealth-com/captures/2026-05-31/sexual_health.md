More questions and answers can be found on our FAQ page

![Toggle Icon](https://images.marekhealth.com/website_assets/homepage/plus.webp)

How quickly do Tadalafil and PT-141 work?

**Tadalafil** typically starts working within **30-60 minutes** and can last up to **36 hours**, which is why it's known as the "weekend pill." **PT-141** may take **30 minutes to 2 hours** to take effect and may last for **6-12 hours.**

![Toggle Icon](https://images.marekhealth.com/website_assets/homepage/plus.webp)

Can I use Tadalafil and PT-141 together?

Yes, they can be used together since they work through different mechanisms. **Tadalafil** improves blood flow for better erections, while **PT-141** enhances sexual desire. Combining both can offer a more comprehensive solution for sexual dysfunction, but always consult with your healthcare provider before using both medications.

![Toggle Icon](https://images.marekhealth.com/website_assets/homepage/plus.webp)

Are there any side effects associated with Tadalafil and PT-141?

**Tadalafil** may cause side effects like **headaches, flushing, indigestion, and nasal congestion**. **PT-141** can sometimes cause **nausea, flushing, or increased blood pressure**. Both medications are generally well-tolerated, but it's important to speak with your provider about any potential interactions or side effects.

![Toggle Icon](https://images.marekhealth.com/website_assets/homepage/plus.webp)

Who should not use Tadalafil or PT-141?

**Tadalafil** should not be used by individuals taking **nitrates** (often prescribed for chest pain) due to the risk of a serious drop in blood pressure. **PT-141** should be avoided by people with **cardiovascular issues** or **uncontrolled high blood pressure**. Always discuss with your healthcare provider if you have any underlying medical conditions.
