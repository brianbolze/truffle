## Frequently Asked Questions

**Who is this product right for?**

It is designed for adults who prioritize sustained cellular energy and ongoing NAD+ support as part of an evidence informed approach to healthy aging.\*

**How does NMN differ from NR?**

Both NMN and NR raise NAD+ levels. NMN is one enzymatic step closer to NAD+, while NR first converts to NMN before becoming NAD+. Some users report subjective differences in response. Human data support NAD+ increases from both, though study designs and doses vary.\*

**What dose is supported by human research?**

Human studies have used daily NMN doses ranging from 250 mg to 2000 mg. Lower doses focus on long term consistency and tolerance. Higher doses are used in clinical research settings and advanced protocols. Dose selection depends on goals, age, activity level, and stacking strategy.\*

**How long does it take to notice effects?**

Changes in NAD+ levels occur before subjective effects. Some users report changes in energy, exercise recovery, or sleep within weeks. Others notice benefits only after sustained use. Individual response varies.\*

**Do lower NMN doses work, or are higher doses necessary?**

Lower daily doses support steady NAD+ precursor availability over time. Higher doses increase total substrate availability but are not required for every user. Consistency matters as much as dose for long term NAD+ maintenance.\*

**Does NMN affect methylation?**

NMN metabolism produces nicotinamide, which is cleared through methylation pathways. At higher doses, some users pair NMN with methyl donors such as TMG to support methyl balance. This is a consideration for advanced dosing, not a requirement for all users.\*

**How should I take NMN?**

NMN works with or without food. Some users prefer fasted dosing for metabolic signaling, while others prioritize comfort and tolerance. Morning dosing aligns with circadian NAD+ rhythms and daytime energy metabolism, though evening dosing works for some without affecting sleep. Timing does not prevent conversion to NAD+, so individual response should guide both food and time of day choices.
