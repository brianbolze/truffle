## Anti-Aging Benefits

**Rising Obesity**

Obesity is one of the biggest health challenges we face today. Nearly 73% of U.S. adults are overweight or obese—and carrying excess weight doesn’t just affect how you feel day-to-day. It’s also linked to a shorter lifespan. In fact, studies suggest that severe obesity (BMI 40–59) may reduce life expectancy by up to 14 years.4-6,17

**Optimized Aging**

GLP-1s are best known for helping regulate blood sugar and support weight loss. But exciting new research suggests they may go even further—potentially slowing the aging process at the cellular level. Early studies across multiple species show that GLP-1s influence cellular pathways linked to not just how long you live (lifespan), but how well you live throughout those years (healthspan).18

## How GLP-1s May Help You Age Better

GLP-1s target several core biological pathways tied to the “hallmarks of aging.” These compounds activate AMPK, a central regulator of cellular energy; suppress the mTOR pathway, closely tied to aging and cellular stress; and enhance Nrf2 signaling, crucial for antioxidant defense. Together, these actions support essential functions that keep your body strong and resilient over time.7,18-22
