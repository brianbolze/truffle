THE PROBLEM

## Construction breaks down in three ways

Tight schedules, lean project management teams, and bespoke scopes collide on every project.

### Drift

Design gaps and execution misalignments between the EPC agreement, IFC drawings, and what actually gets built. By the time they surface, they're expensive to fix and hard to renegotiate.

### Fragmentation

EPC agreements, designs, submittals, RFIs, and schedules spread across Procore, SharePoint, email, and PDFs. No single source of truth, and no one is sure what the current state of the project actually is.

### Late surprises

Issues hit at commissioning and closeout — when rework is costly, remobilization is hard, and financing timelines are already at risk.

Review design drawings and revisions from 30% through IFCs and As-Builts. Confirm compliance with the EPC contract, design specifications, permits, RFIs, and utility approvals. Flag gaps in PV structural plans, loading diagrams, attachment details, and DAS configurations before field deployment.

03

Construction Start

### EPC management

Serve as Owner's Representative and primary liaison between owner and EPC. Weekly stakeholder meetings to track schedule, action items, change orders, and contract compliance. Centralize RFIs and meeting notes in the Euclid Platform.

04

Construction

### Site inspection

Site visits at milestones, Golden Row, and construction checkpoints. Centralize findings for collaboration between owner and EPC. Cross-reference observations against design, EPC agreement, and QA/QC expectations.

05

Commissioning

### System commissioning review

Review testing results — insulation resistance, breaker tests, grounding tests, torque checks, inverter commissioning, capacity testing. Verify performance against modeled performance. Consolidate non-conformances into the substantial completion punchlist.

06

Closeout

### EPC closeout

Review milestone completion packages against MIPA and EPC agreement. Track final documentation, punchlist, and acceptance criteria. Clean handoff to asset management without loss of context.

01

EPC Mobilization

### Validate readiness before the EPC mobilizes onsite

Review IFC designs and confirm the EPC is ready to mobilize — aligned with AHJ requirements, site host expectations, and project-specific prerequisites before field work begins.

02

Golden Row

### Pressure-test the method before broad deployment

Review the methods of procedure against the EPC agreement and approved designs before green-lighting the EPC to continue at scale.

03

MC

### Verify the system has been constructed to spec

Validate that the installed system aligns with the EPC agreement, design package, and permit set, and confirm cold commissioning deliverables are in place.

04

CS

### Confirm the system is operating as modeled

Validate operating performance against EPC requirements, OEM guidance, and industry standards, including functional testing data and capacity testing results.

05

FC

### Enforce closeout requirements before handoff

Validate that the EPC is meeting contractual closeout requirements and prepare the project record for a clean O&M handoff to the asset manager.
