## Our commitments

Euclid's goal is to build relationships that transcend transactions. When we partner with a customer, we are guided by four enduring commitments.

1

### We operate with Excellence, always

We hold ourselves to standards that are relentlessly, uncomfortably high. We push ourselves and each other to grow, even when it isn’t the most profitable path today, and we believe in doing whatever it takes to deliver for our clients.

2

### We practice extreme ownership

At every level of our organization, accountability is our default mode. Our clients are our teammates, and we treat their projects like our own. We believe that ownership demands active participation, but it also means kindness, respect and caring deeply about both results and process.

3

### we care because we've been there

We spent years developing, financing, and building projects before starting Euclid. We saw all the ways it could go right, and all the ways it too often went wrong. We want it to go right more often for you. We're in a high-stakes, global fight to decarbonize the planet while growing prosperity for all, and we know that together we can win.

4

### we constantly innovate for you

We focus every day on how we can take the latest innovations in software, AI, process and people and deliver them to our clients so that we can all get more projects built faster, with fewer mistakes. We keep our eyes on the horizon of an abundant world powered by reliable clean energy.

## Our why

Euclid was started by renewable energy veterans who knew there had to be a better way. Our team is guided by the belief that renewables can change the world. But to get there, today’s teams need the right tools and solutions.

So we're building Euclid to empower renewable energy teams to deliver more projects with greater speed, precision and ease.

### Excellence

Euclideans have relentlessly high standards – for our company, for our industry and for ourselves.

### Ownership

Euclideans get it done. We step up, own issues and resolve them without being asked.

### Belief we can make a difference

Euclideans believe it shouldn’t be so hard to get renewable energy projects done. We've felt the challenges first-hand, and it's personal for us.

### Think big

Euclideans ask “why?” a lot. We question the status quo, and embrace the discomfort and ambiguity of transformative innovation.

### Care

Euclideans give a damn. We care about making the Earth a place where nature and humanity can thrive, starting with our clients, our teammates and ourselves.

### Growth

Euclideans are curious. We don't live by a code of right and wrong, but rather a culture of learning.
