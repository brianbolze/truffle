## How UBS built a 1.5GWh energy storage portfolio with Euclid

#### $750M

in capital managed across 100+ vendors and 1,700 invoices using the Euclid Platform

#### 1.5 GWh

energy storage assets acquired

Working with Euclid, UBS acquired 6 energy storage assets totaling 1.5GWh and developed and commissioned one of ERCOT's largest energy storage fleets. Together, the UBS-Euclid partnership navigated financing that included a 5-lender facility, 2 ITC transfer buyers, preferred equity and specialized insurance – managing the flow of $750 million in capital across 100+ vendors and 1,700 invoices to scale complex operations while maintaining institutional-grade quality.
