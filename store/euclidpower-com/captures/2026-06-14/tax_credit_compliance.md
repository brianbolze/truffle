## From early development to financing close

Each compliance area runs a different playbook. Toggle to see the workflow Euclid runs for your offer.

[feoc](https://www.euclidpower.com/tax-credit-compliance#w-tabs-0-data-w-pane-0) [Domestic content](https://www.euclidpower.com/tax-credit-compliance#w-tabs-0-data-w-pane-1) [pwa](https://www.euclidpower.com/tax-credit-compliance#w-tabs-0-data-w-pane-2)

01

Pre-Procurement

### Country-of-origin & supplier screening

Screen every supplier and component against PFE country lists, OFAC, and BIS Entity Lists before contracts are signed. Identify high-risk components — modules, cells, batteries, critical minerals — and flag where deeper diligence is required.

02

Diligence

### Ownership chain & control analysis

Map ownership up the chain for each in-scope supplier. Test for direct or indirect FEOC control, board influence, and licensing relationships that would trigger a Specified Foreign Entity classification.

03

Calculation

### MACR threshold testing

Run the Material Assistance Cost Ratio against your project's manufactured-product mix. Confirm the project clears the annual MACR threshold under Section 7701(a)(51) and model alternatives if you're close to the line.

04

Financing

### FEOC AUP report & risk register

Deliver a risk-scored FEOC AUP report with supplier-level diligence, MACR calculation, and a register of any residual exposures plus mitigation. Designed for tax equity, lenders, and insurers to rely on at financing close.

05

Operations (10-yr)

### Ongoing monitoring & annual bringdown

Monitor supplier ownership and government designations across the full 10-year credit exposure window. Issue annual bringdown reports, refresh diligence on changes of control, and provide IRS inquiry support if needed.

01

Development

### Equipment strategy & sourcing plan

Review your equipment mix against the latest IRS Safe Harbor tables and Notice 2025-08 guidance. Identify which steel/iron items must be 100% U.S.-melted-and-poured and which Manufactured Products contribute to the adjusted percentage threshold.

02

Procurement

### Supplier certification campaign

Issue certification packages to every supplier in scope — modules, racking, inverters, transformers, batteries, BOS. Collect Direct Cost data or Safe Harbor classifications, validate completeness, and chase any gaps directly with the manufacturer.

03

Calculation

### Safe Harbor or Direct Cost analysis

Run the elected calculation methodology — Safe Harbor table or Direct Cost build-up — against your final equipment list. Pressure-test the result against alternative scenarios so you know your margin above the threshold.

04

Financing

### Domestic Content AUP report

Deliver an Agreed-Upon Procedures report addressed to your project company that lenders, tax equity, and ITC transfer buyers can rely on. Includes methodology, supplier-level exhibits, and qualifying-percentage attestation.

05

COD & Bringdown

### As-built reconciliation

Once construction is complete, reconcile the report to as-built equipment lists, refresh exhibits if substitutions occurred, and issue the bringdown report needed for tax filings.

01

Pre-Construction

### Contractor onboarding & wage setup

We onboard every contractor and subcontractor on your project, pull the correct DOL wage determinations for the work classification and county, and set expectations for certified payroll, apprenticeship ratios, and recordkeeping before the first hour is worked.

02

NTP

### Apprenticeship plan & ratio modeling

Register apprentices through approved programs, model the labor-hour ratios required to hit the apprenticeship threshold, and confirm Good Faith Effort coverage if registered programs aren't available in your market.

03

Construction

### Live labor tracking & weekly review

Ingest weekly certified payroll across every contractor into the Euclid platform. Flag wage underpayments, missed ratios, and missing journeyman supervision in real time — with weekly check-ins so issues get fixed before they compound.

04

Substantial Completion

### Penalty modeling & remediation

If anything fell short, we model penalty exposure, coordinate cure payments, and document Good Faith Effort defense — protecting the 5x credit multiplier from the start.

05

Filing & Audit

### IRA Labor Compliance Report & defense

Deliver a financing- and audit-ready IRA Labor Compliance Report with full methodology, exhibits, and contractor-level findings. Stay on call for IRS inquiries through the credit exposure window.
