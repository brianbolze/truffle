What We Catch

## The risks hiding in your data room

These are real examples of material findings from past reviews. The kind of things that are easy to miss in a first pass and expensive to find later.

### Conflicting mineral rights

A landowner's mineral rights agreement gave a third party the ability to reclaim site control after construction began.

### Hidden schedule dependencies

The project schedule showed COD inside a safe window, but RFR identified expiring land options and permit approvals that would lapse before construction start unless amended.

### Incomplete site control

The data room included executed option agreements, but cross-referencing parcel maps revealed small but critical parcels missing from the option set — creating a layout gap that would not support the planned capacity.

### Unmet permit conditions

A conditional use permit appeared approved in the data room, but the approval included pre-construction conditions requiring a traffic study and road upgrade agreement that had not been completed.

### Unsupported ITC claim

Financial model assumed domestic content adder, but no supply chain documentation or procurement strategy supported the claim.

### Queue position at risk

Queue documentation showed an active position, but milestone deadlines tied to the facilities study deposit would expire before the projected development schedule, putting the project’s queue position at risk.
