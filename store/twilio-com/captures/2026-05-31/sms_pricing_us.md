## SMS/MMS Pricing

SMS pricing is based on the destination and type of message you’re sending, as well as the carrier to which the SMS is being sent. Our pay-as-you-go pricing gives you control over your messaging costs, with discounts as you scale.

| **Phone number type** | **SMS** Outbound\* | **SMS** Inbound\* | **MMS** Outbound\* | **MMS**Inbound\* |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Long codes** A 10-digit number for SMS and voice calls with localized identity. | $0.0083 | $0.0083 | $0.0220 | $0.0165 |
| **Toll-free** Business messaging phone number with a 3-digit prefix (Ex: 887) for high-throughput SMS and voice calls. | $0.0083 | $0.0083 | $0.0220 | $0.0200 |
| **Short codes** A short number (5-6 digits) designed for high-throughput two-way SMS messaging. | $0.0083 | $0.0083 | $0.0220 | $0.0165 |

\\* Text messages are charged per segment. Learn more about [character limits and message segments](https://www.twilio.com/docs/glossary/what-sms-character-limit?_ga=2.120963272.1302977634.1677506553-732055303.1670338818). Prices may change from time to time without notice and additional carrier fees may apply. See the [carrier fees section](https://www.twilio.com/en-us/sms/pricing/us#carrier-fees "http://twilio.com/sms/pricing") below for more detail. US A2P 10DLC are subject to registration [onboarding fees](https://help.twilio.com/articles/1260803965530-What-pricing-and-fees-are-associated-with-the-A2P-10DLC-service- "https://help.twilio.com/articles/1260803965530-What-pricing-and-fees-are-associated-with-the-A2P-10DLC-service-"). A [failed message processing fee](https://help.twilio.com/articles/27502337507483-Failed-Message-Processing-Fees "https://help.twilio.com/articles/27502337507483-Failed-Message-Processing-Fees") of $0.001 per message will be applied only to messages that terminate in a "Failed" status.

## RCS Pricing

RCS pricing is based on the destination and type of message you’re sending, as well as the carrier to which the message is being sent. Our pay-as-you-go pricing gives you control over your messaging costs. No new API, no code changes, and all the reach of the Twilio platform. [Learn more](https://www.twilio.com/en-us/messaging/channels/rcs).

| **Sender type** | **RCS Rich** Outbound\* | **RCS Rich** Inbound\* | **RCS Rich**<br>**Media** Outbound\* | **RCS Rich**<br>**Media**Inbound\* |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **RCS Sender**<br>Rich Communication Services (RCS) enables you to send branded, interactive text messages. | $0.0083 | $0.0083 | $0.0220 | $0.0165 |

\\* **RCS Rich text messages are charged per segment and RCS Rich Media is charged per message.** Learn more about RCS character limits and message classification. Prices may change from time to time without notice and additional carrier fees may apply. See the carrier fees section below for more detail. RCS senders launching on US carriers are subject to carrier [onboarding fees](https://help.twilio.com/articles/29076535334043-RCS-Messaging-Best-Practices-and-FAQ#h_01K34T0MZ4N3C283NHZ0QD08RT). A failed message processing fee of $0.001 per message will be applied only to messages that terminate in a "Failed" status. [Learn more](https://help.twilio.com/articles/29076535334043-RCS-Messaging-Best-Practices-and-FAQ#h_01J7CH6MWN7XSR4BVW9110SPGP).
