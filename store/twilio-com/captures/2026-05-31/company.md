## A TL;DR of our history

Twilio was founded in 2008 to simplify the complexity of the global telecommunications network and bring communications to the cloud. Since then, we’ve evolved into a secure, flexible platform that combines communication channels, contextual data, and AI orchestration to power amazing digital experiences.

![A timeline showing key milestones in Twilio's history from 2008 to 2025.](https://www.twilio.com/content/dam/twilio-com/global/en/company/about/about-us_our-history_twilio.png/_jcr_content/renditions/compressed-1600.webp)
