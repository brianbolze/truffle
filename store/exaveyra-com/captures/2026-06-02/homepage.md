What are 503A and 503B compounding facilities?503A pharmacies prepare patient-specific compounded medications under state oversight. 503B outsourcing facilities are FDA-registered and manufacture in bulk under cGMP for office-use products. ExaVeyra partners with both to supply clinics with compliant regenerative compounds.
