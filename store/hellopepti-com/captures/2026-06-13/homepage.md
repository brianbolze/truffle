What are peptides?

Peptides are short chains of amino acids that act as signaling molecules in your body. They can support recovery, anti-aging, immune function, and more. Our peptides are pharmaceutical-grade and prescribed by licensed physicians.

Are peptides legal?

Yes. All medications prescribed through \[pepti\] are legally prescribed by US-licensed physicians and compounded at FDA-registered 503A/503B pharmacies in accordance with federal and state compounding regulations. Availability of specific peptides may change based on evolving FDA guidance.

Do I need a prescription?

Yes, and we handle that for you. When you complete your assessment, a licensed physician reviews your information and writes a prescription if appropriate. It's all included in your plan.

How long until I see results?

Most patients report noticeable improvements within 2-4 weeks. Results vary depending on the peptide, dosage, and individual factors. Your physician will set realistic expectations during your consultation.

Are there side effects?

Peptides are generally well-tolerated. Some patients experience mild injection site reactions. Your physician will review all potential side effects and ensure the treatment is appropriate for you.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. There are no long-term contracts or commitments. You can pause, change, or cancel your subscription at any time from your account dashboard.

How is my peptide made?

Your peptides are compounded at a licensed 503A/503B pharmacy that meets FDA standards. Each batch is tested for purity and potency before shipping.

Is my information secure?

Absolutely. We are fully HIPAA-compliant. Your personal health information is encrypted and never shared with third parties.

What peptide is right for me?

It depends on your goals. Our free assessment will help match you with the right peptide, and a licensed physician will review and personalize your treatment plan.

Do you accept insurance?

Peptide therapy is typically not covered by insurance. However, our pricing includes everything — consultation, medication, and shipping — with no hidden fees.
