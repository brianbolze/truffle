FAQ

## Common questions

What's the $99 onboarding fee for?

It covers the licensed-physician evaluation of your assessment, your initial baseline lab review, account setup, and the secure intake of your medical history. It's a one-time fee — you only pay it once when you start with pepti, not every month.

What if I'm not approved?

If our physician decides peptide therapy isn't appropriate for you, you don't pay anything beyond the onboarding fee — your medication won't be prescribed and you won't be charged for it. The $99 covers the physician's time evaluating your case.

What does the monthly price include?

Your prescription medication, ongoing physician messaging, refill management, free shipping, and 24/7 customer support. No add-ons, no surprise charges.

Can I pause or cancel?

Anytime. You're not locked into a contract. You can pause your subscription if you want to take a break (e.g., during travel) or cancel outright. We'll continue any active prescriptions through the end of your billing period.

How long until I get my medication?

Most patients receive their first shipment within 5-7 business days of physician approval. Compounded medications are made-to-order at our partner pharmacy, so production adds 2-3 days on top of standard shipping.

Do you take insurance?

Compounded peptide therapies are typically not covered by insurance, so we operate on a transparent cash-pay model. If you want brand-name GLP-1s through insurance, see our coverage check.

What if I need to talk to my doctor?

Just message them through your account dashboard — there's no extra cost. Most replies happen within 24 hours, and our team is online 24/7 for urgent questions.
