## About Pepti

Pepti is a doctor-prescribed peptide and wellness telehealth platform. Where most telehealth companies pick a single lane — weight loss, or hair, or hormones — Pepti offers the full stack of modern peptide and wellness medicine under one platform: GLP-1 weight loss, injury and tissue recovery, hormone optimization, longevity peptides, sexual wellness, and skin.

Every prescription is written by a licensed US physician after reviewing a patient's intake, and every medication is compounded at an FDA-registered 503A or 503B pharmacy. Treatments ship free where available, with transparent pricing that runs a fraction of traditional clinic costs — no insurance required.
