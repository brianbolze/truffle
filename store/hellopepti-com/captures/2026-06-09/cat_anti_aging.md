## What are peptides?

Your body already makes them. We just give it more.

### Think of peptides as text messages for your cells.

Peptides are short chains of amino acids — the same building blocks your body uses every day to heal injuries, burn fat, fight infection, and repair skin.

As you age, your body produces fewer of these signaling molecules. Peptide therapy replenishes them — giving your body the instructions it needs to recover faster, perform better, and look younger.

Every peptide we offer is prescribed by a licensed physician and compounded at an FDA-registered pharmacy. This isn't grey market supplements — this is real medicine.

### How do they work?

Peptides bind to specific receptors on your cells and trigger targeted responses — like telling your body to increase collagen production, release growth hormone, or reduce inflammation.

### Are they safe?

When prescribed by a licensed physician and compounded at a registered pharmacy, peptides have an excellent safety profile. Your doctor monitors your treatment and adjusts as needed.

### How are they different from steroids?

Peptides work WITH your body's natural systems — they signal, they don't override. Steroids replace your hormones. Peptides tell your body to produce more of its own.
