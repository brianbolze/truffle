## About Testosterone Cypionate

Compounded testosterone cypionate 200mg/ml — the gold standard for testosterone replacement therapy. Weekly subcutaneous or intramuscular injection. Includes physician oversight, baseline labs, and dose optimization.

### How it works

Testosterone cypionate is an injectable form of testosterone esterified with cypionic acid, giving it a half-life of ~8 days. After injection, the body slowly cleaves the ester to release bioidentical testosterone into circulation. Testosterone binds to androgen receptors throughout the body — muscle, bone, brain, skin — restoring physiological function in men with clinically low levels.

### Clinical evidence

The Testosterone Trials (TTrials; Snyder et al., NEJM 2016) randomized 790 men age 65+ with low testosterone to TRT vs placebo for 1 year. Treatment produced significant improvements in sexual function, mood, walking ability, and hemoglobin levels. The Bhasin et al. Endocrine Society guidelines (J Clin Endocrinol Metab 2018) provide the current diagnostic and treatment framework: testosterone therapy is indicated for men with consistent symptoms AND unequivocally low testosterone (<300 ng/dL on two morning measurements). Recent long-term safety data, including the TRAVERSE trial (Lincoff et al., NEJM 2023), found no increased cardiovascular risk vs placebo in 5,200 men over a mean follow-up of 33 months.

## Important safety information

+

### Possible side effects

•Erythrocytosis (elevated hematocrit) — common, requires periodic monitoring

•Estradiol elevation (treated with aromatase inhibitor if symptomatic)

•Acne or oily skin

•Testicular shrinkage and reduced fertility (mitigated with HCG or gonadorelin)

•Mild fluid retention

•Increased libido and energy (expected effects)

### Do not use if you have

•Active cancer or history of cancer (unless approved by oncologist)

•Pregnancy or breastfeeding

•Severe kidney or liver disease

•Known allergy to the peptide or excipients

•Prostate cancer or PSA > 4.0

•Untreated severe sleep apnea

•Hematocrit > 54%

•Untreated breast cancer in men

•Plans for biological children in the near term (without HCG/gonadorelin co-therapy)

If you experience severe side effects, call 911 or go to your nearest emergency room.
