BODY SYSTEMS

### What your score is made of.

Eight body systems are tested to create the full picture of your longevity health.

- ![](https://www.agentislongevity.com/_next/image?url=%2Fhealthscore%2Ftubes.png&w=3840&q=75)





#### Hormone Balance



Energy, mood, recovery, and resilience, driven by your hormonal signals.

- ![](https://www.agentislongevity.com/_next/image?url=%2Fhealthscore%2Fbodyscan.png&w=3840&q=75)





#### Metabolic Health



How efficiently your body processes glucose, insulin, and fuel.

- ![](https://www.agentislongevity.com/_next/image?url=%2Fhealthscore%2Fheart.png&w=3840&q=75)





#### Cardiovascular



Heart and vascular markers that predict long-term cardiometabolic risk.

- ![](https://www.agentislongevity.com/_next/image?url=%2Fhealthscore%2Fheart2.png&w=3840&q=75)





#### Organ Health



Liver and kidney function. Systems that quietly shift years before symptoms.

- ![](https://www.agentislongevity.com/_next/image?url=%2Fhealthscore%2Fblood.png&w=3840&q=75)





#### Nutritional Status



Vitamins, minerals, and fatty acids your body needs to perform.

- ![](https://www.agentislongevity.com/_next/image?url=%2Fhealthscore%2Fbrain.png&w=3840&q=75)





#### Cognitive Performance



Memory, processing speed, and executive function tracked over time.

- ![](https://www.agentislongevity.com/_next/image?url=%2Fhealthscore%2Fgrip.png&w=3840&q=75)





#### Physical Function



Grip strength and body composition, validated predictors of longevity.

- ![](https://www.agentislongevity.com/_next/image?url=%2Fhealthscore%2Ftest.png&w=3840&q=75)





#### Stress & Recovery



Cortisol and stress markers that shape how your body handles load.

FDA-cleared evaluation of memory, executive function, and processing speed.



~5 min

~2 min

~1 min
