THE LONGEVITY QUOTIENT

## One number that tells you everything about your health.

Most people don't know how fast they're aging. Your LQ changes that, with clinical precision and a custom action plan to fit your lifestyle

Your LQ score runs from 0 to 99. It measures the gap between your chronological age and your biological age, and tells you exactly where you stand across the systems that determine how well and how long you live: hormones, metabolism, cardiovascular health, cognition, and physical performance.

Powered by your bloodwork and delivered through our national network of physician-led clinics, the LQ gives you a baseline, a benchmark, and a clear path forward.
