### What is Testosterone Cream Prescribed For in Women?

Testosterone replacement therapy is a powerful treatment option for women suffering from testosterone deficiency, as it brings balance back to the body’s hormone levels.

### How Does Testosterone Cream For Women Work?

Testosterone cream is a topical hormone replacement therapy designed to increase testosterone levels in women. Applied directly to the skin, it is absorbed into the bloodstream, helping to rebalance hormones and alleviate symptoms associated with low testosterone, including low libido.

### What to Expect

Testosterone therapy is sometimes prescribed for women during menopause to address symptoms such as decreased libido, fatigue, and mood changes. These changes generally start taking place between one and six months.

## Learn More About Testosterone (Cream)

### Contraindications

Testosterone may cause an increase in blood pressure which can increase your risk of having a heart attack or stroke that may be life-threatening. Tell your doctor if you have or have ever had high blood pressure, heart disease, a heart attack, or a stroke.

### Possible Side Effects

- Breathing problems while sleeping
- Breast cancer
- Liver disease
- Kidney disease
- Mood swings

### Warnings

Consult with your physician as this prescription may have reactions with other medications.
