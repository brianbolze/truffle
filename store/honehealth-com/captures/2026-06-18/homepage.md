## Any Questions?  We’ve got answers.

### Getting Started

Hone is  a comprehensive longevity platform that fixes symptoms and optimizes your life to increase your healthspan. Getting started is easy: purchase advanced lab diagnostics for $65, complete your testing either at-home or at-lab, and then meet with your licensed physician.

### Consult & Plan

During your private, telehealth consult, a Hone-affiliated licensed physician will dive deep into your personal and family history, review your labs, and pinpoint the root cause of your symptoms. You’ll walk away with a personalized plan—including medications, supplements, diet, exercise, stress management, and sleep strategies—designed to relieve your symptoms and optimize your health.

### During Treatment

Your treatment ships straight to your door. Since everyone metabolizes medications differently, your Hone care team stays with you every step of the way—ready to answer questions and fine-tune your plan so you feel your absolute best.

### Membership & Pricing

Hone offers two memberships:

**Basic** – $25/month for advanced lab testing every 6 months, the ability to purchase physician consults, and members-only pricing on select medications and supplements.

**Premium** – $155/month for everything Hone offers: regular lab testing, physician consults, and full access to our medications.
