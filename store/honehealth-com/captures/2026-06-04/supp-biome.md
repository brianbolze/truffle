Hone’s “No List”

### Ingredients that will never be in our products, but not limited to:

**Aspartame, Sucralose, and Saccharin**

Artificial sweeteners that may harm gut bacteria, disrupt glucose regulation, and have been linked to metabolic and neurological concerns.


**BHA and BHT**

Preservatives linked to hormone disruption and oxidative stress, with potential long-term effects on metabolic and cellular health.


**Calcium Stearate**

An anti-caking agent that can interfere with nutrient absorption, potentially reducing the effectiveness of supplements.


**Dairy**

Includes whey, casein, butter, and cheese, which can cause digestive issues, inflammation, and allergic reactions in sensitive individuals.


**Egg**

Products like egg yolk, egg white, and albumin are common allergens that can trigger immune responses or digestive discomfort.


**FD&C Red #40, Yellow #5, and Blue #1**

Artificial dyes associated with hyperactivity, allergies, and potential long-term health risks, particularly in sensitive individuals.


**Fish**

Fish protein and fish oils may cause allergic reactions and are often exposed to environmental contaminants like mercury.


**Gluten**

Found in wheat, barley, rye, oats, and their derivatives, gluten can trigger digestive distress and immune responses in sensitive individuals.


**Genetically Modified Organisms (GMOs)**

No ingredients derived from genetically modified organisms, ensuring clean, sustainable, and naturally sourced formulations.


**High-Fructose Corn Syrup and Corn Sugar Derivatives**

Cheap sweeteners linked to obesity, type 2 diabetes, and fatty liver disease, known to spike blood sugar and promote inflammation.


**Hydrogenated Fats and Oils**

Processed fats that raise bad cholesterol (LDL) while lowering good cholesterol (HDL), increasing the risk of heart disease.


**Microcrystalline Cellulose (Non-Plant-Sourced)**

A synthetic filler with no nutritional value that may hinder efficient nutrient delivery in supplements.


**Peanuts**

Peanut oil, protein, and butter are common allergens that can cause severe immune reactions, including anaphylaxis.


**Sesame**

Includes sesame oil, seeds, and powder, which are rising allergens linked to respiratory and skin reactions.


**Shellfish**

Crab, lobster, and shrimp are major allergens that can trigger severe immune responses in sensitive individuals.


**Soy Protein, Lecithin, and Soy Oil**

Soy can be an allergen and may interfere with hormone balance and thyroid function in some individuals.


**Talc**

A common filler with potential contamination risks, including exposure to asbestos in poorly regulated sources.


**Titanium Dioxide and Shellac**

Coatings that may cause inflammation and accumulate in the body, raising concerns about long-term exposure and safety.


**Tree Nuts**

Includes almonds, cashews, walnuts, pistachios, and hazelnuts, which can cause severe allergic reactions.


**Vanillin and Ethyl Vanillin**

Synthetic vanilla flavorings derived from petrochemicals that lack the antioxidant properties of natural vanilla.
