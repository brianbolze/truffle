### What is Testosterone?

Testosterone is the primary male sex hormone. It plays a role in regulating sex drive, the ability to store and burn fat, red blood cell production, emotions, and mood. If your testosterone levels are too low, you may need testosterone replacement therapy to get them to a healthy range for your age.

Hone offers testosterone cypionate injections as part of a monthly care plan with a licensed physician via telehealth appointments.

### What is Testosterone Prescribed For?

Testosterone is prescribed for hypogonadism, normal male development, and low testosterone levels. Hypogonadism is when your body doesn’t produce enough testosterone.

### How Does Testosterone Replacement Therapy Work?

TRT supplements the body’s natural testosterone levels when they are insufficient due to conditions such as hypogonadism (low testosterone production).

### What to Expect

Taking prescription testosterone cypionate helps restore the levels of this hormone in the blood, reversing the symptoms of low testosterone that are low libido, erectile dysfunction, mood changes, loss in mental acuity, weight gain, hot flashes, and sleep disturbances. People who take it may notice improvements in alertness, sexual function, energy, mood, and overall well-being.

### Contraindications

Testosterone might not be right for you if you have certain medical conditions, including: Breast cancer, Polycythemia, Prostate cancer, Prostate-specific antigen (PSA) >4 ng/mL, Nodules upon digital rectal examination (DRE)

### Possible Side Effects

Breathing problems while sleeping, Breast cancer, Liver disease, Kidney disease, Mood swings.

### Warnings

Consult with your physician as this prescription may have reactions with other medications.
