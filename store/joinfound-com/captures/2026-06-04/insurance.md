## Insurance FAQs

Your insurance questions, answered

## Does Found accept insurance?

Yes! Found partners with select medical insurance carriers across the U.S. to make our care more affordable.

## What’s covered by insurance?

Cost of care will vary by plan. Health insurance plans will typically cover some or all of the following:

-Your visit with a provider

-Cost of your medications

-Labs

-Other covered services

Contact your health plan directly to determine what services are covered. The Found subscription fee is not covered by insurance.

## Do I have to use my health insurance?

No. If you don’t want to use your health insurance, or if Found is not in-network with your health plan, you can choose the self-pay option for either our Core or Plus subscription plans.

## What are the benefits of using insurance?

There are a few other perks that come with using your health insurance to pay for clinical services.

-Your total cost of care will likely be lower!

-Your monthly (or annual) membership fees are much less!

-Our cancellation policy allows more flexibility for members paying with insurance.
