# SEAMLESS INTEGRATION FOR MAXIMUM PERFORMANCE

VerdeGo Aero brings unparalleled expertise in hybrid-electric powerplant integration, with a proven track record in commercial and military applications. As pioneers of hybrid-electric flight we invite you to harness our 15 years of industry-leading experience in this area to power innovation in your aircraft.

Our engineering team has collaborated on dozens of aircraft configurations, leveraging proprietary design tools to determine the optimal hybrid powertrain for a wide range of missions. Whether your focus is urban air mobility, regional cargo transport, or next-generation military aviation, VerdeGo Aero provides the power and efficiency to meet the demands of high-performance flight, with the experience gained from understanding a large number of real-world applications and missions.

VerdeGo Aero assists customers with integrating our powerplants into their next generation aircraft, to accelerate the development cycle and apply our years of hybridization expertise. Leveraging a team that has a deep understanding of the unique ways that hybridization impacts thermal systems, battery management, noise mitigation, and electrical and communications interfaces can cut years of development timeline and significant costs out of our customers’ aircraft programs.  Through advanced modeling and simulation, we provide optimized solutions that enable rapid deployment and accelerated certification.

VerdeGo Aero has been working closely with customers for the last five years to apply our hybrid-electric powerplant technologies to a wide array of new aircraft.  We are actively working with leading aerospace innovators to deliver hybrid electric solutions for:

- Leading Advanced Air Mobility companies developing next-generation aircraft
- Military & Defense Research and Development in support of U.S. Air Force Agility Prime (now Agile Support Prime), DARPA, and Army DEVCOM Aviation & Missile Center programs
- Industry-leading next-generation aircraft configurations under development with NASA
- Cargo Drone & Logistics Missions
- Key enabling population technologies for high-speed VTOL applications
