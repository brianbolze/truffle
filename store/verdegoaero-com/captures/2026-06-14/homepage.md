# POWERING THE ELECTRIC FLIGHT REVOLUTION

VerdeGo Aero is focused on developing and manufacturing cutting-edge hybrid-electric powerplant solutions that give electrified aircraft revolutionary performance.

Electrification promises immense potential, yet the electric-flight industry faces a critical challenge — current energy sources fall short of delivering the range, payload, and speed needed for the most demanding military and commercial missions. While conventional wisdom frames electrification and sustainable fuels as competing paths in commercial aviation, VerdeGo Aero proves they work best together—accelerating progress, enhancing performance, and delivering greater impact.

In defense applications, electric aircraft are often dismissed due to battery limitations, but VerdeGo’s hybrid systems bridge the gap, integrating conventional fuels with electrified propulsion to power new high-performance aircraft and drones, ensuring mission-ready capabilities for defense applications.

## Huge Market Opportunity

#### The Aerospace industry hasn’t seen this level of innovation since the emergence of the jet engine.

VerdeGo Aero’s hybrid-electric powerplants are driving a revolution in aerospace, unlocking new levels of performance, efficiency, and sustainability across commercial and military markets.

With $27 billion of annual electric aircraft and large drone sales forecasted by 2032, VerdeGo Aero is positioned at the leading edge of this huge market opportunity.

Designed to maximize range, payload, and operational flexibility, our hybrid electric propulsion systems can be customized to power a wide range of aircraft, from eVTOL (electric vertical takeoff and landing) air taxis for short range travel, next generation HSVTOL (high speed vertical takeoff and landing) military aircraft, to cargo transport and tactical reconnaissance drones.

By bridging military and civilian applications, we are rapidly scaling hybrid-electric technology to meet the demands of next-generation aviation.
