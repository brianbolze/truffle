# Frequently Asked Questions

##### What is MaleMD?

MaleMD is a 100% online platform specializing in men’s health. Our treatment options include sexual wellness, longevity solutions, hair loss, and sleep treatment.

Our streamlined process is simple: fill out a quick health form, and a U.S. board-certified doctor will review it and prescribe your treatment if appropriate. Your medication is then shipped discreetly, straight to your door.

##### How does MaleMD work?

First, complete an online intake form to give your healthcare provider information about your medical history. Then, a licensed Doctor will assess that information and determine if the treatment is safe for you.

If your provider determines treatment is right for you, it’s delivered in discreet packaging to your shipping address.

##### Who are the providers at MaleMD?

Providers through MaleMD are 100% physicians, ensuring you get the care you deserve; we do not have any nurse practitioners on our platform. They’re licensed in all 50 states and DC, and offer safe, high-quality, evidence-based treatment and care.

##### Does MaleMD require insurance?

Nope! MaleMD doesn’t require insurance. We offer transparent pricing on treatments and your medical visit, ongoing shipments, and provider messaging are all included in one low price.

##### What states is MaleMD available in?

MaleMD is currently available in all 50 states and Washington, DC.

##### Are MaleMD ingredients sourced in the US?

Our partner pharmacies perform routine quality control checks in FDA-registered labs, ensuring every compounded batch meets the highest standards of safety and effectiveness. The ingredients they use are carefully researched and sourced in order to make sure that they are high quality. They verify that each active ingredient complies with specifications set by the United States Pharmacopeia — an independent, not-for-profit organization that sets quality and safety standards for medicine.

##### Are MaleMD pharmacies FDA-regulated?

Yes. 503A pharmacies are licensed by state boards of pharmacy and 503B pharmacies are registered by the FDA. Both types of pharmacies are regulated by the FDA.
