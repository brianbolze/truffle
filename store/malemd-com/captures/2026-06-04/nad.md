The Science

As we age, the body’s natural stores of NAD+ diminish, affecting key areas such as energy
levels, skin vitality, and increasing the likelihood of chronic health issues. In fact,
between the ages of 45 and 60, we can experience up to a 30% drop in NAD+ levels.

This reduction has been associated with symptoms like persistent fatigue, reduced insulin
sensitivity, muscle deterioration, and cognitive challenges, including neurodegenerative
diseases like Alzheimer’s.

Studies highlight that preserving NAD+ levels plays a crucial role in supporting longevity,
boosting energy, and maintaining overall health as we age.

##### What are side effects of NAD+?

NAD+ supplements can have side effects, some of the most common include:

- Nausea: Some people may experience feelings of nausea when taking NAD+
supplements, especially if they take higher doses than recommended.
- Headaches: NAD+ supplements can cause headaches, particularly if you are
sensitive to nicotinic acid (the component of NAD+ that gives niacin its
name).
- Flushing: NAD+ supplements can cause flushing, a sensation of warmth and
redness in the skin, especially when taken on an empty stomach.
- Diarrhea: NAD+ supplements can cause diarrhea, especially if taken in high
doses.
- Dizziness: NAD+ supplements can cause dizziness, especially in people who
are prone to this symptom.

It is important to note that these side effects are usually mild and do not last
long. If you experience any adverse effects when taking NAD+ supplements, you
should stop taking them and connect to your healthcare provider via the MaleMD
Customer portal.
