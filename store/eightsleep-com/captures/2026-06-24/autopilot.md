FAQs

### Why does the Pod need Autopilot? ![expand](https://res.cloudinary.com/eightsleep/image/upload/chevron-down.svg)

The Pod needs Autopilot to ensure sleepers experience optimal sleep stages by adjusting bed temperature and elevation throughout the night. This is crucial because a key factor for poor sleep is maintaining the wrong temperature. Autopilot aims to match your body's natural circadian rhythm and temperature needs for each sleep stage, enhancing the quality of deep and REM sleep.

### Can I buy the Pod without Autopilot? ![expand](https://res.cloudinary.com/eightsleep/image/upload/chevron-down.svg)

Autopilot is required for the first 12 months (cancel any time), and covers two separate users per Pod.

### What is the commitment length for Autopilot? ![expand](https://res.cloudinary.com/eightsleep/image/upload/chevron-down.svg)

Autopilot comes in annual plans and is billed once yearly.

### Do I need to get Autopilot for both sides of the bed? ![expand](https://res.cloudinary.com/eightsleep/image/upload/chevron-down.svg)
