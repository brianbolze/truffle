# What Is Semaglutide?

Semaglutide is a prescription medication that was first approved for weight loss in 2021. It belongs to a class of drugs called [glucagon-like peptide-1 receptor agonists](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/treatments/13901-glp-1-agonists) (GLP-1 RAs). It and similar drugs started out as medications to treat type 2 Diabetes. In the course of using semaglutide for diabetes management, many patients found that it not only helped to better control their diabetes, but they also experienced weight loss.

Clinical trials were started to investigate semaglutide as a specific weight-loss drug. Those trials demonstrated that individuals using semaglutide in conjunction with a reduced-calorie diet and increased physical activity experienced significant weight loss. On average, participants in these trials achieved a weight loss of up to 15% of their body weight, which is considered a noteworthy result.

As a result of these trials, semaglutide became the first drug of its kind to be approved for [obesity.](https://www.kingsbergmedical.com/blog/new-study-finds-that-stopping-growth-hormone-could-lead-to-obesity/)
