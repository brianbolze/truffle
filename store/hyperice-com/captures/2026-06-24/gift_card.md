Can I expense the Hypervolt Plus to my FSA or HSA account?




As a massage therapy device, the Hypervolt Plus is eligible for FSA/HSA reimbursement with a Letter of Medical Necessity (LMN).

What this means is you’ll need a letter written by your doctor verifying that the device you are buying with your FSA/HSA is for a diagnosis, treatment, or prevention of a medical condition.

Once you’ve obtained a LMN, submit your claim to FSA (typically through your employer). You should receive reimbursement for your costs within 10-14 days.


What is the difference between the Hypervolt Go, Hypervolt 2 and the Hypervolt 2 Pro?




For those in need of some serious relief, the **Hypervolt 2 Pro** is our strongest percussion device, offering deep-penetrating relief to stiff muscles, so you can train harder and recover faster. The Hypervolt 2 combines the pressure of a 90 Watt motor and 5 different speed settings to help you warm up and recover like the pros do.

The **Hypervolt 2** is better than ever with an improved ergonomic design, reduced weight (1.8 lbs), and three speeds of powerful percussion to help you get back to doing what you do, even better. Maintain your flexibility and range of motion with its brushless 60 Watt high-torque motor while following along to our step-by-step expert guidance through the Bluetooth® connected Hyperice App.

If you're constantly on the move, the **Hypervolt Go** was designed to move with you. It gives you portable percussion massage in a perfectly light and powerfully small package so you can take it to the gym, on the road, and everywhere in between. At only 1.5 pounds, the Hypervolt Go still boasts 2.5+ hours of battery life per charge, three massage speeds and comes with two adjustable attachments - it's truly fit for your next adventure.


Can I use your products if I'm injured, pregnant or recently had surgery?




We want you to be as safe and healthy as possible with your Hypervolt. We highly recommend consulting with your physician if you have any questions or concerns about using your device.
