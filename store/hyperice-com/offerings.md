## Portfolio overview

Six product lines organized by **therapy modality**, plus bundles and a deep accessory/parts tail. Everything is one-time hardware with a **published** price on the collection card — no gated/quiz pricing anywhere, the simplest visibility profile a roster can have.

The lines map cleanly to a "shop by technology" axis the site itself uses: **percussion** (Hypervolt), **dynamic air compression** (Normatec), **heat + vibration** (Venom), **contrast** (Hyperice X), **vibration** (Vyper/Hypersphere), and the **Nike co-brand** (Hyperboot, heat + Normatec compression in a wearable boot).

Prominence read:
- **Hypervolt 3 Pro ($349) and Normatec Elite** — `[HIGH]`: both lead the homepage ("Shop top picks", "Accelerate your recovery time") and Hypervolt is the top "Popular search."
- **Venom 2 line** — `[MED]`: featured as "Courtside essentials" with a dedicated heat/vibration push.
- **Hyperice X 2 / Hyperboot** — `[MED]`: newest-tech framing ("Restart stronger", co-brand hero) but fewer repeat placements.
- **Normatec** is the price ceiling (Full Body $1,549; bundles to $1,548); **Vyper/Hypersphere** the entry floor ($109–$209).
