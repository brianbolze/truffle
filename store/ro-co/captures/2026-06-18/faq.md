# FAQ

For any additional questions, please reach out to care@ro.co

## Common questions

### Who is Ro for?

Ro is for adults who are experiencing health issues or want to improve or support their health and prefer to do so from the comfort of their home.

### Is my information safe?

We take your privacy and the security of your information very seriously. We’ve designed the Ro platform to comply with all relevant privacy laws and have implemented strict security protocols to protect your information.

Please read our [Privacy Policy](https://ro.co/privacy-policy/) for more details.

### What is Ro?

Ro is the healthcare company that powers Roman. We are a direct-to-consumer telehealth company that handles everything from diagnosis to the convenient delivery of medication. [Learn more about Ro](http://ro.co/).

### What if I need help signing in to my account?

We have some options here for help with accessing your account: [I need help signing in](https://ro.co/contact-us/trouble-signing-in/).
