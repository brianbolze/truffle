# Powering quality care at scale

The Ro Operating System (ro.OS) vertically integrates the core parts of healthcare, bringing together nationwide telehealth, lab, and pharmacy services on one platform. The result? ro.OS makes it easier for patients to access and providers to deliver high-quality care – millions of times over.

Discover the ro.OS apps and capabilities that power every step of the healthcare journey.

Powered by ro.OS

## Patient App

The Patient App is a single stop for patients to message providers, report a side effect, track progress, access medical records, and manage goals.

[Learn more](https://ro.co/os/patient/)

Powered by ro.OS

## Care Delivery App

The Care Delivery App brings together a patient-centric electronic medical record (EMR), quality and safety features, and care management tools to help providers focus on what they do best: deliver high-quality care.

[Learn more](https://ro.co/os/care-delivery/)

Powered by ro.OS

## Pharmacy App

The Pharmacy App integrates pharmacists directly into a patient’s care journey so that they can get real-time patient insights, proactively coordinate care, and offer fast and reliable nationwide medication fulfillment.

[Learn more](https://ro.co/os/pharmacy/)

Powered by ro.OS

## Lab App

The lab application makes lab testing convenient for patients and providers by combining proprietary at-home options with thousands of testing locations and automated results delivery.

## Frequently asked questions

### Who can use and benefit from ro.OS?

ro.OS is exclusively available to Ro’s patients as well as Ro-affiliated providers, pharmacists, and care teams who use it each day to access and deliver high-quality care. It is not currently available to any other telehealth or digital health companies.

If you’re interested in learning more about the products and services available through ro.OS, please visit [Ro.co](https://ro.co/)

### Who built ro.OS?

ro.OS was made possible by the unique knowledge and years long dedication of Ro’s team. Ro brings together in-house experts spanning engineering, data science, product design, privacy and security, and of course, experienced clinical leaders with deep backgrounds in care delivery, quality improvement, clinical research, pharmacy, and lab operations.

In building ro.OS, our team continually sought and integrated patient feedback, insights from Ro-affiliated providers, as well as input from Ro’s network of [renowned medical advisors](https://ro.co/advisors/).

### How can I contribute to building ro.OS?

Ro is always looking for talented individuals – from engineers to nurses, designers to data scientists, and pharmacists to marketers – to join our team. If you’re interested in exploring roles with Ro, visit our [Careers page](https://ro.co/careers/).

### How can I learn more about ro.OS?

To learn more about ro.OS and keep up with the latest news and announcements from Ro, read our [Blog](https://ro.co/blog/articles/) and visit our [Press page](https://ro.co/press/).
