## Your timeline to treatment

From personalized treatment to insurance support, we help you stay on track to reach your goals.

## 1\. Start your online visit

Answer questions about your medical history, lifestyle, and goals and a provider will determine if you're eligible. If you don't qualify for treatment, you won't be charged the ongoing membership fee.

## 2\. Get your GLP-1 prescription

A provider will review your info and prescribe the right GLP-1 treatment for you, if eligible.

Then, get ready for your first dose in 1-2 weeks if paying cash, or 2-3 weeks if using insurance.

The cost of treatment varies by medication and insurance coverage and is billed separately from your membership.

## 3\. Let us handle insurance

If you're using insurance, Ro navigates the process on your behalf. We'll see if coverage is available for your GLP-1 medication and handle all paperwork.

If you're covered, a prescription is sent to your pharmacy. If you require prior authorization, Ro's insurance concierge fights for coverage and submits all paperwork on your behalf. If you’re not covered, your provider will suggest alternative FDA-approved cash pay GLP-1 options. This process takes about 2-3 weeks.

## 4\. Start your treatment

Depending on what medication is prescribed and if you're paying cash or using insurance, you may pick it up at your pharmacy or have it delivered to your door.

## 5\. Get support to stay on track

We’re with you at every step to maximize success of your treatment and navigate the unexpected. From dose titration, side effect management, insurance coverage changes, and unlimited provider messaging—we got you.

## Weight loss FAQs

### How much does the Ro Body membership cost?

The Ro Body membership costs $39 for the first month and as low as $74/month when you prepay for an annual plan. If you stay on a monthly plan, the ongoing cost is $149/month.

Please note that the cost of GLP-1 medication is not included in the membership cost. Medication cost will depend on your treatment and insurance coverage.

### Can I use my insurance through Ro?

Insurance coverage for GLP-1 medications depends on your specific insurance plan and the medication prescribed. Some plans cover GLP-1s, while others may require prior authorization or have additional requirements before approving coverage. At this time, only the Wegovy pen, Zepbound autoinjector pen, and Ozempic are available for insurance coverage through Ro.

When you get started on Ro, we’ll check your insurance coverage and help guide you through the process. If prior authorization is needed, Ro’s insurance concierge can handle the paperwork and work with your insurance company on your behalf.

Even if your insurance doesn’t cover treatment, your provider can still help you explore other FDA-approved cash-pay options that fit your goals and budget.

### What if my insurance doesn’t cover the medication?

If for some reason your insurance will not cover the cost of your medication, you’ll have the option to either select from cash-pay medication options or cancel your Ro Body membership.

### What can I expect after I sign up for the Ro Body membership?

After you complete your [online visit](https://start.ro.co/fdalgd/online-visit/10?referrer=https%3A%2F%2Fwww.google.com%2F&referring_domain=www.google.com), a Ro-affiliated healthcare provider will review your answers and determine whether treatment may be right for you. Depending on your health review, your provider may ask you to take a metabolic lab test. If you’re eligible for treatment, your provider will discuss GLP-1 medication options and recommend a personalized treatment plan.

### I want to lose weight on Ro—is the process completely online?

Yes—most of the Ro Body care can happen completely online. Your online visit, provider communication, and ongoing messaging can all be done from the comfort of home. Depending on how you pay for GLP-1 medication, you may have to go to your local pharmacy to pick up your prescription.
