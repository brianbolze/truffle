## Weight loss FAQs

### What is included in the Ro Body membership?

The Ro Body membership includes access to GLP-1 medication, a personalized treatment plan, dedicated insurance concierge, ongoing provider support with 1:1 messaging, regular check-ins, coaching, and lab testing, if necessary. The cost of medication is not included in the Ro Body membership fee.

### What is Prepay & Save through Ro?

Prepay & Save is a subscription savings program that lets eligible patients pay upfront for multiple months of membership or certain GLP-1 medication at a lower monthly cost.

### How much can I save by prepaying for medication?

Savings can vary depending on your treatment plan and medication. If eligible, you can save $150/mo on a 12-month plan of the Wegovy pen.

### How much can I save by prepaying for membership?

Ro Body membership costs $39 for the first month and as low as $74/month when you prepay for an annual plan. This adds up to $900/year in savings on Ro.

### How much does the Wegovy pill cost?

The Wegovy pill is only available as a cash-pay option. For a limited time, new patients can start on the lower doses of Wegovy pill for $149/mo. The price of Wegovy increases for higher doses, but Prepay & Save on Ro unlocks even more savings.
