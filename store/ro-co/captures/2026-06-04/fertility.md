## Our Fertility Philosophy

### Power in info

Understanding your fertility now gives you more options later.
Healthcare can often feel reactive, and fertility is no different. But “just wait and see” isn’t good enough. As the fertility experts at Ro, we help you find power in being proactive. No matter where you are in your fertility journey, we meet you where you’re at and give you the info you need to plan ahead and build toward the future you want.

### Access for all

Everyone deserves unbiased, clinically sound reproductive health info. We validated the [fertility hormone test](https://ro.co/testing/fertility-hormone-test/ "Modern Fertility Hormone Test") with clinical studies and every sample is tested in a CLIA and CAP accredited lab. We also offer free virtual events with fertility experts and medically reviewed articles you can trust. With Modern Fertility joining Ro, we are now also able to offer more clinical experts and reproductive health services to support you and your health journey.

### Personalized results

We offer [in-depth reports](https://assets.ctfassets.net/jj2wf7627pjc/31rSL6xS0eBE0E7uiIZhFS/15410b88a6ee8da04425954d76f10dcf/Sample_Fertility_Report.pdf) tailored for your hormone levels and goals, [an app](https://ro.co/modern-fertility/cycle-tracking-app/) where you can get personalized predictions for your cycle and fertile window, and a [free virtual community](https://ro.co/fertility/community/join/) where you can meet people like you.

We’ll meet you where you are — because everyone’s reproductive journey is different.

### Better fertility research

We’re on a mission to uncover the “why” behind infertility.

1. #### Today, research is reactive



Most fertility data comes from infertility clinics. That means we have little insight into fertility health earlier in life.

2. #### There’s a gender gap



In 2019, there were only 178 studies dedicated to female infertility. For comparison, there were 1,003 studies about erectile dysfunction.

3. #### There isn’t enough focus on fertility



We’re seeing more funding in women’s health at large, but fertility research is lagging behind.
