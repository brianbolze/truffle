## Frequently asked questions

### What is included in Superhuman Enterprise?

Superhuman Enterprise includes access to Grammarly's AI-powered writing assistance, Coda's collaborative workspace platform, and Superhuman Mail's productivity-focused email client.

This suite is designed to enhance team communication, streamline workflows, and boost productivity across your organization, with new capabilities and integrations added regularly.

### Is Superhuman SOC 2 certified?

Yes, all Superhuman products meet enterprise-grade security standards and have achieved SOC 2 Type 2 compliance.

The bundle includes comprehensive security features such as granular data access controls for Coda; audit events and logging for compliance requirements across Grammarly, Coda, and Superhuman Mail; legal hold capabilities; and robust data encryption both in transit and at rest.

### Does Superhuman support SAML SSO and SCIM provisioning?

Yes, Superhuman Enterprise fully supports enterprise identity management through SAML-based Single Sign-On (SSO) integration with major identity providers.

The bundle also includes SCIM (System for Cross-domain Identity Management) support for automated user provisioning and deprovisioning, making it easy to manage user access at scale and maintain security compliance across your organization.

### Where is Superhuman data stored, and is it encrypted?

Superhuman Enterprise data is stored and processed in secure, SOC 2 Type 2–compliant data centers located in the United States.

All data transmission is encrypted in transit and at rest if it is stored, with granular access controls and audit logging to ensure enterprise-grade security and compliance.

### Can Superhuman Enterprise integrate with tools we already use?

Yes, Superhuman Enterprise integrates seamlessly with your existing tech stack. Grammarly works across all major browsers and applications, Coda connects with 600+ tools including Slack, Google Workspace, and Salesforce, and Superhuman Mail integrates with Gmail and Outlook.

The bundle also supports SCIM for user provisioning and SAML for single sign-on.

### Does Superhuman Enterprise work across devices?

Yes, the Superhuman bundle is designed for seamless cross-device productivity.

Grammarly is designed to work wherever there is a text box on mobile, desktop, and browser clients to provide consistent writing assistance wherever you work.

Coda offers full functionality through web browsers and dedicated mobile apps (iOS and Android), ensuring your collaborative workspaces are accessible on any device.

Superhuman Mail supports macOS, Windows, iOS, and Android operating systems, with your email experience synchronized across all platforms.

All your settings, preferences, and data sync automatically across devices, so you can start work on your laptop, continue on your phone, and finish on your tablet without missing a beat.

### How is pricing structured for Superhuman Enterprise?

Superhuman Enterprise offers significant cost savings compared to purchasing individual licenses.

Pricing is based on a per-user, annual-subscription model with volume discounts available for larger deployments.

### What kind of support do Enterprise customers receive?

Enterprise customers receive our highest level of support, including priority support channels, assigned Customer Success managers for personalized guidance, strategic success planning sessions, quarterly value reviews to track ROI and optimization opportunities, guided onboarding assistance, private webinars and training sessions, and comprehensive Help Center access.

### Does Superhuman offer plans for universities and academic institutions?

Yes! We take pride in our partnerships with a wide range of schools, colleges, and universities to support student learning in the AI era and to modernize how academic institutions drive measurable success. [Explore how we support education](https://superhuman.com/edu).

### Is Superhuman GDPR, CCPA, and FERPA compliant?

Yes. We maintain safeguards and processes designed to support compliance with GDPR, CCPA, and FERPA requirements. These efforts are part of our broader privacy program, which is designed to protect customer data and support global privacy and security obligations.
