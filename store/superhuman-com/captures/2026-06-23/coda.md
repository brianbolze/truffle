## Frequently asked questions

### What is Coda?

Coda is a collaborative workspace that combines documents, spreadsheets, databases, and workflows. It empowers teams to centralize their work and build powerful, customizable tools without needing to code.

### How is Coda different from traditional docs and spreadsheets?

Unlike traditional docs, Coda is interactive and dynamic. You can embed databases, add interactive buttons, automate tasks, and connect to external tools, making your documents act more like apps.

### How much does Coda cost?

Coda core features are available for free and there are tiered paid plans that scale with your team’s needs. Find more information about pricing [here](https://www.superhuman.com/plans).

### Who uses Coda?

Coda is designed for teams of all sizes—from startups to Fortune 500 companies. It’s especially popular with product managers, operations teams, marketers, and engineers who want flexible tools tailored to their workflows.

### Do I need technical skills to use Coda?

No coding knowledge is required. Coda uses simple, intuitive building blocks like tables, formulas, and Packs (integrations) so anyone can build powerful docs.

### Can Coda replace my project management tool?

Yes—many teams use Coda as their project hub. You can create roadmaps, assign tasks, track progress, and even automate reminders, all within one customizable doc.

### How does Coda support collaboration?

Coda lets teams co-edit in real time, leave comments, and create interactive dashboards. With granular permissions, you can control who views or edits specific parts of a doc.

### Does Coda work with my existing tools?

Yes. Coda integrates with dozens of popular tools via Packs and APIs, so your workflow remains connected. You can also embed content like Figma files, YouTube videos, or live dashboards directly into docs.

### Is my data secure with Coda?

Coda offers enterprise-grade security with SOC 2 compliance, SSO (single sign-on), granular access controls, and encrypted data to keep your information safe.
