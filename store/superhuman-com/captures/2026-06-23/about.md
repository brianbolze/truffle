## Over 15 years in the making

Superhuman’s story spans over 15 years of building trusted, human-centered AI. What began as three innovative products has grown into a suite designed to help people write, plan, and communicate with clarity and confidence.

![Max Lytvyn, Alex Shevchenko, and Dmytro Lider](https://superhumanstatic.com/vwfkeyj6n9ac/30OAF7R2eoK4fueEYYXKQ2/47164b796a8168776a33b03085a1598b/alt__3_.png?w=810)

2009: Grammarly is founded

Grammarly is founded by Max Lytvyn, Alex Shevchenko, and Dmytro Lider with one belief: that the right words, delivered with confidence, can improve lives.

[Learn more about Grammarly](https://superhuman.com/products/grammarly)

![Alex DeNeui and Shishir Mehrotra](https://superhumanstatic.com/vwfkeyj6n9ac/piepYTrtE8CqJDx9Do0Bk/dacee9c98f720b3642b44bf2aaa46a13/Gmail.png?w=810)

2014: Coda is founded

Coda is founded by Shishir Mehrotra and Alex DeNeui to reimagine what a document can do—bringing words, data, and action together in one focused place.

[Learn more about Coda](https://superhuman.com/products/coda)

![Rahul Vohra](https://superhumanstatic.com/vwfkeyj6n9ac/5xHtzti3YLh54pwCnjdk18/1cdcc50aa58421c0a101dbb9e7a0d84c/Gmail__1_.png?w=810)

2014: Mail is founded

Superhuman Mail is founded by Rahul Vohra for people who refuse to let their inbox slow them down. Faster, smarter, and built for clarity.

[Learn more about Superhuman Mail](https://superhuman.com/products/mail)

![Coda Project tracker product example](https://superhumanstatic.com/vwfkeyj6n9ac/5yfOVe4oI9YSS9pvUz2E5o/8e57637d651730b3a52effaceb692ce7/mail-fallback.png?w=810)

2019: Built for modern teams

Coda introduces deep integrations across the tools that teams already rely on—Slack, Jira, Salesforce, and more—becoming the place where work comes together and stays in sync.

![AI Chat product exampleA](https://superhumanstatic.com/vwfkeyj6n9ac/32uluvbac2j2EguAqLufb8/bc62655b39fcc3281448df71fa267704/Gmail__2_.png?w=810)

2023: Expanded AI capabilities

Grammarly introduces generative AI, evolving from a writing assistant into a broader partner for clearer thinking and stronger communication across 40 million users.

![Superhuman logo](https://superhumanstatic.com/vwfkeyj6n9ac/13ZpOgBSGRRphYRJ6VW3Dk/c0f95ca0d0cadf7f4db1ec17bae377e8/Group_2147256537__1_.png?w=617)

2025: Becoming Superhuman

Grammarly acquires Coda and Superhuman Mail, launching Superhuman as the unified brand. Superhuman Go launches as an AI assistant that works everywhere you do.

[Learn about our unified brand](https://www.grammarly.com/blog/company/introducing-new-superhuman/)
