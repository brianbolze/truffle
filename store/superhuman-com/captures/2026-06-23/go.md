## Frequently asked questions

### What is Superhuman Go?

Go is the AI assistant that works everywhere you work and helps before you even ask.

### How is Superhuman Go different from other AI assistants?

Superhuman Go is the assistant that figures out what you need, when and where you need it. Go:

- Works wherever you work, understanding and collaborating on what you’re working on.

- Suggests edits, searches for context, and proposes next steps—all without you having to ask.

- Connects to all of your most important apps to give helpful suggestions without manually providing context.

- Orchestrates a team of AI agents to provide suggestions when and where they’re useful.


### What does Superhuman Go cost?

You can get started with Superhuman Go for free by signing up for a Superhuman account. It’s included in all Superhuman plans. Visit our [pricing page](https://superhuman.com/plans) to compare plans and find the option that best fits the way you work—whether you’re getting started on your own or for your team.

### Where does Superhuman Go work?

Superhuman Go is available via the Superhuman Go for Chrome and Superhuman Go for Edge extensions. With a browser extension, you can use Go across the web-based tools you already rely on, including email, documents, customer systems, project tools, and internal apps. The Superhuman Go desktop app for Mac and Windows is coming soon.

### Does Superhuman Go require me to switch tools or platforms?

Not at all. Superhuman Go is designed tofit your flow, not disrupt it. Go works within the tools and platforms you already use, quietly enhancing your experience rather than replacing it.

### What kind of help does Superhuman Go provide?

Superhuman Go can:

- Help you sound clear and confident, everywhere you write

- Schedule meetings from any app

- Give you perfect memory that keeps you prepared

- Handle admin work so you can work on what only you can do


### What are “agents” in Superhuman Go?

Agents are your team of AI collaborators, built for specific workflows—like summarizing long threads, drafting reports, or finding information across tools. Superhuman Go comes with a powerful set of default agents and lets developers build custom ones tailored to your team’s needs.

### Can I build my own agents?

Yes! The Superhuman Agents SDK is in closed beta, giving developers the ability to build custom agents that plug into your organization’s unique tools, systems, and workflows. You can request early access directly from the landing page.

### Is my data secure with Superhuman Go?

Superhuman Go is built with privacy at its core. For Business and Education users, AI training is off by default, and you’re always in control of your data. Go is designed to keep your information private, secure, and yours.

### How do I get started with Superhuman Go?

You can start using Superhuman Go for free today by installing Grammarly for Chrome or Grammarly for Edge. Once installed, you’ll get access to Superhuman Go’s features anywhere you work on the web.

### Is Superhuman GDPR, CCPA, and FERPA compliant?

Yes. We maintain safeguards and processes designed to support compliance with GDPR, CCPA, and FERPA requirements. These efforts are part of our broader privacy program, which is designed to protect customer data and support global privacy and security obligations.
