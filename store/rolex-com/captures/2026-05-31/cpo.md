Second-hand, new life

Because they are built to last, Rolex watches often live several lives. With the Rolex Certified Pre-Owned programme, we offer the opportunity to purchase previously worn timepieces that are certified as authentic, fully serviced and guaranteed by the brand. No matter their past lives, our watches are equipped to accompany you on a new adventure. Perpetually.

> We would never sell a single timepiece that is not technically faultless and that does not give full and complete satisfaction.

Hans Wilsdorf

## Certified authenticity

Our RCPO programme vouches for the authenticity of second-hand Rolex watches that are at least two years old and offered for sale by our Official Jewelers displaying the special Rolex Certified Pre-Owned plaque. The experts in our worldwide network meticulously verify each component of every watch trusted in their care. The RCPO programme therefore guarantees that these watches are, without exception, genuinely authentic. It fully demonstrates our perpetual quest for excellence.

![the most reliable calibres ](https://media.rolex.com/image/upload/q_auto:eco/f_auto/c_limit,w_1920/v1724322592/rolexcom/rolex-retailers/rolex-certified-pre-owned-watches/2024-updates/rcpo-rolex-certified-pre-owned-watches-cpo_2403fb_0009)

## Sustainability, at the heart of our commitment

Providing the same care to pre-owned Rolex watches to give them a new life reflects our willing commitment to sustainability. We have always selected the most robust materials and developed the most reliable calibres so that our watches resist wear-and-tear more efficiently. The Rolex Certified Pre-Owned Programme is an extension of this commitment. RCPO watches are fully serviced and, if necessary, their components are replaced using our scrupulously maintained stock of genuine replacement parts. We therefore guarantee that, wearer after wearer, RCPO watches meet the quality and performance criteria inherent to all our products.

[Discover more about sustainability](https://www.rolex.com/en-us/about-rolex/sustainable-development)

> And the end of the story? There is not yet an end in sight!

Hans Wilsdorf

## The Rolex guarantee and service procedure

Starting from their date of purchase, our Rolex Certified Pre-Owned watches benefit from a two-year international guarantee. They are available at our Official Jewelers who have adhered to the programme. All come with a distinct seal and guarantee card that attest to their authenticity and proper functioning. From arrival in-store to final bracelet sizing, acquiring an RCPO watch is the promise of a true Rolex experience.
