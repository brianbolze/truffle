## What everyone asks first.

Charles from New York asks…How much does Heva actually cost?

Plans start at $149/mo for weight loss, $99/mo for hormone optimization, and a one-time $249 for our comprehensive lab panel. No insurance needed, no hidden fees, and you can cancel any time.

Sarah from Austin asks…Does insurance cover my treatment?

We don't bill insurance directly — Heva is a cash-pay practice, which is what keeps your provider's time focused on you instead of paperwork. Most plans are HSA/FSA eligible, and we provide a superbill on request that you can submit for out-of-network reimbursement.

Mike from Denver asks…What's included in each plan?

Depends on which service you start with. Treatment plans (weight loss, hormone) include the medication, any necessary labs, provider review with dose adjustments, and free 2-day shipping. Lab Testing on its own is the panel + your functional health report ($249 one-time). For everything bundled — four labs a year, provider visits, and a personalized treatment plan — that's Concierge Care, our $99/mo all-in tier (medications still billed at their plan rate).

## The rest of the picture.

Who prescribes my treatment?

A licensed clinician on the Heva care team — board-certified in functional or integrative medicine, licensed in all 50 states. You'll see their name and credentials in your member portal.

Is it safe?

All Heva medications are FDA-approved or compounded at FDA-registered, state-licensed pharmacies under your provider's prescription. Lab work is run through CLIA-certified labs. Treatment plans follow established clinical protocols, not generic templates.

How fast will I see results?

Lab turnaround varies by location, but you'll generally see results in your portal within 7 days of your sample arriving. Treatment timelines vary too — most weight-loss patients see meaningful change by week 4–6, hormone optimization typically takes 6–8 weeks for steady-state effect.

What if I want to stop?

Cancel any time from your member portal — no calls, no retention scripts, no fees. Pause for a month, switch treatments, or end the relationship cleanly. Your data and lab results stay yours.
