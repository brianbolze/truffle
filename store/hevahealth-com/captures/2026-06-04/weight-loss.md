Questions

## Everything you need to know.

How does the medication work?

GLP-1 medications mimic a natural hormone that regulates appetite and blood sugar. They help you feel full sooner, reduce cravings, and support your body's metabolism — working with your biology rather than against it.

What's the difference between Semaglutide and Tirzepatide?

Both are GLP-1 receptor agonists used for weight management. Semaglutide targets one hormone pathway (GLP-1). Tirzepatide is dual-action, targeting both GLP-1 and GIP receptors, which some patients find more effective. Your provider can help determine which is right for you.

What is smart titration?

Instead of a fixed dose, your provider gradually adjusts your medication based on how your body responds — minimizing side effects and optimizing your results. Think of it as a personalized dosing curve built just for you.

Are there side effects?

The most common side effects are mild nausea and digestive changes, especially when starting treatment. Smart titration helps minimize these by adjusting your dose gradually. Most patients find side effects decrease significantly within the first few weeks.

How quickly will I see results?

Most patients notice reduced appetite within the first 1–2 weeks. Meaningful weight loss typically begins within the first month, with results building steadily over time. The 6-month plan is designed for sustained, lasting transformation.

Can I cancel anytime?

Month-to-month plans can be canceled anytime with no penalty. 3-month and 6-month plans offer the best per-month value with a commitment period. You can always reach out to your care team with questions about your plan.
