## Directions of Use

1\. Cleanse your face thoroughly and pat dry.
2\. Apply a thin layer of Soothing Cream to areas of concern or the entire face one time daily.
3\. If needed, follow with a moisturizer suitable for sensitive skin.
4\. Always use a broad-spectrum sunscreen during the day, as rosacea-prone skin can be sensitive to sun exposure.
