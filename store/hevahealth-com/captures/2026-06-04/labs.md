## Everything you need to know.

How do I get started?

It's simple — create your account, complete a quick intake, and we'll generate your lab order. Visit a local lab (or book at-home phlebotomy) and your results will be ready within days, delivered with a personalized Functional Health Report.

What will my results tell me?

You'll get a comprehensive picture of your hormones, metabolism, nutrient levels, heart health, and immune function. Your Functional Health Report translates clinical data into clear, actionable insights — not just numbers, but what they mean for you and what to do next.

Who reviews my results?

Every lab result is available to licensed providers within our platform. If you choose Labs + Concierge Care, you'll get a dedicated 45-minute consultation with a functional medicine provider who will walk through your results in detail and build a personalized plan.

Do I need to visit a lab?

You have options. Walk into any partnered lab near you — we'll help you find one. In eligible areas, you can also book at-home phlebotomy so a certified phlebotomist comes to you. Either way, it's a single blood draw.

Is my data secure?

Your privacy is paramount. We're fully HIPAA compliant, your health data is encrypted end-to-end, and we never sell your information. We follow the strictest security protocols in telehealth.

What states is this available in?

Lab testing with a Functional Health Report is available in all 50 states. Labs + Concierge Care (with provider consultation and prescription access) is currently available in Arizona, Colorado, Florida, Iowa, Idaho, Nevada, New York, Washington, and Wyoming — and expanding.
