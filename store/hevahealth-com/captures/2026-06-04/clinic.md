Our Approach

## Treating the why — not just the symptom.

We practice naturopathic, functional, root-cause medicine. That means we look for the underlying mechanism behind a symptom — metabolic, hormonal, inflammatory, environmental — before we reach for a prescription.

Our clinical team works as a full-service concierge practice with access to the complete Heva pharmacy catalog and the full treatment library. There is no menu of three things. Whatever the patient needs, we have the depth and the breadth to deliver it.

What separates Heva from conventional telehealth is simple: depth of care, breadth of treatment access, and a team that treats the whole patient over time — not a transaction.

Protocol Development Center

## Every Heva protocol starts at Dose.

Dose is the origin point for every treatment protocol Heva delivers nationally. Protocols are developed, clinically tested, and refined here at the bedside before they're ever deployed to patients across the country — the credibility bridge between in-person practice and the direct care model the rest of the country experiences.

- Evidence-based methodology refined at the bedside before national rollout
- Continuous outcomes tracking across the full patient population
- Iterative protocol refinement informed by clinical results, not marketing
