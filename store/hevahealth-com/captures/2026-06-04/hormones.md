## Everything you need to know.

What's included in the monthly membership?

Everything. Labs every 90 days, mobile phlebotomy, medications, a provider-reviewed treatment plan, and ongoing chat support. No hidden fees, no surprise charges.

How is this different from concierge care?

This is a streamlined, efficient program — not a high-touch concierge experience. You get clinical oversight and support when you need it, but without unnecessary appointments or delays.

What states is this available in?

Men's programs are available in 45 states. Women's programs with testosterone are available in 30 states, and without testosterone in all 50 states. Check your eligibility when you get started.

How long does it take to start?

Most patients begin treatment within 1–2 weeks of completing their questionnaire and labs.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. There are no long-term contracts. You can pause or cancel your membership at any time.

What if I have questions during treatment?

You have direct access to our care team via chat. No need to schedule appointments — just message us when you need something.
