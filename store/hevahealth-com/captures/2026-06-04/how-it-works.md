Questions

## Your questions, answered honestly.

Is this legit?

Yes. Heva is a telehealth clinic with licensed providers reviewing every prescription, and we partner with licensed pharmacies to prepare and ship your medication. Everything you receive is a prescription written for you by a licensed provider.

Which pharmacies do you use?

We work with Strive Pharmacy and Stryker Compounding Pharmacy — both state-licensed specialty pharmacies, the same kind used by hospitals and major clinics. A licensed pharmacist verifies every prescription before it ships.

Are the providers licensed?

Yes. All providers are licensed clinicians authorized to prescribe in your state. Your intake is reviewed by a named provider, and their credentials are available on request.

What if I have a side effect or bad reaction?

Message your care team directly from the app — urgent concerns are escalated to a provider the same business day. For medical emergencies, call 911 or go to your nearest emergency room first, then let us know so we can adjust your plan.

How fast do I get my medication?

Once a provider approves your prescription, the pharmacy typically prepares and ships within a few business days. You'll get tracking by text and email the moment your package leaves the pharmacy.

Can I cancel any time?

Yes. You can pause, switch treatments, or cancel from your dashboard at any time — no long-term contracts.

Do you take insurance?

Heva doesn't bill insurance directly — you pay us at checkout and your medication ships straight from our pharmacy partners.
