### Explore new features for communication, writing, and focus.

- ![Messages app showing Live Translation](https://www.apple.com/v/apple-intelligence/g/images/overview/communication-gallery/live_translation__ed6nijle9pci_large.jpg)

Turn on Live Translation to automatically translate texts in Messages,[1](https://www.apple.com/apple-intelligence/#footnote-3) display live translated captions in FaceTime, and get spoken translations for calls in the Phone app.[2](https://www.apple.com/apple-intelligence/#footnote-4)

- ![UI for Writing Tools with a text field to enter prompts, buttons for Proofread and Rewrite, different tones of writing voice, and options for summarize, key points, table, and list](https://www.apple.com/v/apple-intelligence/g/images/overview/communication-gallery/writing_tools__b034ggijij0i_large.jpg)

Transform how you communicate using intelligent Writing Tools that can proofread your text, rewrite different versions until the tone and wording are just right, and summarize selected text with a tap. Writing Tools are available nearly everywhere you write, including third-party apps.

- ![Three iPhone devices showing Apple Intelligence: voicemail summary, record and summarize notes and calls, and summarize email](https://www.apple.com/v/apple-intelligence/g/images/overview/communication-gallery/audio_transcriptions__e41ymnw37moi_large.jpg)

Get the short version of lengthy voicemails, record and summarize Notes and calls, and summarize an email in Mail with a tap.

- ![Smart Reply options in the Mail app are shown on an iPhone.](https://www.apple.com/v/apple-intelligence/g/images/overview/communication-gallery/smart_replies__gdgnkzddtwii_large.jpg)

Use a Smart Reply in Mail to quickly draft an email response with all the right details. Apple Intelligence can identify questions you were asked in an email and offer relevant selections to include in your response. With a few taps you’re ready to send a reply with key questions answered.

- ![Notifications list on an iPhone highlights Priority Notifications at the top of the stack](https://www.apple.com/v/apple-intelligence/g/images/overview/communication-gallery/focus_mode__dte8mya32wa6_large.jpg)

Reduce Interruptions is a Focus that highlights notifications that might need immediate attention. Priority notifications appear at the top of the stack and are summarized, so you can see what matters most at a glance.

- ![iPhone shows inbox in Mail app with important messages at the top](https://www.apple.com/v/apple-intelligence/g/images/overview/communication-gallery/priority_messages__c5mdbohy0f8m_large.jpg)

Priority messages in Mail elevate time-sensitive messages to the top of your inbox — like an invitation that has a deadline today or a check-in reminder for your flight this afternoon.


## Images

### Delightful images created just for you.

Create fun, original images with Image Playground right in your apps or its own dedicated app. You can try out styles like animation, illustration, and sketch and discover additional ChatGPT styles. Turn a rough sketch into a related image that complements your notes with Image Wand. Craft a custom memory movie in the Photos app. And make images inspired by your friends using Genmoji.

![iPhone and iPad showing Genmoji creating a rainbow cactus, and Image Wand turning a sketch into a Mughal pavilion.](https://www.apple.com/v/apple-intelligence/g/images/overview/images_hw__cyp0uzvsglua_large.jpg)

### Create expressive images, unique Genmoji, and custom memory movies.

- ![UI of the Clean Up experience shows a highlighted object in the background of a photo selected to remove](https://www.apple.com/v/apple-intelligence/g/images/overview/images-gallery/clean_up__em7dcrgp4aie_large.jpg)

Remove distractions in your photos with the Clean Up tool in the Photos app. Apple Intelligence identifies background objects so you can remove them with a tap and perfect your shot — while staying true to the original image.[3](https://www.apple.com/apple-intelligence/#footnote-5)

- ![A Genmoji created with Apple Intelligence](https://www.apple.com/v/apple-intelligence/g/images/overview/images-gallery/genmoji_gallery__i6nx573uhyuu_large.jpg)

Create your own Genmoji to express yourself in ways that are personal to you. Type a description, or mix your favorite emoji to create something new. For Genmoji inspired by family and friends, choose from options like customizing hairstyle to match your friend’s latest look.

- ![Image Playground screen with image of a photorealistic storks carrying a bundle in its beak, showing options for custom image styles from ChatGPT](https://www.apple.com/v/apple-intelligence/g/images/overview/images-gallery/playground_app__e73uo18n8ma2_large.jpg)

With Image Playground, produce fun, playful images right in the apps like Messages and Freeform, or using the dedicated app. Choose from Animation, Illustration, and Sketch, as well as brand new styles with ChatGPT. For images based on family and friends from your Photos library, customize facial hair, expression, and more.

- ![A hand holding Apple Pencil draws a circle around a sketch in the Notes app on iPad.](https://www.apple.com/v/apple-intelligence/g/images/overview/images-gallery/image_wand__ca2u79jjz5jm_large.jpg)

Image Wand can transform your rough sketch into a related image in the Notes app. Use your finger or Apple Pencil to draw a circle around your sketch, and Image Wand will analyze the content around it to produce a complementary visual. You can even circle an empty space, and Image Wand will use the surrounding context to create a picture.

- ![Cover of a custom new memory based on the description entered in the text field in the Photos app](https://www.apple.com/v/apple-intelligence/g/images/overview/images-gallery/create_memories__ev2qpdz1sn42_large.jpg)

Create a custom memory movie of the story you want to see, right in Photos. Enter a description, and Apple Intelligence finds the best photos and videos that match. It then crafts a storyline with unique chapters based on themes it identifies and arranges your photos into a movie with its own narrative arc.

- ![A grid of photos based on the search prompt Katie with stickers on her face](https://www.apple.com/v/apple-intelligence/g/images/overview/images-gallery/search_photos_videos__ceq1svclxq6a_large.jpg)

Search for photos and videos in the Photos app simply by describing what you’re looking for. Apple Intelligence can even find a particular moment in a video clip that fits your search description and take you right to it.

## Productivity

### Get more done. More effortlessly.

Siri can assist you in everyday moments to get tasks done. You can speak or type to Siri whenever you need it. It can help you learn something new about your Apple products, and can tap into ChatGPT’s broad world knowledge when appropriate. Plus, Apple Intelligence comes to the Shortcuts app to accelerate your workflows.

![Mac showing Type to Siri, and iPad showing a Shortcut built with Apple Intelligence.](https://www.apple.com/v/apple-intelligence/g/images/overview/productivity_hw__cutg5cod4byq_large.jpg)

### Explore a more helpful Siri, motivation during workouts, and tools for work and school.

- ![A text field at the top of keyboard in iPhone says Ask Siri](https://www.apple.com/v/apple-intelligence/g/images/overview/productivity-gallery/type_to_siri__bm5klo1bwghe_large.jpg)

With a double tap on the bottom of your iPhone or iPad screen, you can type to Siri from anywhere in the system when you don’t want to speak out loud.

- ![An iPhone is shown with step-by-step guidelines on how to schedule a text message to send later](https://www.apple.com/v/apple-intelligence/g/images/overview/productivity-gallery/product_knowledge__d8s2ksvnxncm_large.jpg)

Tap into the expansive product knowledge Siri has about your devices’ features and settings. You can ask questions when you’re learning how to do something new on your iPhone, iPad, and Mac, and Siri can give you step-by-step directions in a flash.

- ![A person checks progress on Apple Watch Series 11 Workout app while running](https://www.apple.com/v/apple-intelligence/g/images/overview/productivity-gallery/workout_buddy__bmdji72i9ik2_large.jpg)

Go the extra mile with Workout Buddy and its personalized pep talks in the style and tone of Apple Fitness+ trainers.[5](https://www.apple.com/apple-intelligence/#footnote-7)

- ![Compare notes in Text and Transcription then summarize the differences, with the words ‘Text’ and ‘Transcription’ highlighted in blue as they appear as they would in the Shortcuts app](https://www.apple.com/v/apple-intelligence/g/images/overview/productivity-gallery/shortcuts__d2m9zacit0a6_large.jpg)

Accelerate your workflows by creating powerful Shortcuts using Apple Intelligence models, as well as features like Writing Tools to summarize text and Image Playground to create images.

- ![A notification in the Apple TV+ app reminds you that a contact shared a show recommendation with you](https://www.apple.com/v/apple-intelligence/g/images/overview/productivity-gallery/on_screen_awareness__0rgsyx0t8zm2_large.jpg)

Apple Intelligence empowers Siri with onscreen awareness, so it can understand and take action with things on your screen. If a friend texts you their new address, you can say “Add this address to their contact card,” and Siri will take care of it.

This feature is in development and will be available with a future software update.

- ![Snippets of information like calendar events, photos, and notes shows the many sources Siri can draw from](https://www.apple.com/v/apple-intelligence/g/images/overview/productivity-gallery/personal_knowledge__dt8slkptn3au_large.jpg)

Awareness of your personal context enables Siri to help you in ways that are unique to you. Can’t remember if a friend shared that recipe with you in a note, a text, or an email? Need your passport number while booking a flight? Siri can use its knowledge of the information on your device to help find what you’re looking for, without compromising your privacy.

This feature is in development and will be available with a future software update.

- ![Photos library is shown on an iPhone along with a search description. A second iPhone is open to a single photo favorited based on the search. A third iPhone shows the photo incorporated into a note in the Notes app.](https://www.apple.com/v/apple-intelligence/g/images/overview/productivity-gallery/siri_across_apps__dqyowq3lmkuq_large.jpg)

Seamlessly take action in and across apps with Siri. You can make a request like “Send the email I drafted to April and Lilly” and Siri knows which email you’re referencing and which app it’s in. And Siri can take actions across apps, so after you ask Siri to enhance a photo for you by saying “Make this photo pop,” you can ask Siri to drop it in a specific note in the Notes app — without lifting a finger.

This feature is in development and will be available with a future software update.


## Great powers come with great privacy.

Apple Intelligence is designed to protect your privacy at every step. It’s integrated into the core of your iPhone, iPad, and Mac through on-device processing. So it’s aware of your personal information without collecting your personal information. And with groundbreaking Private Cloud Compute, Apple Intelligence can draw on larger server-based models, running on Apple silicon, to handle more complex requests for you while protecting your privacy.

## Private Cloud Compute

- Your data is never stored
- Used only for your requests
- Verifiable privacy promise

## ChatGPT, seamlessly integrated.

With ChatGPT from OpenAI integrated into Siri, Writing Tools, visual intelligence, Image Playground, and Shortcuts, you get even more expertise when it might be helpful for you — no need to jump between tools.

You control when ChatGPT is used and will be asked before any of your information is shared. Anyone can access ChatGPT for free, without creating an account. ChatGPT subscribers can connect accounts to access paid features within these experiences.

## New possibilities for your favorite apps.

The Foundation Models framework, along with App Intents, APIs, and frameworks, are built with privacy at the center. Any app can tap into the on-device models that power Apple Intelligence, and the features you build work offline. And it’s all at no cost per request. App developers can easily integrate features like Siri, Writing Tools, and Image Playground.
