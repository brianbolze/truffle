## Frequently Asked Questions

The most common questions about Snowflake's AI features, including Cortex AI, Snowflake ML, model types and governance are answered here.

What models are available in Snowflake?

Snowflake offers a variety of AI models through Cortex AI and Snowpark ML. This includes Snowflake's own Arctic LLM, leading third-party LLMs (from Meta, Anthropic, Mistral, OpenAI, etc.), task-specific models (e.g., for translation, summarization,sentiment, document analysis), and capabilities to train and deploy traditional predictive ML models. Fine-tuning is also available for select models.

How does pricing work for your AI features?

Pricing for Snowflake's AI features, including those within Cortex AI and Snowpark ML, generally follows Snowflake's consumption-based model. Visit the official [Snowflake Pricing Page](https://www.snowflake.com/en/pricing-options/) and the [Snowflake service consumption table](https://www.snowflake.com/legal-files/CreditConsumptionTable.pdf) for the most detailed and up-to-date information on credit consumption.

Where can I learn about how customers are using Snowflake’s AI features in production?

You can visit our [Customer Stories](https://www.snowflake.com/en/customers/all-customers/?tags=product%2Fai) page or [Snowflake Blog](https://www.snowflake.com/en/blog/) to read about real-world AI and ML use cases implemented by customers across various industries. [Download our customer ebook for more examples](https://www.snowflake.com/en/the-secrets-of-gen-ai-success-customer-book/?utm_source=sales&utm_medium=internalpromo&utm_campaign=na--en-&utm_content=sales-qbr-slides).

Do you offer capabilities in both gen AI and predictive ML?

Yes, Snowflake supports both. Cortex AI is a comprehensive platform that enables businesses to analyze [unstructured data](https://www.snowflake.com/en/product/use-cases/unstructured-data-analytics/), build [conversational agentic applications](https://www.snowflake.com/en/product/use-cases/data-agents/), and industry-leading large language models directly within Snowflake’s secure perimeter. Cortex AI enables users of all technical levels to gain real-time, trusted AI-driven insights easily and efficiently. [Snowflake ML](https://www.snowflake.com/en/product/features/end-to-end-ml-workflows/) enables you to build, train and deploy traditional predictive ML models for forecasting, classification and more.

What are the governance capabilities to ensure data privacy for my gen AI use cases?

Snowflake offers robust governance via [Snowflake Horizon Catalog](https://www.snowflake.com/en/product/features/horizon/), including Role-Based Access Control (RBAC), data masking, row-access policies, object tagging and audit logs. Features like [Cortex Guard](https://docs.snowflake.com/en/user-guide/snowflake-cortex/llm-functions#label-cortex-llm-complete-cortex-guard) help filter model outputs. These help your data remain [secure and private](https://www.snowflake.com/en/legal/compliance/snowflake-ai-trust-and-safety/) when using Cortex AI.

[Check out our ebook on practical framework for securing AI systems.](https://www.snowflake.com/en/resources/white-paper/snowflake-ai-security-framework/)
