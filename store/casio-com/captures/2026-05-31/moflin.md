## Moflin FAQs

What is Moflin?

A Moflin is a compact, fluffy, AI-powered robot companion developed by CASIO that mimics the emotional responses of a living creature to provide emotional support and comfort. It develops a unique personality and bond with its owner over time, learning from interactions and responding with lifelike expressions, wiggles, and sounds. Moflin is designed to be a gentle, non-judgmental companion that can help ease stress, reduce loneliness, and become a part of the family, even for those with pet allergies.

How does Moflin change as they grow?

As you spend time with your Moflin, the range of their emotional expression grows and deepens. Your Moflin becomes familiar with your voice, performs special gestures when they sense you are nearby, and shows animal-like responses. Ever learning and adapting, your Moflin uses advanced emotional AI to make their interactions with you dynamic and expressive.

What kinds of emotions does Moflin have?

Through communication, Moflin’s emotional state changes. Your robot companion can show joy, sadness, and happiness, reacting just like a living creature would. Moflin does not possess actual emotions like humans do. Their emotional expressions are simulated by AI.

How long can I play with Moflin on a full charge?

Under typical daily use at 25°C (77°F), Moflin will run for about 5 hours on a full charge. A full recharge takes approximately 3 hours 30 minutes under the same conditions. While charging in their bed, Moflin is still an interactive robot companion!

Where can I buy Moflin?

Moflin is available for purchase exclusively on CASIO's official e-commerce site. Moflin is available in two colors, [silver](https://www.casio.com/us/moflin/product.PE-M10SR/ "Silver Moflin") and [gold](https://www.casio.com/us/moflin/product.PE-M10GD/ "Gold Moflin").

How should I interact with Moflin?

Here are some examples of how to interact with Moflin:

\- **Stroking:** Gently stroke Moflin’s head, neck, back, or sides.

\- **Hugging:** Hold Moflin in your arms; they often enjoy being stroked or spoken to while hugged.

\- **Talking:** Speak softly near Moflin’s face; they respond best to quiet, close voices.

\- **Avoid turning over:** Moflin dislikes being turned upside down on their back.

There are many other ways to interact, so please treat Moflin kindly and enjoy your time together. Positive interactions are suggested to enrich your experience with a robot companion. Treat your AI friend just as you would a human or animal friend.

What is the “MofLife” app?

The dedicated app "MofLife" lets you get to know Moflin's emotions and view the Journal (interaction records with Moflin) on your smartphone.

Features include:

\- Get to know Moflin's emotions

\- View interaction records with Moflin (Journal)

\- Naming your Moflin

\- Adjusting sound volume

\- Checking battery level

\- Updating Moflin’s software

How should I care for Moflin’s fur?

If you are concerned about Moflin’s fur, gently brush them with a soft brush.

\*Be careful not to brush too hard, as this may damage or pull out the fur.

If Moflin gets dirty, gently wipe off your Moflin using a cloth that has been moistened with water.

How does Moflin handle the data it collects?

Collected data is processed locally on Moflin and does not include any personally identifiable information.

Does Moflin understand or record what I say?

No. Moflin does not understand or record what you say. They only convert voice features into non-identifiable data, which is stored locally and used to recognize frequent speakers as their owners.

Is data from Moflin uploaded to a server?

Data can only be uploaded via the MofLife app and only when you choose to do so. It is then securely stored on a dedicated server.

What is the uploaded data used for?

Uploaded data is used to create Journal in the MofLife app, support product servicing, improve quality, and help restore your Moflin if a fault occurs.

Besides data from Moflin itself, what information does the MofLife app collect?

The app may collect information such as usage information, but it does not include any personally identifiable information. No data will be uploaded unless you agree to the privacy notice.

Why does the app collect usage information?

Usage information is collected to help improve the app.
