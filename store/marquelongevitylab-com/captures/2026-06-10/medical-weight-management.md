## Common Questions

Are all treatments medically supervised?

Yes. All optimization, injections, hormones, and peptides are prescribed, monitored, and overseen by licensed providers.

Are GLP-1 medications the only medical option for weight loss?

No, our science backed tool kit includes an entire suite of options for weight less depending on the patient, their health and their needs.  Other options such a tesofensine, phentermine, naloxone or other medications have a role in weight management for many patients.
