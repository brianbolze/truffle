What is The Marque Longevity Lab?

The Marque is a precision wellness and longevity clinic integrating advanced diagnostics, aesthetic medicine, hormone therapy, recovery modalities, and performance optimization under one cohesive model.

What makes The Marque different from other wellness clinics?

Our approach is data-driven, medically supervised, and designed to create measurable, long-term change. We combine aesthetics, hormone science, metabolic tools, and recovery therapies in a fully integrated, personalized experience.

Do I need to be a member to receive services?

No. Memberships offer additional value, but all individual services are available à la carte.

How does the initial consultation work?

You’ll complete an intake, review labs, and meet with our clinical team. We evaluate your metabolism, hormones, goals, and lifestyle to create a personalized roadmap **.**

Do you accept insurance?

We do not bill insurance, but HSA/FSA may apply to certain medical services. Itemized receipts are available.

Who is our partnered pharmacy?

Olympia Pharmaceuticals 6700 Conroy Windermere Rd, Suite 155, Orlando, FL 32835, Phone (800) 991-9079

Which states are served via telehealth?

Virginia and Florida

What labs do you typically run?

We test hormones, metabolic markers, inflammation, thyroid function, micronutrients, lipid profile, recovery metrics, and longevity indicators.

How often should I repeat labs?

Diagnostic frequency is tailored to the patient and their goals. Many members repeat labs every 3–4 months to track progress and optimize dosing or protocols; however more or less frequent testing is always dependent on patient goals and the therapies used.

Are all treatments medically supervised?

Yes. All optimization, injections, hormones, and peptides are prescribed, monitored, and overseen by licensed providers.

How does The Marque ensure safety?

We follow medical-grade protocols, require comprehensive screening, and maintain full transparency with ingredients, dosing, and clinical methodology.

Do I need specific symptoms to start hormone therapy?

No. Many clients begin proactively or to address subtle energy, mood, or metabolic issues.  Hormone replacement therapy is appropriate for many more patients than currently using it.  The symptoms of hormone imbalance can be both subtle and develop over time.  Without the opportunity for comprehensive assessment, many people miss the accumulating signs of deficiency or imbalance until they come to us.

Do you use bioidentical hormones?

Yes. We use bioidentical and clinically validated therapies tailored to your biomarkers.

How long does it take to feel the effects of hormone optimization?

Some improvements occur within weeks, while full optimization may take 8–12 weeks depending on your baseline levels.

How does The Marque support metabolic health and weight management?

Once we understand your specific needs and circumstances we leverage medication, lifestyle coaching, nutraceutical supplementation and other treatment modalities to bring your body back to homeostasis.

Do you offer GLP-1 medications like semaglutide or tirzepatide?

Yes — when clinically appropriate and monitored closely.

Is this program only for people trying to lose a lot of weight?

No. Many clients pursue body recomposition, metabolic tuning, or performance enhancement **.** Our focus is on effecting the metabolic syndrome that leads to weight gain and its long term effects.

What are peptides and how do they work?

Peptides are targeted amino acid sequences that influence recovery, metabolism, sleep, repair, inflammation, or cognitive function.  The list of validated approved peptides is ever growing and part of the excitement around this therapy.

How long until I see benefits from peptides?

The results of peptide therapy, like all interventions, are person and peptide specific.  Some show immediate effects while others show their true potential over long term use.  Our staff will help guide you through what to expect for each therapy.

What does NAD+ help with?

NAD+ supports cellular repair, mitochondrial energy, cognitive clarity, recovery, detoxification, and healthy aging.

Do injectables look natural?

Yes. The Marque aesthetic philosophy centers on subtle, balanced enhancement that honors natural expression.

Can I combine multiple aesthetic treatments?

Absolutely. Many protocols blend injectables, skin treatments, and regenerative therapies for stronger results.

What does red light therapy help with?

Cellular repair, inflammation reduction, hormonal balance, skin quality, muscle recovery, and circulation.

What does hyperbaric oxygen therapy help with?

HBOT supports healing, brain clarity, circulation, post-injury recovery, inflammation, and performance **.**

What injections are available?

We offer nutrient, anti-inflammatory, metabolic, and performance-enhancing injections including B12, amino blends, taurine, L-carnitine, glutathione, and more.

Do I need to come weekly as a member?

Not necessarily. Visit frequency depends on your goals — some come weekly, others monthly.

Can I pause my membership?

Yes, we offer flexible pause options for travel or life changes.

Do members get preferred pricing?

Members receive exclusive savings, bundles, priority access, and included services based on their tier.

How soon will I see results?

Some improvements — energy, sleep, recovery — appear quickly. Others — body composition, collagen, metabolism — take longer.

We track everything through biomarkers and progress metrics.

Will someone guide me throughout my program?

Yes. You’ll have ongoing access to your clinical team for adjustments, education, and long-term support.

Do your protocols require that I come into the center for all treatments?

No. After an initial consultation in the center, many clients use a combination of clinic treatments, at-home medications/peptides/supplements, and digital tools.  We build treatment plans that integrate into your everyday life.

Can I combine aesthetics, hormones, weight management, and recovery?

This is the core advantage of The Marque — integrated wellness creates the strongest and most sustainable results **.**
