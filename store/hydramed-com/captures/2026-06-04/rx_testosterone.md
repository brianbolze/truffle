## Testosterone Replacement Therapy FAQs

## What leads to low testosterone levels?

A decline in testosterone levels as men grow older is entirely typical and can arise from diminished testicular activity and everyday habits, including physical activity levels.

## What is testosterone?

Testosterone is a naturally occurring hormone found in both males and females, with males having it in larger quantities. It is generated in the testicles, ovaries, and adrenal glands and originates from cholesterol. While its main role is in shaping the male reproductive organs, it's also influential in features like muscle build-up, body hair, fat distribution, vocal pitch, and libido. Notably, a portion of testosterone in the body is transformed into dihydrotestosterone and estrogen, both of which are vital for men's equilibrium.

## How can I be evaluated for TRT (Testosterone Replacement Therapy)?

TRT is a comprehensive 6-month treatment, and the pathway involves:

- **Health Evaluation:** Once you complete your online health questionnaire, the next step will send you for blood work. It's required that your blood work is done before we set up your initial virtual meeting with HydraMed's physicians.

- **Discovering Your Tailored Plan:** Once we've analyzed your results, a video consultation will be scheduled with a licensed US doctor. This will allow for a thorough discussion of your findings and the formulation of a fitting treatment strategy.

- **Customized Treatment Options:** Your medications will be consistently and confidentially dispatched to your residence every 4 weeks through our FDA-registered pharmacy. This lets you center on recovery, leaving the logistics to us.

- **Continuous Care and Development:** 3 months into the treatment, and subsequently every 6 months, you'll need to undergo additional blood tests to track your improvement. Based on these results, a virtual session will be set up with our specialists to review your progress and make any necessary adjustments to your treatment.
