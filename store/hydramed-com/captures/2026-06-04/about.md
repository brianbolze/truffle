[IV therapy](https://hydramed.com/iv-therapy/) is the administration of vitamins and nutrients directly into the bloodstream via a tiny tube placed into a vein. The IV line is used to administer a personalized combination of nutrients, including [medicines](https://hydramed.com/blog/understanding-medications-in-an-iv-bag-how-they-work-and-why-they-re-used), [vitamins](https://hydramed.com/blog/the-strong-benefits-of-b-complex-vitamins-iv-therapy), [minerals](https://hydramed.com/blog/inside-the-bag-the-role-of-minerals-in-iv-therapy), and [amino acids](https://hydramed.com/blog/amino-my-goodness-the-surprising-benefits-of-amino-acids-in-your-iv-bag), based on the patient's needs and treatment objectives. The nutrients are administered directly into the bloodstream, bypassing the digestive system for fast absorption. [IV therapy](https://hydramed.com/iv-therapy/) is administered by a certified and medically trained professional, and the duration of a single treatment varies from person to person, with the exception of NAD+ IV treatments, which normally take 2 to 4 hours per session.
