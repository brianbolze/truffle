## Why was my medication prepared by a compounding pharmacy, and how is that different from a regular pharmacy?

Compounding pharmacies specialize in creating medications that are customized to fit individual patient needs, unlike regular retail pharmacies that only dispense mass-produced, commercially available medications. Our partner pharmacies are 503A certified, meaning all compounded medications are prepared under the supervision of licensed pharmacists with advanced training in compounding.

These pharmacies strictly follow state board regulations and FDA guidelines, ensuring that every product meets the highest safety and quality standards. Compounded medications are made in FDA-registered facilities, undergo rigorous quality control testing, and include a Certificate of Analysis (COA) to verify their purity and potency.

Some medications offered by HydraMed, like [NAD+](https://hydramed.com/rx/nad/), are only available through compounding because they require a personalized formulation. For medications that do not need compounding, such as Metformin, our pharmacies source them from the same FDA-approved manufacturers and distributors as major retail chains like Walgreens and CVS.

Many compounding pharmacies also function as traditional retail pharmacies, offering both custom formulations and ready-made medications. By partnering with licensed 503A compounding pharmacies, HydraMed ensures that every medication you receive is tailored to your needs, safe, and of the highest quality.
