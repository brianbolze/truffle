#### [Shipping](https://trtnation.com/faqs/\#1775838515250-a0e1f113-5713)

#### How long does it take to receive my medication after my prescription is approved?

Once your prescription is reviewed and approved by a TRT Nation provider, your medication is typically processed and shipped within 1–3 business days. Standard delivery takes 3–5 business days depending on your location, so most patients have their medication in hand within one week of approval.

#### Is my medication shipped discreetly? Will anyone know what's inside the package?

Yes — all orders ship in plain, unmarked packaging with no reference to TRT Nation, hormone therapy, or the medication inside. The return address is that of our pharmacy partner, not a branded medical company. Your privacy is a priority at every step, from consultation to delivery.

#### Does TRT Nation ship to all 50 states?

TRT Nation currently serves patients in the majority of U.S. states. State-by-state telehealth regulations vary, and some treatments may not be available in certain states. During your online intake, your eligibility by state is confirmed automatically before you proceed. We are continuously expanding our coverage.

#### What happens if my package is lost or damaged during shipping?

In the rare event that your order is lost, delayed, or arrives damaged, contact our support team and we will work with the pharmacy to investigate and arrange a replacement shipment. We stand behind every order and will make it right.

#### Do I need to be home to sign for my medication delivery?

Most TRT Nation shipments do not require a signature and can be left at your door. Certain controlled medications may require an adult signature upon delivery per carrier and pharmacy regulations. Your shipping confirmation email will indicate whether a signature is required for your specific order.

Your provider will work with you to adjust your protocol. Hormone optimization is not one-size-fits-all, and ongoing monitoring is built into the TRT Nation model specifically so we can fine-tune your treatment based on how you’re responding.

You’re never left figuring things out alone. TRT Nation patients have direct access to their care team through our secure messaging platform between appointments. Whether you have a question about a side effect, need clarification on your protocol, or want to discuss adjusting your treatment, our team is responsive and available. #### [Labs](https://trtnation.com/faqs/\#1775838634337-b7eae2d5-ba37)

#### Do I need blood work before starting treatment, and how do I get it done?

Yes — labs are required before most treatments to ensure you receive the right protocol and to establish a safe baseline. TRT Nation makes this simple: we order your labs digitally, and you complete the blood draw at a local LabCorp, Quest Diagnostics, or similar lab near you — no doctor’s order needed on your end. Most patients get their draw done within 1–2 days. Results are reviewed by your TRT Nation provider, not by a call center or automated system.

#### What does my lab panel test for, and will I understand my results?

Depending on your treatment, your panel may include total testosterone, estradiol (E2), CBC, PSA, and CMP markers. Your TRT Nation provider will walk you through what each value means in plain language, how it relates to your symptoms, and what it indicates for your treatment plan. You’ll never be handed a lab report without context.

#### How often do I need to get labs done while on treatment?

Ongoing lab monitoring is very important to determine how the patient is responding to the medications and to assess if any changes are needed to maintain each patient’s goals. We require blood work after the first 10 weeks of treatment, 6 months after the first lab appointment, and then once yearly thereafter. Monitoring frequency may vary depending on your treatment type and how your body responds early on.

#### Can I use labs I already have from my doctor?

In many cases, yes. If you’ve had recent labs drawn (typically within 120 days) that include the relevant markers, your TRT Nation provider may be able to use them to expedite your intake. Simply upload your results during the onboarding process or share them with your care team. If your existing labs are incomplete or outdated, we’ll notify you immediately.

#### Is there an additional cost for lab work, or is it included in my program?

Lab costs vary depending on your program. Ordering TRT blood work through TRT Nation is $129. Our Weight loss blood work panel is $129. If you are interested in getting both a TRT + Weight loss panel together the cost is $179. TRT Nation is transparent about all costs upfront, and our care team is happy to will clarify if you have any additional questions.

#### [Do I Qualify](https://trtnation.com/faqs/\#1775838637545-1fdca337-bec8)

#### What are the requirements to qualify for TRT or hormone therapy through TRT Nation?

To qualify for TRT or hormone therapy, you generally need to be an adult male (24+) experiencing symptoms consistent with low testosterone, and have blood work confirming clinically low levels. Beyond labs, our providers consider your full health picture — symptoms, medical history, medications, and goals. Many men who were told they “don’t qualify” by a primary care doctor are successfully treated through TRT Nation because our providers specialize in hormone optimization and use symptom-informed clinical judgment.

#### Can I qualify for weight loss medication if I'm not obese?

Your health is the most important thing to us. You need to meet a specific BMI threshold to be considered for weight loss treatment for safety reasons. Additionally, our providers evaluate your overall metabolic health, weight history, and goals. Patients who are moderately overweight and struggling with appetite, energy, or weight-related health concerns are potentially good candidates for tirzepatide or phentermine.

#### Can I get treatment if I already have a health condition like diabetes, high blood pressure, or heart disease?

Many of our treatments are compatible with — and even beneficial for — patients with common conditions like type 2 diabetes, hypertension, or metabolic syndrome. Tirzepatide, for example, was originally developed as a diabetes medication and has strong cardiovascular benefits. That said, certain conditions (such as active prostate cancer or specific heart conditions) may be contraindications. Your provider reviews your complete medical history to determine what is safe and appropriate for you individually.

#### I'm in my 20s or 30s — is it too early to start hormone or anti-aging therapy?

Not at all. Low testosterone affects men of all ages, and symptoms in your 20s and 30s are more common than most people think — often driven by chronic stress, poor sleep, environmental factors, or genetic predisposition. Anti-aging therapies like NAD+ and sermorelin are also increasingly used by younger, high-performing individuals seeking to maintain peak function. If you have symptoms, your age is not a barrier to treatment.

#### Will pre-existing prescriptions or medications disqualify me?

Not necessarily. Many patients on existing medications are successfully treated by TRT Nation providers. However, certain drug interactions require careful management — for example, nitrates cannot be combined with ED medications like tadalafil or sildenafil. During your intake, you’ll list all current medications so your provider can identify any interactions and determine the safest protocol for you.
