#### [Who is a good candidate for anti-aging therapies?](https://trtnation.com/anti-aging/\#1775830690651-14fa6e86-c366)

Anyone noticing the effects of aging: fatigue, slower recovery, brain fog, reduced motivation, changes in body composition, or declining skin health — may benefit from these therapies. Our providers conduct a full intake to recommend the right protocol for your age, health status, and goals.

#### [How long before I notice anti-aging results?](https://trtnation.com/anti-aging/\#1775830799607-33b54aaa-f8d8)

NAD+ effects can be felt quickly, often within the first treatment or first week.

Sermorelin typically takes 4–8 weeks to show meaningful changes and peaks around the 3–6 month mark.

Glutathione’s antioxidant and skin effects are often noticed within 4–8 weeks of consistent use.

#### [What is NAD+ and why does it matter for aging?](https://trtnation.com/anti-aging/\#1590083317836-5ccee321-fef4)

NAD+ (Nicotinamide Adenine Dinucleotide) is a coenzyme found in every cell of your body. It plays a critical role in energy production, DNA repair, and cellular communication. NAD+ levels naturally decline with age — by your 50s you may have half the NAD+ you had at 20. Supplementing NAD+ via IV or injection can support energy, cognitive function, metabolism, and overall cellular health.

#### [How will I feel after NAD+ therapy?](https://trtnation.com/anti-aging/\#1590083786406-a9a18791-df7f)

Many patients report enhanced mental clarity, better energy, improved mood, and reduced brain fog — sometimes within days of treatment. Results accumulate with consistent use. NAD+ is particularly popular among high-performing professionals and anyone noticing the cognitive effects of aging.

#### [What is Sermorelin and how is it different from HGH?](https://trtnation.com/anti-aging/\#1590084014474-610261cd-3036)

Sermorelin is a growth hormone-releasing hormone (GHRH) analogue. Rather than directly introducing synthetic HGH into your body, Sermorelin signals your pituitary gland to produce and release its own natural growth hormone. This means the response is more physiologic, more regulated by your body’s own feedback systems, and carries a significantly better safety profile than direct HGH therapy.

#### [What benefits can I expect from Sermorelin?](https://trtnation.com/anti-aging/\#1590083851160-2283e1bc-5aee)

Patients typically report improved sleep quality (especially deep sleep), increased lean muscle mass, reduced body fat, better recovery from exercise, improved skin tone, and higher energy levels. Because Sermorelin works gradually and naturally, results build over 3–6 months.

#### [What is Glutathione and why do people use it?](https://trtnation.com/anti-aging/\#1775830617974-93e65a72-cf04)

Glutathione is the body’s master antioxidant, produced naturally in the liver. It neutralizes free radicals, supports immune function, aids in detoxification, and is directly involved in skin health. Levels decline with age, stress, alcohol use, and illness. IV or injectable glutathione replenishes what your body is no longer producing at optimal levels.

#### [Are these treatments safe?](https://trtnation.com/anti-aging/\#1775830756688-1325563c-458c)

Yes. NAD+, Sermorelin, and Glutathione all have strong safety profiles when administered under medical supervision. TRT Nation providers review your health history before prescribing and monitor your response over time.
