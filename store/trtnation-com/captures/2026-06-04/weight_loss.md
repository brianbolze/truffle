#### [Who qualifies for weight loss medications?](https://trtnation.com/weight-loss-therapy/\#1672779131702-9f75dc10-f756)

Eligibility is determined by a healthcare provider and isn’t guaranteed. Typically, candidates include Adults 25+ with a BMI over 25.

#### [What makes medical weight loss different from dieting on my own?](https://trtnation.com/weight-loss-therapy/\#1775831959561-67b78d29-36c0)

Diet and exercise alone work for some people, but for many, hormonal resistance, metabolic dysfunction, and hunger signaling make sustainable weight loss extremely difficult. Medical weight loss uses prescribed and clinically proven medications to work with your biology — not against it — so you can finally see results that last.

#### [What is tirzepatide and how does it work?](https://trtnation.com/weight-loss-therapy/\#1775832029021-e690f52f-19e0)

Tirzepatide is a dual GIP/GLP-1 receptor agonist, meaning it activates two separate hormonal pathways that regulate blood sugar, appetite, and fat storage. It slows gastric emptying, reduces appetite dramatically, and helps your body use energy more efficiently. Clinical trials have shown average weight loss of 15–20%+ of total body weight. It is one of the most effective weight loss medications ever developed.

#### [How is tirzepatide different from semaglutide (Ozempic/Wegovy)?](https://trtnation.com/weight-loss-therapy/\#1766006195776-a9a0f5a8-f6af)

Both are in the GLP-1 class, but tirzepatide activates an additional receptor (GIP) that semaglutide does not. This dual-action mechanism has shown superior weight loss results in head-to-head clinical comparisons. Many patients who plateaued on semaglutide have found renewed progress with tirzepatide.

#### [What is phentermine and who is it best for?](https://trtnation.com/weight-loss-therapy/\#1672840367888-d0042a51-cb6d)

Phentermine is an appetite suppressant that has been used safely for decades. It works by stimulating the central nervous system to reduce hunger. It is best suited for patients who need strong short-to-medium term appetite control and want a more affordable, proven option. TRT Nation providers will assess your health history to confirm it’s right for you.

#### [How much weight can I expect to lose?](https://trtnation.com/weight-loss-therapy/\#1672840548785-2a98a754-2415)

Results vary by individual, but patients on tirzepatide in clinical studies lost an average of 15–22% of their body weight over approximately 72 weeks. Phentermine patients typically see meaningful results in the first 12 weeks. Your provider will set realistic, personalized expectations based on your starting point and goals. [Source](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35658024/)
