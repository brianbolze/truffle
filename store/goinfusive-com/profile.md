## Overview

- **Business:** Supply-chain service and software company for wellness clinics.
- **Core promise:** Streamline vendors, pricing, procurement, inventory, and supply reliability for clinics trying to scale.
- **Audience:** IV therapy, peptide, hormone, functional/integrative, and wellness clinics.
- **Origin story:** Site says the company grew from supply-chain problems at VYVE Wellness beginning in 2021.

## What They Offer

- **Infusive platform:** Unified ordering portal, vendor controls, pharmacy integration, inventory management, reporting, financial insights, and audit documentation. `[on-request]`
- **Core partnership:** Self-service ordering, unified portal access, reporting, proactive support, and standard catalog discounts. `[on-request]`
- **Growth partnership:** Infusive-assisted ordering, custom reporting, shared account management, priority support, preferred discounts, and monthly consulting. `[on-request]`
- **Elite partnership:** Full-service supply-chain management with advanced ordering, inventory, patient-management, reporting, and strategic-partnership support. `[on-request]`
- **Vendor network:** Pharmacy, medical supply, lab, education, oversight, equipment, and financing partners for wellness clinics. `[on-request]`
