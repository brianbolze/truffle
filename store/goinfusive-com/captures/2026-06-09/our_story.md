## Patient Demand Is Growing So Fast And So Is The Complexity Of Ordering Our Supplies.

Behind the scenes, I’m starting to drown in spreadsheets, post-it notes, and constant frustration. I’m managing 10-15 different vendors who couldn’t care less about a small clinic’s supply problems.

![Our Story Line Image](https://goinfusive.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/os-sec3-arrow-opt.webp)

## “Across From Me Sits The Highly Recommended Lawyer Turned Executive Coach Darren Frank”

Darren has a successful consultancy helping a lot of physician entrepreneurs. We discuss my pain points. Though initially skeptical, our meeting sticks with me, and a few days later, I reach out and hire him, ready to follow his lead.

For months, Darren witnesses the common side-effect of success; success is accompanied by increasing operational demands. I’m desperately trying to duct-tape solutions together .

## Then Comes The Turning Point...

![Our Story Group Image](https://goinfusive.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/os-sec5-img-opt.webp)

**During one of our regular business meetings at the Warmack in Charlotte** _(our go-to spot for strategic discussions)_ **I propose something unexpected to Darren:**

**Would you like to build a business that solves the problems we've struggled with?** For other clinic owners in the same shoes as me. If we can build a proper back-end foundation, we can take the power back from the vendors, and give it to the people actually doing the medicine.

![Our Story Line Image](https://goinfusive.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/os-sec5-arrow-opt.webp)

Working together over the next year, we built an epic team that is dedicated to handling the painstaking, inventory management so clinics can focus on patients.

We established direct relationships with vendors, securing backups and allocations for our partners CEOs.
