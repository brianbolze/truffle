No. You maintain ownership of your vendor accounts. Infusive centralizes, manages, and optimizes them so you stay in control while we handle the chaos.

[See how Infusive integrates with your existing systems](https://goinfusive.com/vendor/)

We already have someone managing inventory — won’t this replace them?

Not at all. Infusive empowers your team with better systems, data, and pricing leverage. We _amplify_ what they do, freeing them from busywork and manual processes.

[Learn how we strengthen internal teams](https://goinfusive.com/infusive-software/)

What if you can’t source a specific product I use?

We maintain a **curated catalog** used by hundreds of clinics. If a product becomes unavailable, we proactively identify **safe, consistent alternatives** and notify you before it impacts your business.

How is Infusive different from a buying group or vendor rep?

Buying groups connect you to discounts. Infusive **manages your entire supply chain,** from sourcing and vendor negotiation to fulfillment, analytics, and growth strategy. It’s partnership, not procurement.

On average, Infusive partners see **10–30% savings** across nutrients, peptides, and medical supplies, plus countless hours saved on admin and ordering.

Is this just for big national brands?

Our model supports clinics of all sizes ready to scale, from multi-location operators to emerging franchises. If you’re growth-minded, you’re our kind of partner.
