## Vendor FAQs

### Can I request my preferred vendors?

Yes. You maintain ownership of your vendor accounts. If there’s a supplier you love, we’ll work to bring them into the Infusive network so you can continue using them seamlessly.

### How do vendors qualify to join the Infusive network?

Each partner is vetted by our clinical and operations teams for reliability, quality, and compliance. Only the best make the cut.

### What categories of vendors are included?

Nutrients, peptides, IV solutions, consumables, and compounding pharmacies. We also make new additions based on client needs.

### Do I have to switch my existing vendors?

No. We simply centralize and manage your existing accounts to give you better pricing, stability, and service.
