How does testosterone therapy help women in midlife?

Testosterone supports motivation, mood, and energy while helping maintain lean muscle, bone strength, and healthy metabolism. For many women, therapy restores drive and focus that naturally decline with age.

How soon will I notice results from testosterone cream?

Benefits such as improved motivation, focus, or mood may begin within 2–4 weeks. Noticeable changes in strength, body composition, and sexual wellbeing often take up to 3 months as hormone levels stabilize.

Why is a baseline lab required before receiving treatment?

A baseline lab gives your clinical team the biological information needed to prescribe hormones responsibly and personalize your protocol from the start. Because hormones influence multiple systems in the body, symptoms alone don’t provide enough clarity to determine the most appropriate treatment approach.

How do I use topical testosterone cream?

Apply to clean, dry skin—typically the inner thigh, lower abdomen, or forearm. Rotate sites daily and wash hands afterward. Dosing is personalized, though many women use 0.5 mL per day as prescribed by their clinician.

What are common side effects of testosterone therapy?

Most women tolerate therapy well. Possible side effects include mild acne, slight facial hair growth, or mood changes if overdosed. These are rare and dose-dependent. Regular hormone testing helps ensure optimal levels.

Is testosterone therapy safe for women?

When prescribed and monitored by licensed clinicians, testosterone therapy is considered safe. Healthspan’s program includes regular lab reviews and follow-ups to track hormone response and minimize side effects.

Why is membership required for testosterone cream?

Testosterone cream requires ongoing clinical oversight to be prescribed safely and effectively. At Healthspan, testosterone therapy is delivered through membership because hormone levels, symptoms, and side effects change over time and must be actively monitored.

Membership includes baseline and follow-up lab testing, clinician review, and ongoing support to ensure dosing stays within safe ranges, treatment remains effective, and risks like hormone imbalance or suppression are addressed early. This care model supports long-term health outcomes rather than one-time prescribing.

How much does testosterone cream cost with membership?
