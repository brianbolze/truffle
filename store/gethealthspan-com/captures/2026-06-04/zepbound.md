### Supporting brain function

GLP-1s cross the blood–brain barrier and have been studied for effects on neuroinflammation, mood regulation, and cognitive resilience, with early evidence suggesting neuroprotective potential.

![Protecting cardiovascular health](https://methodical-vitality-96814f361f.media.strapiapp.com/TP_Metabolic_Pathways_Stress_and_Metabolic_Health_8435313a93.png)

### Protecting cardiovascular health

Large outcome trials show reductions in major adverse cardiac events, linked to improved endothelial function, lower inflammation, and favorable lipid and blood pressure changes.

![Supporting kidney function](https://methodical-vitality-96814f361f.media.strapiapp.com/TP_Mitochondrial_Pathways_AMPK_and_m_TOR_Balance_ddc589b03d.png)

### Supporting kidney function

GLP-1s have been associated with reduced albuminuria and slower progression of diabetic kidney disease in some populations.

![Improving liver health](https://methodical-vitality-96814f361f.media.strapiapp.com/TP_Menopause_Pathways_HPO_Axis_Dysregulation_3dca7278a2.png)

### Improving liver health

GLP-1s are well studied in NAFLD and NASH, with evidence showing reductions in liver fat and improvements in insulin sensitivity and liver enzymes.

![Supporting PCOS & fertility](https://methodical-vitality-96814f361f.media.strapiapp.com/TP_Metabolic_Pathways_Insulin_and_Glucose_Regulation_23bb8d7701.png)

### Supporting PCOS & fertility

GLP-1 therapy has been associated with improvements in insulin resistance, ovulatory function, and androgen levels in individuals with PCOS, with emerging evidence for fertility benefits.

![Reducing chronic inflammation](https://methodical-vitality-96814f361f.media.strapiapp.com/TP_Metabolic_Pathways_Mitochondrial_Energy_Production_697e990ec8.png)

### Reducing chronic inflammation

GLP-1 signaling has been linked to lower systemic inflammation, including reductions in CRP and improvements in endothelial and neuroinflammatory markers.

GLP-1s influence fat loss and visceral fat reduction while impacting appetite regulation and metabolic efficiency; preserving lean mass remains a key clinical consideration.

How effective is Zepbound for weight loss?

In clinical trials, patients lost an average of 20% of their body weight when Zepbound was paired with lifestyle changes. Without those changes, real-world results are closer to 10–15%. Even at that level, Zepbound delivers more weight loss than any other GLP-1 medication — and with Healthspan’s coaching to support healthy habits, you can work toward the higher results seen in trials.

How does Zepbound actually work?

Zepbound is the first dual-agonist medication. It activates GLP-1 and GIP pathways to slow digestion, reduce appetite and food cravings, and improve blood sugar balance.

How quickly will I see results?

Many patients notice changes within the first few weeks, though results build over months. Your clinician will monitor progress and adjust dosing as needed.

What are the possible side effects?

Like other GLP-1 medications, Zepbound can cause nausea, vomiting, or digestive changes. Our clinicians help manage dosing and side effects for safety and comfort.
