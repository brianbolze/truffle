Optimize your warm-up and recovery routines with the Hyperboot, a Nike x Hyperice collaboration. The wearable technology offers heat and Normatec dynamic air compression for feet and ankles that you can customize on the go.

#### Advanced Heating and Massage

A system of dual-air Normatec bladders bonded to warming elements evenly distributes heat throughout the upper. It helps drive heat deep into the muscle and tissue in the feet and ankles.

#### Relaxation on the Move

Designed for standing, walking, sitting or traveling, the battery-powered shoes work while your feet relax.

#### Quick Adjustments

Buttons on the shoe or a Bluetooth connection to the Hyperice app let you choose from three levels of heat and Normatec dynamic air compression.
