Nike Membership is access to the very best of Nike through any of our apps, exclusive products, and Member-only experiences. Nike Members also enjoy free shipping on orders of $50 or more, 60-day Wear Test, and receipt-less returns. It’s free and easy to join. [Sign Up.](https://www.nike.com/register)

Am I a Nike Member?

Maybe! If you’ve ever logged into a Nike app, then yes, yes you are. Welcome back! If not, let’s change that. [Sign Up.](https://www.nike.com/register)

Is Nike Membership free?

100% yes. Members enjoy all the benefits of Nike Membership at no cost, because once you’re in, we got you. Zero money gets you access to all of it. [Sign Up.](https://www.nike.com/register)
