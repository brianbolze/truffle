## Common Questions

Check out our FAQs for medically-reviewed information about the conditions we treat, medications we offer, insurance, and more.

- [**What types of treatments does Nurx offer?**](https://www.nurx.com/#)


Nurx providers can recommend and prescribe medically appropriate treatments for a wide range of common health concerns, thanks to our innovative and patient-centered approach to care. From birth control to prescription skincare, from mental health treatment to options for weight management, (and beyond!), we’re proud to offer expert specialty-level care for women across all life phases. There is no one-size-fits-all approach to treating health concerns, especially ones that are complex and often interrelated—so we specialize in treating ‘you’.

- [**Does Nurx accept insurance?**](https://www.nurx.com/#)


Yes, we accept most forms of private health insurance for medications, including:


Aetna Anthem


Blue Cross Blue Shield


Cigna


CVS Caremark


Express Scripts


OptumRx


United Health Care




You can read more about the insurance we accept [here](https://www.nurx.com/faq/does-nurx-accept-health-insurance-for-medication/).





Please note that we do not accept insurance for medications prescribed for anti-aging (fine lines and wrinkles), melasma, eyelash growth, and women’s hair loss treatment, as insurance companies typically consider these non-medically necessary medications that they do not cover.





If you’d like to know if we accept your health insurance plan, please connect with our team at support@nurx.co.

- [**How long does it take for birth control pills to work?**](https://www.nurx.com/#)


The length of time it will take for your [birth control](https://www.nurx.com/birthcontrol/) pills to prevent pregnancy can range from immediately to seven days, depending on the type of contraceptive. After the initial waiting period, birth control pills can be 99% effective at preventing pregnancy when taken exactly as directed and 93% effective with typical use.

- [**What medications do Nurx providers prescribe for anxiety and depression?**](https://www.nurx.com/#)


Your Nurx medical provider will create a personalized treatment plan based on your symptoms and health history. Nurx providers can prescribe a comprehensive selection of mental health treatments including SSRIs, SNRIs, tricyclic antidepressants, bupropion (generic Wellbutrin), trazodone, and medications that may be used to treat panic attacks. Nurx providers prescribe these treatments according to evidence-based clinical guidelines and based on each patient’s individual symptoms and health history.





Please note: Nurx does not provide talk therapy or crisis management.

- [**What type of anti-aging treatment do Nurx providers prescribe?**](https://www.nurx.com/#)


Nurx providers can prescribe a range of dermatologist-trusted skincare treatments to reduce fine lines and wrinkles. After reviewing your health history and the photos you shared, a Nurx medical provider will prescribe the best treatment for your skin. If you have used one of a retinoid cream in the past and would like to use it again, please share that information when you make complete your consultation.

- [**What types of acne treatments do Nurx providers prescribe?**](https://www.nurx.com/#)


Nurx providers can prescribe several different treatments to target and reduce acne. When you complete your consultation, a Nurx provider will review your medical history and the images of your skin you provide to determine what type of acne you have and the best way to treat it. From there, you can check in with your Nurx provider to update your treatment (to a different strength or formula, when medically appropriate) as your skin adjusts. [Get in contact](https://www.nurx.com/acne-treatment/) with a member of our medical team today to learn more about what options are available to you.

- [**Are Nurx’s services available in languages other than English?**](https://www.nurx.com/#)


At this time, we only provide care in English. We are committed to creating an inclusive experience and are exploring options to expand language support in the future.
