## **Methylene Blue: Important Safety Information**

## **1\. Introduction**

### **What is Methylene Blue?**

Methylene Blue (MB), also known as methylthioninium chloride, is a synthetic compound historically used as a diagnostic dye and therapeutic agent. In supplement form, it is being explored for its **potential antioxidant, neuroprotective, and mitochondrial-supportive properties**.

### **Mechanism of Action**

Methylene Blue supports cellular health primarily by **enhancing mitochondrial function**, acting as an alternative electron carrier to bypass impaired complexes in the electron transport chain. It may help **reduce oxidative stress** and support **ATP (energy) production**, especially in the brain and other high-demand tissues.

### **Regulatory Status**

Methylene Blue is **not FDA-approved** as a dietary supplement for any disease or medical condition. Its use in supplement form should be considered **experimental and investigational**. It is available over-the-counter in low doses, often marketed for cognitive support and cellular energy.

**2\. Potential Uses & Research**

### **Mitochondrial Support**

MB may improve mitochondrial efficiency and reduce reactive oxygen species (ROS), making it a candidate for **anti-aging support**, **cellular repair**, and **general vitality**.

### **Cognitive Function**

Studies suggest MB may enhance **memory, focus, and mood**, and is being investigated for use in **Alzheimer’s disease** and other neurodegenerative conditions by reducing beta-amyloid plaques and oxidative stress in the brain.

### **Longevity & Skin Health**

Preliminary data indicate that MB may attenuate cellular aging markers, promote collagen synthesis, and **support youthful skin appearance** through fibroblast rejuvenation.

_Note: These benefits are theoretical and based primarily on preclinical and early-stage human studies. Human evidence remains limited._

## **3\. Contraindications & Precautions**

You should **not use Methylene Blue supplements** if you:

- Have a **known G6PD deficiency** (can cause hemolytic anemia).

- Are **pregnant or breastfeeding**.

- Are currently taking **SSRIs, SNRIs, MAOIs**, or other **serotonergic medications** (risk of serotonin syndrome).

- Have a history of **allergy or hypersensitivity** to methylene blue.


Consult your healthcare provider before use if you have:

- **Liver or kidney disease**
- **Neurological or psychiatric disorders**
- Any condition requiring ongoing medical treatment


## **4\. Possible Side Effects**

Although low-dose oral Methylene Blue is generally well tolerated, possible side effects may include:

### **Mild and Common**

- **Urine or stool discoloration** (blue or greenish tint)

- **Digestive upset** (nausea, abdominal discomfort)

- **Headache or dizziness**

### **Less Common but Serious**

- **Mood changes** or mild agitation

- **Photosensitivity**
- **Allergic reaction** (rash, itching—discontinue use if these occur)


### **Risk of Serotonin Syndrome**

When combined with serotonergic drugs, MB may raise serotonin levels excessively. Avoid concurrent use unless under medical supervision.

_Always begin with the lowest effective dose and monitor your response. Discontinue use and consult a healthcare professional if adverse symptoms occur._

**5\. Administration & Dosage**

### **Formulation**

This product contains **Methylene Blue in capsule form** for oral use only.

### **Suggested Use**

- Follow the **serving size recommended on the label** or as directed by a qualified healthcare professional.

- Do not exceed suggested dosage.

- Take with food or water to minimize GI discomfort.


### **Tolerance & Monitoring**

- Begin with a low dose to assess tolerance.

- Some users may experience dose-dependent effects; individualized adjustment may be appropriate under guidance.


**6\. Warnings & Limitations**

- **For dietary supplement use only**; not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease.

- Should not be used as a substitute for medical treatment.

- Avoid use in children unless recommended by a healthcare professional.

- Do not combine with **serotonergic agents**, especially without supervision.


**7\. Storage & Handling**

- Store in a **cool, dry place**, away from sunlight and heat.

- Keep out of reach of children.

- Do not use if the seal is broken or the product shows signs of contamination.


**8\. Disclaimer**

- These statements have **not been evaluated by the FDA**.

- Individual responses may vary.

- Consult a licensed healthcare provider before starting any new supplement, especially if you are taking medications or managing chronic health conditions.
