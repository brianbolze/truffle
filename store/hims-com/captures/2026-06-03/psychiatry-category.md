#### Got questions? Get answers.

How does the online psychiatric evaluation work?

You complete an intake form in the comfort of your own home and your answers are reviewed by a medical provider. Currently, our telepsychiatry services cover mental health issues related to depression and anxiety disorders. We do not currently provide access to treatment for other mental health conditions, such as post-traumatic stress disorder, eating disorders, ocd or obsessive-compulsive disorder, bipolar disorder, other mental illness, etc.

Do you offer other online mental health services?

In addition to psychiatry services and psychiatric medication, you can use our platform to connect with licensed therapists for online therapy (talk therapy).
