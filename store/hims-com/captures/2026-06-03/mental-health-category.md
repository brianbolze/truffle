#### Got questions?  Get answers.

Are all mental health issues treatable online?

Online therapy platforms, telehealth, telemedicine, and teletherapy are great for some conditions, but not all behavioral health issues or severity levels are appropriate for online treatment. As such, many but not all conditions or situations are treated through our platform (eg, substance abuse, eating disorders, schizophrenia, couples therapy and relationship issues, etc). Find out more by taking a free mental health assessment online. If you do not qualify for online treatment, consider in-person therapy, mental health counseling, psychiatry, psychotherapy, emotional support groups, family therapists, clinical social workers, pride counseling, or other appropriate care options.

Do I need an insurance plan for mental health care through Hims?

We focus on providing exceptional care at an exceptional price and currently do not work with insurance companies or accept health insurance. Pricing is straightforward without the hassle of insurance providers and wondering what your insurance covers. We do not currently accept medicare or medicaid.

What types of mental health treatment does Hims offer?

Our telehealth platform gives access to licensed healthcare providers that offer personalized treatment plans, if prescribed. In addition, we provide a number of free mental health resources: an online mental health assessment tool that measures anxiety and depression symptoms, articles on our blog, and more. Our platform’s privacy and security is consistent with HIPAA standards.

How does getting medication online work?

We work with healthcare providers to create a personalized treatment plan that is appropriate for you. If prescribed, medication will be discreetly and directly sent to you, free of any shipping charges.
