## FAQ

### How many biomarkers are included in each Specialized Panel?

Each Specialized Panel includes a robust set of biomarkers, tailored to the specific area of focus: Heart Health Panel: 81 biomarkers, Performance Health Panel: 90 biomarkers, Metabolic Health Panel: 78 biomarkers, Women’s Health Panel: 81 biomarkers, Men’s Health Panel: 77 biomarkers.

### What additional biomarkers are tested for in Specialized Panels?

Specialized Panels in WHOOP Advanced Labs include additional unique biomarkers tailored to each area of focus, giving you deeper, more targeted insight into your health and performance:

**Heart Health Panel:** LDL Particle Number, LDL Small/Medium, HDL Large, MPO, Lp-PLA2, ADMA/SDMA, Omega panel (EPA, DPA, DHA, ratios), Cystatin C, Uric Acid

**Performance Health Panel:** IGF-1, Creatine Kinase, Omega panel, Reticulocyte Count, Free T3/T4, B12, Folate, Magnesium

**Metabolic Health Panel:** C-Peptide, 1,5-Anhydroglucitol (1,5-AG) , Fructosamine, Leptin, Adiponectin, Zinc, Selenium, Iodine, Copper, Free T3/T4, GGT

**Women’s Health Panel:** AMH, Progesterone, Prolactin, TPOAb, Free T3/T4, Leptin, B12, Folate, Magnesium, Phosphorus

**Men's Health Panel:** PSA (Free, Total, % Free), DHT, Prolactin, Leptin, Uric Acid, Zinc, Magnesium

These additional biomarkers help provide a more comprehensive and focused view of each health area.

### What is WHOOP Advanced Labs and how is it different?

WHOOP Advanced Labs is an add-on to your membership that combines clinical lab testing with your 24/7 WHOOP data. Other tests stop at the results. WHOOP Advanced Labs keeps going—combining what’s happening inside your body with your daily habits to coach you to live healthier, every day. With the option to retest over time, it’s a clearer, smarter way to understand and support your long-term health.

### How does it work?

WHOOP Advanced Labs enhances the WHOOP experience with two parts: uploading test results (free with membership) and in person testing (add-on subscription).

Uploads let you store results from any provider, track trends, and connect them to your WHOOP data.

WHOOP Advanced Labs testing gives you 75+ biomarkers per panel, a clinician-reviewed report, and an action plan in the app. WHOOP testing has a separate fee based on the costs associated with in person blood testing.

### Why combine blood testing with WHOOP?

Each WHOOP Advanced Labs test looks at 75 + biomarkers alongside your 24/7 WHOOP data. Get a deeper view of these metrics that you wouldn’t see otherwise. With WHOOP Advanced Labs testing, you’ll get results, a clinician-reviewed report, and a personalized action plan that integrates into your Weekly Plan and Journal. Plus, as part of your membership at no extra cost, you can upload previous labs to keep all your results in one place, view long-term trends, and connect them with your 24/7 WHOOP data.

### What do I get after taking a lab test?

You’ll receive a detailed Clinical Report reviewed by a clinician, a personalized Action Plan, and in-app guidance to help you take action on your results.

### Where do I go to get tested?

WHOOP Advanced Labs testing is powered exclusively by Quest®, with 2,000+ locations nationwide. When you schedule, just pick the time and Quest® location that works best for your blood draw. You can also add in previous test results for free with WHOOP membership within the app, no need to travel.

### Who views my results?

All lab results are reviewed by licensed clinicians who interpret your biomarkers and provide guidance directly in the WHOOP app.

### How quickly will I get my results?

Results are typically available in the WHOOP app within 7-10 business days after your test.

### Can I share my results?

Yes - you can export a summary of your lab results across all tests, or results for any individual test to share at any time.

### How is my data handled?

We take privacy seriously. Your data stays yours. All data is encrypted and never shared without your consent. You can withdraw your consent at any time in the WHOOP app.

Blood Pressure Insights is not intended for use if you are pregnant or on blood pressure medication.
