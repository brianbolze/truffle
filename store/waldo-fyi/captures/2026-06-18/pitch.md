## FAQs

Is this just ChatGPT with a brief pasted in? How is it different from an LLM?

Pitch runs purpose-built agents across proprietary data sources that no general-purpose LLM can access: live social listening, audience intelligence, paid ad libraries, trends data, and Waldo's own proprietary databases. It's built by a team with decades of combined agency experience and 25 years of AI expertise.

The output isn't a wall of text. It's a sourced, presentation-ready playbook built around your agency's DNA, with 100 Strategy Agent credits for follow-up questions and deeper digging.

To put it plainly: ChatGPT will tell you to "leverage TikTok for brand awareness." Pitch will tell you the client's CMO cares about earned over paid, that a competitor just cut 40% of their social budget and left a gap you can own, and that the audience the brief is chasing lives on Reddit, not Instagram, with proof points sourced and ready to drop into your deck.

If a competing agency runs Pitch on the same RFP, won't we get the same playbook?

No. Every playbook is built around your agency's DNA: credentials, case studies, capabilities, and whatever else you feed it. Different agencies get completely different pitch angles, gap analyses, and strategic paths. The verdict (Full Send / Toss-Up / Walk Away) might even tell one agency to pursue aggressively and another to walk away. Same RFP, completely different paths to win.

Is our data secure? Can we upload confidential RFP materials?

Yes. SOC-2 Type II compliant, fully firewalled. Nothing you upload is ever used to train models, shared with third parties, or reused in any way. Your RFPs, creds, and client materials stay yours.

Does it work without a formal RFP or written brief?

Absolutely. You can run Pitch with just a brand name, perfect for outbound prospecting, chemistry meeting prep, or proactive outreach before an RFP even drops. You'll get a "Why Now" analysis instead of Brief Decoded. Everything else (pitch angles, the room, gems, the landscape) is the same.

Does it only help with new business, or existing clients too?

Both. Every engagement is a mini-pitch, and staying sharp on your existing book of business matters as much as winning the next one. Agencies use Pitch to uncover expansion opportunities, new audience segments, and strategic ammunition that turns QBRs into growth conversations, not just retention ones.

Can I upload my own files (creds decks, case studies, previous work)?

Yes. The upload step is built around your RFP or brief, but it accepts multiple files in standard formats (PDF, Word, PowerPoint, Excel, CSV, plain text, and common image types), so you can drop creds decks, case studies, capability docs, or relevant background in alongside the brief. There's also a context field for pasting in anything that isn't in a file: client relationship history, internal POV, intel from a chemistry meeting. The more you feed it, the sharper the playbook.

Can I refine the playbook or test angles after it's generated?

Yes. Every playbook comes with Strategy Agent, a live AI assistant with full context of your playbook and access to all of Waldo's data sources. Ask follow-up questions, test specific angles, pressure-test objections, brainstorm, or ask it to rewrite sections directly. It's a sparring partner, not just a search bar.

Can the playbook evolve as the pitch process moves between rounds?

Yes. As you move through rounds and get new information (feedback from the first meeting, revised scope, new people added to the room) you can feed all of that into Strategy Agent and have it update the playbook, generate new angles, or pressure-test revised positioning. For a completely different brief or a fundamental re-scope, running a fresh playbook is the right move.

Can I export the playbook to share with my team or clients?

Yes. Workspace members can export the full playbook to PDF, Word, or Google Docs, and share it however you'd like. You can also send a direct link to anyone, including clients. People outside your workspace get a read-only view of the playbook with plain-text export, but Strategy Agent and the full export formats stay inside your team.

We already pay for GWI / Meltwater / similar tools. Does Pitch replace them?

Pitch complements rather than replaces. GWI is a data partner of ours, and Pitch surfaces GWI audience data inside every playbook so you have it where you need it during pitch prep. If your team uses GWI for custom segment building, deep audience analyses, or ongoing brand tracking, those workflows live where they always have. Same with social listening tools: Pitch isn't a social media management platform, but it does pull live signals through its data infrastructure for pitch-time intelligence.
