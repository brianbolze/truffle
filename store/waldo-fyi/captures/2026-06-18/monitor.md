## FAQs

What do you mean by "Interactive Intelligence Briefing"?

Each of these scans is a recurring report that pulls from Waldo's full data layer — live social (Instagram, TikTok, Reddit, X, Facebook, YouTube, LinkedIn), paid ad libraries, GWI audience panels, a proprietary trends database, and the open web — and combines it with a strategist's perspective on what it means and what to do. The data is examined and analysed by 10,000+ AI agents trained by former agency strategists and brand experts to think the way you do.

Once the report is produced, Strategy Agent (our AI strategy assistant) lives inside every briefing with full access to your report and Waldo's data. So when you want to chase a thread further, pressure-test assumptions, or ask follow-up questions, you can.

How is this better than the dashboards we already pay for?

Dashboards describe what's happening. And give you a thousand ways to cut millions of data points. FYI already recommends what to do about it, where its conclusions came from, and lets you ask follow-up questions when you want to go deeper. You're not staring at charts trying to interpret them. You're reading a perspective and pressure-testing it in real time.

Can I customize a briefing?

You can customize the inputs such as audience, topic, brand, category, platforms, or reader. You can also set the cadence for how often you want to automatically receive them. The recurring structure, however, stays the same. That's what makes briefings comparable across weeks and across clients.

Can I track a topic that's not my category?

Of course! You can pair any audience with any topic.

What if I want to track multiple audiences?

Each briefing has one focus. If you're tracking two audiences for the same brand, you'll run two scans.

How do the four briefings stay out of each other's way?

Each one has a single subject, and has been trained to dive deep on their respective area of expertise. They can reference each other as context, but never analyze one other or produce identical answers, since they're fundamentally about different things.

How do I organize and find older reports?

They live in your workspace, organized by type and date. You can also ask Strategy Agent to pull a specific past briefing or compare findings across briefings.

What do you need to start?

Nothing more than a brand, category, or topic to focus on. The setup form takes about a minute. We will do the rest!

How long do I have to wait for my report?

You'll get your first report in under two hours. After that, the cadence depends on the type: trends daily, brand and audience scans weekly or monthly, category scans weekly or monthly.

Do I need a subscription?

You can buy a single briefing on the website, or talk to us about a subscription if you want recurring delivery.
