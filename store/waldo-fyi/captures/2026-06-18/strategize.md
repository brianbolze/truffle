## FAQs

How is this different from ChatGPT or a custom GPT?

Waldo gives your GPT/Claude superpowers it can't otherwise build on its own. ChatGPT — even with a custom GPT — has no access to live social feeds, paid ad libraries, GWI audience panels, or trend databases. It can search the web and summarize what it finds, but it can't analyze 4,000 TikTok posts in parallel or pull real-time competitive ad spend data. Strategize is purpose-built for agency strategists: the data layer, the frameworks, and the output format are designed for how marketers actually think. It integrates seamlessly into your existing LLM stack to give you the best of both. See the comparison table above for a side-by-side.

Do I need prompting experience to use it?

No! You literally just connect Waldo to your GPT/Claude and explain, in plain English, what questions you're looking to solve. Strategy Agent is trained to think like a marketer/brand strategist if you need a thinking sparring partner. Workflows are pre-built to know exactly how to run structured, source analysis for you (which you can then further pressure-test or query).

How do I know the outputs aren't making things up?

Every claim in every output links to the specific post, article, video, or data point it came from — clickable and verifiable. Second, Waldo doesn't work like a generic LLM guessing from training data. Our agents ingest, normalize, and analyze real data before an output is ever generated — thousands of posts encoded into structured JSON, videos transcribed second-by-second, images analyzed for logos and context. The analysis is already done before you see the result.

What data does Strategize actually pull from?

Waldo's data layer spans live social listening across Instagram, TikTok, Reddit, X, YouTube, and LinkedIn. We also pull paid ad intelligence from Meta and Google ad libraries, GWI audience panels, a proprietary trends database, events data, and the open web. Strategy Agent can also pull from any files you upload — briefs, decks, past research — for project-specific context.

Can Strategy Agent dig deeper into something a workflow surfaced?

Yes — that's exactly what it's for. Run a workflow, spot something interesting, then open Strategy Agent to chase the thread. It shares context with your workflows, so it already knows what you've been researching. Ask it to pressure-test a finding, explore a tangent, or go deeper on a specific data point. You can even ask it to pull in additional research live.

Does it cover paid ads and competitive intelligence?

Yes. Waldo tracks ads across Meta and Google ad libraries — for your brand and your competitors — and refreshes daily. You can ask Strategy Agent to synthesize key themes, compare creative approaches, or identify shifts in spend and messaging across your competitive set.

Can I use this for existing clients, not just new business?

Both. Agencies use Strategize for pitch prep, quarterly reviews, proactive client recommendations, ongoing brand tracking, and expansion opportunities. If it requires research, Strategize handles the assembly — whether you're winning the account or growing it.

Can my team see and share what others have worked on?

Yes. Shared workspaces let everyone on your team see what's been researched, build on each other's work, and avoid duplicating effort. You can export full outputs or highlight specific sections to share just what's relevant — to PDF, Google Suite, MS365, or directly to Slack and Teams.

Is my data secure?

We're SOC 2 (Level II) compliant, fully firewalled, and nothing you upload or generate is ever used for model training. Each workspace is isolated. Your client data stays yours — period. Unlike pasting confidential briefs into ChatGPT or Gemini, Waldo is built for enterprise agency workflows where security isn't optional.
