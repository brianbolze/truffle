## Making. Healthier. Human. Futures.

For more than 40 years, IDEO has helped the world's leading organizations make the future. Together with our clients, we are on a mission to shape a healthier, more human future for everyone. With decades of experience in healthcare innovation, we're pushing boundaries to design solutions that are as compassionate as they are groundbreaking.

Find out how design can set you apart, help you grow, and solve your toughest challenges. Discover how we’re partnering with organizations to reimagine patient care, optimize digital health, and build systems that truly prioritize people.

## Meet some of our   Healthcare design leaders.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/68c99b3e3a4f357e8af47a40/68cbe70dc29326449f202cf4_67472edfc239c499a57da887_Matthew-Higham_IDEO.png)

Matthew Higham

Partner

Matthew focuses on the use of design as a powerful tool for change in the healthcare sector. He partners closely with organisations to understand their needs and works tirelessly to build the right systems, approaches, and structures to translate these needs into superlative products and experiences.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/68c99b3e3a4f357e8af47a40/68cbe6dfe33215facbe29a97_6747685ad3f420b894061842_Ari-Adler_IDEO.jpeg)

Ari Adler

Partner

As a Partner in the Cambridge studio, Ari spends his time helping clients and teams grow and bring innovative products, services, and experiences to the world. He is particularly interested in how emerging technologies help us reimagine everyday human experiences and fundamentally change how we relate to each other and to the world around us.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/68c99b3e3a4f357e8af47a40/68cbe6e28f61b2a285feaab3_682f31294b6aff4a893e921f_betsy_smith.webp)

Betsy Fields Smith

Executive Design Director

Betsy is the Executive Design Director of Health at IDEO London. For over two decades, she has built transformative client relationships and led teams driving innovation across sectors, with deep expertise in designing products, services, and experiences that improve patient journey.s and encourage proactive engagement in health

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/68c99b3e3a4f357e8af47a40/68cbe7247549b2082b41e719_67ec065b20585adda1e3eab5_image%2520(8).png)

Tara Safaie

Executive Director

Tara partners with health organizations to spark change from the inside out—designing innovation products and portfolios, cultivating new capabilities, and evolving organizational cultures. She has launched innovation labs, designed more cohesive patient journeys through a cardiology department, redefined employee value propositions, and more. Tara's work has stretched from pharma to care systems, championing human-centricity at every turn.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/68c99b3e3a4f357e8af47a40/68cbe6fc5afa46926078585c_67531b42eb0cced929946713_jesse_fourt.webp)

Jesse Fourt

Executive Director

Jesse's hands-on process enables organizations to launch meaningful, impactful, challenging products. At IDEO since 2000, Jesse has led and engineered a wide range of products including drug delivery devices, vascular access products, surgical tools, consumer and industrial electronics, furniture, and household goods.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/68c99b3e3a4f357e8af47a40/68cbe6e3d987ac1d377c733f_6747631356cc26d114a4f75c_Brandy-Parker_IDEO.png)

Brandy Parker

Senior Director

Brandy leads IDEO Chicago’s health portfolio. Her design work covers everything from medical devices to patient and provider experiences, adherence, services, health equity, and digital tools.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/68c99b3e3a4f357e8af47a40/68cbe72b29c98aca728c28fb_674763b2ab96515ceee6a42b_YC-Sun_IDEO.png)

Yicheng “YC” Sun

Senior Director

YC helps lead IDEO San Francisco’s healthcare portfolio, specializing in building digital products and emerging technologies. He applies human-centered design in service of individual and collective wellbeing and is constantly thinking about how to bring healthcare ventures from ideation to market.
