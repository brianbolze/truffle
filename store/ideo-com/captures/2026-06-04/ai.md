## Meet some of our   emerging tech leaders.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/68c99b3e3a4f357e8af47a40/68cbe71f1dbf3576d109aded_684b24cc68ec77391ff4b1d2_Kunovsky_Savannah.webp)

Savannah Kunovsky

Managing Director, Emerging Technology

Savannah runs IDEO’s emerging technology business, working at the frontier of AI and new technologies to help organizations shape what to build, how it should behave, and why it matters.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/68c99b3e3a4f357e8af47a40/690e798fc2ca38ec832f7200_jenna_fizel.webp)

Jenna Fizel

Managing Director, Emerging Technology

Jenna Fizel leads IDEO's Emerging Technology practice, blending computational geometry, interactive design, and fashion technology to make technical skills accessible and to innovate in design processes with AI, XR, and digital fabrication.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/68c99b3e3a4f357e8af47a40/693c2fd22b5374482e228ecf_headshot_zoeyZhu.webp)

Ziyuan ‘Zoey’ Zhu

Software Designer

Zoey is a creative technologist and multidisciplinary designer. She creates tangible futures through the lens of human-centered design, emerging technology, and sustainability.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/68c99b3e3a4f357e8af47a40/6941a03bd7f5fe9e96148eb6_68cbe6e1a0bc318b4bd66940_682b80dd68a9647cdc9ad970_Becca-Carroll.png)

Becca Carroll

Chief Strategy Officer

Becca leads IDEO’s long-term strategy and emerging growth initiatives. Her career spans entrepreneurship, financial services, and technology, anchored in designing sustainable businesses that make systems and products work better for people.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/68c99b3e3a4f357e8af47a40/69651a42d7717bc1ce7ae0d8_headshot_juhoParviainen.webp)

Juho Parviainen

Executive Design Director

Juho Parviainen is an interaction designer who believes in democratising technology, and making it work for people, rather than the other way around. Over the past two decades, he's helped lead IDEO Europe, Product Design in North America, and worked across everything from retail to gaming, telecommunications, media, and health.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/68c99b3e3a4f357e8af47a40/693c326d04183d535979262d_headshot_sergioFregoni.webp)

Sergio Fregoni

Executive Director

Sergio helps organizations design and scale digital, climate, and circular solutions that keep people's needs at the center. He guides organizations toward future fitness and empowers them to navigate the digital and technological landscape with confidence.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/68c99b3e3a4f357e8af47a40/690e795ec6e0c5a31df5bbae_danny_deruntz.webp)

Danny DeRuntz

Executive Design Director

A 20+ year IDEO veteran, Danny has been prototyping and exploring the intersection of emerging technology with core human needs for as long as he can remember.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/68c99b3e3a4f357e8af47a40/68cbe7000604f3a3ec3fadfc_6865c962870807b48c6a5489_Kaii-Tu.png)

Kaii Tu

Design Director

Kaii is a product and environment designer who blends analytical thinking and design craft. At IDEO, he specializes in designing complex products and systems, including urban living, mobility, and learning across China, Asia, and globally. He leads foresight and futuring at IDEO Shanghai, helping organizations anticipate human shifts and design preferred futures.
