About Weight Management

Over the past 30 years, the prevalence of obesity in the United States has risen— [60% of adults](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC3033553/) are currently obese or overweight. Obesity is a significant health risk, as it increases the risk of developing cardiovascular diseases, diabetes, hypertension, and other medical conditions.

**Several factors have contributed to this challenge, including:**

- Increased intake of energy-dense foods high in sugars and fat.
- Reduced physical activity due to sedentary lifestyles and urbanization.

**While dietary habits and lack of exercise contribute to obesity, genetics and external factors also influence this condition. Some of the most common weight management strategies incorporate the following:**

- **Dietary modifications:** Reduce the intake of processed, ready-made, and refined food with high fat and sugar content. Meanwhile, increase the consumption of nutritious, high-fiber foods to aid in weight loss.
- **Physical activity:** The CDC recommends at least 150 minutes of moderate-intensity aerobic activity and 75 minutes of intense aerobic activity per week to maintain a healthy weight.
- **Surgery:** Bariatric surgery can help maintain weight by changing the digestive system, such as making the stomach smaller. Individuals with severe obesity or serious health problems like diabetes can benefit from bariatric surgery.
- **Weight management programs:** In formal weight management programs, a trained weight management specialist creates a customized plan for weight management. This plan can include specific diet changes, physical activity, and habit management.
- **Weight loss medication:** Doctors can prescribe weight loss medications alongside lifestyle change recommendations. Unlike traditional weight loss medications, compounded weight loss medications are more personalized to the individual’s needs. Strive Compounding Pharmacy currently compounds popular weight loss medications such as [Semaglutide](https://www.strivepharmacy.com/medications/semaglutide) and [Tirzepatide](https://www.strivepharmacy.com/medications/tirzepatide), [Amlexanox](https://www.strivepharmacy.com/medications/amlexanox), as well as many others.

While there are several ways to manage weight, most people encounter various roadblocks during their weight management journey. For example, physical hindrances can include fatigue, discomfort, and underlying medical conditions. Environmental barriers can also include lack of time, limited access to healthy food, or poor social support.

Regarding weight management, it’s crucial to remember that everyone is unique, and each journey is different. A weight loss compounding pharmacy like Strive Pharmacy considers this and formulates customized weight management medications to help you take charge of your health.
