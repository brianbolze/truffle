## Ingredients That Make a Difference

The journey to trustworthy medicine begins with the legitimate, responsible, and compliant sourcing of active pharmaceutical ingredients (APIs).

FDA-Registered Partners

Every ingredient we use comes from FDA-registered facilities.

FDA Green List Facilities

Our GLP-1 ingredients are sourced exclusively from FDA-official [**"GLP-1 Green List"**](https://www.strivepharmacy.com/higher-standards#) facilities, ensuring they are legitimate, compliant, and authentic.

Verified Quality in Every Batch
