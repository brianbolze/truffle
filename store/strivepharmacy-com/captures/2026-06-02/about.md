### Why We Strive

In 2018, we saw the gap: providers with limited options, patients with unmet needs, and a healthcare system too focused on the one-size-fits-all approach. So, we built something different. Strive was born from a desire to bring personalization, precision, and compassion back to medicine.

02 / 05

### Relentless Pursuit of Accessibility & Individuality

Strive was built upon the belief that healthcare should be accessible, adaptable, and formulated around patient’s unique needs. Health is highly individual. Sensitivities and wellness factors exist. So why are providers and patients so often handed inflexible dosing options, ingredients, and forms? Strive exists to bridge that gap with solutions that support you and your patients by delivering truly personal care.

### Uncompromising Quality, Since Day One

From inception, Strive was designed to set a new standard. Providers deserve confidence in the precision and quality of the medications they prescribe. That’s why we go above and beyond industry testing and potency standards, and our pharmacists adhere to rigorous regulations and evolving best practices—so you can trust that every compounded formulation is made with the highest quality and unwavering excellence.

04 / 05

### Building Better Baselines

Strive has been built and sustained by a thriving workforce of vital individuals. We work and live hard, adventuring with our families, exploring with our pets, biking, surfing, and just generally living. We believe that wellness is priceless, and people deserve more than a reactive approach to health. Strive delivers solutions that support proactive care, helping shift patients’ baseline health for long-term well-being.
