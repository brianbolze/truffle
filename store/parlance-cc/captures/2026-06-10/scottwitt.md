# About&Contact

## Scott Witt \| Founder

![](https://framerusercontent.com/images/hJA5CNNy2KVsykw1cXx5VzStc.png?width=1500&height=1377)

Parlance was founded in 2023 by Scott Witt —recovering perfectionist, former Apple Creative Director, Chief Brand & Creative Officer at venture-backed startups, Cannes Titanium Lion and D&AD Black Pencil winner.

For over two decades, Scott has built brands, teams, products, and market value for companies driving seismic shifts in consumer behavior —streaming music, connected fitness, digital therapeutics, and sustainable fashion.

In founding Parlance, Scott sought to work closer to the source of tectonic change: the inventors, investors, and creators shaping the next brand world order. Parlance is a Studio for these firms and their ambitions.

**Creating breakthroughs is what you do.**

**Helping people understand them is what I do.**
