## Our platforms

### Credit

Through our industry-leading direct lending business and complementary strategies, we work closely witha broad range of private equity-backed and non-sponsored companies to originate, execute, and manage investments across various loan and equity-related instruments.

[Learn about Credit →](https://www.blueowl.com/credit)

Highlights

- $195B in gross originations

[Learn about Credit →](https://www.blueowl.com/credit)

### Real Assets

We specialize in providing flexible capital to organizations across asset classes and geographies. Backed by our expertise in investing across the entire capital stack, our proprietary origination platform, and enhanced deal structures, we aim to provide our partners with the certainty that they need and our investors with the confidence that they desire.

[Learn about Real Assets →](https://www.blueowl.com/real-assets)

Highlights

- Flexible solutions with $85.1B AUM
- 6,145+1 properties owned
- More than 8651 tenant relationships/partnerships

[Learn about Real Assets →](https://www.blueowl.com/real-assets)

### GP Strategic Capital

With extensive experience providing long-term minority equity and financing to private capital investment managers, we have established ourselves as an innovator within the industry. We partner with alternative managers who want to add to their success and continue to be leaders in their space.

[Learn about GP Strategic Capital →](https://www.blueowl.com/gp-strategic-capital)

Highlights

- Capital provider with $70.6B AUM
- 70+ partnerships since inception
- Strategic support through our Business Services Platform
