#### Blue Owl Capital Corporation II

Blue Owl Capital Corporation II (“OBDC II”) is a non-traded business development company formed primarily to originate and make loans to, and make debt and equity investments in, US middle-market companies. OBDC II primarily invests in senior secured or unsecured loans; subordinated loans or mezzanine loans; and, to a lesser extent, equity-related securities—including, but not limited to, common and preferred stock, securities convertible into common stock and warrants, and preferred stock. OBDC II is externally managed by Blue Owl Credit Advisors LLC, an indirect affiliate of Blue Owl Capital, Inc. OBDC II's registration statement on Form N-2 has expired.
