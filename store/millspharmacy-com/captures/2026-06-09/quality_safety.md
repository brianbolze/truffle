State-of-the-Art Technologies and Innovative Techniques

We leverage state-of-the-art technologies and innovative techniques to create the custom-made compounds prescribed by physicians. This ensures that each medication is tailored precisely to meet the unique needs of every patient, whether it’s a sterile or non-sterile preparation.

Highly Trained Professionals

Our team of professionals is highly trained, delivering quality-customized medications with expertise and precision. Thorough training is conducted by skilled personnel both internally and externally through globally recognized pharmacy organizations, such as the Professional Compounding Centers of America (PCCA). This rigorous training process occurs at hiring and continues throughout the year to maintain the highest standards of compounding practice.

Regular Calibration and Inspection of Compounding Equipment

Our compounding equipment and devices are regularly calibrated to ensure proper functioning. This meticulous attention to detail guarantees the optimal preparation of compounded prescriptions, safeguarding both the quality of the medication and the health of our compounding personnel from potential chemical exposure.

Compliance with USP and State Regulations

Our facility operates in strict compliance with USP 795, USP 797, and USP 800 standards, as well as state-board regulations. This commitment ensures that our compounding practices meet national benchmarks for both sterile and non-sterile preparations, providing patients with the highest quality customized medications.

Stringent Safety Protocols

We implement stringent safety protocols throughout our compounding processes. From the initial preparation to the final dispensing of medications, every step is monitored to ensure the highest quality preparation.

PCAB-Accredited Compounding Pharmacy

Being PCAB accredited is a testament to our compounding pharmacy’s dedication to adhering to the highest standards of quality and safety in the preparation of customized medications. The Pharmacy Compounding Accreditation Board (PCAB) accreditation is a rigorous, voluntary process that evaluates our pharmacy’s practices against stringent industry standards, ensuring compliance with best practices for both sterile and non-sterile compounding. This prestigious accreditation signifies that our facility meets or exceeds the quality benchmarks established by PCAB, which includes thorough documentation, regular inspections, and continuous staff training. Patients and healthcare providers can trust that our compounded medications are prepared with exceptional precision, using state-of-the-art equipment and techniques, all within a framework of comprehensive safety protocols. Our commitment to PCAB standards underscores our dedication to providing superior, personalized healthcare solutions.

By adhering to these rigorous standards and continuously improving our practices, we are dedicated to delivering the highest quality compounded medications while maintaining a safe and compliant environment. Our commitment to quality assurance and safety underscores our dedication to patient care and professional excellence in compounding pharmacy.
