# History

![](https://millspharmacy.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Mills_About-2-1024x400.webp)

At Mills Pharmacy, nurturing the health of our customers has been our mission since 1946. After all, we believe the wellness of each individual is connected to that of the entire community and, in turn, our business.

## 1946

Ever since we opened our doors in 1946, we’ve been honored to fulfill that mission for generations of families. Some of our customers still remember the days when we were best known for our selection of penny candies and the long-retired soda fountain. Our reputation for exceptional service has endured because it’s built on a foundation of trust that gets stronger with every prescription we fill and every customer we welcome back.

![](https://millspharmacy.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Mills_Apothecary-2-1024x400.webp)

## 2010

What keeps them coming back is our commitment to innovation and an unforgettable shopping experience. This commitment was brought to life in 2010 when inspired by the style of the modern pharmacies of New York and Paris, we reimagined both our facade and the products and services we provide.

![](https://millspharmacy.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/Mills_Pharmacy-5-1024x400.webp)

## Present

From the addition of a state-of-the-art compounding pharmacy to curating Metro Detroit’s most unique selection of premium beauty and personal care products, we transformed our quaint neighborhood store into a boutique fit for the most discerning tastes. Yet while our surroundings look quite different than they did in 1946, our mission remains the same: To nurture the health of our customers and the community we serve. We are humbled by our success and proud to continue to fulfill that mission for generations of Mills customers to come.
