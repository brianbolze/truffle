# Energy Storage Systems

It's not magic, it's electrochemical potential.

## Powerful Peace of Mind

BETA energy storage systems feature multiple redundant protections against unlikely thermal runaway events, communications glitches, non-uniform discharges and environmental interruptions. The packs we deliver to our partners meet stringent requirements per DO-160G and minimize additional hazards during a worst-case thermal runaway condition. BETA energy storage systems deliver safe energy across multiple aircraft platforms.

## New to Electric Energy?

Batteries are among the most safe and stable potential energy packages that humanity has ever captured.

- High voltage electricity powers electric engines

- Stored energy is measured in kilowatt-hours (kWh)

- Energy draw is measured in kilowatts (kW)


![trad-vs-electric](https://beta.team/hs-fs/hubfs/trad-vs-electric.jpg?width=1200&height=276&name=trad-vs-electric.jpg)

## System

The energy storage system that powers the aircraft is composed of multiple battery packs connected together in parallel through a distribution system designed to provide safety and redundancy for all power requirements.

## Inside the Pack

BETA battery packs are built from thousands of individual battery cells. These work together to achieve market-leading energy density with maximal redundancy.

## Safety Features

These features ensure occupant safety, and also provide first responders safe, easy access in the event of an emergency.

- High reliability design for safety
- Built-in fire detection system
- Sub-pack containment for worst case thermal runaway
- Overboard emergency exhaust venting

## Validated Against Aircraft Standards

BETA’s battery facility allows our team to test above and beyond regulatory safety requirements. We put our families in our aircraft, and we ensure pack safety to the highest standards.

- Majority of DO-160G testing performed onsite — including temperature, vibration and humidity.
- Abuse testing including cell, module, and pack TR test bunkers with dedicated air scrubbers
- Thermal Management System (TMS) testing onsite
- Subpack crash testing onsite
