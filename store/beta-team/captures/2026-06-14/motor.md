## We fly what we build. Safety, reliability and performance are the byproducts of BETA’s in-house validation and piloted flight test.

## Endurance Testing

Our remotely controlled motor endurance stands, driven by 5.3MW of power and 24/7 live feed, let us use higher thrust loads that surpass the most rigorous customer requirements and mimic operations exposed to rain, sleet, snow, flying pigs, or boring sunshine.

## Dynamic Power Analysis

Dynos — with up to 4800 Nm of torque and 3500 rpm are remote controlled, operate with regenerative power, and are under 24/7 live camera feed. All of our dynos are equipped with instrumentation such as HBM power analyzers, accelerometers, temperature sensors, torque cells, rpm meters, high speed data acquisition up to 10 kHz, and Speedgoat and Simulink controls.

We manipulate load side motors to mimic the operational behaviors of our customers' use cases, ensuring that when customers take delivery, the motor's hardest working days are behind them.

## Sweet Silence

Electromagnetic interference (EMI) is unwanted noise or interference in an electrical path or circuit caused by an outside source. Our semi-anechoic and reverberation EMI chambers support DO-160G compliance testing, including high-intensity radiated field (HIRF) and lightning. The only signal these motors hear is the one the operator sends.
