# Training

## Flight Training

Permanent installation Thunderdome simulators, trailer-borne Microdome units, and mobile VR training solutions. All simulators feature advanced avionics, and support networked mission training and remote instruction.

## Flight Simulation

BETA has strived for accurate modeling of vehicle systems, including propulsion and energy systems in its simulators. As a result, we’re equipped to train for limitations and failure cases. Our partnership with X-Plane has allowed us to make a flight simulator that is modular, informative, and fun.
