## JetBoots PRO Plus Size Chart

For a proper fit of your JetBoots PRO Plus compression boots, measure the inseam of your leg from the bottom of the groin to the ankle bone. Then, measure your the length of your leg from the bottom of the groin to the floor (no shoes). If you are in-between sizes, we recommend sizing down to ensure the best fit.

| Size | LeG Length | Inseam (pant length) |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Short | 68-81 cm27-32” | 60-73 cm24-29” |
| Regular | 81-93 cm32-37" | 73-86 cm29-34" |
| Long | 93 cm+37"+ | 86 cm+34"+ |

## How to measure

### 1\. Inseam

Measure from the bottom of your groin to the ankle bone.

### 2\. Leg Length

Measure from the bottom of your groin to the floor (no shoes).
