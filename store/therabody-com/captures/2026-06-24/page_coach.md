Coach by Therabody creates intelligent, personalized recovery plans based on your fitness goals, activity data and the latest scientific research - helping you feel better, move better, sleep better and ultimately perform better.

Throughout the day, Coach adjusts your recovery plan based on your activities and changing needs. It notifies you in real-time with customized, science-backed recommendations to help you use your Therabody device for more effective recovery, so you can continue to perform at your best.
