What is Lipo-C?

A compounded blend of lipotropic nutrients— methionine, inositol, choline, vitamin B5, and vitamin C—that supports the metabolic pathways your body uses to process fat, support liver function, and produce energy. Not a fat burner. A smarter foundation for how your metabolism works.

How do I take it?

Typically one to two times per week as prescribed. Stay consistent. This is a therapy that works with your metabolic systems over time, not a shortcut.

Common side effects

Lipo-C is well tolerated by most patients. Mild injection site irritation, nausea, or gastrointestinal discomfort can occur. Your provider will adjust dosing based on how you respond.

## Frequently  asked questions

Is this a fat burner?

Not in the traditional sense. Lipo-C doesn't flood your system with stimulants to spike your metabolism. It supports the underlying pathways your body uses to process and utilize fat—which is a fundamentally different mechanism, and a more sustainable one.

Will this give me energy?

Many patients report more consistent, steady energy as their metabolic function improves. Unlike stimulant-based products, there's no spike and no crash—just more efficient energy production over time.
