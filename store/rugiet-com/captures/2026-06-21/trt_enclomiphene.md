What is Enclomiphene?

Daily medication that signals your body to increase its own testosterone production to help you restore healthy levels without shutting down what's already working. Enclomiphene can work well for men with secondary hypogonadism, but it is not effective for men who have a diagnosis of primary hypogonadism.

How do I take it?

One oral dose each day. Simple routine, taken with or without food.

Common side effects

Most men experience minimal side effects. When they occur, they're usually temporary and mild. Common side effects include: GI upset, headache, and increased estrogen levels. Seek medical attention immediately if there is an allergic reaction or if side effects are severe.

## Frequently  asked questions

How does Enclomiphene work differently than TRT?

Traditional TRT replaces testosterone directly. Enclomiphene stimulates your body to produce more on its own. That means your natural production stays active, and fertility isn't suppressed.

Will Enclomiphene work for me?

It's designed for men whose bodies can still produce testosterone but need a signal to make more. Your doctor determines if you're a good candidate based on your labs and health history.

Does Enclomiphene affect fertility?

No. Unlike traditional TRT, Enclomiphene doesn't suppress sperm production. It's the go-to option for men who want to address low testosterone while preserving fertility.

How fast does Enclomiphene work?

As testosterone levels normalize — typically within the first few weeks — some men begin to notice improvements in energy and mood. Physical changes like muscle mass and body composition take considerably longer, generally requiring at least 3 months to become measurable, with continued improvement over the first year. Individual results vary and depend on consistent training, nutrition, and adherence to treatment.

What if Enclomiphene doesn't work?

Some men respond better to direct testosterone replacement. If Enclomiphene doesn't raise your levels enough, your doctor can help you transition to another treatment option.

Is Enclomiphene safe long-term?

When properly monitored, it can be. Regular labs track your testosterone levels and overall health markers to ensure treatment stays effective and safe over time.

Why would I start with Enclomiphene instead of TRT?

If your body can still produce testosterone and you want to preserve fertility, Enclomiphene lets you restore healthy levels without affecting natural production.

How is progress monitored?

Regular bloodwork tracks your testosterone levels and other health markers. Your doctor adjusts dosing based on how you feel and what your labs show. All ongoing monitoring is included in your treatment cost- no hidden fees. Plans start at just $139/month.
