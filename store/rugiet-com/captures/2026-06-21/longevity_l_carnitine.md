What is L-Carnitine?

A naturally occurring amino acid derivative that helps transport fatty acids into your cells to be used for energy, supporting metabolism, physical performance, and recovery.

How do I take it?

Administer as a subcutaneous injection as prescribed. Stay consistent with your dosing schedule to support optimal energy and metabolic benefits.

Common side effects

L-carnitine is generally well tolerated. Some patients may experience mild injection site discomfort, nausea, or muscle soreness. In some cases, a temporary “fishy” body odor can occur. Your provider will guide dosing and monitor your response to ensure the best results.

Will this help with fat loss?

L-carnitine supports how efficiently your body uses fat as a fuel source — particularly during physical activity. It's not a shortcut, but it can meaningfully improve what you get out of a consistent training and nutrition routine by making fat more accessible as energy.

No. L-carnitine doesn't work by artificially elevating heart rate or masking fatigue signals. It supports the underlying metabolic process that converts fat into energy — so the benefit is cleaner and more sustainable than anything stimulant-based.
