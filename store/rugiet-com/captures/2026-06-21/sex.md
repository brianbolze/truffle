## Frequently  asked questions

How do I know which treatment is best for me?

Start with what you're trying to solve. Need fast, reliable erections on demand? Rugiet Ready® combines three medications including apomorphine to address ED at both the brain and body level. Available in multiple strengths, even for men who haven't responded to traditional options.

Want to last longer and get harder? **Go Long addresses premature ejaculation through both the brain and body, so you can confidently get hard and go longer.**

Prefer daily medication for always-on support? **Daily Boost provides consistent erection support plus DHEA to support testosterone production.**

Dealing with both ED and hair loss? Grower combines tadalafil with minoxidil for a 2-in-1 solution.

The best part: you can switch treatments anytime. If one medication isn't delivering the results you want, talk to your doctor about trying another.

Does this treatment qualify for HSA/FSA?

Yes. Contact your plan provider to confirm eligibility and reimbursement details. If you need documentation after checkout, our patient support team can help.

Do I need a prescription?

Yes.We only provide prescription medications, prescribed after a consultation with a board-certified physician. Your prescription is included at no additional cost. Complete a short medical questionnaire, and if prescribed by your physician, the medication ships discreetly to your door. You're only charged if prescribed, so there's no risk to get started.

I've tried other medications that didn't work. Can Rugiet help?

Many men don't respond well to single-ingredient treatments like generic sildenafil or tadalafil. Rugiet offers prescription treatments, such as Rugiet Ready, which combines multiple proven medications with a delivery method designed for maximum absorption and reliability—to help address ED more comprehensively. For many men, this approach delivers results where other options have failed.

Are your partner pharmacies FDA-regulated?

Pharmacies are licensed and regulated at the state level by Boards of Pharmacy and are also subject to applicable federal laws, rules, and regulation
