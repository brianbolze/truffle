What is sermorelin?

A prescription peptide that signals your brain to produce more of your own growth hormone. As GH declines with age, recovery slows, sleep gets shallower, and body composition shifts. Sermorelin works with your body's existing systems to help restore what's been lost.

How do I take it?

5x per week subcutaneous injection, ideally at bedtime. Avoid eating (especially carbohydrates and fats) for about an hour before and after dosing. Consistency matters here. This is a therapy that compounds over time.

Common side effects

Sermorelin is well tolerated by most patients. The most commonly reported effects are mild: injection site redness, occasional headache, or flushing. Some patients experience temporary dizziness or nausea. Your provider monitors your progress and adjusts treatment as needed.
