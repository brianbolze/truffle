## Frequently  asked questions

Who is this for?

For men and women looking to support energy, recovery, body composition, and how they age. Whether you're feeling run down or you're already optimizing and want a sharper edge, these therapies are designed to support those goals.

How do I choose the right treatment?

Start at the top of this page by selecting the outcome you want to improve—energy, recovery, weight, detox. You'll complete a quick intake, and a licensed medical provider will review your responses to determine if the treatment is appropriate for you.

Is this prescribed?

Yes. Every therapy in this category is prescribed by a licensed U.S. healthcare provider based on your intake and health profile. Nothing here is sold over the counter.

How long does it take to get started?

A few minutes. Choose your therapy, complete the intake, and a provider reviews your information before your order is processed. Prescriptions arrive in days, not weeks.

When will labs and additional therapies be available?

Soon. Optional lab-based tracking and additional peptide therapies are coming as part of the protocol's next phase. Join early access to be the first to know when they go live.

Are these FDA-approved?

These therapies are compounded by licensed U.S. pharmacies and prescribed by licensed providers. Compounded medications are not FDA-approved as a category, but the ingredients used are recognized and the pharmacies are regulated.
