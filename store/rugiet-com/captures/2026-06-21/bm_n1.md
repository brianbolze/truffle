Rugiet Ready comes in the form of a sublingual troche, which dissolves under the tongue and is directly absorbed into the bloodstream, bypassing the liver and digestive system. Rugiet Ready's effects typically last 24-36 hours. For those with liver or kidney disease, the medication may remain in the body longer, so it's best to consult your doctor before use.
