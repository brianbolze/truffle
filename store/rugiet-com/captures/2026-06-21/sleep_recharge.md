What is Recharge?

A prescription sleep medication combining three ingredients to help you fall asleep quickly and stay asleep through the night. Stronger than melatonin, safer than sedatives, designed for the nights when your brain won't shut off.

Is Recharge habit-forming?

No. Unlike traditional prescription sleep aids, Recharge is designed for occasional use without building dependency. Take it when you need it, up to four times weekly, and skip it when you don’t.

How is Recharge different from melatonin?

Recharge contains ramelteon, which is both stronger—and more effective—than melatonin. Recharge activates your brain’s actual sleep receptors, helping your body fall asleep naturally and stay asleep longer. It’s prescription-strength without the addiction risk.

Can I use Recharge every night?

Recharge is designed for occasional sleeplessness, up to 4 nights per week. It gives you control over your sleep schedule without creating long-term dependence.
