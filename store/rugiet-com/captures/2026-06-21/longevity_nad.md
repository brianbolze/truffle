What is NAD+?

A coenzyme present in every cell in your body. It's essential for producing energy, repairing cellular damage, and keeping your metabolism running efficiently. Levels decline naturally with age. When they drop, everything from your energy to your cognitive sharpness can follow.

How do I take it?

Available as a nasal spray or subcutaneous injection, dosed daily or multiple times per week depending on your prescription. Your provider sets the schedule. Consistency is what drives results. Follow the protocol and let the therapy build.

Common side effects

NAD+ therapy is well tolerated by most patients. When side effects occur, they tend to be mild: nausea, flushing, headache, or minor injection site irritation. Sensitivity can vary by dose and delivery method. Your provider will adjust your regimen if needed.

## Frequently  asked questions

What's the difference between NAD+ and supplements like NMN or NR?

NMN and NR are precursors—your body has to convert them into NAD+ before they can do anything. This therapy delivers NAD+ directly, which means your cells get what they need without the conversion step. Consistency of absorption is also more predictable when dosing is provider-guided and pharmacy-sourced.

Will this give me immediate energy like caffeine?

No—and that's the point. Caffiene is a short term stimulant while NAD+ is designed to boost overall energy and provide a higher baseline of energy.
