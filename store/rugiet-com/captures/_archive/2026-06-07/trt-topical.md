What is Topical TRT?

Bioidentical testosterone cream is applied daily to your skin. An easy, needle-free way to restore healthy levels and take back what low T has taken.

How do I take it?

Apply once daily to clean, dry skin. Let it dry completely before getting dressed. Your doctor determines the right amount based on your labs.

Common side effects

Common side effects include skin irritation at the application site, acne, and changes in red blood cell levels. Serious side effects are possible. Seek medical attention immediately if you experience severe side effects. **Important:** Testosterone gel must never come into contact with women, children, or pets — accidental transfer can cause serious hormonal effects. Your provider will review your complete risk profile and monitor your labs throughout treatment.

## Frequently  asked questions

Will topical TRT transfer to others?

Testosterone gel can transfer to others through direct skin contact. To prevent transfer: allow the gel to dry completely before dressing, wash the application site with soap and water before any skin-to-skin contact, and keep the area covered around women and children. Pregnant women in particular should avoid any contact, as testosterone exposure poses risks to fetal development. If accidental contact occurs, the exposed person should wash the area with soap and water immediately.

Is topical TRT as effective as injections?

Many men achieve excellent results with topical TRT. The right choice depends on your labs, how your body absorbs the medication, and your personal preference.

How long does it take to dry?

Most formulations dry within a few minutes. Your doctor provides specific instructions based on the product prescribed.

Does it smell or feel messy?

Modern formulations are designed to be clean and unobtrusive. Most men find them easy to work into their daily routine.

How fast does topical TRT work?

Libido is typically the first improvement men notice, often within the first few weeks. For some men, mood may begin to stabilize over the first 1–2 months, though full mood benefits take longer to develop. Physical changes like muscle mass and body composition require consistent training and nutrition alongside treatment, and generally take at least 3 months to become measurable — with continued progress over the first year.

Who shouldn't use topical TRT?

Your doctor screens for contraindications during consultation. Some men absorb topical testosterone less effectively and may need alternative treatment options.

Can I apply it anywhere on my body?

Your doctor provides specific application instructions. Common sites include shoulders, upper arms, or other areas as directed.

How is progress monitored?

Regular bloodwork tracks your testosterone levels along with other health markers. Your doctor adjusts application amount based on how you feel and what your labs show. All monitoring is included in your treatment cost. Plans start as low as $139/month.
