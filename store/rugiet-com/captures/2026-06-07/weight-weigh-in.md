What is Weigh In?

A personalized combination of prescription weight loss medications designed to address the biological drivers of weight gain. Weigh In is designed to help reduce cravings, support appetite control, and help regulate blood sugar, making it easier to lose weight without constant hunger.

Is Weigh In an injection?

No. Weigh In is 100% oral medication. Easy-to-take daily pills prescribed by your provider based on your personalized plan. No needles, no weekly shots.

How is Weigh In different from other weight loss treatments?

Most weight loss programs rely on willpower alone. Weigh In addresses the hormones controlling your hunger and metabolism, fixing the biology that's been working against you.

What medications are in Weigh In?

Your provider customizes your treatment from three proven medications: bupropion and naltrexone are used together to help reduce appetite and curb cravings, while metformin helps regulate blood sugar and support feelings of fullness. You get what your body needs, not a one-size-fits-all approach.

## Frequently  asked questions

How long does it take to see results?

Results can vary by individual, but many patients notice decreased cravings and improved appetite control within the first few weeks. Many see measurable weight changes in about 4 to 8 weeks.

Is Weigh In injectable or oral?

This powerful stack is 100% oral — no needles or injections. You’ll receive easy-to-take daily pills, prescribed by your provider based on your personalized plan.

Can I take Weigh In with other medication or supplements?

Your provider will review your current medications to ensure there are no interactions with what you currently take. Be sure to share all of your medications and supplements during your intake and consultation.

Do I need to take Weigh In daily?

For the best results, take the prescribed oral medications daily or as directed by your doctor. Consistency is crucial to long-term weight loss success.

Is Weigh In safe for long-term use?

Our weight loss treatments are closely monitored by your provider to ensure safety and results over time. Consult your doctor to learn more.

Does this qualify for HSA/FSA?

Yes, please contact your savings plan provider to confirm eligibility and reimbursement amounts, as the process can vary. If additional documentation is needed after check out, our customer care team is here to assist you.
