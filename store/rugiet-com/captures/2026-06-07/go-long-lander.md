## Frequently  asked questions

What is Go Long?

A dual-action oral dissolving tablet designed to help you get hard faster and last longer. Working through the brain and body, Go Long combines paroxetine and tadalafil to help put you in charge of when sex starts and stops.

Is Paroxetine an antidepressant or SSRI?

It is, when prescribed at depression doses for depression. At the doses used in PE treatment, paroxetine acts on your ejaculatory reflex pathway — not your mood. You won't feel like you're "on antidepressants." Your libido won't disappear. Your licensed Rugiet provider prescribes a PE-specific protocol, not a psychiatric one.

Can Go Long help if I also struggle with erections?

Yes. Go Long includes tadalafil, which supports blood flow to help you get and maintain erections, while paroxetine helps improve climax control.

How quickly does Go Long work?

Go Long is taken as needed. We recommend taking one tablet at least 1 hour before sexual activity, though many men find it works best when taken 3–6 hours beforehand.

How long do the effects last?

Go Long can stay active for up to 36 hours, giving you a longer window of support when you want it.

How is this different than taking Cialis?

Tadalafil (Cialis) alone treats erection quality — it doesn't directly address the ejaculatory reflex. Go Long combines both active ingredients because clinical research shows the combination produces better outcomes than either alone.

Do I need a prescription?

Yes. Go Long is prescription medication. You'll complete an online health assessment and consult with a licensed physician who'll determine if it's right for you.

Does this qualify for HSA/FSA?

Yes. Go Long qualifies for HSA and FSA reimbursement. Save your receipt and submit through your benefits provider.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Is my online consultation private?

Completely. Your consultation is HIPAA-compliant, conducted with a U.S.-licensed physician, and your prescription ships in discreet packaging.
