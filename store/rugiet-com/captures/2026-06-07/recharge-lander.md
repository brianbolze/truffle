## Frequently  asked questions

What is Recharge?

A prescription sleep medication combining three ingredients to help you fall asleep quickly and stay asleep through the night. Stronger than melatonin, safer than sedatives, designed for the nights when your brain won't shut off.

Is Recharge habit-forming?

No. Unlike traditional prescription sleep aids, Recharge is designed for occasional use without building dependency. Take it when you need it, up to four times weekly, and skip it when you don’t.

How is Recharge different from traditional prescription sleep meds?

Recharge is prescription sleep support built for better nights without the baggage of controlled sedatives.

Instead of using the same kind of sedative or anti-anxiety medication found in many traditional sleep prescriptions, Recharge is powered by ramelteon — a non-controlled active ingredient that works with your body’s sleep-wake signaling to help you fall asleep and stay asleep.

Is Recharge habit-forming like some sleep medications?

Recharge is designed to be non-habit forming. Its active ingredient, ramelteon, is not a controlled substance and is not associated with physical dependence, making it a different kind of prescription sleep option than many traditional sedative sleep meds.

Is Recharge safe to take with my other medications, or if I use a CPAP?

A licensed provider will review your health history, current medications, and any sleep conditions during your online visit to make sure Recharge is appropriate for you, including if you use a CPAP or have sleep apnea.

Can I use Recharge every night?

Recharge is designed for occasional sleeplessness, up to 4 nights per week. It gives you control over your sleep schedule without creating long-term dependence.

Will Recharge make me feel groggy the next day?

Many prescription sleep aids leave you groggy, Recharge is designed to deliver consistent sleep without the strange side effects.

Do I need a prescription?

Yes. A licensed provider will review your online assessment and determine whether Recharge is right for you.

Does this qualify for HSA/FSA?

Yes. Recharge qualifies for HSA and FSA reimbursement. Save your receipt and submit through your benefits provider.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?

Yes. No calls, no hoops. Cancel or pause directly from your account dashboard. We don't make this hard because we're confident you won't want to.
