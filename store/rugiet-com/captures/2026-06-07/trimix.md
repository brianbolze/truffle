#### What is Rugiet Ready?

Rugiet Ready is a combination of three ED medications - sildenafil, tadalafil, and apomorphine - in a sublingual dose.

Sublingual (under the tongue) absorption allows for faster, more consistent, and more efficient absorption of the medications in Rugiet Ready.

#### How is Rugiet Ready stronger than traditional ED treatments?

Traditional ED medications, known as PDE5 inhibitors, help maintain blood flow by blocking an enzyme (PDE5), but don't help boost the initial signal for an erection—often the real problem for more persistent ED.

In addition to PDE5 inhibitors, Rugiet Ready includes apomorphine, an ED treatment that works directly on the brain to boost arousal signals. This dual-action approach both initiates and maintains the signals needed for stronger, longer-lasting erections.

#### Should I try Rugiet Ready before penile injections like trimix?

Yes, Rugiet Ready is designed to be an excellent option to try before moving to injections. Its unique 3-in-1 formula often works for men who haven't had success with standard ED pills, potentially eliminating the need for injection therapy.

Penile injections like trimix are typically recommended only after oral ED pills stop working. While effective, they involve needles, precise dosing, and potential complications that many men prefer to avoid.

Rugiet Ready offers a powerful non-invasive alternative by combining brain-targeting apomorphine with traditional ED medications. This dual-mechanism approach addresses ED more comprehensively, providing stronger erections without the downsides of penile injections.

#### If Viagra/Cialis didn’t work for me, will Rugiet Ready?

![](https://www.rugiet.com/IMGS/icons/plus2.svg)

There are many people who have successfully treated their ED with Rugiet Ready, even after single-ingredient treatments did not yield results.

Rugiet Ready combines both sildenafil and tadalafil, which are short and long acting PDE5 inhibitors. It also includes a third ingredient, apomorphine. Because the medication dissolves under the tongue, it increases absorption directly into the bloodstream, making it work faster.

Furthermore, our physicians can customize doses of each medication specifically to each patient’s need. We cannot promise it will work for everyone, but our doctors will work with you to try to deliver optimal results while minimizing side effects.

#### How long does Rugiet Ready take to start working?

![](https://www.rugiet.com/IMGS/icons/plus2.svg)

Rugiet Ready comes in the form of a sublingual troche, which dissolves under the tongue and is directly absorbed into the bloodstream, bypassing the liver and digestive system. This allows for a typical onset time of 15 minutes or less. For best results, we recommend taking Rugiet Ready at least 30 minutes prior to sexual activity.

#### How long do the effects of Rugiet Ready last?

![](https://www.rugiet.com/IMGS/icons/plus2.svg)

Rugiet Ready's effects typically last 24-36 hours. For those with liver or kidney disease, the medication may remain in the body longer, so it's best to consult your doctor before use. Be aware that taking certain other medications alongside Rugiet Ready can extend its effects or potentially cause adverse effects.
