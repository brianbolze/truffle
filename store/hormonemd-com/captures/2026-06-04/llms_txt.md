\## Core Medical Protocols

\### Hormone Optimization
\\* \*\*\[Testosterone Replacement Therapy (TRT)\](/testosterone)\*\*: Comprehensive treatment for hypogonadism (Low T) to restore energy, muscle mass, libido, and vitality. Includes regular blood work monitoring.
\\* \*\*\[Estrogen Therapy (BHRT)\](/estrogen)\*\*: Bioidentical hormone replacement for women to effectively manage menopause symptoms, including hot flashes and night sweats.
\\* \*\*\[DHEA Therapy\](/dhea)\*\*: Precursor hormone therapy to support adrenal function, immune health, and mood stability.

\### Medical Weight Loss
\\* \*\*\[Semaglutide (GLP-1)\](/semaglutide)\*\*: Clinically proven GLP-1 receptor agonist treatment to suppress appetite, regulate blood sugar, and drive significant weight loss.

\### Longevity & Anti-Aging
\\* \*\*\[Rapamycin\](/rapamycin)\*\*: Advanced therapy focused on mTOR inhibition to potentially extend healthspan and delay age-related decline.
\\* \*\*\[Metformin\](/metformin)\*\*: Prescribed off-label for its potential benefits in insulin sensitivity, inflammation reduction, and metabolic longevity.
\\* \*\*\[Sermorelin\](/sermorelin)\*\*: A Growth Hormone-Releasing Hormone (GHRH) used to naturally stimulate the pituitary gland to produce HGH, aiding in sleep, recovery, and body composition.
