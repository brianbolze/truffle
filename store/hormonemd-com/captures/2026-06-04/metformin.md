What is Metformin?

Metformin is a widely used medication that improves insulin sensitivity and helps regulate blood sugar levels.

It has also shown promise as an anti-aging therapy by enhancing cellular health and reducing inflammation.

How does Metformin support anti-aging?

Metformin works by improving metabolic efficiency, reducing oxidative stress, and promoting cellular repair.

These effects help combat the molecular causes of aging, supporting better energy levels, weight management, and overall vitality.

Can Metformin help with weight management?

Yes, Metformin can support weight management by improving insulin sensitivity and reducing fat storage.

It is especially effective when combined with a healthy diet and exercise.

How often do I take Metformin?

Metformin is typically taken once or twice daily, depending on your individual prescription.

Your provider will customize the dosage based on your specific health needs.

Results vary, but many patients notice improved energy levels and metabolic benefits within a few weeks.

Anti-aging and long-term metabolic health improvements typically develop over several months of consistent use.

Is Metformin safe?

Yes, Metformin has been extensively studied and is considered safe when prescribed by a qualified provider.

Regular monitoring and professional guidance ensure its effective and safe use for anti-aging and metabolic health goals.
