## Learn about our DHEA restoration therapy

What is DHEA?

DHEA (dehydroepiandrosterone) is a hormone naturally produced by your adrenal glands. It acts as a precursor to vital hormones like estrogen and testosterone, helping maintain hormonal balance and overall health.

What are the benefits of DHEA?

DHEA supports hormonal balance, boosts energy levels, enhances mental clarity, improves bone density, and promotes overall vitality.

Who can benefit from DHEA replacement therapy?

DHEA therapy is ideal for individuals experiencing symptoms of hormonal imbalances, such as fatigue, low mood, reduced libido, or bone loss, particularly as they age.

How is DHEA taken?

DHEA is typically taken as an oral supplement or topical cream. Your provider will tailor the dosage to your specific needs.

How long does it take to see results with DHEA?

Many patients notice improvements in energy, mood, and overall well-being within a few weeks of consistent use. Bone density and hormonal balance improvements may take longer.

Is DHEA safe?

Yes, DHEA is safe when prescribed and monitored by a qualified provider. Regular lab testing ensures proper dosing and minimizes any potential side effects.

Can DHEA improve bone health?

Yes, studies show that DHEA can improve bone mineral density, particularly in older adults, reducing the risk of fractures and bone loss.

Does DHEA have anti-aging benefits?

Yes, DHEA helps maintain youthful hormone levels, promotes energy, and supports immune function, making it a popular choice for anti-aging therapy.
