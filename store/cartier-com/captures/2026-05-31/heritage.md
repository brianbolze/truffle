## Living Heritage

Since 1847, the Maison has explored its heritage with creativity. Discovery, drawing on world cultures, enhancing beauty: all are ways for Cartier to share its vision, as demonstrated by the numerous exhibitions of the Cartier Collection the world over, as well as Cartier’s bibliography.
