## Who is Numan?

Numan is a UK-based, [Care Quality Commission](https://www.cqc.org.uk/)(CQC)-regulated healthcare provider (Company No: 11440237) dedicated to extending human healthspan.

Founded in 2018, we integrate personalised medical treatments, advanced diagnostics, and clinical coaching to provide comprehensive care for metabolic health, hormonal balance, and longevity.

Our fully regulated online clinic brings together GMC-registered doctors, GPhC-registered pharmacists, and specialist clinicians. We make high-quality, evidence-based healthcare accessible from anywhere in the UK.

## Our prescribing process

Prescribing at Numan is a rigorous, four-step safety process, designed to ensure every prescription is medically appropriate and safe for your specific health needs.

Digital health consultation

You complete a detailed medical questionnaire reviewed by our clinical team. This captures your health history, current medications, and relevant lifestyle factors.

Clinical review

A registered doctor reviews your consultation and, if appropriate, issues a prescription. All prescriptions are made by named, regulated clinicians and are never automated.

Personalised treatment plan

If your clinician decides treatment is appropriate, they will put together a personalised plan. This may include a specific medication, a recommended dose, and guidance on how to use the treatment safely.

Secure dispensing and delivery

Your prescription is sent electronically to our GPhC-registered pharmacy. Your medicine is dispensed by a qualified pharmacist, packaged discreetly, and dispatched for delivery to your door.
