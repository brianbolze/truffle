GLP-1 medication

(Weekly injection or daily pill if clinically appropriate)

Prescription medication that reduces hunger and "food noise", helping you naturally eat less and maintain a calorie deficit without constant hunger or reliance on willpower. Numan currently offers [Mounjaro,](https://www.numan.com/weight-loss/mounjaro)[Wegovy injections](https://www.numan.com/weight-loss/wegovy), [Wegovy pills](https://www.numan.com/weight-loss/wegovy-pill), and [Alli (orlistat)](https://www.numan.com/weight-loss/alli). Your clinician will determine which is most appropriate for you.

Clinician-backed care

Our registered clinicians review your assessment and prescribe medication if appropriate. From there, they are available whenever you need them, whether that's to discuss how your treatment is working, request a dose adjustment, or raise a medical concern.

## Help if side effects arise

NauseaVomitingDiarrhoeaConstipation

The most common side effects are gastrointestinal, including nausea, vomiting, diarrhoea, and constipation. Side effects are reported by approximately 12–18% of patients3,4 and are usually temporary. If they persist, your clinician can adjust your treatment. For a complete list, see the Summary of Product Characteristics at medicines.org.uk. ## Guidance on long-term effects

We know long-term use raises questions. Obesity is a chronic condition, and for many people, ongoing treatment provides the best long-term outcomes. Your clinical team will work with you to make the right decisions for your health, both now and in the future.

\_
