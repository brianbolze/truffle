## What is TRT?

Testosterone replacement therapy is a treatment that helps raise low testosterone levels in men. It’s typically used when testosterone drops below a healthy range and starts to affect everyday life: things like energy, mood, sex drive, muscle strength, and motivation.¹

## How does TRT work?

Testosterone replacement therapy is a medical treatment provided only under clinical supervision.

### **1\. Restores testosterone levels**

TRT raises circulating testosterone back into a healthy range, increasing the amount available to act on tissues throughout the body.3

### **2\. Activates hormone pathways**

Testosterone works by binding to certain receptors, triggering cellular signals that influence energy, mood, muscle, bone, and sexual function.3

### **3\. Stability**

Different formulations release testosterone at controlled rates. Regular blood tests ensure levels stay balanced, effective, and safe over time.3

Changes don’t happen overnight, and everyone responds differently, but treatment is designed to support steady, clinically-monitored progress.4

## Who is eligible for TRT?

To be considered eligible for TRT, you must meet our criteria. Below are the testosterone level ranges to look out for after your Venous Blood Test.

While some clinics follow guidance that focuses on a threshold of 12 nmol/L, we know that some men can still experience symptoms even when their levels sit above that number. That’s why we look at your symptoms, your health history, and your blood results together, so any decision about treatment is thoughtful, balanced, and right for you.2

| --- | --- | --- |
| >15 | Normal | TRT is usually not recommended unless free testosterone levels are low and symptoms are present. |
| <15 | Low | Testosterone deficiency is likely. If you’re experiencing symptoms, TRT may be advised. |
