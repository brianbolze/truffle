### What causes hair loss?

Hair loss is increasingly common as you age but it can also affect men from an early age. There are several different types of hair loss but the most common is male pattern baldness which is mainly caused by genetics and the hormone dihydrotestosterone (DHT).

![How can I treat hair loss?](https://a.storyblok.com/f/273742/1200x1200/cece1294dc/james-park_034.png/m/364x350/filters:focal(603x304:604x305)/)

### How can I treat hair loss?

Some treatments prevent hair loss, especially when used early. Finasteride is a medication that targets the root cause of male pattern baldness. Minoxidil is an alternative treatment that protects the hair at the follicles. They can be used in combination to optimise results.

## The science behind hair loss treatments

Discover how clinically proven hair loss treatments counteract two main causes of male pattern baldness: dihydrotestosterone (DHT) and shrinking follicles.

![Why is hair lost](https://a.storyblok.com/f/273742/2218x1664/24f2ab03b7/hairloss_1.png/m/320x200/)

Why is hair lost?

DHT shrinks your hair follicles over time, causing hair to become thinner and shorter, eventually stopping growth altogether.

![How finasteride helps](https://a.storyblok.com/f/273742/2218x1664/a09025fe8c/hairloss_2.png/m/320x200/)

How finasteride helps

Finasteride blocks the enzyme that makes DHT. This prevents DHT from binding to hair follicles and causing irreversible damage.

![How minoxidil helps](https://a.storyblok.com/f/273742/2218x1664/fb0c29c20a/hairloss_3.png/m/320x200/)

How minoxidil helps

Minoxidil reactivates damaged follicles by increasing the supply of blood and nutrients. This helps your scalp replace thin, weak hairs with new, stronger ones.
