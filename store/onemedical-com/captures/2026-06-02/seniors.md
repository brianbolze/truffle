## Exceptional primary care for all ages

In One Medical offices, we’ll always take the time to listen to you, respect your opinion, and care for you as a whole person. We care for all ages — for everything from common illnesses to chronic diseases and mental health concerns. And because we’re a national practice, you can get care at 125+ offices around the country.

- ### Same/next-day appointments — in person or over video, for any concern

- ### Traveling soon? Go to any of our 125+ offices nationwide (insurance coverage varies)

- ### Drop-in lab services, including outside lab orders — no appointment, no problem.


We work with your insurance (yes, including Medicare) just like a regular doctor’s office, but offer so much more.
