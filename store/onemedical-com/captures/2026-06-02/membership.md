## A modern approach to primary care

This is no ordinary doctor’s office. Rather than do things the typical way, we’ve created a membership-based primary care practice truly designed around our patients’ needs.

We work with your [insurance](https://www.onemedical.com/insurance/) just like a regular doctor's office would, but we offer so much more. A One Medical membership makes it faster, easier, and more enjoyable to look after your health. All for just $199 a year. Membership subscription continues until canceled.
