Navigating the healthcare maze can feel overwhelming when you have a chronic condition, like diabetes or hypertension. That’s why we created the Impact by One Medical program. It combines a provider-led team of experts with smart technology to help you manage and prevent chronic conditions — so you have all the support and care you need to find your way to better health.

## What to expect from Impact

- ### **Long-term care for better long-term health**







You’ll work with a team of primary care providers, nurses, care navigators, and coaches to create a personalized care plan that changes as your needs change. This can include 1:1 coaching, group classes, educational resources, and, if necessary, coordinating care with specialists.

- ### **A personalized, guided experience**







Your care team will work closely with you to create a truly personalized care plan designed around your needs and help you overcome any obstacles. We can also sync data from wearable devices through Apple Health so your team can track your progress and see when you might need extra support.

- ### **A better understanding of your health**







We’ll help you better understand how different aspects of your health and lifestyle (like your diet, exercise, and mental health) are connected — helping you make more informed decisions with your provider.
