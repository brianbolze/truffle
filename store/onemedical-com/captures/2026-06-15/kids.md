## Real life care for real families

For all the parents who’ve missed an entire day of work to make it to the pediatrician. Who’ve turned to message boards to diagnose a 3 a.m. stomach bug. And who want the absolute best care for their children with less waiting, more communication, and easier access. There’s a new way to take your family to the doctor.

One Medical is real life care for real families — designed to fit your schedule, your needs, and your parenting philosophy. From same/next-day appointments that start on time to 24/7 Video Chat with providers who listen, we make getting exceptional care for your whole family as painless as possible.

- ### 24/7 peace of mind







We’re here for you whenever you need us. Book same/next-day appointments when your kid is sick or video chat with a provider anytime, day or night — right from your phone.

- ### Kid-approved offices







Our calming offices have ample stroller parking, modern books and toys, and comfy couches in the exam rooms to put everyone at ease and minimize those tantrums, tears, and bribes.

- ### Your family's home for healthcare







From babies who won’t latch to mystery illnesses to behavioral issues, we’re the place to turn for all aspects of your kid’s health. And if they need specialty care, we’ll get you a quick referral to top specialists.

- ### Convenient locations







Our offices are located where you live, work, and play, so you don’t have to rearrange your life for an appointment. And with on-site labs, lactation consultants, and more, you can avoid running all over town.

- ### A foundation for better health







We believe in teaching kids to be stewards of their own health. This means taking the time to listen to you and your child and explaining things in a way that everyone can understand. When children are engaged with their health from an early age, they grow into healthier, happier adults.

- ### Evidence-based care for the real world







Our providers approach your child’s health through data-backed science. But when the evidence says things like “screen time is terrible,” we may agree, but we aren’t going to guilt-trip you about a movie on a plane.

## FAQ

01. ### What should I expect from a pediatric visit?








    We’ve built a positive and collaborative wellness home for you and your family. When you walk into our office, you’ll be greeted by one of our friendly team members, who can help you coordinate your family’s healthcare, including in-office visits, camp or school physicals, and specialist referrals. You’ll spend your entire visit with a board-certified pediatrician, or a family provider who can care for your entire family.



    Our providers are eager to build strong and lasting relationships with their patients. They want you to feel comfortable sharing your parenting experiences during visits, emailing them questions, and calling our practice for questions about something as sudden as a fever or as routine as a well-child visit.

02. ### What’s the difference between family and adult offices?








    One Medical has two different office types:



    **Adult offices** have providers and staff ready to care for patients 14+.



    **Family offices** have pediatricians and specially trained teams who can see patients of all ages, in addition to having pediatric medical equipment and pediatric vaccines. Family offices often have extra amenities in the lobby, such as children’s books, toys, and stroller parking. There are also private Nurture Rooms for feeding newborns and infants.

03. ### Where can children under 14 get lab work done?








    Kids can get their labs done at our [Family offices](https://www.onemedical.com/locations/all/?pediatric_services=true&sees_seniors=false). The labs in these locations have the special pediatric inventory and specifically trained pediatric lab specialists needed to care for your child.

04. ### What lab services are available to children under 14 at Family Practice Offices?








    Blood draw, point of care testing (lead and hemoglobin), vaccines, hearing and vision screening, stool and urine testing, breathing treatments via nebulizer/metered-dose inhaler, ear lavage, EKG and PPD placement/reading.

05. ### What’s the difference between a pediatrician and a family medicine provider?








    **A pediatrician** sees patients up to age 21 years old. Pediatricians complete four years of medical school followed by a 3-year residency in pediatrics. They’re specialists in child medicine, so the scope of their practice is focused on care of children. Pediatricians emphasize preventive care and monitoring of growth and development for children through age 21 years. They diagnose, manage, and treat many common conditions affecting all organ systems as well as mental health.



    **A family medicine provider** sees all ages. Family medicine doctors complete a 3-year residency in family medicine after graduating from medical school. Family medicine doctors, physician assistants (PAs) and nurse practitioners (FNPs) are all trained to offer primary care services for patients of all ages, including preventive care, the management of acute and chronic illnesses, and mental health.

09. ### Can my child see the same provider as me?








    Many people like the convenience and holistic healthcare experience of having one provider for everyone in the family. Seeing the whole family gives a provider greater insight into the family’s health history, lifestyle, and values that inform their care. Because family providers have expertise in treating people of all ages, your child can continue seeing them as an adult. Other people prefer having a pediatrician who specializes just in caring for children. It’s really a matter of your personal preference. We’re confident that your kid will get exceptional care whatever you choose.

12. ### How do I register my baby for pediatric care with One Medical before they’re born?

13. ### Who can bring my child to their appointments?








    We recommend that a parent or legal guardian is present at all [well-child visits](https://www.onemedical.com/blog/healthy-living/your-guide-well-child-visits-what-expect-and-how-prepare/) to discuss their child’s health history as well as any concerns and recommendations the provider may have. As your child reaches adolescence, they’ll have [time alone with their provider](https://www.onemedical.com/blog/healthy-living/doctor-teen-confidentiality-why-its-important-teens-speak-their-provider-privately/) to discuss sensitive topics.



    If an adult other than a parent or legal guardian will be bringing your kid to their appointment, you will need to complete an authorization form.



    If your kid is 12 years or older, you will need to complete a consent form allowing them to attend visits without an adult present. Please note that state regulations allow adolescents to seek care without parental consent for certain medical issues.

14. ### What is One Medical’s stance on vaccines?








    We strongly support and follow the routine childhood vaccination schedule put forth by the Center for Disease Control (CDC), American Academy of Pediatrics (AAP), and American Academy of Family Physicians (AAFP). It is [our professional opinion](https://onemedical.com/blog/healthy-living/one-medical-vaccine-care-philosophy/) that immunizing your child fully and on schedule is important in keeping your child healthy and preventing devastating diseases. We welcome questions about immunization to help parents navigate any confusion around vaccines.

15. ### Is One Medical testing kids for COVID-19? Where can I find more resources about COVID-19?








    Keeping your kids healthy is a regular daily challenge — even without a pandemic. As local and state health authorities continue to issue new guidance about COVID-19 each day, you may be more worried about your kid’s health than usual. One Medical is here to help your family navigate the evolving situation with:





    **Testing**



    We test kids 14 and younger at all offices in locations where we offer pediatric services. Children over 14 can be tested at any One Medical location in all markets. All testing is by appointment.





    **Advice and care**



    If you have questions about your child’s symptoms, or they need to be examined, we suggest booking a COVID-19 Symptom Consult & Testing visit or a Sick Visit.





    COVID-19 testing appointments are for testing only, while COVID-19 Symptom Consult and Testing visits and Sick Visits include time to discuss symptoms, get your kid examined, and ask questions. Please note that if multiple kids are being tested, each one needs their own appointment, which can be booked with the One Medical app.





    Have an urgent concern? You can reach out to our virtual medical team, 24/7.
