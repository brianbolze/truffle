## FAQ

01. ### Is One Medical the same as insurance?








    One Medical is not insurance. One Medical is a primary care provider that accepts insurance like any other doctor’s office. We accept health plans from most insurance carriers as well as Medicare.

02. ### What insurance plans does One Medical accept?








    One Medical accepts health plans from most insurance carriers, select Medicare Advantage plans as well as Original Medicare and Medigap plans. Many of the plans we accept are listed above. Because the list isn’t exhaustive, and may not include smaller plans, it changes periodically. If you don’t see your plan listed, please call us to verify if your plan is accepted.



    Unfortunately, we aren’t able to accept Medicaid. Even if you pay for your membership fee out-of-pocket, Medicaid contracts are very specific about where you receive care. We won’t be able to provide care long-term because we won’t be able to authorize referrals and procedures, lab testing, ordering prescriptions, etc. For these reasons, we recommend establishing care with another provider who is in-network who can meet your health care needs.



    Everyone should be able to access quality health care, so if you have a high-deductible health plan or are not insured, we also offer affordable [options for working together based on financial criteria](https://www.onemedical.com/faq/membership-fee-alternatives/).

03. ### What if I don’t have insurance?








    If you do not have insurance, you can still get care with “self-pay”. You can pay for your in-office appointment or scheduled remote visits directly. The cost will vary based on your visit. We believe that quality health care should be accessible to everyone, so if you are not insured, we also offer affordable [options for working together based on financial criteria](https://www.onemedical.com/faq/membership-fee-alternatives/).



    Email admin@onemedical.com or call us at 202-627-1903 for more information on expected costs.

04. ### Are all providers covered by my insurance?








    Yes. If we accept your health plan, all One Medical providers work with your insurance.

05. ### How does insurance work? What is a co-pay? What is a deductible? What is co-insurance?








    **Health insurance** is a plan, or policy, you pay a premium for that helps cover some of the costs associated with healthcare, like doctors’ appointments, trips to the hospital if you get sick or injured, and medication. Health insurance often pays for preventive care like yearly checkups, vaccinations, and wellness programs.



    A **co-pay** is a fixed amount the patient pays for medical services. The remaining balance is covered by the patient's insurance company. Co-pays may vary within the same plans, depending on the service provided.



    A **deductible** is a fixed amount a patient must pay each year before their health insurance benefits begin to cover the costs. After your deductible has been met, patients typically pay coinsurance — a percentage of the total costs — for any services that are covered by the plan. They continue to pay the coinsurance until they meet their out-of-pocket maximum for the year.



    **Co-insurance** is the amount your insurance company will begin to pay (usually a set portion of call healthcare costs) once you reach your annual deductible.

06. ### How will my insurance company get billed?








    For in-office appointments and remote visits, we will bill your insurance. Your visit will be coded based on industry standard guidelines, which in turn creates a claim that we file electronically with your insurance carrier.



    To ensure proper and prompt processing of your claim, please verify the insurance information we have at the time of booking. If you're covered under two insurances, please let us know before your visit.



    Email admin@onemedical.com or call us at [202-627-1903](tel:202-627-1903) with any questions.

07. ### I’m 65+ years old and on Medicare. Does One Medical have an oﬀering for me?








    One Medical provides comprehensive primary care for seniors on Medicare who can receive care in-person. [Learn more about One Medical's primary care for seniors](https://www.onemedical.com/sixty-five-plus/sixty-five-plus-old/).

08. ### I only have Medicaid. Can I receive care from One Medical?








    Except for residents of Washington, DC, One Medical cannot care for individuals only on Medicaid.

09. ### Which ‘preventive’ procedures are typically covered by my insurance?








    Most insurance plans cover the costs associated with preventive care such as annual physicals and wellness exams. While most lab tests are covered by insurance, you may want to contact your insurance carrier to ensure coverage before having any tests completed.



    _Please note:_ Sick visits and procedures are usually billed towards your deductible/coinsurance or copay, as these are non-preventive services.

10. ### Does FSA/HSA cover the cost of a One Medical membership?








    The Annual Membership Fee is not a covered benefit under most health insurance plans or other healthcare benefit plans such as the Health Saving Account or Flexible Spending Account. Please contact your benefits manager or administrator to confirm.
