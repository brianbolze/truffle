## **Frequently asked questions**

What is Amazon One Medical?

Amazon One Medical is a modern approach to medical care—allowing people to get care on their terms, on their schedule. One Medical members and patients receive ongoing support for their healthcare needs, using the One Medical app to book in-office doctors’ appointments at locations near them, and to request 24/7 on-demand virtual care at no extra cost. Amazon One Medical On-Demand Care (formerly Pay-per-visit) gives Amazon customers the option to seek out one-time virtual care for 30+ common conditions.

How is One Medical Membership different from Amazon One Medical On-Demand Care (formerly Pay-per-visit)? Which should I choose?

Amazon One Medical gives people two ways to get medical care: Membership and On-Demand Care (formerly Pay-per-visit).

**Membership** provides a differentiated primary care experience if you live near a One Medical office. One Medical members can use the One Medical app to access 24/7 on-demand virtual care at no extra cost, including Direct Message Care. They can also use the app to message with their providers, easily request prescription refills and renewals, access their health records and care plans, and book same- and next-day appointments for In-Person or Video Visits. Scheduled Visits are billed to you or your insurance, with most major insurance plans accepted. One Medical memberships can be purchased by Prime members for $99/yr for the first member and $66/yr for each additional family member (maximum 5). If you're not a Prime member, One Medical memberships are available at [OneMedical.com](https://www.onemedical.com/) for $199/yr.

**On-Demand Care (formerly Pay-per-visit)** provides fast, convenient treatment for 30+ common health conditions through one-time virtual care. With On-Demand Care (formerly Pay-per-visit), you pay a flat, out-of-pocket fee starting from $29 for Direct Message Care or $49 for Video Care.\* If you don’t live near a One Medical office or if you already have a primary care provider, but you need a quick, one-time virtual treatment for a common condition, On-Demand Care (formerly Pay-per-visit) may be a good option for you.

_\*Direct Message Care availability varies by state. Prices subject to change._

Does Amazon One Medical take insurance?

Amazon One Medical accepts health insurance for Scheduled Visits, including In-Person Visits and Scheduled Video Visits, which are billed to your insurance with copays and deductibles applying. Amazon One Medical does not accept health insurance for One Medical membership and On-Demand Care (formerly Pay-per-visit), including Direct Message Care and Video Care.

If you use insurance to pay for your medications, you can do that with medications prescribed through all Amazon One Medical services.
