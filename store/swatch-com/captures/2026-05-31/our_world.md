# Discover the world of Swatch

Welcome to the Swatch universe, where we celebrate living life to the fullest through the spirit of sport, the creativity of art, the power of innovation and more. Each part of our world is a reflection of our passion for innovation, joy of life, positive provocation and creativity. Here, time is just the beginning.

## Swatch loves sports

From the start, we've been deeply connected to action sports, championing once unconventional pursuits like skateboarding and snowboarding and bringing them to the forefront. Our playful, provocative spirit led us to support pioneering athletes, sponsor groundbreaking events, and serve as official timekeepers worldwide, cementing sports as a core part of who we are.
