# Shaping the future of watchmaking with Swatch BIOCERAMIC

With BIOCERAMIC we’ve created a one-of-a-kind, composite material that stands out as one of the most innovative in the watchmaking industry. The unique blend combines two-thirds zirconium oxide ceramic powder, renowned in high-end watchmaking, with one-third biosourced material derived from castor oil, resulting in a material with unparalleled strength and softness.

![MoonSwatch Bioceramic](https://www.swatch.com/dw/image/v2/BDNV_PRD/on/demandware.static/-/Library-Sites-swarp-global/default/dwc601eff2/images/Swatch/brand/2024/bioceramic_refresh/Bioceramic_card1_desktop_v1.jpg)

## Why we love BIOCERAMIC and why you'll love it too

The unique composition of BIOCERAMIC makes for a very versatile and durable material. This allows us to get even more creative with our watch shapes, create our own colors, and develop watch components like cases, crowns, and bezels to bring you the very best of Swiss watchmaking.

Strong yet soft to the touch, this innovative material is extraordinary light and adapts to the body's temperature thanks to its thermal properties, making it super comfortable to wear.

All Swatch watches are proudly Swiss-made. The complex and delicate manufacturing process of BIOCERAMIC also takes place at our production facilities in Switzerland.
