# OMEGA X Swatch - The legend of the Moonwatch

In 2022, OMEGA and Swatch created an innovative Swatch take on the legendary OMEGA Speedmaster Moonwatch, the first watch on the Moon. This unexpected, provocative and visionary partnership gave rise to a collection of chronographs that has captivated people around the world, with its celestial charm.

Made from our innovative Bioceramic material in rarely seen colors, these watches feature all the hallmarks of the OMEGA Speedmaster Moonwatch, which thanks to this unique collaboration, is accessible to fans everywhere.

## What is Bioceramic?

Developed and patented by Swatch, Bioceramic is a composite material that stands out as one of the most innovative in the watchmaking industry. Its unique blend of 2/3 ceramic used in high-end watchmaking and 1/3 biosourced material derived from castor oil, creates a very robust material with a silky matte finish.
