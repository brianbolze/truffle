## FAQs

* * *

**How does Amazon Pharmacy work?**

Amazon Pharmacy is a program that helps patients and providers maintain easy access to prescriptions like Wegovy® Pill. Amazon Pharmacy requires a prescription from a doctor to access Rx medications like Wegovy® Pill. That’s where AgelessRx comes in.

AgelessRx provides a consultation to consider your request for a Wegovy® Pill prescription, along with ongoing healthcare monitoring for the duration of your prescription, if approved.

If your AgelessRx prescriber approves your prescription request, they will send your prescription directly to Amazon Pharmacy. From there, Amazon Pharmacy will text you directly to confirm your identity, complete payment, and manage your Wegovy® Pill deliveries.

The initial consultation and ongoing monitoring from AgelessRx costs $50/month. The cost of the medication is separate and paid directly to Amazon Pharmacy, not AgelessRx. **Please note that you will not receive your Wegovy® Pill delivery until you complete payment with Amazon Pharmacy**. If you pause or delay your refills for more than 14 days, your AgelessRx prescriber may ask that you restart your titration schedule.

**How much weight can I lose?**

[Clinical trials have shown](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2500969) that Wegovy® Pill users can lose up to 17% of their initial body weight. **Important safety information and common side effects**

* * *

Wegovy® Pill should be used with a reduced-calorie meal plan and increased physical activity.

Wegovy® Pill contains Semaglutide and should not be used with other GLP-1 or GIP receptor agonist medications, or any other products containing Semaglutide.

**Most common side effects**

Wegovy® Pill may have the following [side effects](https://www.mayoclinic.org/drugs-supplements/semaglutide-oral-route/description/drg-20492085): nausea, diarrhea, vomiting, constipation, abdominal (stomach) pain, headache, fatigue, dyspepsia (indigestion), dizziness, abdominal distension, eructation (belching), hypoglycemia (low blood sugar) in patients with type 2 diabetes, flatulence (gas buildup), gastroenteritis (an intestinal infection), and gastroesophageal reflux disease (a type of digestive disorder).

Common side effects normally go away on their own within a few days, or up to 2-3 weeks. If you find that any side effects persist for longer than this or get worse over time, stop taking Wegovy® Pill and contact your prescriber as soon as possible.

**Serious side effects**

Semaglutide has been associated with the following rare but serious side effects.

- Thyroid C-cell tumors
- Diabetic retinopathy
- Hypoglycemia
- Kidney problems
- Gallbladder problems
- Pancreatitis
- Worsening of mood

Your AgelessRx prescriber will weigh these potential risks against your unique medical history when considering a prescription. For a full list of potential warning signs for these conditions, please see the [Mayo Clinic’s Semaglutide resources](https://www.mayoclinic.org/drugs-supplements/semaglutide-oral-route/description/drg-20492085).

**Other important safety information**

Eligibility and approval for Wegovy® Pill is always at the discretion of your prescriber based on an assessment of your complete health profile. Approval is not guaranteed.

Do not use Wegovy® Pill if you are allergic to Semaglutide.

Do not use Wegovy® Pill if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, or planning to become pregnant or breastfeed.

Wegovy® Pill should not be used by anyone who has a personal or family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma (MTC), multiple endocrine neoplasia syndrome 2 (MEN2), or pancreatitis.

[Semaglutide may make oral contraceptives less effective](https://www.healthline.com/health/ozempic-and-birth-control). If you’re taking an oral contraceptive, ask your prescriber about potential alternatives before starting Semaglutide, or switch to a non-oral contraceptive.

The FDA is currently evaluating whether GLP-1 medications like Wegovy® Pill may increase the risk of suicidal thoughts or actions. Though [the FDA has found no evidence supporting this risk](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-safety-and-availability/update-fdas-ongoing-evaluation-reports-suicidal-thoughts-or-actions-patients-taking-certain-type), patients with a history of suicidal thoughts may be recommended an [alternative weight loss therapy](https://agelessrx.com/products-weight-management/).

Tell your healthcare provider about all the medicines you take, including prescription and over-the-counter medicines, vitamins, and herbal supplements. Wegovy® Pill may affect the way some medicines work, and some medicines may affect the way Wegovy® Pill works.

**How is my BMI calculated?**

Your Body Mass Index, or BMI, is calculated by dividing your total weight by the square of your total height. This provides a representation of your health relative to weight that is more accurate than weight alone, allowing healthcare providers to broadly categorize a spectrum of weight classes.
