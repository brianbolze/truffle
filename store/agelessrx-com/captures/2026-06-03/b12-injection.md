## The science of B12

**Clinically essential and widely studied:** Vitamin B12 plays a vital role¹¹ in red blood cell formation, neurological function, and mitochondrial energy production. First introduced in the 1950s, B12 injections have a long-standing record of safety and efficacy in treating deficiency and restoring metabolic health.

**Supports brain and cellular health:** B12 may support mitochondrial energy production, oxygen delivery, and neurological signaling¹¹—all of which helps support brain cell integrity and DNA repair. It has also been shown to lower homocysteine levels¹², a molecule linked to inflammation and cognitive decline, with longitudinal studies¹³ linking optimal B12 levels with improved cognitive function, mood and reduced risk⁴ of neurodegenerative disease.

### FAQs

* * *

**Why should I choose an injection over an OTC supplement?**

Injections have better bioavailability than oral supplements as they bypass the digestive system completely, providing a direct supply of B12 into the bloodstream to fuel your body. Oral supplements might work for some, however, as we age, our ability to break down oral supplements often decreases.

* * *

**How much B12 is administered per injection?**

The solution contains 1000mcg of B12 per mL/cc.

* * *

**How often do I administer the injections?**

Start by injecting 1ml/cc per injection once per week. If you feel you’re not getting your expected results, please [contact your prescriber](https://customer.agelessrx.com/Login).

**How can B12 shots help with longevity?**

B12 shots aren’t just a great way to enjoy more energy throughout the day. **Important safety information and common side effects**

* * *

The most common side effect of B12 injections is irritation at the injection site. If this occurs, consider changing placement on the body or use injections less often.

For a complete list of possible side effects, [click here](https://www.mayoclinic.org/drugs-supplements-vitamin-b12/art-20363663).
