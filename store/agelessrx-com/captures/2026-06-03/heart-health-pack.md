## FAQs

* * *

**What does the Heart Health Pack consist of?**

The Heart Health Pack is a combination of Douglas Labs Berberine Balance and Pure Encapsulations Garlic Complex 120 supplements. You will take both supplements during the course of treatment.

* * *

**How often will I take the supplements?**

You will take 1 Berberine Balance capsule and 2 Garlic Complex capsules twice daily with meals. Ideally, you will take your first dose after your first meal of the day (in the morning) and your second dose with dinner.

* * *

**How much Berberine and Garlic are in each capsule?**

Each Berberine Balance capsule contains 500mg of Berberine HCI (from Berberis aristata extract, root). Each Garlic Complex capsule contains 600mg of aged fermented black garlic extract (Allium sativum). Both capsules are vegetarian.
