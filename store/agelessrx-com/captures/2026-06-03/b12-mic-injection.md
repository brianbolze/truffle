**Important safety information and common side effects**

* * *

Most side effects of B12/MIC are generally mild in nature and dissipate within a short time frame following administration.

The most common B12/MIC side effect is pain, redness, or itching at the injection site. If irritation continues to occur, apply an over-the-counter itch cream, preferably with hydrocortisone, or discontinue use entirely.

In more rare yet severe cases, you may experience:

- Itching or a feeling of swelling all over the body
- Muscle cramps and weakness
- Upset stomach and urinary problems
- Swelling in the ankles or feet
- Unusual tiredness
- Irregular heartbeat or trouble breathing

If these side effects occur, consult your healthcare provider before continuing B12/MIC.

**Other important safety information**

Do not use B12/MIC if you are currently pregnant, breastfeeding or plan to become pregnant or breastfeed.
