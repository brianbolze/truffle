**How does the ordering process work for Zepbound®?**

AgelessRx offers a prescription consultation for Zepbound® single-use vials.

To get started, submit your medical intake to an AgelessRx prescriber, who will review your unique medical history and health goals to determine if Zepbound® is right for you. If approved, your AgelessRx prescriber will refer your prescription to LillyDirect®.

From there, you will receive text messages from LillyDirect®, who will prompt you to verify your identity and complete payment. Make sure to add injection supplies to check out, if prompted. Once payment is received, LillyDirect® will ship your prescription directly to your doorstep.

If approved, your AgelessRx prescriber will write you a new prescription each month according to the doctor-guided titration schedule that they will provide you. For most patients, you will stop increasing your dose after 4 months, unless you request to stay at your current dose.

**Please note**: you can pause your subscription at any time, but if you pause for more than 14 days after your previous prescription has ended, LillyDirect® may charge you a higher price.

LillyDirect® will text you directly to verify your personal information through a third-party platform called GiftHealth. **Please note that your prescription will not be shipped until you complete payment with LillyDirect®.**

**How quickly can I lose weight with Zepbound®?**

For the best results, Zepbound® should be taken regularly for at least [6-12 months](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2206038) with a reduced-calorie meal plan and regular exercise. [Clinical trials show](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2206038) an average of 15-20% weight loss within 72 weeks, or about 17 months, with many participants showing up to 8% weight loss within 12 weeks. However, this can vary depending on your health history, exercise level, and diet.

**Important safety information and common side effects**

* * *

Zepbound® should be used with a reduced-calorie meal plan and increased physical activity.

Zepbound® contains Tirzepatide and should not be used with other GLP-1 or GIP receptor agonist medications, or any other products containing Tirzepatide.

**Most common side effects**

Zepbound® may have the following [side effects](https://www.mayoclinic.org/drugs-supplements/tirzepatide-subcutaneous-route/side-effects/drg-20534045?p=1): nausea, diarrhea, vomiting, constipation, abdominal (stomach) pain, headache, fatigue, dyspepsia (indigestion), dizziness, abdominal distension, eructation (belching), hypoglycemia (low blood sugar) in patients with type 2 diabetes, flatulence (gas buildup), gastroenteritis (an intestinal infection), and gastroesophageal reflux disease (a type of digestive disorder).

Common side effects normally go away on their own within a few days, or up to 2-3 weeks. If you find that any side effects persist for longer than this or get worse over time, stop taking Zepbound® and contact your prescriber as soon as possible.

**Other important safety information**

Eligibility and approval for Zepbound® is always at the discretion of your prescriber based on an assessment of your complete health profile. Approval is not guaranteed.

Do not use Zepbound® if you are allergic to Tirzepatide.

Do not use Zepbound® if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, or planning to become pregnant or breastfeed. Stop taking Zepbound® at least 2 months before trying to conceive.

Zepbound® should not be used by anyone who has a personal or family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma (MTC), multiple endocrine neoplasia syndrome 2 (MEN2), or pancreatitis.

[Tirzepatide may make oral contraceptives less effective](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37940101/). If you’re taking an oral contraceptive, ask your prescriber about potential alternatives before starting Tirzepatide, or switch to a non-oral contraceptive.

The FDA is currently evaluating whether GLP-1 medications like Zepbound® may increase the risk of suicidal thoughts or actions. Though [the FDA has found no evidence supporting this risk](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-safety-and-availability/update-fdas-ongoing-evaluation-reports-suicidal-thoughts-or-actions-patients-taking-certain-type), patients with a history of suicidal thoughts may be recommended an [alternative weight loss therapy](https://agelessrx.com/products-weight-management/).

Tell your healthcare provider about all the medicines you take, including prescription and over-the-counter medicines, vitamins, and herbal supplements. Zepbound® may affect the way some medicines work, and some medicines may affect the way Zepbound® works.
