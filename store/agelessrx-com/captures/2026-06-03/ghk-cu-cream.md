## FAQs

**What is GHK-Cu Cream?**

GHK-Cu (Copper Tripeptide-1) is a naturally occurring peptide found in the body that supports skin repair and renewal processes, including collagen production and overall skin quality.4 Levels of GHK-Cu naturally decline with age.1 While GHK-Cu is not FDA-approved to treat medical conditions, it is commonly used in cosmetic dermatology for skin appearance and rejuvenation.2

* * *

**How does GHK-Cu Cream work?**

GHK-Cu Cream works locally within the skin layers (epidermis and dermis).1 Research suggests it may help support collagen and elastin production, promote tissue repair and remodeling, help regulate inflammatory signaling in the skin, support skin barrier integrity, and provide antioxidant activity within the skin.5 7 This treatment is intended for external skin use only.

**Important safety information and common side effects**

* * *

#### Side effects

The most common side effects of GHK-Cu Cream include:

- Mild redness
- Temporary tingling or warmth
- Dryness
- Mild irritation or sensitivity

These side effects are typically mild and often go away on their own within a week or two.

For a full list of side effects, please review [Healthline’s Copper Peptide resources](https://www.healthline.com/health/copper-peptides).

#### Potential drug interactions

GHK-Cu Cream has no known major drug interactions due to systemic absorption. However, certain skincare products may increase irritation when used together:

- **Retinoids** (Tretinoin, Adapalene)

May increase skin sensitivity.

- **Exfoliating acids**(AHA/BHA products)

May cause irritation when layered together.

- **Benzoyl peroxide** May increase dryness or irritation.

#### Other important safety information

GHK-Cu Cream is intended for external use only. Do not use GHK-Cu Cream if you are allergic to copper peptides or any peptide components, or if you have an active skin infection, active inflammatory dermatologic condition, or open wounds.

Avoid contact with eyes and mucous membranes. If accidental contact occurs, rinse thoroughly with water.

Do not use GHK-Cu Cream if you have an active cancer, or if you have had cancer within the last two years.

Do not use GHK-Cu Cream if you have significant heart dysfunction (ejection fraction <50% or NYHA Class 3 or greater).

Do not use GHK-Cu Cream if you are pregnant, breastfeeding, or planning to become pregnant or breastfeed. If you become pregnant during treatment, discontinue immediately and consult your OB.

Though rare, allergic reactions to GHK-Cu Cream are still possible. If you notice any shortness of breath or swelling around your face, tongue, or throat after using GHK-Cu Cream, stop use and contact emergency services right away.

As with any prescription medication, the dose prescribed is for you and you alone. Do not, under any circumstances, allow anyone else to use your GHK-Cu Cream.
