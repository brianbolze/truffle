## FAQs

* * *

**How does Tran-Q Sleep work?**

Each of the ingredients in Tran-Q Sleep works together to increase GABA activity in the body. [Gamma-aminobutyric acid (GABA)](https://my.clevelandclinic.org/health/articles/22857-gamma-aminobutyric-acid-gaba) is an inhibitory neurotransmitter that works by blocking certain signals sent between the brain and the central nervous system. GABA helps to calm hyperactive neurotransmitters that keep us alert, awake, and sometimes anxious. Increasing GABA activity in the body can produce a calming effect that may reduce anxiety and improve quality, restorative sleep.

* * *

**How long does it take for Tran-Q Sleep to work?**

Most users report feeling the calming effects of Tran-Q Sleep within 30-60 minutes of taking 1-2 tablets. Results may vary based on your dose, metabolism, or unique medical background.

* * *

**Will Tran-Q Sleep make me groggy?**

Tran-Q Sleep is a natural supplement that [has no known side effects](https://www.metagenics.com/tran-q-sleep?utm_source=Facebook+&utm_medium=Metagenics_Social+&utm_campaign=Metagenics_Social+&utm_content=DrBlakeSleepSeries&srsltid=AfmBOopSbLsHwqZmiOdMDRDmqKUn6y3JcFB-7TrCu7bERjZ_RhVQfXKl#!reviews), but its ingredients may make some users experience mild drowsiness after waking up. Though the risk of next-day drowsiness is low, users should be cautious to observe how they feel in the morning within the first few weeks of starting Tran-Q Sleep. If you do feel groggy after taking Tran-Q Sleep, avoid driving, operating heavy machinery, or performing other hazardous tasks requiring concentration and coordination.

**Important safety information and common side effects**

**Most common side effects**

There are [no known side effects of Tran-Q Sleep](https://www.metagenics.com/tran-q-sleep?utm_source=Facebook+&utm_medium=Metagenics_Social+&utm_campaign=Metagenics_Social+&utm_content=DrBlakeSleepSeries&srsltid=AfmBOopSbLsHwqZmiOdMDRDmqKUn6y3JcFB-7TrCu7bERjZ_RhVQfXKl#!reviews) when taken as recommended in the doses provided. However, the individual ingredients of Tran-Q Sleep may cause some mild side effects in certain people: [passionflower](https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/passionflower#:~:text=What%20Do%20We%20Know%20About,(ataxia)%20in%20some%20people.) may cause drowsiness and confusion, while [jujube extract](https://www.netmeds.com/health-library/post/ber-jujube-nutrition-health-benefits-ayurvedic-uses-recipes-and-side-effects#:~:text=Side%20Effects,-Jujube%20or%20ber&text=However%2C%20if%20you%20are%20taking,and%20malaise%20in%20rare%20cases.) and [lavender](https://www.webmd.com/vitamins/ai/ingredientmono-838/lavender) may cause drowsiness, diarrhea, or constipation. Any side effects should be mild and go away on their own within a week or two, as your body adjusts to the medication.

In rare cases, some individuals may experience an allergic reaction to the ingredients of Tran-Q Sleep. Allergic reactions may cause fever, rash, hives, swelling, or difficulty breathing. Do not take Tran-Q Sleep if you know you are allergic to any of its ingredients.

Those taking Tran-Q Sleep should avoid driving or other activities requiring mental acuity or coordination until they are sure side effects such as drowsiness and confusion will not affect them.

**Other important safety information**

Tran-Q Sleep may affect the way some medicines work, including antidepressants, blood thinners, and blood pressure medications. Tell your healthcare provider about all the medicines you take, including prescription and over-the-counter medicines, vitamins, and herbal supplements.

Do not take Tran-Q Sleep if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, or plan to become pregnant or breastfeed while taking Tran-Q Sleep.

Do not take Tran-Q Sleep if you are allergic to passionflower, jujube, poppy, or lavender.

Tran-Q Sleep is an over-the-counter supplement that does not require a prescription. However, your AgelessRx provider may recommend an alternative solution if you are currently taking medications that may interact with Tran-Q Sleep.
