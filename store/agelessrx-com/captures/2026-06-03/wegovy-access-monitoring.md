## FAQs

* * *

**How does NovoCare work?**

NovoCare is a program that helps patients and providers maintain easy access to prescriptions like Wegovy®. NovoCare requires a prescription from a prescriber to access Wegovy®. That’s where AgelessRx comes in.

AgelessRx provides a consultation to consider your request for a Wegovy® prescription, along with ongoing healthcare monitoring for the duration of your prescription, if approved.

If your AgelessRx prescriber approves your prescription request, they will send your prescription directly to NovoCare. From there, NovoCare will text you directly to confirm your identity, complete payment, and manage your Wegovy® deliveries.

The initial consultation and ongoing monitoring from AgelessRx costs $50/month. The cost of Wegovy® is $199 for the first two months for first time patients, then $349/month for all doses. The cost of the medication is separate and paid directly to NovoCare, not AgelessRx. **Please note that you will not receive your Wegovy® delivery until you complete payment with NovoCare**. If you pause or delay your refills for more than 14 days, your AgelessRx prescriber may ask that you restart your titration schedule.

* * *

**How much does Wegovy® cost?**

Wegovy® costs $199 for the first two months for first time patients, then $349 per month after for all doses, no matter where you’re at in your journey. The ongoing prescription access, consultation, and health monitoring from AgelessRx costs $50 per month. Together, the total monthly charge for Wegovy® is $249 for the first two months for first time patients, then $399 per month after.

Please note that AgelessRx only charges for prescription access for Wegovy® and ongoing health monitoring. Pricing and shipping of Wegovy® is not provided by AgelessRx or any of its pharmacy partners.

The cost may be subject to change at any time according to NovoCare. However, we have no indication that the price will change at this time.

**How much weight can I lose?**

[Clinical trials have shown](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2032183#sec-2) that Wegovy® users can lose up to 15% of their initial body weight. For a 200lb individual, that equals up to 30lbs. However, individual results may vary based on your prescribed dose, medical history, and lifestyle choices. For the best results, use Wegovy® with a reduced-calorie meal plan and regular exercise. If you feel that you’re not achieving your expected results, [contact your prescriber](https://customer.agelessrx.com/Login?ReturnUrl=%2f&_gl=1*juom2g*_gcl_au*MTg5MDc0MzY4LjE3MjA0NTE2MTY.*FPAU*MTg5MDc0MzY4LjE3MjA0NTE2MTY.) to see what’s best for you.

* * *

**How quickly can I lose weight with Wegovy®?**

For the best results, Wegovy® should be used regularly for at least 6-12 months with a reduced-calorie meal plan and regular exercise. [Clinical trials show](https://www.nejm.org/doi/full/10.1056/NEJMoa2032183#sec-2) an average of 15% weight loss within 68 weeks, or about 15-16 months, with many participants showing up to 8% weight loss within 12 weeks. However, this can vary depending on your health history, exercise level, and diet.

It’s okay to stop taking Wegovy® if, for example, side effects become too difficult to manage, as long as you tell your prescriber first. If you do abruptly stop taking Wegovy®, please note that your appetite and weight may return just as quickly. Do not stop taking Wegovy® for more than 14 days, or you may be asked to restart your dosing schedule.

**Important safety information and common side effects**

* * *

Wegovy® should be used with a reduced-calorie meal plan and increased physical activity.

Wegovy® contains Semaglutide and should not be used with other GLP-1 or GIP receptor agonist medications, or any other products containing Semaglutide.

**Most common side effects**

Wegovy® may have the following [side effects](https://www.mayoclinic.org/drugs-supplements/semaglutide-oral-route/description/drg-20492085): nausea, diarrhea, vomiting, constipation, abdominal (stomach) pain, headache, fatigue, dyspepsia (indigestion), dizziness, abdominal distension, eructation (belching), hypoglycemia (low blood sugar) in patients with type 2 diabetes, flatulence (gas buildup), gastroenteritis (an intestinal infection), and gastroesophageal reflux disease (a type of digestive disorder).

Common side effects normally go away on their own within a few days, or up to 2-3 weeks. If you find that any side effects persist for longer than this or get worse over time, stop taking Wegovy® and contact your prescriber as soon as possible.

**Serious side effects**

Semaglutide has been associated with the following rare but serious side effects:

- Thyroid C-cell tumors
- Diabetic retinopathy
- Hypoglycemia
- Kidney problems
- Gallbladder problems
- Pancreatitis
- Worsening of mood

Your AgelessRx prescriber will weigh these potential risks against your unique medical history when considering a prescription. For a full list of potential warning signs for these conditions, please see the [Mayo Clinic’s Semaglutide resources](https://www.mayoclinic.org/drugs-supplements/semaglutide-oral-route/description/drg-20492085).

**Other important safety information**

Eligibility and approval for Wegovy® is always at the discretion of your prescriber based on an assessment of your complete health profile. Approval is not guaranteed.

Do not use Wegovy® if you are allergic to Semaglutide.

Do not use Wegovy® if you are pregnant or breastfeeding, or planning to become pregnant or breastfeed.

Wegovy® should not be used by anyone who has a personal or family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma (MTC), multiple endocrine neoplasia syndrome 2 (MEN2), or pancreatitis.

[Semaglutide may make oral contraceptives less effective](https://www.healthline.com/health/ozempic-and-birth-control). If you’re taking an oral contraceptive, ask your prescriber about potential alternatives before starting Semaglutide, or switch to a non-oral contraceptive.

The FDA is currently evaluating whether GLP-1 medications like Wegovy® may increase the risk of suicidal thoughts or actions. Though [the FDA has found no evidence supporting this risk](https://www.fda.gov/drugs/drug-safety-and-availability/update-fdas-ongoing-evaluation-reports-suicidal-thoughts-or-actions-patients-taking-certain-type), patients with a history of suicidal thoughts may be recommended an [alternative weight loss therapy](https://agelessrx.com/products-weight-management/).

Tell your healthcare provider about all the medicines you take, including prescription and over-the-counter medicines, vitamins, and herbal supplements. Wegovy® may affect the way some medicines work, and some medicines may affect the way Wegovy® works.

**_Note: This product alone is not intended to diagnose, treat, cure, or prevent any disease. Please consult a medical professional to determine whether or not GLP-1 products are appropriate for you._**

**How is my BMI calculated?**

Your Body Mass Index, or BMI, is calculated by dividing your total weight by the square of your total height. This provides a representation of your health relative to weight that is more accurate than weight alone, allowing healthcare providers to broadly categorize a spectrum of weight classes.
