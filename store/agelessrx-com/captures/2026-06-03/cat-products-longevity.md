## FAQs

What is longevity?

Longevity means living as long and as healthily as possible. It means spending more time with the people you love, doing the things you love. It means staving off age-related disease and frailty to the very last sliver of our lives.

Longevity science shares these same goals, and AgelessRx shares the goals of longevity science. Together with leading longevity scientists and expert medical prescribers, we’re dedicated to bringing the latest in longevity science directly to your doorstep, so you can make longevity your reality.

What is healthspan and how is it different from lifespan?

Where lifespan is the length of time we live, healthspan is the length of time we live _in good health_. That means free of disease, frailty, and other age-related conditions that limit our quality of life. Think of healthspan vs. lifespan as quality vs. quantity. When it comes to longevity, it’s not enough to have long years if they’re spent in poor health, and not enough to live healthily if we don’t live long. The goal of longevity is both health span and lifespan, to enjoy both a long life _and_ a healthy life.

What is biological age and how can I check it?

Biological age is a measure of the body’s cellular health that reflects how fast we’re truly aging. While chronological age simply measures the length of time since our birth, biological age considers the lifestyle, environmental, and genetic factors that cause our skin, organs, cells, and DNA to age. In this way, biological age is a more accurate representation of how we’re aging than chronological age.

What are the most important lifestyle factors for longevity?

Regular exercise, quality sleep, social connection, stress management, and healthy diet. It’s also important to avoid unhealthy lifestyle factors like tobacco smoking and excessive alcohol consumption. By including all these factors in your daily life, you can take control of your health for years to come.

Don’t my genes determine my health and longevity? Why should I bother?

Experts agree that our [genes only influence about 20% of longevity](https://mcpress.mayoclinic.org/healthy-aging/how-much-do-genetics-influence-the-aging-process/). That means 80% of our health is within our control—all we need is the right combination of lifestyle choices and medical guidance!
