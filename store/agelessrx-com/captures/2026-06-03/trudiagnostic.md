* * *

**How is this test different from genetic testing?**

Other genetic tests, such as 23andMe, simply look at the genetic sequencing of an individual, which you have no control over. With an epigenetic test, such as TruAge™, you can address the modifiable parts of your DNA (gene expression) with lifestyle changes to better take control of things like disease risk and rate of aging.

As an example, think about a light bulb and its components. Looking at the way the light bulb is constructed is like looking at our DNA sequence (genetics). However, looking at our epigenetic modifications is like looking at if the light bulb is on or off.
