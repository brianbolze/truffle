**How does Tadalafil work?**

## A science-backed approach to longevity

- Tadalafil is a PDE5 inhibitor that blocks the protein responsible for regulating cGMP, the molecule that regulates healthy circulation, brain function, and blood vessel widening.¹
- cGMP, or cyclic guanosine monophosphate, stimulates blood flow to organs throughout the body, hence its success for treating ED when taken as needed.²
- As we age, our cGMP levels decline, contributing to compromised organ function, ED, chronic inflammation, and age-related diseases.³
- By blocking PDE5, research shows that Tadalafil helps restore cGMP to youthful levels, protecting against age-related decline for both men and women. ⁴

**How do I take Tadalafil?**

When taking Tadalafil for longevity purposes, you will take a single Tadalafil tablet (2.5 or 5 mg, as prescribed) with a glass of water at any time—day or night, with or without food.

* * *

**What are the benefits of taking Tadalafil daily versus as needed?**

Taking Tadalafil daily at a lower daily dose (2.5mg or 5mg) reduces many common side effects and allows the freedom to be intimate on your own time without having to plan ahead.

The benefits go far beyond sexual health. When taken daily over the course of many years, Tadalafil may offer numerous longevity-enhancing benefits. Recent clinical research suggests that Tadalafil’s ability to improve blood flow may [reduce the risk of heart disease](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC9037966/) and even lower the risk of [dementia](https://www.neurology.org/doi/full/10.1212/WNL.0000000000209131#con1). These benefits stem from Tadalafil’s primary mechanism of action of improving blood flow.

As-needed tadalafil is dosed at 10mg and 20mg typically, but should only be taken no more frequently than once every 3 days. These higher, less-frequent doses are only used for erectile dysfunction in men and are not expected to have much in the way of longevity benefits.

**Important safety information and common side effects**

* * *

As with any drug product, there may be side effects. Generally, patients who experience side effects usually get them within 12 to 24 hours after taking Tadalafil. Side effects usually resolve on their own within 48 hours. Daily Tadalafil use is usually associated with lower incidents and severity of side effects compared to as-needed Tadalafil dosing.

**Most common Tadalafil side effects**

- Headache
- Indigestion
- Back pain
- Muscle pain
- Flushing
- Stuffy nose

Some more serious side effects have been reported. While rare, it is important to stop taking Tadalafil and contact a doctor right away if any of the following occur:

- An erection will not go away (priapism). If you have an erection that lasts longer than 4 hours, get medical help right away. If not treated right away, priapism can permanently damage the penis.
- Sudden vision loss in one or both eyes. Sudden vision loss can be a sign of a serious eye problem called non-arteritic anterior ischemic optic neuropath (NAION).
- Sudden hearing decrease or hearing loss. Some may also have ringing in the ears (tinnitus) or dizziness.

For a complete list of possible side effects, [click here](https://www.mayoclinic.org/drugs-supplements/tadalafil-oral-route/proper-use/drg-20067204?p=1#:~:text=Tadalafil%20works%20on%20the%20PDE5,the%20workload%20of%20the%20heart.).

**Possible drug interactions**

Some drugs/medications may interact with Tadalafil. You should not take Tadalafil if you are taking nitrates (such as nitroglycerin tablets or patches) because the combination of these with Tadalafil could significantly lower blood pressure and lead to fainting or even death. Additionally, do not take Tadalafil if you are taking Cisapride, other ED medications (e.g. Viagra), Adcirca, or Riociguat.

For a complete list of possible drug interactions, [click here](https://www.drugs.com/drug-interactions/tadalafil-index.html).

**Other important safety information**

Patients who have a condition called “left ventricular outlet obstruction” from valvular problems or heart muscle enlargement may get side effects such as fainting or light-headedness. Because patients with recent heart attacks or stroke, heart pains (angina), heart failure, uncontrolled blood pressure or uncontrolled irregular heart beats, severe liver disease, and retinal eye problems have not been studied, Tadalafil (Cialis) is not recommended for these patients. There may be rare occurrences of priapism or painful, prolonged erections. This is a serious condition that requires immediate medical attention. Patients should seek prompt medical attention if their erection lasts longer than four hours.

After taking a single tablet, Tadalafil can remain in the body for up to two days. It can remain longer if patients have problems with their kidneys or liver, or they are taking certain other medications.
