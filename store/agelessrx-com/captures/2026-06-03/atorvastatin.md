## FAQs

* * *

**How does Atorvastatin work?**

Atorvastatin is a member of a class of prescription medications called ‘statins’ that work to reduce the amount of cholesterol in your liver and help remove cholesterol already in the blood. As a result, it helps to lower your overall cholesterol levels.

* * *

**How often will I need to take Atorvastatin?**

Atorvastatin is taken once daily around the same time. Your dose can be taken either with or without food, on a full or empty stomach.

* * *

**How do I know if Atorvastatin is working?**

Most statins, like Atorvastatin, require approximately 4-6 weeks to help cholesterol levels stabilize. You will complete a blood test within the first 3-6 months of treatment to check cholesterol levels (e.g. LDL, HDL, Total Cholesterol and Triglycerides) to identify measurable improvements.

**Important safety information and common side effects**

* * *

Like all medications, Atorvastatin may cause side effects in some patients. Generally mild in nature, side effects are likely to improve after the first few days or weeks as your body gets used to the medication.

**Atorvastatin side effects**

- Muscle weakness
- Cold-like symptoms
- Nausea or indigestion
- Headache
- Constipation
- Sore throat

The above list is only a summary. For a full list of possible side effects, [click here](https://www.mayoclinic.org/diseases-conditions/high-blood-cholesterol/in-depth/statin-side-effects/art-20046013). Talk to your medical professional about any long-term or severe side effects.

**Other important safety information**

Do not take Atorvastatin if you currently take another statin, niacin-laropiprant, verapamil, beta-blockers, diltiazem or diuretics. Do not consume grapefruit juice while taking Atorvastatin.

Do not take Atorvastatin if you are pregnant, breastfeeding, or planning to become pregnant or breastfeed within the next six months.

Do not take Atorvastatin if you are allergic to it or any of its ingredients.

Consult with a medical professional prior to use if you are taking other medications for hypertension, diabetes, or thyroid conditions.

Do not stop taking Atorvastatin without first consulting your medical professional.

Most medical professionals also highly recommend you supplement Atorvastatin with 100mg/day of CoQ10.
