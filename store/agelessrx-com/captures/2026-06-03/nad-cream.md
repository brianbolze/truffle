## Background and history

- NAD stands for Nicotinamide Adenine Dinucleotide
- Skin requires higher amounts of NAD+ to repair damage from daily external (UV) and age-related stressors
- Signs of skin aging are linked to NAD+ depletion
- Topical NAD+ helps to protect from UV damage/sensitivity and improves signs of aging (wrinkles, age spots, hyperpigmentation)
- Many skin creams use Niacinamide in hopes it will boost NAD+ levels—but none contain actual NAD+
- A [2006 clinical study​](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17035720/) suggests “topical NAD+ therapy can be a potential alternative to the conventional treatment of psoriasis without side effects”

## FAQs

* * *

**How do I apply the cream and how often should I use it?**

Splash water on your face to moisten your skin, then apply an even layer once before bed. Allow the cream enough time to absorb and dry before adding any other cosmetic creams or products.

* * *

**How long before I start to see results?**

Likely, you will see initial benefits of brightness within 30-minutes to 1-hour of using the product. Longer-term benefits, such as wrinkle reduction and improved texture, will result with prolonged use.

* * *

**Is it safe to apply make-up over my face cream?**

Yes. Most of our patients report that their make-up goes on smoother after they have applied the cream (and let it dry first).
