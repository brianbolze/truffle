## FAQs

**What is the Stelo Glucose Biosensor?**

The Stelo Glucose Biosensor is a pioneering over-the-counter (OTC) device, approved in early 2024. Since the summer

of the same year, it’s been available to buy without a prescription across the United States.

It is designed for a broad audience — for example, people with type 2 diabetes who are not on insulin, those with

prediabetes, and even those who want to learn more about how their eating habits affect their glucose levels. As one

of the first non-prescription glucose biosensors, Stelo offers a simple, no-frills option for beginner glucose monitoring.

- **Accuracy:** Stelo provides reliable glucose readings. While sensors are designed to last up to 15 days, [note that around 20 percent](https://www.stelo.com/faqs/using-stelo/how-long-can-i-wear-my-stelo-glucose-biosensor) may not last the entire duration, and 10 percent may only last for 12 days.
- **App Compatibility:** The Stelo app integrates with Apple Health, providing basic glucose tracking without advanced analytics. When paired with Nutrisense, users benefit from deeper data insights and better tracking options

**What are the benefits of using a Glucose Biosensor?**

A glucose biosensor like [Stelo](https://www.stelo.com/how-it-works) offers a window into how your body responds to what you eat, how you move, how you

sleep, and how you manage stress.
