## What is PhenoAge?

- Based on a UCLA study of 11,432 adults that identified **9 blood markers** to calculate your Phenotypic Age
- Multi-system aging measure that quantifies biological age using observable characteristics in routine bloodwork
- Correlates biological age to your risk of all-cause mortality
- Easily repeated bi-annually to gauge effectiveness of lifestyle changes and longevity interventions
- Online calculator is FREE to use, as long as you provide blood markers (if not, this lab-based test will provide them to you)
