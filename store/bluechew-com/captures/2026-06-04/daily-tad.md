Tadalafil is the FDA-approved active ingredient in DailyTAD. It has many medical uses, but it’s mainly prescribed to help patients achieve harder and longer-lasting erections. When it comes to enhancing-erections, tadalafil is one of the top recommended medications with some of the [best results](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/pmc/articles/PMC5603624/) as reported by both patients and their partners.

**Sexual Benefits**

- Boosts erections by [increasing blood flow](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/36779746/#:~:text=Conclusions%3A%20A%20small%20dose%20of,but%20rather%20as%20complex%20entities.) to the penis.

Tadalafil has gained attention for its growing list of potential health benefits.
