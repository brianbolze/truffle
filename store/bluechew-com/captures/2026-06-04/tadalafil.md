How does BlueChew TAD work?![Expand](https://bluechew.com/assets/svg/icons/uparrow.svg)

Tadalafil belongs to a class of medications known as [phosphodiesterase-5 inhibitors](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK549843/) or PDE5i for short. It works by blocking the action of an enzyme called phosphodiesterase-5 (PDE5). This enzyme normally breaks down a molecule called cyclic guanosine monophosphate (cGMP), which is crucial for releasing nitric oxide (NO). When NO is released, it helps to dilate blood vessels. What does this mean for you? When you take tadalafil, you can expect harder and longer-lasting erections that are easier to achieve when you become sexually aroused. This boost can lead to better sex and increased confidence in the bedroom.
