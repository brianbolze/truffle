What is the difference between SIL, TAD, and VAR?

Sildenafil and vardenafil have a shorter (4-6 hour) active time frame.

Tadalafil remains active in the body much longer (up to 24-36 hours in most cases), resulting in a longer window of opportunity for intimate moments. This extended timeframe may increase spontaneity and be best for patients who want to have flexibility in their sex lives.

On the other hand, sildenafil and vardenafil might be a better choice for patients anticipating a special occasion like a date night or anniversary.

Who is BlueChew for?

BlueChew is for patients who are 18 years or older, are living in the United States, and want stronger and longer-lasting erections.

Are the medications offered through BlueChew FDA approved?

The medications offered through BlueChew are compounded. The FDA does not approve or evaluate the safety or effectiveness of compounded medications.

The medications offered through BlueChew contain the active ingredients sildenafil, tadalafil, vardenafil, caffeine, apomorphine, and oxytocin. Sildenafil is the active ingredient in Viagra®. Tadalafil is the active ingredient in Cialis®. Vardenafil is the active ingredient in Levitra®.

Do I need a prescription to use BlueChew?

Yes. Your BlueChew-affiliated healthcare provider will write you a prescription if medically appropriate, after reviewing your medical profile.

Important safety information

Do not take sildenafil (sildenafil citrate), tadalafil, or vardenafil if you take medication containing nitrates or guanylate cyclase stimulators for pulmonary hypertension, as this could cause an unsafe drop in blood pressure.

I tried the product, but it did not work for me. What can I do?

If you tried one of our medications but it didn't work well for you, we recommend trying one of our other options as everyone responds to specific medications in different ways.

BlueChew offers different active ingredients and dose options to suit your specific needs. If one treatment did not work as expected, and you are not sure which one to try next, please reach out to your medical team via email at [support@bluechew.com](mailto:support@bluechew.com).

Unfortunately, BlueChew cannot accept returns for prescription treatments. If you have any questions or are dissatisfied with the treatment you received, we encourage you to contact our support team at [support@bluechew.com](mailto:support@bluechew.com) or via chat on our website. For subscription plans, you authorize recurring charges to your selected payment method at your chosen interval. Billing occurs at the start of each cycle at the then-current standard price and includes product, shipping, and applicable taxes. We will provide at least 3 days' notice prior to any changes in standard pricing.

You may cancel anytime through your account by selecting "Put On Hold" or by contacting [support@bluechew.com](mailto:support@bluechew.com). To avoid your next charge, submit your request at least 24 hours before your renewal date.

Yes. Although we partner with our own compounding pharmacies to offer fair prices and quick turnaround times for BlueChew customers, you are welcome to use a different compounding pharmacy if you prefer.

For assistance in requesting a digital prescription to take to your chosen pharmacy, please email us at [medical@bluechew.com](mailto:medical@bluechew.com).
