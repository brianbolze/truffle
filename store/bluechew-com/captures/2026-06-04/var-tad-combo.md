Better blood flow means harder and longer-lasting erections when you take Tadalafil.

This medication kicks in after approximately 30 minutes and can keep you going for an impressive 24 to 36 hours.

With Tadalafil, there's no pressure and no time constraints - just around-the-clock erection-boosting effects, giving you the edge over spontaneity in your sex life.

How does BlueChew VMAX work?![Expand](https://bluechew.com/assets/svg/icons/uparrow.svg)

This combination medication contains two ingredients geared towards enhancing your erections: 14 mg of Vardenafil and 18 mg of Tadalafil.

These medications both belong to a class of drugs known as PDE5Is, which work by stopping the effects of an important enzyme in the body - phosphodiesterase 5. When this enzyme is blocked, it causes the accumulation of a molecule termed cyclic guanosine monophosphate (cGMP), essential for releasing nitric oxide (NO).

When NO is released, it makes blood vessels relax, ultimately boosting blood flow.

So, what does this mean for you? When you take Var + Tad Max Combo, you can anticipate harder and longer-lasting erections that are easier to achieve when you get sexually aroused. This erection enhancement can lead to better sex and amped up confidence in the bedroom.
