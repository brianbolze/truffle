How does BlueChew VAR work?![Expand](https://bluechew.com/assets/svg/icons/uparrow.svg)

Vardenafil belongs to a class of medications known as [phosphodiesterase-5 inhibitors](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/books/NBK549843/) or PDE5i for short.

It works by blocking the action of an enzyme called phosphodiesterase-5 (PDE5). This enzyme normally breaks down a molecule called cyclic guanosine monophosphate (cGMP), which is crucial for releasing nitric oxide (NO). When NO is released, it helps to dilate blood vessels.

By stopping PDE5 from breaking down cGMP, vardenafil increases the levels of NO. This relaxes the smooth muscles in blood vessels, allowing more blood to flow to the penis.

What does this mean for you?

When you take vardenafil, you can expect harder and longer-lasting erections that are easier to achieve when you become sexually aroused. This boost can lead to better sex and increased confidence in the bedroom.
