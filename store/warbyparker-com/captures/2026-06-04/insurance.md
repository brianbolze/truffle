# Out-of-network benefits

You pay upfront and then get reimbursed by your carrier.

[Learn how to submit a claim](https://www.warbyparker.com/reimbursement)

How it works:

![](https://img.warbyparker.com/v2/payment-1002dbeedfc145c992a5793c367495ee.svg?originWidth=24&originHeight=24)

Pay full price at checkout

![](https://img.warbyparker.com/v2/prescription-810573d1b6db4c299946aa942ca17eaa.svg?originWidth=24&originHeight=24)

Obtain an itemized receipt (either from your account or by contacting us)

![](https://img.warbyparker.com/v2/success_checkmark_blue-3134d05381e041efab212a31b10cee73.svg?originWidth=24&originHeight=24)

Submit a claim form and your itemized receipt to your carrier
