## As seen at Google I/O 2026

At I/O, Google shared a deeper look at our forthcoming Intelligent Eyewear, launching this fall. These glasses are designed by Warby Parker and built with Google’s AI, alongside Samsung’s leadership in hardware engineering.

In the sneak peek, they revealed our first design—with a deeper dive on the inspiration and process from our co-founders and co-CEOs, Dave Gilboa and Neil Blumenthal—and demonstrated how Warby Parker Intelligent Eyewear will work to help you throughout your day-to-day: understanding your context, connecting the dots, and providing seamless hands-free support.
