### About UserTesting

At UserTesting, we bring real human feedback to the forefront, helping Product, Marketing, Digital, and CX teams validate every decision, co-innovate at scale, and craft customer experiences that feel personal, scalable, and human. Powered by the world’s strongest participant network, AI-driven insights, and expert services, we help organizations create seamless, intuitive experiences that truly connect with customers.

Rated #1 by G2, UserTesting is headquartered in Bellevue, Washington, and has over 3,000 customers including 75 of Fortune 100.
