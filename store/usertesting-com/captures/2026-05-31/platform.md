## Frequently asked questions

### What is the UserTesting Human Insight Platform and how does it work?

The UserTesting Human Insight Platform helps teams gather fast feedback on digital experiences before, during, and after launch.

UserTesting provides flexible ways to test:

- Idea designs
- Prototypes
- Websites
- Apps
- Campaigns
- Competitive experiences

This is so that you can understand how people think, feel, and behave. The platform combines methods like Live Conversations, usability testing, surveys, and other research tools, then helps teams analyze feedback and turn it into action.

Explore the [UserTesting Feedback Engine](https://www.usertesting.com/platform/feedback-engine) to see how UserTesting supports testing across the customer journey.

### How fast can I get feedback on UserTesting?

UserTesting is designed to help you get feedback quickly. Depending on your audience and study type, teams can move from question to insight in hours rather than waiting through slower research cycles. To learn more about audience access and speed, visit [Audience Solutions](https://www.usertesting.com/platform/network).

### Who is the UserTesting Human Insight Platform for?

The UserTesting Human Insight Platform is built for cross-functional teams including UX researchers, product managers, marketers, and designers, along with broader enterprise teams working across customer experience and digital experience.

UserTesting provides team-specific workflows, templates, and sharing capabilities so organizations can scale insights across departments instead of keeping research siloed. You can explore solutions for [UX teams](https://www.usertesting.com/solutions/teams/ux-research), [product management](https://www.usertesting.com/solutions/teams/product-management), [marketing](https://www.usertesting.com/solutions/teams/marketing), and [design](https://www.usertesting.com/solutions/teams/design).

### Does UserTesting use AI to help analyze results?

Yes. UserTesting provides AI-powered features that help teams synthesize large volumes of video, text, and behavioral data to surface themes, patterns, and insights faster. UserTesting AI is built to accelerate time to insight, while analytics and visualization features help teams identify trends and interpret findings more efficiently. To learn more, see [UserTesting AI](https://www.usertesting.com/platform/AI) and [analytics and visualizations](https://www.usertesting.com/platform/analytics-visualizations).

### What tools and apps does UserTesting integrate with?

UserTesting integrates with a range of tools such as Figma, Jira, FigJam, and other platforms that help teams bring customer insight into the systems they already use every day. You can browse the full [integrations and plugins page](https://www.usertesting.com/solutions/integrations), or explore the [Figma integration](https://www.usertesting.com/solutions/integrations/figma) and [Jira integration](https://www.usertesting.com/solutions/integrations/jira) for more detail.

### How does UserTesting help reduce product rework and development costs?

UserTesting helps teams catch friction, confusion, and usability issues earlier. UserTesting cites business impact from a 2025 Forrester Consulting Total Economic Impact study it commissioned, reporting 415% ROI, $9.4M in savings and benefits over three years, and a payback period under six months. For more context, see [how prelaunch testing cut 25% of iterations](https://www.usertesting.com/blog/reduce-guesswork-prelaunch-testing) and the [Forrester TEI study](https://www.usertesting.com/resources/reports/forrester-study-total-economic-impacttm-usertesting-human-insight-platform).
