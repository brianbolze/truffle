## Plans

Plans determine how your team uses and pays for UserTesting. Each plan gives you access to your edition’s capabilities, with differences in usage, scale, and feature access.

## Test-based Consumption

- Pay based on test usage across your team​
- Flexible scaling based on research needs
- Unlimited users across the enterprise (no per-seat charges)
- Access to all capabilities included in your edition
- Best for teams getting started or with variable research needs

![Hero Images for Jobs to be Done Pages](https://usertesting.widen.net/content/fd4a5a57-e3ed-4a7d-ab68-0b6f6e38a3e8/web/HERO-2025-jtbd-Digital-Exp-Usability-Testing-725x512.png?v=fd4a5a57-e3ed-4a7d-ab68-0b6f6e38a3e8)

## Team-based Unlimited

- Unlimited tests within a defined enterprise scope
- Predictable pricing for scaling across teams and geographies
- Access to all capabilities included in your edition
- Includes automatic access to select capabilities (e.g., UserTesting for Figma plugin)
- Best for organizations scaling insights across multiple teams

## Frequently asked questions

Why choose UserTesting?

UserTesting enables organizations to understand what it’s like to be their customer with a video-first experience testing platform and access to a global opt-in network of test participants. Through UserTesting’s Human Insight Platform, anyone in an organization can quickly get a vivid, first-person understanding of any experience and build greater customer empathy.

[Learn more](https://www.usertesting.com/start-here-form)
