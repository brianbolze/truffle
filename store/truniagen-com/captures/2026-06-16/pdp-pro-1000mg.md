NAD+ (Nicotinamide Adenine Dinucleotide) is a crucial coenzyme found in every cell of your body that powers hundreds of metabolic processes. It serves as a key player in cellular energy production, DNA repair, and cellular communication. NAD+ works by transferring electrons during cellular metabolism, enabling your cells to convert nutrients into energy and supporting essential functions that keep your body running optimally.

NAD+ levels naturally decline from birth, accelerating around age 30, and dropping by up to 65% by age 70. Environmental and lifestyle stressors also diminish NAD+ levels, as do immune and health challenges.
