## Frequently Asked Questions

What is NAD+ and how does it work?


NAD+ (Nicotinamide Adenine Dinucleotide) is a crucial coenzyme found in every cell of your body that powers hundreds of metabolic processes. It serves as a key player in cellular energy production, DNA repair, and cellular communication. NAD+ works by transferring electrons during cellular metabolism, enabling your cells to convert nutrients into energy and supporting essential functions that keep your body running optimally.

What are the potential benefits of taking NAD+ supplements?


NAD+ levels naturally decline from birth, accelerating around age 30, and dropping by up to 65% by age 70. Environmental and lifestyle stressors also diminish NAD+ levels, as do immune and health challenges. Taking a clinically proven supplement like Tru Niagen helps maintain your NAD+ baseline levels with daily use—to keep your body running smoothly as you age.

What is the difference between Tru Niagen and other NAD brands on the market?


Tru Niagen is a superior choice compared to other NAD supplements because it contains Niagen®, our patented nicotinamide riboside (NR), a more bioavailable precursor to NAD+. Niagen® NR is backed by 35+ human clinical studies, and crosses cell membranes easily and is more efficiently converted into NAD+ inside cells, effectively boosting NAD+ levels.

This enhances energy metabolism, supports cellular repair, and promotes healthy aging, making Tru Niagen a more reliable, high-quality, pure, and potent option for raising NAD+ compared to other supplements. Tru Niagen® is clinically shown to increase NAD+ levels by over 50% with 300mg daily and by up to 150% with 1000mg daily and is backed by third-party quality-testing from NSF Certified for Sport and Alkemist Labs.
