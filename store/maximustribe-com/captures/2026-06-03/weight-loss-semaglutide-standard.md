## How it works.

Semaglutide, an FDA approved GLP-1 receptor agonist, mimicks the hormones that regulate blood sugar levels and appetite.

![](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/qag74ilj/production/4caadc91eb3bfec62dc7c56f666c305e70ebf9f3-752x752.jpg?auto=format&q=90&w=1100&fit=max)

↑ Insulin Secretion

Semaglutide stimulates the pancreas to release insulin when blood sugar levels are high, helping to lower blood glucose.

![](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/qag74ilj/production/cd96af4284f1c7b62b0eaf6691aa40bfcce6dd65-752x752.jpg?auto=format&q=90&w=1100&fit=max)

↓ Appetite Cravings

Semaglutide affects areas of the brain that regulate appetite, leading to reduced hunger and increased feelings of fullness. This effect makes Semaglutide particularly useful for weight loss.

![](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/qag74ilj/production/95a62eb4a80a69cba09806abd793b382994aa26f-752x752.jpg?auto=format&q=90&w=1100&fit=max)

↓ Weight Loss

In addition to helping regulate blood sugar, Semaglutide has been shown to help people lose significant amounts of weight by reducing caloric intake.

![](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/qag74ilj/production/5f4b68c4bba9e7f65925e64c2d28356e190891e4-752x752.jpg?auto=format&q=90&w=1100&fit=max)

↓ Gastric Emptying

Semaglutide slows down the rate at which the stomach empties its contents into the intestines, which helps reduce the rate at which sugar is absorbed into the bloodstream.

## Side effects

Contact your provider immediately if you have severe abdominal pain that does not go away (possible pancreatitis); a lump/swelling in your neck, hoarseness, or trouble swallowing (possible thyroid tumor); severe vomiting or diarrhea with signs of dehydration; symptoms of low blood sugar; or signs of a serious allergic reaction. Regular monitoring may be needed to track your response to treatment.

Compounded medications are not FDA-approved for safety, efficacy, or quality.

### Common side effects may include:

- nausea
- diarrhea
- vomiting
- constipation
- decreased appetite
- abdominal pain
- fatigue

### Rare, but serious side effects may include:

- severe gastrointestinal events leading to dehydration and kidney injury
- pancreatitis (severe abdominal pain)
- gallbladder disease
- severe allergic reactions
- hypoglycemia when used with insulin or a sulfonylurea
- diabetic retinopathy complications in those with type 2 diabetes
