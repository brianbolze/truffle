### Who is eligible for GLP-1 weight loss treatment?

GLP-1 weight loss medications may be appropriate if you’re trying to lose weight, reduce appetite, improve blood sugar control, or support long-term metabolic health.

After you sign up, a Maximus doctor will review your medical history and determine whether Tirzepatide or Semaglutide is safe and appropriate for you.

If you’re not approved for treatment, you’ll receive a full refund (minus the cost of the doctor consultation).

### What states is Weight Loss Protocol available in?

Our Weight Loss Protocol — including Semaglutide and Tirzepatide options — is available nationwide in all states but AL, MS, LA.

### Can I use my HSA or FSA card to pay for Weight Loss Protocol?

Yes. Maximus accepts both HSA and FSA cards for weight loss treatment.

Simply enter your HSA/FSA card information at checkout as you would any debit or credit card.

Coverage varies by plan, so if you're unsure whether your HSA/FSA approves GLP-1 medications, check with your plan administrator.

### If I’ve previously been prescribed GLP-1 medication, can I start at the same dosage with Maximus?

If you have documentation of prior GLP-1 use, your Maximus provider will help determine the most appropriate dose for your treatment plan.

### Do you charge extra for higher doses?

We offer flat-rate monthly plans that include access to all dose tiers.

Your Maximus provider will help you determine the safest and most effective dose for your goals.

### What side effects should I be aware of?

Common side effects of GLP-1 medications include:

\- Nausea

\- Diarrhea

\- Vomiting

\- Constipation

Side effects are typically mild and temporary. If you experience discomfort, your Maximus doctor may adjust your dosing schedule or recommend supportive strategies.

### How long until I see results?

Most people notice:

\- Reduced appetite within the first few days

\- More stable cravings within 1–2 weeks

\- Visible weight loss within several weeks once medication reaches steady-state levels

Results vary based on your dose, metabolism, and adherence to habits like diet and physical activity.

### Are GLP-1 medications safe for long-term use?

GLP-1s like Semaglutide and Tirzepatide have been extensively studied for long-term use in weight, metabolic, and diabetes management.

Your Maximus provider will monitor your health markers and adjust your treatment plan as needed.

### Can this be used with other Maximus Protocols?

Yes. The Weight Loss Protocol works well alongside other Maximus protocols -including Testosterone, Hair Growth, Blood Flow, and Oxytocin Calming Cream — as part of a comprehensive health optimization plan.

Your provider will ensure there are no contraindications.

### Is the Weight Loss Protocol available for both men and women?

Yes — Maximus weight loss treatments are available and effective for both men and women.

### Is this compounded with base form or salt form?

Maximus uses only the base form of Semaglutide and Tirzepatide — the same form studied, tested, and approved in clinical trials.

Some pharmacies use salt forms (e.g., “Semaglutide Sodium” or “Semaglutide Acetate”), which are different chemicals, are not equivalent, and have not been tested for safety or effectiveness.

Using salt-form compounds may be unsafe or ineffective.
