### Who is Oxytocin Calming Cream best for?

Oxytocin can be a great fit for adults looking for support with mood, stress, focus, emotional balance, or sleep. It’s especially helpful for people with busy, high-performing lifestyles, those feeling overstimulated or “on edge,” or anyone wanting a natural, non-sedating way to feel more grounded and well-rested.
