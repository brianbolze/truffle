## Frequently asked questions

### What makes Maximus different from other online clinics?

Most telehealth companies are prescription delivery services — they match you with a provider and ship medication. Maximus is a performance medicine company. We actively conduct clinical research on our own protocols, publish outcomes, and develop proprietary formulations like our patent-pending Oxytocin Calming Cream. Our protocols are designed by board-certified clinicians affiliated with institutions like UCSF, UNC, and UCLA — and every prescription is tailored to your biomarkers and goals, not a one-size-fits-all template.

### What is "performance medicine"?

Performance medicine is healthcare designed around optimization, not just treating illness. Traditional medicine waits until something is wrong. We help you improve how you feel, look, and perform — whether that's restoring energy and drive, improving body composition, managing stress, or maintaining hair growth. You don't need a clinical deficiency to benefit. You just need to want more from your health.

### How does Maximus ensure the quality and safety of its protocols?

Every protocol we offer is backed by clinical evidence we believe in — if we haven't seen strong data supporting a treatment's efficacy, we don't offer it. We conduct our own clinical studies and publish the results. Our medical team includes specialists from Harvard, UCSF, UNC, Tulane, and Cedars-Sinai. And we hold our pharmacy partners to the highest compounding and quality standards in the industry.

### What protocols does Maximus offer?

We offer clinically validated protocols across several areas of performance health: testosterone optimization (with fertility-preserving options), medical weight loss (GLP-1 and GIP therapies), hair regrowth, blood flow and sexual health, stress and sleep support, growth hormone peptides, comprehensive lab testing, and foundational nutrition. Your clinician will recommend the right approach based on your labs, health history, and personal goals.

### How do we select our pharmacy partners?
