## What you'll learn from the test.

![total testosterone levels](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/qag74ilj/production/e239b6ec6398877873a09d0f0cd65e3319a5695f-2240x504.jpg?auto=format&q=90&w=1100&fit=max)

Total Testosterone

Total testosterone refers to the overall amount of the hormone in the bloodstream, including both bound and unbound forms.

![](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/qag74ilj/production/df0a91b94620d22f3415b1e30ec63d7ff401a8b2-438x18.svg)

Normal Range: 300-1000 ng/dL

![free testosterone levels](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/qag74ilj/production/0886543681b7543d142bb7c36a07dbb5a0a5c455-2240x504.jpg?auto=format&q=90&w=1100&fit=max)

Free Testosterone

Free testosterone is the bioavailable unbound form of testosterone. It is the key biomarker to track in optimizing health through testosterone therapy.

![](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/qag74ilj/production/c6a906feb71c87f9b5cae2fdb0f07de6ea02b17f-438x18.svg)

Normal Range: 46.0-224.0 pg/mL

![shbg testosterone levels](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/qag74ilj/production/83f62fc7906d16065e9a9f8d5540e5ad10e2c8be-2240x504.jpg?auto=format&q=90&w=1100&fit=max)

Sex Hormone Binding Globulin

Sex hormone binding globulin (SHBG) is a protein that regulates the amount of testosterone available in the body by binding to testosterone in your blood.

![](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/qag74ilj/production/bccfe0026882a9bb9428a96305362c9530a987d0-438x18.svg)

Normal Range: 10-50 nmol/L

![testosterone to estradiol](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/qag74ilj/production/cc7c41890644e6c8f4fd8c1dcba39f2655d2ab55-2240x504.jpg?auto=format&q=90&w=1100&fit=max)

Estradiol (E2)

A form of estrogen that men need in small amounts. It helps with bone health and libido but high levels can lead to unwanted fat gain or mood changes.

![](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/qag74ilj/production/ca3f2fa316a00aea74eda8435bae57a3ebe528cb-438x18.svg)

Normal Range: ≥39 pg/ml

![luteinizing hormone testosterone](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/qag74ilj/production/b55040754e599399718f530debe8ecdd9a97cbdc-2240x504.jpg?auto=format&q=90&w=1100&fit=max)

Luteinizing Hormone

A hormone secreted by the anterior pituitary gland that helps regulate the reproductive system by stimulating Leydig cells to produce testosterone.

![](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/qag74ilj/production/088b147bba812da5019028a9dbcd8d9851e3b8f5-438x18.svg)

Normal Range: 1.5-9.3 mIU/mL

![follicle stimulating hormone testosterone biomarker](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/qag74ilj/production/6db5470352ff80b000aaf0de1ba702111d646d0e-2240x504.jpg?auto=format&q=90&w=1100&fit=max)

Follicle-Stimulating Hormone

FSH (Follicle-Stimulating Hormone) is key for men’s reproductive health. It helps regulate sperm production by stimulating the testes.

![](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/qag74ilj/production/f53d32254d6383651679623965b16ed82fa2cb5d-438x18.svg)

Normal Range: 1.4-12.8 mIU/mL

![prostate-specific antigen testosterone](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/qag74ilj/production/9275a291fbed12df558c93fd8ce41ef3bb92b027-2240x504.jpg?auto=format&q=90&w=1100&fit=max)

Prostate-Specific Antigen (PSA)

A protein made by the prostate. High levels can indicate prostate issues, including enlargement or cancer risk.

![](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/qag74ilj/production/5cfeed7eeb2df52d325863e3b4d5b575a68bdc2a-438x18.svg)

Normal Range: ≥2.5 pg/ml

![hematocrit testosterone biomarker](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/qag74ilj/production/18be2089d494143c2172d16407dca01e799c634e-2240x504.jpg?auto=format&q=90&w=1100&fit=max)

Hematocrit

Hematocrit measures the proportion of red blood cells in the blood. These markers are monitored to maintain a balance between improved oxygen delivery and efficient circulation.

![Hermatocrit](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/qag74ilj/production/b147119d99759060c88fd67adf3e89b5ec83feea-438x18.svg)

Normal Range: 38-54%

![hemoglobin testosterone](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/qag74ilj/production/b20182299e65f1c5af3ea59fbc5dd6838c812d78-2240x504.jpg?auto=format&q=90&w=1100&fit=max)

Hemoglobin

Hemoglobin is the protein in red blood cells that carries oxygen throughout the body. This marker maintain a balance between oxygen delivery and efficient circulation.

![](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/qag74ilj/production/d994de221e811e2f403e8b65461237d36a0b7a0a-438x18.svg)

Normal Range: 13.2-18 g/dl

![albumin testosterone](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/qag74ilj/production/7f57f4d55f3415ba81aa54d04fb987edbe92579c-2240x504.jpg?auto=format&q=90&w=1100&fit=max)

Albumin

Albumin is the main blood protein made by the liver, helping maintain fluid balance and transport hormones/nutrients. Low levels can signal liver issues, inflammation, or poor diet.

![](https://cdn.sanity.io/images/qag74ilj/production/cd45ff20dc51c06218e6c1fe531dd5b227616fcc-438x18.svg)

Normal Range: 3.6–5.01g/dL

Our at home blood testing kits, [dubbed Tasso devices](https://www.tassoinc.com/), utilize a small lancet instead of needles. The vacuum draws blood out of the capillaries and into a compatible container attached to the bottom of the testing device. Maximus customers frequently report how easy and nearly painless the device is to use.
