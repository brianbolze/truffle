What are Vardenfail, Sildenafil, and Tadalafil?

Vardenafil, Tadalafil, and Sildenafil are potent prescription PDE5 inhibitor medications used to increase blood flow for stronger, longer-lasting intimacy and improved circulation to your muscles to support better workouts and recovery, plus better circulation to your brain for enhanced focus.

How do PDE5 inhibitors work?

PDE5 inhibitors are prescription medications that improve blood flow by blocking the enzyme phosphodiesterase type 5 (PDE5). They're commonly used to treat erectile dysfunction (ED), but also enhance overall circulation, supporting better sexual performance, workout endurance, and daily focus. By increasing nitric oxide signaling, PDE5 inhibitors like sildenafil, tadalafil, and vardenafil improve whole-body oxygenation, especially the muscles and the brain—leading to sharper focus, improved mood, and greater mental clarity. This makes PDE5 inhibitors powerful tools for optimizing your sexual, physical, and mental performance.

Are PDE5s right for me?

Anyone looking to enhance sexual or workout performance, improve daily stamina, and boost mental focus through better vascular function may benefit from PDE5 inhibitors. While the benefits of PDE5 inhibitors are not limited to those with pre-existing conditions, they’re especially effective for those with age-related vascular decline, or underlying conditions like hypertension or diabetes that impair circulation. PDE5 inhibitors are also ideal for men experiencing erectile dysfunction and reduced sexual performance. Always consult a medical provider to determine if PDE5 inhibitors are right for your health profile and goals.

Disclaimer: Erectile Dysfunction Treatment: HSA Eligibility

ERECTILE DYSFUNCTION TREATMENT: REQUIRES A PRESCRIPTION TO BE ELIGIBLE WITH A HEALTH SAVINGS ACCOUNT (HSA)

Erectile dysfunction treatment medicines are eligible and may qualify for reimbursement with a prescription with a flexible spending account (FSA), health savings account (HSA), or a health reimbursement arrangement (HRA). Erectile dysfunction treatment medicines are not eligible for reimbursement with a dependent care flexible spending account (DCFSA) or a limited-purpose flexible spending account (LPFSA).

What are erectile dysfunction treatment medicines?

There are several types of medicine available for the treatment of erectile dysfunction. Erectile dysfunction can be the result of a variety of different medical conditions related to the brain, nerves, muscles, and blood vessels. Stress and mental health concerns can exacerbate or worsen these issues, or cause erectile dysfunction separately (Urology Care Foundation).

Medicines for the treatment of erectile dysfunction include pills that increase the effect of nitric oxide which aids in obtaining an erection due to sexual stimulation. Other forms of medication include injections, urethral suppositories, testosterone hormone replacement therapies, penis pumps, penile implants, and blood vessel surgeries (Mayo Clinic).

A psychologist or counselor may also aid in treating erectile dysfunction that is a result of a psychological or mental health condition. Regardless of which treatment medicine is used, a prescription must be obtained for reimbursement eligibility under a consumer-directed savings account.
