## The art of watchmaking

![Grand complications](https://img.alange-soehne.com/page-push-responsive-1/18b7e4d9dc4b989c0cbb36baaf5029cd19d4c9c4.jpg)

Grand complications

Grand complications represent the pinnacle of precision watchmaking. Discover the most complicated timepieces of A. Lange & Söhne.

[Read more](https://www.alange-soehne.com/us-en/timepieces/selections/grand-complications)

![Manufacture movements](https://img.alange-soehne.com/page-push-responsive-1/f7bb5d42beabf70c172905357a25cdf6c776c274.jpg)

Manufacture movements

Every A. Lange & Söhne timepiece is equipped with a manufacture movement. To be more precise: all calibres are developed, crafted, finished and assembled in our manufacture in Glashütte.

[Read more](https://www.alange-soehne.com/us-en/manufacture/art-of-watchmaking/manufacture-movements)

![Jumping numerals mechanism](https://img.alange-soehne.com/page-push-responsive-1/4271cb357750c678b6647144b5434e3b35611835.jpg)

Jumping numerals mechanism

The ZEITWERK is a mechanically driven digital-display watch.

[Read more](https://www.alange-soehne.com/us-en/manufacture/art-of-watchmaking/jumping-numerals-mechanism)
