## Have questions? We’re here to help

### Where do I get my blood drawn?

It’s easy! After you check out, we’ll email you your lab order and a link to book your appointment at a nearby, Quest, or BioReference Labs location (for those in NY and NJ).

Want extra convenience? If available in your area, you can upgrade to an in-home blood draw for a $100 fee — a phlebotomist will come right to your door.

* * *

### What is the best way to prepare for my lab test?

To ensure the most accurate results, follow these preparation guidelines:

- Fast for 12 hours before your test (water is fine, but avoid food, coffee, and other beverages).
- Avoid intense exercise the night before, as it can temporarily affect certain biomarkers.
- Schedule your test in the morning, as hormone levels are typically most stable at this time.

Following these steps will help ensure reliable and accurate lab results.

* * *

### How long until my lab results are ready?

Lab processing times vary, but most results are available within 7-14 days. Once your results are ready, you will receive a notification and can access them through your patient portal. You will then receive an email to schedule a lab review with one of our providers, who will guide you through your results and next steps.

* * *

### What can I expect from Joi + Blokes?

Your goals AND safety are our top priorities:

- Personalized treatment plans by (certified) hormone therapy specialists.
- A variety of treatment options to ensure you get the best results. Includes Testosterone, peptides, sexual performance, and more.
- Regular (convenient) virtual visits with our providers.
- Regularly scheduled blood draws to ensure both optimal treatment and your safety.
- Accessibility. Real, live people answering our phones, chats and texts!

* * *

### Should I stop supplements or medications before my lab draw?

We recommend holding off on supplements the morning of your blood draw unless otherwise instructed. Prescription medications should be taken as directed by your provider.

If you’re on TRT or thyroid medication, it’s best to complete your blood draw right before your next scheduled dose to get the most accurate baseline levels.

* * *

### I have labs from another clinician, can I use them?

Labs within the last 90 days can be used in some instances. We cannot guarantee that you won’t need new labs based on your lab values and the biomarkers that were ran previously. You are still welcome to schedule a consultation for $59 to see whether this is needed while uploading your previous labs. We will help guide you in the best way possible and our licensed clinicians will evaluate whether new labs are needed for further evaluation or prescribed medications.

* * *

### Do you accept insurance?

At Joi+ Blokes, we take a holistic, preventative approach to care, which means many advanced treatments and lab tests are not typically covered by insurance. However, we do offer insurance-pay options for certain prescriptions:

- **Women’s health medications** like estrogen and progesterone can be sent to your local pharmacy, where you may use your insurance benefits.

- **Thyroid medications** will be sent to your local pharmacy, allowing you to utilize your insurance coverage.

- **FDA-approved GLP-1/GIP medications** for weight loss and type 2 diabetes, including Ozempic, Wegovy, Zepbound, and Mounjaro, may also be eligible for coverage.


While we assist with prior authorizations and prescription submissions, we do not bill insurance directly. We recommend checking with your insurance provider for specific coverage details.

* * *

### Can I use my HSA or FSA?

Yes—you can pay with your HSA or FSA through Flex in just a few quick steps. First, shop our site and add any health-related items you need to your cart. When you’re ready to check out, choose **Flex \| Pay with HSA/FSA** as your payment option.

Once everything’s verified (and the letter approved, if required), Flex handles the HSA/FSA payment for the eligible amount, and you can cover any remaining balance with your secondary payment method to complete your purchase.

* * *

### Who is my health coach?

Our Joi + Blokes Coaches are here to support you and be a listening ear during your Joi/Blokes experience. Consider them your health journey BFF’s who can help you navigate your results and support you along the way! We know how challenging (and oftentimes isolating) health issues can be, and we want you to know you’re not alone on this path. Our coaches can provide guidance, general information, and most importantly, be your liaison when you need answers from your provider.

Our health coaches’ experience and expertise range from nurses, registered dietitians, personal trainers, naturopathic doctors to navy seals.

Please note: Health Coaches are not doctors and cannot prescribe, provide specific medical advice or diagnose you with any medical conditions.

**Joi + Blokes health coaches are here to:**

- Listen to your experience – you are not alone!

- Help you understand your results

- Provide information on therapies we offer that may help you meet your goals

- Be your direct contact for any questions you have during your journey, on your order, or for your provider – they are here to help!

All prescription products require a medical consultation with a licensed provider, either asynchronously or synchronously, who will determine if a prescription is appropriate. If not deemed appropriate, you will be immediately refunded.
