### What are symptoms of low testosterone?

Experiencing any one of these symptoms alone may not indicate low testosterone, but having three or more is a strong reason to get tested:

- Irritability & Mood Changes (crabbiness, increased frustration)
- Low or Poor Sex Drive
- Weight Gain & Belly Fat
- Loss of Endurance & Stamina
- Erectile Dysfunction
- Brain Fog & Poor Sleep
- Difficulty Concentrating & Mental Fatigue
- Decreased Strength & Muscle Loss
- Joint Pain & Stiffness
- Lack of Drive & Motivation
- Persistent Fatigue & Low Energy

If you’re experiencing multiple symptoms, getting your testosterone levels tested can help determine if TRT is right for you.

* * *

### What can I expect on TRT?

While the full benefits of testosterone replacement therapy (TRT) typically take 6 months to a year, many men notice improvements within weeks of starting treatment. Common benefits include:

- **Improved Sleep** – Deeper, more restorative rest
- **Enhanced Recovery** – Faster muscle repair and reduced soreness
- **Increased Libido** – Boosted sex drive and performance
- **Greater Strength** – Improved muscle mass and endurance
- **More Energy** – Sustained vitality throughout the day
- **Reduced Inflammation** – Less joint pain and stiffness
- **Better Mood** – Improved mental clarity, motivation, and confidence

Individual results vary, but consistent treatment and healthy lifestyle habits help maximize benefits.

* * *

### My testosterone level is 350, and my primary care provider said it's ‘normal,’ but I still have symptoms. Am I a candidate for TRT?

Yes, you may still be a candidate for Testosterone Replacement Therapy (TRT). Many men experience symptoms of low testosterone even if their levels fall within the ‘normal’ range.

Testosterone levels can vary significantly, with a general reference range of 156 ng/dL to 1200 ng/dL. However, what’s considered “normal” is often based on age-related averages rather than individual optimization.

Unlike many primary care providers who rely strictly on lab values, we take a symptom-based, personalized approach to determine what’s optimal for you, not just what fits within a standard range. Some men feel great at 500 ng/dL, while others experience low-T symptoms at the same level. Our goal is to help you feel your best, regardless of the numbers.

### Are injections my only option for treating low testosterone?

While we believe injections are the best option, we understand that some guys just don’t like needles. That’s why we offer alternatives, including capsules and creams. It’s best to discuss the available options with your provider to determine what works best for you.

### How often do I do labs and clinician follow-ups?

After starting your treatment, we recommend a lab test and clinician follow-up around 6 weeks to assess your response and adjust dosage if needed. From there, you’ll typically have check-ins every 3 months. Once your dosage is optimized and you’re feeling your best, follow-ups may be spaced out based on your needs and provider’s guidance.

* * *

### Do you accept insurance?

At Joi+ Blokes, we take a holistic, preventative approach to care, which means many advanced treatments and lab tests are not typically covered by insurance. However, we do offer insurance-pay options for certain prescriptions:

- **Women’s health medications** like estrogen and progesterone can be sent to your local pharmacy, where you may use your insurance benefits.

- **Thyroid medications** will be sent to your local pharmacy, allowing you to utilize your insurance coverage.

- **FDA-approved GLP-1/GIP medications** for weight loss and type 2 diabetes, including Ozempic, Wegovy, Zepbound, and Mounjaro, may also be eligible for coverage.


While we assist with prior authorizations and prescription submissions, we do not bill insurance directly. We recommend checking with your insurance provider for specific coverage details.
