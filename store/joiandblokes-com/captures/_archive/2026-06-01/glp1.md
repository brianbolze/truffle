## Have questions? We're here to help

### What is Semaglutide & Tirzepatide?

**Semaglutide** is a glucagon-like peptide-1 (GLP-1) receptor agonist, a medication that mimics the natural GLP-1 hormone produced in the small intestine. This hormone plays a crucial role in blood sugar regulation by stimulating insulin release and lowering glucagon levels, helping the body maintain stable glucose control.

Beyond improving glycemic balance, semaglutide also reduces appetite, curbs food cravings, and supports better control over eating. These effects make it an effective tool not only for diabetes management but also for weight loss and overall metabolic health.

**Tirzepatide** is a medication that works on two natural hormones in the body, GIP and GLP-1, which help control blood sugar and appetite. By improving how your body releases insulin and reducing hunger, tirzepatide can lower blood sugar levels, reduce food cravings, and support weight loss. It’s used to treat type 2 diabetes and has also shown benefits for people looking to improve weight and overall metabolic health.

### How long does it take to see results with GLP-1s?

Most patients begin noticing benefits within the first few weeks, though some may take longer to see significant changes. Consistency is key, and results can vary based on individual factors. Semaglutide & Tirzepatide is most effective when combined with healthy lifestyle changes, including diet and exercise. Your dedicated health coach will provide guidance and support to help you stay on track throughout your journey.

* * *

### How are GLP-1s administered?

Semaglutide & Tirzepatide is a subcutaneous injection, meaning it is administered into the fatty tissue just beneath the skin. Common injection sites include the abdomen, upper arms, and front of the thighs.

We provide insulin-sized needles, making the injection process easy and virtually painless.

* * *

### What are the most common side effects of GLP-1s?

The most common side effects of Semaglutide & Tirzepatide are mild and often improve over time as your body adjusts. These may include:

• Nausea

• Vomiting

• Diarrhea

• Constipation

• Bloating or gas

• Decreased appetite

• Fatigue

To minimize side effects, it’s important to start with a lower dose and gradually increase as tolerated. Staying hydrated and eating smaller, protein-rich meals can also help. If side effects persist or worsen, your healthcare provider can help adjust your treatment plan.

* * *

### Will I gain all the weight back when I stop GLP-1s?

GLP-1s help support sustainable weight loss by promoting healthier lifestyle habits. If you maintain the positive changes in diet, exercise, and overall wellness that contributed to your weight loss, it’s possible to keep the weight off after stopping the medication. However, reverting to poor eating habits and a sedentary lifestyle may lead to weight regain. Your health coach can provide guidance to help you maintain long-term success.
