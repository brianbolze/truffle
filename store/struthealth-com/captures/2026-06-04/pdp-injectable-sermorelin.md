##### Safety Info

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6095ae7ee40374852364f71d/689d946eac15f57211415b71_Arrow.png)

**Warnings:**

- Sermorelin medications should not be used along with other medications containing sermorelin, or sermorelin-like medications.
- Sermorelin and growth hormones may have a negative effect on tumor growth. Do not use sermorelin medications if you have cancerous or benign tumors.
- Sermorelin may cause an increase in blood glucose levels or worsening insulin resistance. For this reason, this medication may not be appropriate for people with diabetes.
- Sermorelin may interfere with thyroid function and thyroid medications. This medication should not be used in people with thyroid conditions.
- There are not adequate studies determining the risk of sermorelin during breastfeeding or pregnancy. Do not use this medication during pregnancy, when trying to become pregnant, or while breastfeeding.

‍

**Before taking this medication:**

**You should not use sermorelin if you are allergic to it, or:**

- You have a known allergic reaction to Sermorelin-like medications or human growth-hormone related peptides (hGHRPs).
- You are pregnant or breastfeeding.
- You have cancerous or benign tumors.

**Let your doctor know if you have ever had:**

- Type 1 or type 2 diabetes
- Cancer history
- Tumor history
- Thyroid disease
- Autoimmune diseases

‍

It is unknown if it is safe to use sermorelin while you are pregnant or breastfeeding, so it should be avoided.

‍

Sermorelin should not be used in anyone under 18 years of age.

‍

**Drug Interactions:**

- It may be possible that other drugs, over-the-counter medications, or supplements interact with sermorelin. Be sure to tell your doctor about all other medications and supplements that you use before starting on this treatment.
- Other medications containing sermorelin, sermorelin-like medications, or human growth hormone medications.
- Thyroid medications
- Steroid medications
- Type 2 diabetes medications
- Glucocorticoids, or other immunosuppressive therapy medications

‍

**Side Effects:**

**Get emergency medical help if you have signs of an allergic reaction to sermorelin:**

- Hives, itching, dizziness, fast heartbeat, difficulty breathing, swelling of your face, lips, tongue, or throat.

**(Call your doctor at once if these occur)**

- Anaphylaxis
- Difficulty breathing
- Difficulty swallowing
- Rash
- Hives
- Itching
- Chest tightness
- Increased cortisol levels
- Blurred vision
- Swelling or fluid buildup
- Swelling of the eyes, lips, tongue, or face

**Common sermorelin side effects may include:**

- Sleepiness

- Hyperactivity
- Nausea
- Vomiting
- Joint pain
- Temporary change in taste or dry mouth (for oral dosage forms)
- Pain, redness, swelling, or tenderness at the injection site (for injectable dosage forms)

### Your Questions, Answered

##### What is Sermorelin?

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6095ae7ee40374852364f71d/60aad20f9d709c89f55c2b01_%2B.svg)

Sermorelin is a peptide medication that mimics the release of growth hormone-releasing hormone in the body. By mimicking this hormone, it signals the pituitary gland to release more growth hormone into the body. This is different from using actual human growth hormone (HGH), because it is stimulating the body to produce more growth hormone on its own, instead of injecting it directly. Because of this, it may be a preferable way to increase growth hormone levels in the body.

##### How does Sermorelin work?

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6095ae7ee40374852364f71d/60aad20f9d709c89f55c2b01_%2B.svg)

Sermorelin works by mimicking the growth hormone-releasing hormone in the body. This causes the body to release more of its own growth hormone into the body.
