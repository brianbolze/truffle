**Important Safety Information for Strut Mojo**

**Contraindications for using Tadalafil medications:**

- Do not take tadalafil if you are also using a nitrate drug for chest pain or heart problems, including nitroglycerin, isosorbide dinitrate, isosorbide mononitrate, and some recreational drugs such as "poppers". Taking tadalafil with a nitrate medicine can cause a sudden and serious decrease in blood pressure.

- Some medicines can cause unwanted or dangerous effects when used with tadalafil. Tell your doctor about all your current medicines, especially riociguat (Adempas). Do not take tadalafil with riociguat.
- Contact your doctor or seek emergency medical attention if your erection is painful or lasts longer than 4 hours. A prolonged erection (priapism) can damage the penis.

**Contraindications for using Enclomiphene medications:**

- Do not use Enclomiphene if you have an allergy or hypersensitivity to Clomiphene.
- Enclomiphene should not be used in patients with liver disease or a history of liver dysfunction.
- Enclomiphene should not be used in patients with thyroid dysfunction, adrenal dysfunction, or an intracranial lesion including a pituitary tumor.

‍

**Before taking this medication:**

**You should not use Tadalafil if you are allergic to it, or:**

- If you take other medicines to treat pulmonary arterial hypertension, such as riociguat (Adempas).
- If you are also using a nitrate drug for chest pain or heart problems. This includes nitroglycerin, isosorbide dinitrate, and isosorbide mononitrate. Nitrates are also found in some recreational drugs such as amyl nitrate or nitrite ("poppers"). Taking tadalafil with a nitrate medicine can cause a sudden and serious decrease in blood pressure.

**To make sure Tadalafil is safe for you, tell your doctor if you have ever had:**

- Heart problems (chest pain, a heart rhythm disorder, heart attack)
- Pulmonary hypertension
- High or low blood pressure
- Previous stroke
- Blood circulation problems
- Retinitis pigmentosa (an inherited condition of the eye)
- Severe vision problems (including NAION)
- Blindness in one or both eyes
- Bleeding problems
- A stomach ulcer
- Pulmonary veno-occlusive disease (PVOD)
- Liver or kidney disease or require dialysis
- A blood cell disorder such as sickle cell anemia, multiple myeloma, or leukemia
- A physical deformity of the penis (such as Peyronie's disease)
- If you have been told you should not have sexual intercourse for health reasons
- If you have ever had an erection lasting 4 hours or longer

**You should not use Enclomiphene if you are allergic to it, or:**

- You are a woman or child - this medication is only meant for off-label use in men.
- You are at risk for blood clots, or have history of blood clots.
- You have heart disease.
- You have an intracranial lesion or pituitary tumor.
- You have normal or high testosterone levels.
- You have a history of hormone-related cancers

**To make sure Enclomiphene is safe for you, tell your doctor if you have ever had:**

- Liver disease or increased liver enzymes
- Thyroid disease
- Adrenal dysfunction
- Intracranial lesions including pituitary tumors
- Blood clots, or you take blood-thinning medications
- Heart disease or cardiac conditions
- Cancer
- High cholesterol or triglycerides

**Drug Interactions:**

- **Nitrates:** Nitroglycerin, isosorbide dinitrate, isosorbide mononitrate, and some recreational drugs such as "poppers". Taking a nitrate medicine with tadalafil can cause a dangerous decrease in blood pressure.
- **Guanylate cyclase stimulators:** Adempas® (riociguat)
- **Alpha-blockers:** Hytrin® (terazosin HCl), Flomax® (tamsulosin HCl), Cardura® (doxazosin mesylate), Minipress® (prazosin HCl), Uroxatral® (alfuzosin HCl), Jalyn® (dutasteride and tamsulosin HCl), or Rapaflo® (silodosin)
- **HIV protease inhibitors:** ritonavir (Norvir®), indinavir sulfate (Crixivan®), saquinavir (Fortovase® or Invirase®), or atazanavir sulfate (Reyataz®)
- **Oral antifungal medicines:** ketoconazole (Nizoral®) and itraconazole (Sporanox®)
- **Antibiotics:** clarithromycin (Biaxin®), telithromycin (Ketek®), or erythromycin
- Other medicines that treat high blood pressure
- Other medicines or treatments for ED
- Tadalafil is also found in a drug called Adcirca used for pulmonary arterial hypertension (PAH). Do not use tadalafil with Cialis, Adcirca, or with any other PDE5 inhibitor or erectile dysfunction treatment (sildenafil).

- Other medications or treatments that increase testosterone

**Side Effects:**

**Get emergency medical help if you have signs of an allergic reaction to this medication:**

- Hives, difficulty breathing, swelling of your face, lips, tongue, or throat.

**Stop taking this medication and get emergency medical help if you have:**

- heart attack symptoms - chest pain or pressure, pain spreading to your jaw or shoulder, nausea, sweating
- vision changes or sudden vision loss
- erection is painful or lasts longer than 4 hours (prolonged erection can damage the penis)
- ringing in your ears, or sudden hearing loss
- irregular heartbeat
- swelling in your hands, ankles, or feet
- shortness of breath
- seizure (convulsions)
- a light-headed feeling, like you might pass out
- signs of a blood clot - shortness of breath, chest pain, swelling in the legs

‍

**Common Tadalafil side effects may include:**

- flushing (warmth, redness, or tingly feeling)
- headache, dizziness
- heartburn, nausea, or upset stomach
- abnormal vision (blurred vision, changes in color vision, vision with a blue tinge)
- runny or stuffy nose, nosebleeds
- rash
- nausea
- muscle pain, back pain

‍

**Enclomiphene side effects may include:**

- Headache
- Visual symptoms (blurring, spots, flashes, floaters, waves)
- Abdominal or pelvic pain or bloating
- Hot flashes
- Nausea/vomiting/diarrhea
- Breast discomfort or tenderness
- Dizziness
- Muscle spasms
- Fatigue
- Increased appetite
- Aggression
- Irritability
- Mood swings
- Anxiety, depression
- Acne
- Increased risk of blood clots
- Changes in liver function tests
- Cholesterol or triglyceride level changes

Heart attack, irregular heartbeat, stroke, and death have occurred rarely in men taking Tadalafil. The majority of these men had severe cardiac problems before taking Tadalafil. It is not known if Tadalafil caused these problems. Let your doctor know about any existing cardiac issues, or any new symptoms.

This is not a complete list of side effects and others may occur. Call your doctor for medical advice about side effects. You may report side effects to the FDA at 1-800-FDA-1088.

##### What is Tadalafil?

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6095ae7ee40374852364f71d/60aad20f9d709c89f55c2b01_%2B.svg)

Tadalafil is a PDE5 inhibitor that is most commonly used for erectile dysfunction (ED). Tadalafil is the active ingredient in the brand-name medication, Cialis. Tadalafil is in the same class of medications as Viagra (Sildenafil), Levitra (Vardenafil), and Stendra (Avanafil). Tadalafil helps to relax the blood vessels and smooth muscles in the penis to make it easier to achieve and maintain an erection for up to 36 hours.

##### What is Enclomiphene?

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6095ae7ee40374852364f71d/60aad20f9d709c89f55c2b01_%2B.svg)

Enclomiphene is a selective estrogen receptor modulator (SERM). This medication is an antagonist of the estrogen receptor, and leads to increased testosterone production due to the estrogen negative feedback loop. Enclomiphene can be prescribed off-label for male hypogonadism (low testosterone production).

##### What is Strut Mojo?

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6095ae7ee40374852364f71d/60aad20f9d709c89f55c2b01_%2B.svg)

Strut Mojo is an off-label compounded oral medication that can contain either only Enclomiphene or Enclomiphene along with Tadalafil. Strut Mojo may help increase testosterone levels in men with low testosterone (hypogonadism). Formulations that include Tadalafil may also help reduce symptoms of erectile dysfunction.

##### What is Tadalafil?

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6095ae7ee40374852364f71d/60aad20f9d709c89f55c2b01_%2B.svg)

Tadalafil is a PDE5 inhibitor that is most commonly used for erectile dysfunction (ED). Tadalafil is the active ingredient in the brand-name medication, Cialis. Tadalafil is in the same class of medications as Viagra (Sildenafil), Levitra (Vardenafil), and Stendra (Avanafil). Tadalafil helps to relax the blood vessels and smooth muscles in the penis to make it easier to achieve and maintain an erection for up to 36 hours.

##### What is Enclomiphene?

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6095ae7ee40374852364f71d/60aad20f9d709c89f55c2b01_%2B.svg)

Enclomiphene is a selective estrogen receptor modulator (SERM). This medication is an antagonist of the estrogen receptor, and leads to increased testosterone production due to the estrogen negative feedback loop. Enclomiphene can be prescribed off-label for male hypogonadism (low testosterone production).

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6095ae7ee40374852364f71d/60aad20f9d709c89f55c2b01_%2B.svg)

The Enclomiphene in Strut Mojo may help increase your testosterone production by stimulating the brain to send signals to the testes to increase testosterone production. This medication is not testosterone itself, but it signals your body to make more of its own testosterone.
The optional Tadalafil component of Strut Mojo may help reduce symptoms of erectile dysfunction by helping to relax the smooth muscles in the penis and increase blood flow to make an erection easier to achieve and maintain.
