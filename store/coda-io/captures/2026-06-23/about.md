## Our Founders

Some things are just meant to be. This sentiment rings true for Coda’s co-founders, Shishir Mehrotra (CEO) and Alex DeNeui (CTO), who first met in college at MIT where Shishir was driven by elaborate spreadsheets and Alex, cranking out code. It was inevitable that these friends, turned co-workers (Microsoft, Google), would find a way to join forces and use their powers to transform the docs and workspace world for the better. Or as we know it today: Coda.
