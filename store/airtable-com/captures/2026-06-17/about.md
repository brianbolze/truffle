# About Airtable

**Airtable enables any team, regardless of technical skill, to create apps on top of shared data and power their most critical and unique workflows.**

The world’s largest enterprises use Airtable to build customized connected apps for their most critical and unique workflows — from managing product roadmaps to launching marketing campaigns to tracking job applicants — all on a single platform.

What we do

## We help organizations achieve their most ambitious goals and move with greater agility.

With our next-generation app platform, teams easily design and deploy flexible and engaging apps that power critical workflows and make valuable data actionable across the enterprise.

## Leadership

Meet the Airtable leadership team

![Howie Liu](https://www.airtable.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fcdn.sanity.io%2Fimages%2Fhlcyrtq5%2Fproduction%2F40a2b450d43ea186c95f6b41004b1adb1cc5216c-1350x1800.jpg%3Ffm%3Dwebp&w=3840&q=75)

### Howie Liu

Co-founder and CEO

is the Founder and CEO of Airtable. Founded in 2012 as the pioneering no-code app platform, Airtable has enabled more than 500,000 organizations, including 80% of the Fortune 100, to accelerate work, automate complex workflows, and turn the power of AI into measurable business impact. Airtable has raised $1.4B total in funding, with a last funding valuation of $11.6B.

![Ambereen Toubassy](https://www.airtable.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fcdn.sanity.io%2Fimages%2Fhlcyrtq5%2Fproduction%2F162c632a3b6c919f5edcb4a6dc4f8718956b4402-2560x2560.webp%3Ffm%3Dwebp&w=3840&q=75)

### Ambereen Toubassy

Chief Financial Officer

Ambereen leads Airtable’s finance and legal teams. She previously served as CFO of Quibi and as operating partner and CFO of media and technology holding company WndrCo. Her career spans multiple investing roles as partner and portfolio manager at JMB Capital, Ivory Capital Management, and Empyrean Capital Partners. She previously focused on software investment banking at Goldman Sachs.

![Paul Ohls](https://www.airtable.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fcdn.sanity.io%2Fimages%2Fhlcyrtq5%2Fproduction%2F6ee59509c0d20f59e1fca5c345ff9c66b80278b2-2560x2560.webp%3Ffm%3Dwebp&w=3840&q=75)

### Paul Ohls

Chief Revenue Officer

Paul leads Airtable’s customer-facing teams including sales, customer success, services, partnerships, and support. He comes to Airtable after serving as the CRO at Sprinklr, where he developed consultative sales and success motions that helped accelerate its revenue growth to nearly $800 million. Before Sprinklr, Paul’s career spanned leadership roles at Tenfold, Fuze, Medallia, Lattice Engines, and Zilliant.

![Andrew Ofstad](https://www.airtable.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fcdn.sanity.io%2Fimages%2Fhlcyrtq5%2Fproduction%2Fdd0eef495d7231f213ab359d67608a0ad9a90956-2560x2560.jpg%3Ffm%3Dwebp&w=3840&q=75)

### Andrew Ofstad

Co-founder

Andrew spearheads Airtable’s long-term product bets and represents the voice of the customer in major product decisions. After co-founding the company, he helped scale Airtable’s original product and engineering teams. He previously led the redesign of Google's flagship Maps product, and before that was a product manager for Android.

![Emmett Nicholas](https://www.airtable.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fcdn.sanity.io%2Fimages%2Fhlcyrtq5%2Fproduction%2Ff6dd97d35c4c2cd9761d407fe61da2314357524d-432x540.png%3Ffm%3Dwebp&w=3840&q=75)

### Emmett Nicholas

Co-founder

Emmett is a senior engineering leader who is responsible for designing Airtable’s technical architecture. Prior to co-founding the company, Emmett was a founding engineer of Stack Overflow, which he helped scale to hundreds of millions of users, and a software engineer at Microsoft.
