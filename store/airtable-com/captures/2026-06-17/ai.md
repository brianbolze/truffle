## You have questions? We have answers.

What is Omni?

Omni is Airtable’s conversational AI builder. It helps you create complete apps—including tables, interfaces, and automations—just by describing what you need.

Do I need technical experience to use Omni?

Not at all. Omni is designed for everyone. Whether you're a seasoned builder or just getting started, you can build powerful workflows using plain language—no code required.

Can I edit what Omni builds?

Yes. Everything Omni creates is fully editable in Airtable. You can tweak tables, modify interfaces, or refine logic visually with drag-and-drop tools.

What kind of context can I give Omni?

You can add attachments like briefs, meeting notes, or docs. Omni uses this context to tailor apps and insights to your exact business needs.

What kinds of apps can I build with Omni?

From marketing workflows to operations dashboards, campaign planners to product trackers—Omni can help you build almost anything you can describe.

Is there an additional cost for asking questions to Omni?

Asking Omni to build and iterate on your apps comes at no additional cost. Analysis questions about your data cost 10 credits per response. See more [details on AI pricing](https://support.airtable.com/docs/airtable-ai-billing).
