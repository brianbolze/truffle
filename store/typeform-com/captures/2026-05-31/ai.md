## Typeform AI FAQs

### What is Typeform AI and how does the AI form generator help me build forms?

Typeform AI is an AI prompt-based builder  that helps you build, personalize, and analyze forms, surveys, and quizzes across the platform. With the AI Form Builder, you describe the form you need and it automatically generates questions, designs, and a branded layout in seconds that you can fully edit.

### How does Creator AI differ from a basic AI form generator?

Creator AI goes beyond generating questions: it can import existing forms (for example from Google Forms), translate forms into 25+ languages, and optimize wording through AI Content Optimizer. This makes it easier to launch on‑brand, multi‑language forms without manual rework.

### What is Interaction AI and how does it adapt questions for each respondent?

Interaction AI powers “Clarify with AI,” which automatically asks interview‑style follow‑up questions when people give vague open‑ended responses. That way, your AI‑enhanced forms collect richer, clearer data without you having to design every possible follow‑up in advance.

### How does Insights AI analyze responses from my AI‑generated forms?

Insights AI lets you “Ask AI” about your results and get instant qualitative and quantitative analysis. It summarizes key findings, uncovers themes and sentiment in open‑ended answers, and generates charts and highlights from closed‑ended data so you can move from responses to decisions faster.

### Which AI providers power Typeform AI, and is my data safe?

Typeform AI uses large language models from Anthropic, OpenAI, and AWS, but none of your data is used to train these models. For qualitative analysis, Typeform uses a private Anthropic instance; for some features, anonymized form content is shared with providers without respondent personal data, and no data is stored by them.

### Is customer data from my AI‑powered forms ever used to train external AI models?

No. None of your data is used to train any models, regardless of the feature or provider. Any data sent to model providers is anonymized and limited to what is needed to power the AI feature you’re using.

### Which parts of Typeform benefit from AI today (beyond just the form generator)?

AI is built into multiple stages: form creation (building, importing, translating, branding, and optimizing questions), interaction (clarifying open‑ended responses), and analysis (summarizing themes, suggesting charts, and answering questions about your data). This means AI can save you time across your entire workflow, not just at setup.

### Can I control or disable certain AI features if my organization has strict policies?

Some AI‑powered capabilities, like qualitative Smart Insights, can be disabled in Admin settings, and respondent data is protected and not used for model training. This allows stricter teams to tailor how and where AI is applied within their account.
