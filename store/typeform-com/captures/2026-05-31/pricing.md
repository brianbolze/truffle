## Frequently asked questions

If you have any additional questions, [visit our help center](https://help.typeform.com/hc/en-us).

### What are the benefits of a paid vs free plan?

Paid plans unlock powerful features, more responses, and tons of customization options, making it easier and faster for you to collect valuable data and take action on it.

### What’s included in the free plan?

The free plan is a great way to explore Typeform and get a feel for how Typeform can help grow your business. It includes basic features with a limited number of responses per month. Once you’ve got a feel for the platform, upgrading to more advanced features is just a few clicks!

### Can I upgrade, downgrade or switch between plans?

Yes, you can upgrade, downgrade or switch between plans at any time. We’ve noticed our customers often upgrade as their businesses grow and the complexity of their use cases requires additional features. Typeform scales as you grow.

### Can I pause a paid subscription?

While you currently cannot pause a prepaid subscription, if for some reason you need to lower your usage we recommend just downgrading your plan vs cancelling. We understand some seasons are busier than others.

### Do you offer discounts if I pay yearly instead of monthly?

Yes, for annual plans you will receive a discount. Depending on the plan you select, you could be saving up to 16% with an annual plan.

### Is there a limit to the number of responses I can collect?

Each plan includes a set number of responses per month, with different limits depending on the plan selected. Need more? No problem. You can easily upgrade to collect even more responses.

‍

If you’re looking to collect over 50,000 responses/month, [just reach out to our sales team](https://tfsales.typeform.com/to/lhcebvr6?source=website&source2=public%20pricing%20page&_gl=1*xvipgj*_gcl_au*MTUwOTEwOTQzOS4xNzQzNTIwMTYw)—we’ll work with you to create a custom plan that’s tailored to your specific business needs.

### What happens if I cancel my subscription?

The changes will take effect at the end of the prepaid period. Your forms and previously collected responses will not be deleted after the paid plan expires. However, depending on your plan change, your forms may be closed to new responses and seat count reduced.

### How secure is Typeform?

The security and integrity of your data are critically important for us, and we’ve built our services around this idea. Once your information enters Typeform’s systems (located in AWS), it’s secured with multiple levels of encryption and access controls, for example:

‍

- Your data in-transit is encrypted using secure TLS cryptographic protocols (TLS 1.2).
- Your data at rest is encrypted using Advanced Encryption Standard (AES) with a 256-bit key.
- Redundant backups of critical data are also in place to ensure business continuity in case of disaster.

‍

For more detail on the security measures, please visit [Security at Typeform](https://help.typeform.com/hc/en-us/articles/360029259552-Security-at-Typeform?_gl=1*1vtalac*_gcl_au*MTUwOTEwOTQzOS4xNzQzNTIwMTYw).

### What can I do with my data once it's collected?

Your data is all yours to explore! Dive into your responses in our Results area, create stunning reports, and easily share them with colleagues or your team.

‍

Plus, Typeform seamlessly integrates with hundreds of apps, opening up endless possibilities to supercharge your workflow.

‍

For the tech-savvy, you can take it a step further—set up webhooks and tap into our powerful APIs to craft custom solutions that perfectly fit your business. Check out our Developer Portal to get started!

‍

### I have additional questions, who do I contact?

We’ve here to help you. Drop [us a request](https://help.typeform.com/hc/en-us/requests/new) and we’ll reach out to you as soon as possible.
