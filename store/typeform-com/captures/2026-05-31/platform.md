## Design engaging forms

Get more responses with conversational, beautifully-designed forms that are easy to create, too. Start with a professional template or generate questions fast with AI.

[Find out more](https://www.typeform.com/forms)

## Connect your tools

Send responses straight to marketing tools like Hubspot, take payments with Stripe, and let people book into your calendar directly with Calendly. You can auto-sync responses to tools like Google Sheet and Notion, too.

[Find out more](https://www.typeform.com/connect)

## Reach your audience

Reach more potential form takers. Paste a link to post your on form social, or copy ready-made code to embed in emails and landing pages. You can use QR codes, too.

[Find out more](https://help.typeform.com/hc/en-us/articles/360029252892-Share-your-form)

## Automate your workflows

Qualify new leads as they fill out your form. Then route them to the right tools and teams, keep everyone updated with instant notifications, and trigger follow-up emails.

[Find out more](https://help.typeform.com/hc/en-us/articles/33526973403924-Create-Workflows-for-your-form)

## Analyze your data

Work with AI to surface trends, outliers, and recommendations from the data you collect, so you can act faster. It also helps you understand how people feel, along with key themes from open-text responses.

[Find out more](https://help.typeform.com/hc/en-us/categories/37273206968724-Analysis-Reporting)

## Share your insights

Turn data into action. Use video responses as web testimonials, share auto-generated (and impressive-looking) charts with stakeholders, and use data to build your next Big Report.

[Find out more](https://help.typeform.com/hc/en-us/categories/37273206968724-Analysis-Reporting#section-anchor-37273328714260?smooth-scroll=true&offset=88px)

## Frequently asked questions

### What is Typeform and how is it different from a typical online form platform?

Typeform is a platform that combines an interactive form builder, CRM features, integrations and automations, and AI agents to help you collect up to 3.5x  more data from forms, surveys and quizzes than traditional tools.

Unlike basic form builders, it focuses on visually appealing, conversational experiences with tools to create and-to-end workflows for lead generation, research, onboarding flows and more.

### Which tools are included in the Typeform platform (forms, surveys, quizzes, etc.)?

Within the Typeform platform you can build forms, surveys, quizzes, tests, polls, application forms, landing pages, NPS surveys, registration forms, and short forms—each powered by the same no‑code builder and 3,000+ templates. Contacts & Automations, industry leading data enrichment and AI-powered analytics and insights let you run everything from lead gen and onboarding to research and feedback in one place.

### How does Typeform’s no‑code form builder work for non‑technical teams?

You start from a professional template or a blank canvas, then customize questions, layouts, colors, fonts, and logos using a drag‑and‑drop editor—no coding required. You can also use AI to generate questions, apply logic to personalize flows, and publish via link, embed, or QR code directly from the platform. Typeform guides you through integrating with your other tools, like Slack or Hubspot, with no technical knowledge required.

You can easily set up automations for your end to end workflows, and get expert AI-powered analysis of your results and data.

### How does Typeform connect to my existing tools and workflows?

Typeform integrates with 300+ apps, including HubSpot, Salesforce, Mailchimp, Slack, Google Sheets, Stripe, Calendly, Notion, and more. You can send responses into your CRM, trigger workflows in other tools, take payments, or schedule meetings directly from form submissions.

### What kinds of automation and workflows can I build with the Typeform platform?

Using Typeform AI, Workflow Builder and Contacts & Automations, you can qualify and route leads, notify teams, trigger follow‑up emails or Slack alerts, create tasks, and launch onboarding or feedback sequences as soon as someone completes a form. These automations help you act on form data in real time instead of manually exporting spreadsheets.

### How does Typeform help me analyze and share data from my forms?

The platform includes Analytics and reporting within an Insights Hub that surfaces response trends, outliers, sentiment, and key themes using AI. You can view visual charts in real time, share auto‑generated reports with stakeholders, and even reuse video responses as testimonials or content on your site.

### Who is the Typeform platform designed for?

Typeform is built for marketing, product and UX, growth, HR and talent, customer success, operations, research, founders, and any team that needs to collect and act on data online. Common use cases include lead generation, audience engagement, research and feedback, recruitment and hiring, onboarding, education, and event management.

### How does pricing work for the Typeform platform, and what does the free plan include?

The free plan lets you create unlimited forms and access 3,000+ templates, with up to 10 responses per month. Basic, Plus, Business, , Growth Pro, Talent, and Enterprise plans unlock higher response limits, advanced logic, deeper analytics, custom branding, and more robust integrations and security controls.
