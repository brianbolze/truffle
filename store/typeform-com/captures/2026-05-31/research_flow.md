Concept 2 dominates on premium feel and natural credibility

Email drafting is the gateway to AI adoption across all roles

How Do Feel About E-bikes in Your Area?

Report

203 responses

Last updated on Apr 29 · 8:32 AM

Executive summary

AI-powered analysis

1

E-bikes appeal in theory, but clutter and reckless riding kill goodwill

Over half view premium e-bikes positively, and most open feedback welcomed rentals for convenience and environmental benefits. Yet abandoned bikes blocking pavements and riders speeding through pedestrian zones were the most emotionally charged complaints, directly eroding the enthusiasm that otherwise dominates.

2

Missing bike lanes block adoption more than lack of interest

Walking and cars dominate daily travel, with e-bikes under 5% — but respondents who praised e-bikes for commuting said they won't ride without safe lanes. Fragmented infrastructure, hostile drivers, and poor road conditions surfaced across themes, connecting safety fears to low current use.

3

Speed upgrades excite riders but alarm pedestrians and drivers

Faster performance is a top draw for premium e-bikes, yet it triggers the sharpest pushback around pavement riding, red-light running, and collisions. Any speed increase likely needs visible riding rules and enforcement to avoid public backlash.

4

Enforcement and docking infrastructure could flip skeptics into users

Many who expressed reservations didn't oppose e-bikes — they opposed the lack of structure. Calls for speed limits, docking bays, age checks, and consequences for misuse appeared alongside clutter and antisocial behaviour complaints. A visible accountability framework would address concerns spanning multiple themes at once.

## FAQ

### What is Research Flow by Typeform?

Research Flow is an end-to-end, AI-powered market research workflow that combines quantitative scale with qualitative depth in a single study.

From designing your study and recruiting participants to running AI-moderated conversations and generating insights, everything happens in one continuous flow—so teams can go from question to decision-ready insight in hours, not weeks.

### How is AI-moderated research different from a survey?

Traditional surveys are great for structured responses, but they’re limited in depth.

Research Flow blends structured questions with AI-moderated conversations that probe deeper in real time—capturing not just what people say, but why they think and feel that way.

The result is a richer dataset with themes, sentiment, quotes, and video clips automatically surfaced—without the need for manual interviews or synthesis.

### Who is Research Flow for?

Research Flow is built for teams who need high-quality insights without the time and overhead of traditional research:

- Product marketers testing concepts, messaging, and positioning
- Product teams understanding user behavior and validating ideas
- UX and research teams scaling qualitative research
- Growth and strategy teams exploring market trends and customer sentiment

### How long does a study take to run?

Studies can be designed and launched in minutes, with insights generated in hours as responses come in.

Because moderation and synthesis are automated, there’s no need to wait weeks for interviews, transcription, and analysis—teams can iterate quickly and make decisions faster.

### Where do participants come from?

Research Flow supports multiple ways to source participants, including access to a verified panel as well as your own audience.

Participants are screened and filtered based on your criteria, with built-in protections to ensure response quality—so you’re getting insights from the right people.

### What kinds of research can I run?

Research Flow supports a wide range of use cases across customer, market, and public opinion research, including:

- Concept and messaging testing
- Customer feedback and experience research
- Usage and attitudes studies
- Brand perception and positioning
- Exploratory and qualitative deep dives

### Does Research Flow replace researchers?

No—Research Flow augments research teams.

It automates the most time-intensive parts of the process—moderation, transcription, and synthesis—so researchers and teams can focus on interpreting insights, making decisions, and driving impact.
