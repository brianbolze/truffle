## FAQ

### How does the enrichment work?

Growth Flow uses AI agents that instantly pull from third-party data sources the moment a form is submitted. This allows you to automate your intelligence and capture details like job titles, company size, and industry without adding a single extra field to your form.

### Is Contacts & Automations now included?

Yes. To make sure you can act on your enriched data immediately, the Contacts & Automations add-on is now a core part of the product. Previously sold separately, it is now included with Growth Flow by default. This integration allows you to move seamlessly from capturing a lead to triggering an automated, data-rich follow-up sequence within the same platform.

### What is lead capture software?

Lead capture software collects contact and intent information from potential customers and moves it into your pipeline — usually via forms, landing pages, or chatbots. The best tools go beyond collection: they qualify leads, enrich their data, and trigger follow-up automatically, so your team spends time on leads that are ready to talk, not on manual data entry.

### How is Growth Flow different from a standard lead capture form tool?

Most lead capture tools give you a form and a database of submissions. Growth Flow adds what comes next: AI enrichment that fills in missing contact and company data at submission, and automated follow-up sequences that fire immediately after. Instead of exporting a CSV and handing it to sales, you get a pipeline that qualifies and moves leads on its own.

### What types of businesses use Growth Flow?

Growth Flow is built for growing teams running high-volume lead generation who can't afford to lose conversions to slow follow-up or incomplete data. Marketing agencies, demand generation teams, and growth-focused companies use it to capture more leads, qualify them faster, and convert them without scaling headcount.

### How does Growth Flow compare to a CRM?

A CRM stores and organizes customer data — but it typically relies on your team to manually enter, qualify, and act on that data. Growth Flow automates the collection layer: it captures leads, enriches their profiles with AI, and triggers follow-up sequences automatically, so your CRM receives cleaner, more complete records with less manual work upstream.

### How does Growth Flow handle lead follow-up?

When a respondent submits a form, Growth Flow can automatically trigger a text (SMS) or email follow-up — no manual step required. Follow-up sequences are configured once and run 24/7, so every lead receives a response at the moment their intent is highest, regardless of when they submit.

### Can Growth Flow replace my email marketing tool?

Growth Flow's automation layer handles immediate, triggered follow-up based on form responses — which covers a specific and high-intent slice of the email workflow. It's designed to work alongside your existing email marketing stack through Typeform's 300+ integrations, not replace it. If your current tool handles list-based campaigns and Growth Flow handles response-triggered sequences, the two complement each other.

### How does AI lead enrichment improve lead nurturing?

Nurturing is only as effective as the data behind it. When you know a lead's role, company size, and industry from the moment they submit, you can trigger the right follow-up immediately — not a generic sequence, but a message tailored to who they actually are. Growth Flow utilizes AI lead enrichment to provide industry-leading match rates reaching up to 92% for B2B companies and 71% for B2C companies. By automating this process at submission, we ensure every nurture sequence starts with deep context and verified intelligence instead of just a name and an email address.
