## Our mission

At Typeform, we give forms a better look and feel—so our customers can get more out of them. Historically, forms haven't been great, but businesses need them and people can’t avoid them.

‍

That's why we designed a form that allows brands to give as much to people as they’re trying to get. The bottom line is: you should give people something before you can ask them for anything.
