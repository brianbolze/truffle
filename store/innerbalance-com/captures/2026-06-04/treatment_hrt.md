TREATMENTS & SAFETY

Is Oestra® safe to use?

Yes. Oestra® contains estradiol and progesterone—bioidentical hormones that are FDA-approved for use in treating hormone deficiency. Safety isn’t just a promise—it’s clinically supported.

Is Oestra® safe to use long-term?

Yes. Hormone therapy is one of the most extensively studied treatments in medicine, with decades of research supporting its safety when used under medical supervision. Oestra® uses bioidentical estradiol and micronized progesterone — hormones identical to those naturally produced by your body — to support healthy hormone balance.

If you ever choose to stop treatment, you can do so at any time. Your hormone levels will simply return to their natural baseline.Our medical team monitors your treatment and guides adjustments to ensure it remains safe and effective for you.

Is Oestra® third-party tested?

It goes beyond that. Oestra® is made by a 503B FDA-regulated compounding pharmacy that’s inspected by the FDA, DEA, and state licensing boards. Every prescription batch undergoes rigorous potency, purity, and safety checks before it ever reaches your door.

Are compounding pharmacies safe?

Yes, when they’re held to the highest regulatory standards—as ours is. Our partner pharmacy is LegitScript-certified, meaning it meets stringent quality and safety criteria. You’re getting the best of personalized medicine with pharmaceutical-grade oversight.

Is Oestra® bioidentical or synthetic?

Oestra® uses only bioidentical hormones—compounds identical to the ones your body used to make, derived from plant sources. In plain terms: they’re natural to your body, better tolerated than synthetics, and designed to restore—not override—your natural balance.

How is Oestra® different from other hormone replacement therapies?

Two major ways:

– Delivery: Oestra® is a vaginal cream, allowing for faster absorption and more stable hormone levels than oral pills or topical patches.

– Formulation: It’s an all-in-one treatment. One pump a day delivers estradiol, progesterone, and testosterone (via conversion), saving you from juggling 4–6 separate prescriptions.

Does it work better than supplements?

Yes. Herbal supplements may temporarily ease symptoms, but they can’t restore hormone levels. Your body doesn’t make Dong Quai or Chaste Berry—it makes hormones. Oestra® gives your body what it actually needs, in the correct ratios, with prescription-level quality and precision.
