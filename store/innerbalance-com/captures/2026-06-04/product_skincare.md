## Can BodyMatched™ Anti Aging Face Cream be used with Oestra®?

Yes. BodyMatched™ Anti Aging Face Cream works locally on the skin, while Oestra® is a systemic hormone treatment taken through the body. Because they act in completely different ways and in different locations, they can be safely used together. Many women choose to combine them to target both the whole-body and visible skin aging at the surface.

## What is the difference between the two creams?

Both creams are ideal for women of any age with signs of aging like wrinkles, dryness and uneven skin tone. Finesteride is proven to prevent and lessen unwanted facial hair.

## Is this product safe for sensitive skin?

Yes, the dose and combination is well-tolerated by sensitive skin and can be started at every other day at first for a few weeks in those with extra sensitive skin. Some mild irritation may occur at first—especially from tretinoin—but this usually fades with regular use.

## What makes this cream different from other anti aging face products?

This formula combines hormone-informed care (like estriol and finasteride) with clinically proven dermatological actives (tretinoin), offering results far beyond over-the-counter skincare.

Ingredients & Benefits

## What does finasteride do in this face cream?

Topical finasteride blocks DHT, a hormone that causes unwanted facial hair, oily skin, and large pores. It helps reduce hair growth and smooth skin without major side effects.

## Why is estriol included?

Estriol is a gentle, bioidentical estrogen that helps increase collagen, hydrate dry skin, and reduce fine lines. It’s especially helpful for peri- and postmenopausal women.

## How does tretinoin help my skin?

Tretinoin boosts cell turnover, smooths wrinkles, treats acne, and improves skin texture. It’s one of the most studied and effective anti aging ingredients.

## What is niacinamide good for?

Niacinamide (vitamin B3) helps fade dark spots, calm redness, shrink pores, and strengthen your skin barrier. It’s safe and effective for almost all skin types.

Usage & Results

## How often should I use it?

Start with once daily, preferably at night. If your skin is sensitive, you may want to begin every other day and increase slowly.

## When will I see results?

Some women see improvements in hydration and texture within 2–4 weeks. Wrinkle reduction and hair thinning can take 8–12 weeks, with best results at 3–6 months.

## Can I use it with other skincare products?

Yes, but avoid using other retinoids or exfoliants (like acids or scrubs) at the same time, especially when first starting. Always wear sunscreen during the day.

Common Questions

## Will it make my facial hair disappear completely?

No—it helps reduce the thickness, darkness, and growth rate of unwanted facial hair over time. Most women experience softer, finer hair.

## Can this help with oily or acne-prone skin?

Yes! Both finasteride and niacinamide help reduce oil production, shrink pores, and calm inflammation.

## Is it safe to use around the eyes?

Avoid applying directly on eyelids or too close to the lash line, but it’s safe for use around the orbital bone area unless otherwise directed by an Inner Balance clinician.

## Are there any side effects?

You may experience slight dryness, redness, or peeling in the first few weeks. These effects are temporary and often resolve as your skin adjusts. Avoid the sun with use.
