## What is compounded HRT?

HRT, or hormone replacement therapy, is a medical treatment used to restore hormone levels in women experiencing symptoms caused by menopause or hormonal imbalances. It typically involves estrogen, progesterone, and sometimes, testosterone to help relieve symptoms like hot flashes, night sweats, and mood swings.

Compounded HRT is a patient-specific medication prescribed by a licensed healthcare provider based on a medical assessment. It is compounded in a 503(A) Compounding Pharmacy or 503(B) Outsourcing Facility.

Unlike commercial drugs, compounded medications are not FDA-approved and do not undergo pre-market review. They may vary in quality, efficacy, and safety and should not be considered equivalent to FDA-approved products. Data from clinical trials of FDA-approved products should not be used to assess the benefits or risks of compounded medications.

What are the side effects of HRT?

Each HRT treatment is personalized to every woman, so side effects will vary. Common side effects may include mood changes, nausea, headaches or other symptoms depending on the treatment. Your provider will talk you through any side effects and risks based on your prescribed treatment plan.

Is HRT safe?

HRT is a safe, effective treatment for many women when started at the right time and taken as prescribed by a clinician. Your provider will talk you through any side effects and risks based on your prescribed treatment plan.

Am I eligible for HRT medication?

You need to be at least 35 and below the age of 60 to be considered eligible for HRT treatment. A licensed healthcare provider will review your health and medical history to determine if you qualify for treatment and discuss your options with you before prescribing medication.
