How does Henry work?

Henry makes it easy to get the personalized care you need in just a few steps.

Answer a few questions about your health and goals.
A licensed healthcare provider will review your information and recommend treatment if necessary.
If prescribed, you’ll get your medication by mail within a few days.

There’s no insurance stress or surprise bills. Just one monthly payment that includes your provider visit, medication and supplies, and ongoing care.

How soon will I get my meds?

Most people receive their medication within 8-10 business days after they speak to their provider. After that, you’ll get a shipment every 45 days for injectables or 90 days for oral tablets as prescribed by your provider. Just a heads up, people in California may experience increased fulfillment time due to additional compound testing requirements.

What if I need to cancel?

You can cancel at any time in your account page of the patient portal, by contacting our support team at ask@henrymeds.com, or by calling 909-787-2342. If you’re on a multi-month plan, you may be required to pay out the rest of your balance unless you’re unable to continue for
medical-related reasons as determined by a healthcare provider

What are potential side effects of testosterone therapy?

Many people tolerate Testosterone therapy well when prescribed and monitored by a licensed provider, but some may experience side effects like changes in sleep or mood, mild swelling, increased sweating, and acne or oily skin. Your provider will talk through risks and any concerns you may have before prescribing treatment.

How much does testosterone treatment cost?

The monthly price for Oral Kyzatrex treatment is $179, and compouded treatments start at $129 per month. That price includes everything—your healthcare provider visits, labwork, medication and supplies, shipping, and ongoing support. There are no hidden fees or long-term contracts.

Am I eligible for testosterone treatment?

You need to be a man ages 35 to 65, or a woman ages 35 to 60, and meet certain clinical requirements based on lab results to be considered eligible for testosterone treatment. Certain medical conditions may affect your eligibility.
