What is Phentermine?

Phentermine is an FDA-approved appetite suppressant that has been widely used to help support weight management when used in conjunction with diet and exercise.

Henry Meds offers a compounded version of phentermine, formulated by a state-licensed 503(A) compounding pharmacy or 503(B) outsourcing facility.

Compounded medications are not FDA-approved and do not undergo a pre-market review process. They may differ in efficacy, safety, risk, and side-effect profiles from commercially available or FDA-approved drugs.

What are potential side effects of Phentermine?

Phentermine is a stimulant-type of appetite suppressant, so its side effects look different from GLP-1 medication. Common side effects include increased heart rate, elevated blood pressure, dry mouth, insomnia, anxiety, constipation, or mood swings. It’s important to seek immediate medical attention for any rare but serious side effects, like palpitations or chest tightness, hypertension, arrhythmia, or worsening feelings of anxiety.

Am I eligible for Phentermine?

You need to be at least 18 and no older than 70 to be considered eligible for Phentermine treatment. A licensed healthcare provider will review your health and medical history to determine if you qualify for Phentermine treatment and discuss your options with you before prescribing medication.
