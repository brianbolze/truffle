## What are Compounded   GLP-1 Medications?

Compounded GLP-1 Medications are patient-specific medications prescribed by a licensed healthcare provider based on a medical assessment. The medications are compounded in a 503(A) Compounding Pharmacy or 503(B) Outsourcing Facility.

Compounded medications do not undergo pre-market review or an FDA-approval process. They may differ from commercially available or FDA-approved drugs in efficacy, safety, risk, and side-effect profiles. Data from clinical trials on FDA-approved medications should not be used to make assessments related to compounded medications.

**Henry Meds exclusively works with licensed compounding pharmacies in the United States.**

What are potential side effects of compounded GLP-1 medication?

Side effects like nausea, constipation, diarrhea, and bloating tend to come from starting or changing your dosage and usually improve over time. Your licensed provider will gradually increase your medication dosing over time to help minimize side effects. Less common side effects may include fatigue, headaches, dizziness, or injection site reactions. It’s important to seek immediate medical attention for any serious side effects.

How soon will I get my meds?

Most people receive their medication within 8-10 business days after they speak to their provider. After that, you’ll get a shipment every 45 days for injectables or 90 days for oral tablets as prescribed by your provider. Just a heads up, people in California may experience increased fulfillment time due to additional compound testing requirements.

Am I eligible for GLP-1 medication?

You need to be at least 18 and no older than 70 to be considered eligible for GLP-1 treatment. A licensed healthcare provider will review your health and medical history to determine if you qualify for GLP-1 treatment and discuss your options with you before prescribing medication.
