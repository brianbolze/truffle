## Do you need Progesterone too

If you still have a uterus and you're taking systemic estrogen, progesterone is prescribed as well to help protect your uterine lining. We make it easy — oral progesterone is added for free to your treatment plan during your initial intake.

### Balanced hormone delivery

​

Estrogen alone can thicken the uterine lining over time, increasing your risk of endometrial cancer. Progesterone helps keep things balanced.

### Supports better sleep

​

Many people find progesterone has a calming effect and helps improve sleep quality.

### Eases anxiety + mood swings

​

It can help smooth out emotional ups and downs.

### Whole-hormone harmony

​

Estrogen and progesterone work best as a team, especially during menopause.

## Frequently asked questions

What is Effecty?

​

Effecty offers treatments for weight loss, longevity and women’s hormone therapy, connecting customers with compassionate, licensed practitioners through telehealth for personalized care and guidance.

How does it work?

​

At Effecty, we believe in being your comprehensive ally in wellness. Our user-friendly, three-step journey makes starting treatment effortless and effective:

1) Fill out a medical questionnaire.

2) Engage in a chat-based telehealth visit with a physician licensed in your state.

3) If eligible, receive your medication directly to your doorstep from our trusted pharmacy partner.

Our goal is to foster a lasting, connection between you and your provider, ensuring tailored, continuous care for your weight-loss journey.

Do you accept insurance?

​

Effecty does not accept insurance at this time.‍

You may be able to submit a claim with your insurance carrier on your own after you pay Effecty directly, but Effecty doesn't provide superbills or communicate with insurance carriers in any case. Patients can submit their payment receipts on their own to their insurance carrier if they want to; however, there are no guarantees or representations that a patient will receive any reimbursement. Effecty staff do not and will not communicate with insurance carriers about claims in any case, including, without limitation, denied claims, EOBs or prior authorizations.

What pharmacies does Effecty work with?

​

Effecty works with state-licensed compounding pharmacies for treatment fulfillment. Compounding is regulated by state boards of pharmacy.

What states do you ship to?

Effecty ships to all states nationwide.
