## General Questions

What is Effecty?

​

Effecty offers treatments for weight loss, longevity and women’s hormone therapy, connecting customers with compassionate, licensed practitioners through telehealth for personalized care and guidance.

How does it work?

​

At Effecty, we believe in being your comprehensive ally in wellness. Our user-friendly, three-step journey makes starting treatment effortless and effective:

1) Fill out a medical questionnaire.

2) Engage in a chat-based telehealth visit with a physician licensed in your state.

3) If eligible, receive your medication directly to your doorstep from our trusted pharmacy partner.

Our goal is to foster a lasting, connection between you and your provider, ensuring tailored, continuous care for your weight-loss journey.

How much weight can I expect to lose with Effecty?

​

Weight loss varies from person to person, as obesity is a complex condition influenced by genetics and other factors. However, Effecty 9 out of 10 Effecty patients report weight loss (from a self reported study of 106 participants). GLP-1 medications work best with diet, exercise and lifestyle changes for sustained weight loss after you stop taking the medication.

Is Effecty right for me?

​

Navigating the journey of weight loss, anti-aging, perimenopause and menopause can be daunting, but at Effecty, we’re here to make it simpler and more approachable. We recognize the hurdles and challenges that come with these conditions. That's why we've crafted our services to not only be effective but also understanding and supportive. Our holistic approach seamlessly blends medical expertise with a deep understanding of individual challenges and needs. With Effecty, you're not just embarking on a journey of transformation but also experiencing care, support, and guidance every step of the way. We're committed to making treatments more accessible and compassionate journey for everyone.

## Medication Questions

Are there any side effects?

​

While GLP-1 medications are generally well-tolerated, some common side effects include nausea, vomiting, diarrhea, constipation, and abdominal pain. It's important to note that there are also rarer and more significant side effects associated with these medications, such as pancreatitis, gallstones, and kidney issues. Please see our full informed consent and list of potential risks here. [Check out our blog post](https://effecty.com/wellness-library/homeopathic-guide-to-countering-side-effects) on holistic ways to alleviate side effects.

Looking for a more flexible payment option? We offer Affirm and Klarna, so you can spread your payments over time.

How long will I need to stay on the medication?

​

Determining the duration of medication use varies significantly from person to person, as obesity is a complex and often chronic condition influenced by genetics and other factors. For many, ongoing treatment with weight loss medication may be necessary, even after achieving weight goals, to help maintain the loss and prevent associated chronic conditions like heart disease, high blood pressure, and type 2 diabetes. The Obesity Medicine Association recommends considering long-term medication as a standard part of obesity care. As you near your target weight, your Effecty medical provider can assist in developing a tailored maintenance plan. Should you choose to discontinue the medication, your provider will guide you on how to safely taper off the treatment.

Who are Effecty’s providers?

​

Effecty works with board certified US-licensed healthcare professionals. You will be paired with a licensed provider in your state.

What if I am not approved for a medication?

​

At Effecty, your health and safety are always at the forefront of our decision-making. There are occasions when pre-existing conditions, specific aspects of your medical history, current health data, or potential medication interactions may indicate that our prescription options might not be right for you. In such cases, we might not be able to provide medication-based treatment. It's important to remember that while prescription medication can be a powerful tool, it's not the only path. We're committed to supporting your wellness journey and encourage you to explore other avenues to health and success.

How long does the process take?

​

After you purchase the medication, you can expect a doctor to reach out to you within 24 hours. After the prescription is written, it can take up to 5 - 7 business days for your prescription to ship (via free expedited 2 day shipping). However, orders are currently shipping quicker than that.

How do I cancel?

​

If you decide to cancel your subscription for any medication that has not yet been sent to the pharmacy for fullfillment, simply reach out to our customer support team. You can email us at hello@effecty.com with the subject line 'Cancel my account.' Our team will assist you with the cancellation process promptly and efficiently. Once the prescription has been sent to the pharmacy, you can only cancel your subscription for future shipments.

Do you accept insurance?

​

Effecty does not accept insurance at this time.

You may be able to submit a claim with your insurance carrier on your own after you pay Effecty directly, but Effecty doesn't provide superbills or communicate with insurance carriers in any case. Patients can submit their payment receipts on their own to their insurance carrier if they want to; however, there are no guarantees or representations that a patient will receive any reimbursement. Again, Effecty staff do not and will not communicate with insurance carriers about claims in any case, including, without limitation, denied claims, EOBs or prior authorizations.

## Provider Questions

Why do I need to pay before speaking to a provider?

​

Healthcare provider support is a key part of our Effecty's benefits. To access this service, purchase and completion of a medical intake form are necessary prerequisites. This process ensures that a licensed medical provider can thoroughly evaluate your eligibility for medication. Please note that your credit card is not charged immediately; instead, a hold is placed until your prescription is written. In the event a prescription is not written, rest assured that your card will not be charged and the hold will be released.

How do I see a medical provider?

​

Beginning your journey with Effecty is seamless. After you submit your medical intake forms, a licensed medical provider will contact you through our secure, HIPAA-compliant telehealth system. Your consultation will be conducted via a chat-based exchange, offering you the flexibility and convenience of 24/7 access to your provider. You can anticipate a response within 24 hours. Furthermore, as long as your subscription is active, you'll have ongoing access to your medical provider for continuous support and guidance.

Can I choose my medical provider?

​

We assign providers to our members based on state licensing requirements and the availability of our medical professionals. We understand the importance of comfort and trust in a healthcare relationship. If, for any reason, you prefer not to consult with the assigned provider, please let us know. Our team is committed to accommodating your needs and will endeavor to schedule your appointment with an alternative provider if available.

What states do you ship to?

​

For GLP-1’s, effecty ships within the USA to all states nationwide. For Longevity and HRT, Effecty ships nationwide.
