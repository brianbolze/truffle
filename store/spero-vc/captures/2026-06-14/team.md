## Our Team

At Spero, we understand that meaningful progress isn’t built in grand gestures and a meaningful mission does not make a successful company.

To help our founders deliver on both the big and small of their world changing work, we’ve assembled a team of investors and operators with experience building and scaling some of the world’s most influential companies, including Tesla, Stripe, and eBay.

We offer on-call guidance when you’re in the weeds, strategic perspective when you need to see the horizon, and we’ll believe in your purpose as deeply as you do.
