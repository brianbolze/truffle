## **Clinically Studied Regenerative Therapy**

![Mayo Clinic logo](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/694bd7eea6624d2162babab6/6a14a82583f5595e75a7e4d8_NIH.png)

GHK-Cu (glycyl-L-histidyl-L-lysine) is a naturally occurring copper-binding tripeptide first isolated in human plasma in 1973. Published research in BioMed Research International confirms that GHK-Cu stimulates collagen, elastin, and glycosaminoglycan synthesis, tightens loose skin, improves skin density and firmness, reduces fine lines and wrinkles, and accelerates wound healing of skin and hair follicles. Plasma levels of GHK decline from approximately 200 ng/mL at age 20 to 80 ng/mL by age 60 - a decline that directly correlates with visible skin aging and reduced regenerative capacity.

![NIH - National Library of Medicine / National Center for Biotechnology Information](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/694bd7eea6624d2162babab6/6a14a82583f5595e75a7e4d7_SVG.svg)

A landmark review published in the International Journal of Molecular Sciences established that GHK-Cu modulates the expression of over 4,000 human genes essentially resetting cellular DNA to a more youthful state. The peptide up-regulates 59% of affected genes (including those governing repair and regeneration) while suppressing 41% (including those linked to inflammation and disease progression). Researchers identified GHK-Cu as uniquely capable of stimulating blood vessel and nerve outgrowth, supporting dermal fibroblast function, and restoring replicative vitality to cells damaged by radiation.

![MDPI logo (your choice)](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/694bd7eea6624d2162babab6/6a14a82583f5595e75a7e4d6_SVG.svg)

A 2024 multicenter clinical study found that 0.05% GHK-Cu gel applied after fractional laser resurfacing produced 25% faster epithelial recovery compared to standard care, with inflammatory markers reduced by 30% within 72 hours. A separate peer-reviewed study in women using GHK-Cu facial cream daily for three months showed increased skin density and thickness, reduced sagging, and measurable improvement in fine lines — outperforming placebo and vitamin K cream. In trichology, a 2025 trial observed a 7% increase in hair count after 16 weeks of topical copper peptide application.

## Frequently Asked Questions

##### What is GHK-Cu?

GHK-Cu (glycyl-L-histidyl-L-lysine copper) is a naturally occurring copper-binding tripeptide found in human plasma, saliva, and urine. It was first isolated in 1973 and has since been extensively studied for its ability to stimulate collagen and elastin production, accelerate wound healing, promote hair follicle health, reduce inflammation, and modulate the expression of thousands of human genes. Plasma levels of GHK-Cu decline significantly with age by more than 50% between ages 20 and 60 - contributing to visible skin aging, slower healing, and hair thinning.

##### What can GHK-Cu therapy treat?

GHK-Cu is physician-prescribed for skin regeneration, anti-aging, photoaging and sun damage repair, hair thinning and follicle reactivation, wound healing and scar reduction, and general skin barrier restoration. Your licensed physician will determine the appropriate formulation and protocol based on your specific concerns.

##### How does the at-home Retatrutide program work?

GHK-Cu is a naturally occurring peptide available through licensed U.S. compounding pharmacies via physician prescription. It is not an FDA-approved drug for specific indications, but it has a long history of use in cosmetic formulations and is prescribed by physicians as part of personalized regenerative protocols. Your physician will determine if GHK-Cu is appropriate for you.

##### Is GHK-Cu FDA approved?

All providers are licensed healthcare professionals with clinical experience in metabolic medicine and peptide therapy. Each goes through a rigorous vetting process and is available for ongoing support throughout your treatment.

##### How does the at-home GHK-Cu program work?

Start with a quick online health assessment. If you're a good candidate, your physician will prescribe a personalized GHK-Cu protocol which may include a topical serum, injectable solution, or both. Your kit ships directly to your door with full instructions. You self-administer at home with unlimited provider messaging for ongoing guidance.

##### Topical vs. injectable. what's the difference?

Topical GHK-Cu serums work directly on the skin surface and are effective for skin texture, tone, fine lines, and scalp/hair concerns. Injectable GHK-Cu (subcutaneous) delivers the peptide systemically and may produce broader regenerative effects. Many patients use both. Your physician will recommend the protocol that matches your goals.
