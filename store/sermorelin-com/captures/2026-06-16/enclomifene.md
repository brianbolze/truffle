## Frequently Asked Questions

##### What is Enclomifene?

Enclomifene is an oral medication that stimulates your body to naturally produce more testosterone. It works by blocking estrogen receptors in the brain, which signals your pituitary gland to release more LH and FSH, the hormones that trigger testosterone production. Unlike testosterone replacement therapy (TRT), Enclomifene supports your body's own production, helping restore energy, drive, muscle tone, and overall vitality.

##### What is the difference between Enclomifene and TRT?

Traditional testosterone replacement therapy (TRT) adds external testosterone to your body, which can shut down your natural production and may cause testicular shrinkage and infertility. Enclomifene works differently by stimulating your body to produce its own testosterone naturally. This preserves fertility, maintains testicular function, and avoids the dependency that can come with TRT.

##### What is the difference between Enclomifene and Clomid?

Clomid (clomiphene citrate) contains two isomers: enclomifene and zuclomifene. Zuclomifene has estrogenic effects that can cause side effects like mood swings, visual disturbances, and emotional changes. Enclomifene is the isolated trans-isomer without zuclomifene, meaning you get the testosterone-boosting benefits with fewer estrogenic side effects.

##### Will Enclomifene affect my fertility?

Unlike TRT, which can significantly reduce sperm production, Enclomifene actually supports fertility. By stimulating natural testosterone production through increased LH and FSH, Enclomifene maintains or may even improve sperm production. It's often prescribed specifically for men who want to optimize testosterone while preserving or improving fertility.
