## Clinically Studied Peptide Therapy Backed by Published Research

![Mayo Clinic logo](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/694bd7eea6624d2162babab6/69aac217a7044ae99c9a19f6_046bf18a5abe45974fc054101310f96f_SVG.avif)

The Mayo Clinic recognizes sermorelin as a synthetic version of a naturally occurring substance that stimulates the release of growth hormone from the pituitary gland. Sermorelin therapy works with your body's own endocrine system rather than introducing external hormones, making it a physician-supervised approach to addressing growth hormone decline.

![NIH - National Library of Medicine / National Center for Biotechnology Information](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/694bd7eea6624d2162babab6/69aac217a7044ae99c9a19f8_e18237fd96a94c13235335cd385bafee_SVG.avif)

Sermorelin is a synthetic analog of growth hormone-releasing hormone (GHRH) that stimulates the pituitary gland to increase natural growth hormone production. Published research in Clinical Interventions in Aging confirms that sermorelin preserves the body's hormonal feedback loops, supporting a safer alternative to direct HGH replacement therapy.

![MDPI logo (your choice)](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/694bd7eea6624d2162babab6/69aac217a7044ae99c9a19f7_2b5cb5df7fce0d09244f1313ef58d8cd_SVG.avif)

A peer-reviewed study in Translational Andrology and Urology found that growth hormone secretagogues like sermorelin are potent stimulators of GH and IGF-1 levels, shown to improve body composition by reducing fat and supporting lean muscle mass. Sermorelin uniquely promotes the body's natural pulsatile growth hormone release pattern, unlike exogenous HGH injections.

##### What are Sermorelin injections?

Sermorelin is a growth hormone-releasing hormone (GHRH) analog that stimulates your pituitary gland to naturally produce and release growth hormone. Sermorelin injections help restore declining hormone levels, supporting energy, recovery, muscle tone, sleep quality, and healthy aging.

##### How does the at-home Sermorelin program work?

Both deliver the same physician-prescribed sermorelin therapy. Injections are administered subcutaneously at bedtime and have the longest clinical track record.
