## Maximizing your earnings

As an Uber driver, you control how you earn. Go online when you're ready, and accept trip requests that align with your strategy. Here are 4 ways that can help you make the most of your time online:

![](https://cn-geo1.uber.com/image-proc/crop/resizecrop/udam/format=auto/width=552/height=552/srcb64=aHR0cHM6Ly90Yi1zdGF0aWMudWJlci5jb20vcHJvZC91ZGFtLWFzc2V0cy8yYWQ2MjVhZi1mM2Q2LTRkNzEtOTVmMy03NzUzZjI3YTI3ZGUucG5n)

### Heatmap

Use the in-app [rides heatmap](https://youtu.be/fQn4u_OJLiw) to identify where it’s busy, what wait times to expect, and areas where you could earn extra.¹ Look for the shortest estimated wait times in red, followed by orange, and then yellow. Surge is shown in purple on the rides heatmap. Any trip you get in the purple area while surge pricing is available will include extra earnings that will be included in your fare.

![](https://cn-geo1.uber.com/image-proc/crop/resizecrop/udam/format=auto/width=552/height=552/srcb64=aHR0cHM6Ly90Yi1zdGF0aWMudWJlci5jb20vcHJvZC91ZGFtLWFzc2V0cy8wMjRiOGZlYy1lZWM4LTQzNDItODAxNS1lNWYzYzM5NmI3NTcucG5n)

### Promotions

**Quest:** Earn more for completing a certain number of trips in a specified amount of time. Go to **Menu** in the Driver app, then **Opportunities** to see available Quests and opt in to participate.

**Boost+:** Earn extra on top of base fares by completing trips that start in a certain area within a specific time window. Go to **Menu**, then **Opportunities** to see if you’re eligible for Boost+ and to sign up. These are offered occasionally.

![](https://cn-geo1.uber.com/image-proc/crop/resizecrop/udam/format=auto/width=552/height=552/srcb64=aHR0cHM6Ly90Yi1zdGF0aWMudWJlci5jb20vcHJvZC91ZGFtLWFzc2V0cy9jZTBmYmU2Ni05NWQ5LTRhNjktOThiYy05NmEzYzdiNmZmNGUucG5n)

### Reservations

Riders can schedule trips ahead of their desired pickup time, which can help you plan your day.

![](https://cn-geo1.uber.com/image-proc/crop/resizecrop/udam/format=auto/width=552/height=552/srcb64=aHR0cHM6Ly90Yi1zdGF0aWMudWJlci5jb20vcHJvZC91ZGFtLWFzc2V0cy8yNThiN2Q1ZS1kYmIzLTQ2NjAtOTg0OC1iNTUzNzFkYzZlODgucG5n)

### Events

Large events—like concerts or sports—can increase trip requests. Go to **Menu** in the app, then open the **Opportunities** section to track upcoming events and see estimated peak times.

### Upfront Fares

In most cities, see how much you’ll make and where you’ll go before you accept a trip. Upfront Fares are based on a number of factors, including base fares, estimated trip lengths and durations, pickup distance, and surge pricing. Any applicable adjustments, as well as tips from riders, are added after the trip.

![](https://cn-geo1.uber.com/image-proc/crop/resizecrop/udam/format=auto/width=552/height=368/srcb64=aHR0cHM6Ly90Yi1zdGF0aWMudWJlci5jb20vcHJvZC91ZGFtLWFzc2V0cy9lZmVhM2VkNC02OWQ2LTRlYWMtODljNC05MGI5MjRiMzgzNjYucG5n)

### Per-minute and per-mile fares

In some cities, you’ll earn a base fare plus the time and distance you travel. Your earnings will be calculated and displayed at the end of every trip.

![](https://cn-geo1.uber.com/image-proc/crop/resizecrop/udam/format=auto/width=552/height=368/srcb64=aHR0cHM6Ly90Yi1zdGF0aWMudWJlci5jb20vcHJvZC91ZGFtLWFzc2V0cy82NDU3MjE4NS01YjQyLTQ3MGYtYjhiOC1hNmJjNzIzNGVlODEucG5n)

### Delayed ride guarantee

For most trips, if a trip lasts longer than 5 minutes past the original estimated time, your fare will [automatically](https://www.uber.com/us/en/blog/delay-ride-guarantee/) increase to reflect the time and distance of the trip.

![](https://cn-geo1.uber.com/image-proc/crop/resizecrop/udam/format=auto/width=552/height=368/srcb64=aHR0cHM6Ly90Yi1zdGF0aWMudWJlci5jb20vcHJvZC91ZGFtLWFzc2V0cy82YmI1NzMxMi1mMjZlLTQyNDMtYWFjNy00ZWQ1NGJkN2RjMGIucG5n)

### Wait Time Fee

After you arrive at a pickup, you’ll earn extra for longer wait times. Per-minute fees start if your rider hasn’t entered the vehicle after 2 minutes for most trips, or 5 minutes for Uber Black and Uber SUV.

![](https://cn-geo1.uber.com/image-proc/crop/resizecrop/udam/format=auto/width=552/height=368/srcb64=aHR0cHM6Ly90Yi1zdGF0aWMudWJlci5jb20vcHJvZC91ZGFtLWFzc2V0cy9hMGM1YThhNy03MmQ0LTRiYzQtOWRmNC1lMDBiN2U1Mzc2YTIucG5n)

### Tolls

When your vehicle is charged a qualifying toll or surcharge during a trip, the toll amount is charged to your rider and paid to you.

![](https://cn-geo1.uber.com/image-proc/crop/resizecrop/udam/format=auto/width=552/height=368/srcb64=aHR0cHM6Ly90Yi1zdGF0aWMudWJlci5jb20vcHJvZC91ZGFtLWFzc2V0cy82NTM2OGNiOC1jMWEyLTQ2MjgtOWU3OS1lMjkwMjQxMDI5NGMucG5n)

### Cancellations

When a rider cancels after a certain time period and after you’ve started driving toward them, they’re charged a cancellation fee and you’re paid for the inconvenience. For most trips, the time period is 2 minutes after you accept; it’s 5 minutes for Uber Black and Uber SUV, and 1 hour before pickup for Reserve trips.

![](https://cn-geo1.uber.com/image-proc/crop/resizecrop/udam/format=auto/width=552/height=129/srcb64=aHR0cHM6Ly90Yi1zdGF0aWMudWJlci5jb20vcHJvZC91ZGFtLWFzc2V0cy8xZjcxMmM0YS1lYjk2LTRjOTQtOWE4ZC1mOTY5NjVkNjNkYzAucG5n)

### Amount Uber keeps

We keep the difference between what a customer pays and what a driver earns on a trip, excluding tips, tolls, and certain fees, taxes, and surcharges. It changes each trip and week based on things like promotions to encourage rider demand, available drivers, and more.

![](https://cn-geo1.uber.com/image-proc/crop/resizecrop/udam/format=auto/width=552/height=552/srcb64=aHR0cHM6Ly90Yi1zdGF0aWMudWJlci5jb20vcHJvZC91ZGFtLWFzc2V0cy9mYzZkYjI5Mi1lNjI4LTQ4MDUtYWZkYS00NGE0MGJkMWI5NmIucG5n)

### Tips

You’ll always receive 100% of your tips, and they’re part of your total take-home each week.

## Cashing out

Choose exactly how you’d like to get paid: weekly deposits directly into your bank account, or cashing out to your account or a debit card after every trip.

- ### Instant Pay















[Cash out your available earnings](https://help.uber.com/driving-and-delivering/article/cashing-out-with-instant-pay?nodeId=1287939c-a6bb-448b-8d1a-20fa887aa7cb) at any time, up to 6 times per day, for a small fee.³ Funds typically arrive in your account within minutes but can take up to 2-3 business days, depending on your bank.

























![](https://cn-geo1.uber.com/image-proc/crop/resizecrop/udam/format=auto/width=657/height=370/srcb64=aHR0cHM6Ly90Yi1zdGF0aWMudWJlci5jb20vcHJvZC91ZGFtLWFzc2V0cy9mODRjZTdiMi03YzcyLTRjNmEtOGQ2NS05ZTRlNDJmODk5MGEucG5n)

- ### Weekly deposits















If you don’t request Instant Pay beforehand, funds [will automatically be deposited](https://help.uber.com/en/driving-and-delivering/article/when-earnings-are-deposited?nodeId=8b92abc3-6202-4f70-85cc-532d45b1a1b6) into your account, usually on Thursdays.

- ### Uber Pro Card















Apply for [the Uber Pro debit card](https://www.uber.com/us/en/drive/uber-pro/debit-card/) to get access to your earnings after every trip without having to transfer to your bank.⁴ Plus, get cash back on select purchases like gasoline.

## Frequently asked questions

- How do I view my earnings?Down Small













Open the Driver app and choose **Earnings**. You’ll see your daily and weekly earnings, plus a trip-by-trip breakdown. You can also access your full Earnings Hub for more details, including adjustments, promotions, and upcoming deposits.

- How do I cash out what I've earned from driving with Uber?Down Small













These are the primary ways to cash out your earnings in the Driver app:



  - At any time to your bank account using [Instant Pay](https://help.uber.com/driving-and-delivering/article/cashing-out-with-instant-pay?nodeId=1287939c-a6bb-448b-8d1a-20fa887aa7cb)
  - By [weekly automatic deposit](https://help.uber.com/en/driving-and-delivering/article/when-earnings-are-deposited?nodeId=8b92abc3-6202-4f70-85cc-532d45b1a1b6)
  - With the [Uber Pro Card](https://www.uber.com/us/en/drive/uber-pro/debit-card/)

- How can I increase my earnings as a driver?Down Small













Here are a few ideas that may help boost your earnings:



  - Complete [promotions](https://www.uber.com/us/en/drive/promotions/) like Quest and Boost+
  - Drive during high-demand times and in busy areas (use the [rides heatmap](https://youtu.be/fQn4u_OJLiw))
  - Accept [Reserve](https://help.uber.com/driving-and-delivering/article/reserve-faq?nodeId=edd655fe-d600-44bf-97cf-e917fbd6cc72) trips to plan your day in advance
  - Explore [additional types of trips](https://www.uber.com/us/en/drive/basics/#ride-types), like Uber Comfort, or choose delivery if you’re eligible
