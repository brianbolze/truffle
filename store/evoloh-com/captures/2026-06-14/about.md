**Decarbonizing the chemical industry with a** low-cost electrochemical platform

Leadership Team

We are a team interested in decarbonizing fuels and chemicals production with a novel electrochemical platform.

Dr. Jimmy Rojas

Chief Executive Officer

Dr. Rojas has climate-tech venture capital and technology development experience, having previously worked for Baruch Future Ventures and having co-invented the core IP for a successful start-up. He holds a Ph.D. in hydrogen production and energy systems from Stanford University (under Dr. Arun Majumdar), as well as two M.S. degrees from Stanford and two B.S. degrees from MIT.

[LINKEDIN](https://www.linkedin.com/in/jimmy06/)

Scott Blanchet

Chief Operating Officer

Scott has a 25+ year career in the hydrogen industry. He has previously worked for Nuvera Fuel Cells, FuelCell Energy, and GM where he drove innovative technologies from lab to market. While CTO at Nuvera, he led the development and commercialization of many fuel cell and hydrogen products. He has been awarded >260 patents, and holds an M.S. from Stanford University and an Executive Certificate in Management and Leadership from MIT.

[LINKEDIN](https://www.linkedin.com/in/scottblanchet/)

Dr. Art Shirley

Chief Strategy Officer

Dr. Shirley has a distinguished 35+ year career in the hydrogen and energy industry with Linde, Airgas, BOC and BP. He has deep experience in business development, technology commercialization and developing global partnerships. He holds a Ph.D. from Princeton University.

[LINKEDIN](https://www.linkedin.com/in/artshirley/)

Mike Hill

Chief Commercial Officer

Mr. Hill is a seasoned global sales and commercial operations executive with over 30 years of experience in the Power Generation, Water and Hydraulic Industries, working for companies like ABB, General Electric (GE) and Danfoss Power Solutions.  Among his leadership roles, he served as President of Global Sales – Chief Commercial Officer for Danfoss Power Solutions, Vice President of Global Sales for GE Water, and Asia Pacific Regional GM for GE Power & Water.

[LINKEDIN](https://www.linkedin.com/in/mike-hill-b7844330/)
