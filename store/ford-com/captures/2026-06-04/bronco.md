## G.O.A.T Modes®

#### Adapt to Your Surroundings

Take on roads, trails, or two-tracks in nearly any condition with G.O.A.T. Modes® (Goes Over Any Type of Terrain) on the 2026 Bronco SUV. Depending on the model, you’ll have either five or seven G.O.A.T. Modes available.

Normal

An optimal balance of practicality and performance, engineered for everyday driving.

Eco

Focused on efficiency, so you can save your fuel for the trail.

Sport

Increases accelerator pedal response and holds onto lower gears longer, helping the vehicle accelerate faster, while providing improved handling and a more dynamic steering feel.

Slippery

Perfect for when road conditions... aren’t. Lower throttle response, optimized shifting, and more restrictive traction and stability controls give you tighter control on snow, ice, or any wet or slick terrain.

Sand

Engages the four-wheel drive lock for off-road driving on soft, dry sand or deep snow. Standard on Base, Big Bend®, Outer Banks®, and Heritage Edition.

Off-Road

Enhances vehicle performance to traverse muddy, rutted, or uneven terrains. Standard on Badlands, Stroppe Edition, and Raptor®.

Rock Crawl

Engages the four-wheel drive lock and rear differential lock for, you guessed it, rock crawling. At slow speeds, the front trail camera automatically gives you a better view of oncoming obstacles. Standard on Badlands, Stroppe Edition, and Raptor.

Baja

Optimized suspension and throttle control improves responsiveness and torque delivery for improved high-speed performance on loose terrain, like sand. Standard on Badlands, Stroppe Edition, and Raptor.

#### Suspend Your Disbelief

The HOSS (High-Performance Off-Road Stability Suspension) systems on Bronco® SUVs deliver trail-tested handling and confident control for the ultimate in off-road fun.

HOSS 1.0

High-strength, specially tuned hardware calibrated specifically for higher-speed trail running. Standard on Outer Banks and Base models.

- Passive damping system
- Twin forged alloy A-arms with long-travel coil-over springs
- HOSS-tuned heavy-duty dampers in front and rear
- 220-mm solid rear axle with long-travel, variable-rate coil-over springs

HOSS 2.0

When you’re ready to dial up your adventure, this system is ready to deliver. Standard on Badlands and Heritage Edition, and on any model equipped with the available Sasquatch® Package.

- Passive damping system
- Twin forged alloy A-arms with long-travel HOSS-tuned Bilstein® Position Sensitive Dampers with end-stop control
- End-cap control valves
- Independent front suspension

HOSS 3.0

Component upgrades for true trail champions. Available on Badlands, standard on Stroppe Edition.

- FOX™ Internal Bypass Dampers
- Front steel bash plates
- Ford Performance severe-duty steering rack and tie rod ends

HOSS 4.0

The Raptor model-exclusive HOSS 4.0 system is a marvel of off-road engineering. Standard features include:

- FOX™ Live Valve Dampers with integrated front reservoirs and remote rear reservoirs
- Terrain monitoring sensors
- Ford Performance control arms for max wheel travel of 13" front/14" rear

### What is the towing capacity for the 2026 Ford Bronco®?

It depends on the model. Most Bronco series have a max towing capacity of 3,500 lbs, but the off-road-focused Raptor® model has a max towing capacity of 4,500 lbs.

For Base, Big Bend®, Outer Banks®, Badlands®, Stroppe Edition, and Heritage Edition models, you'll need additional equipment (such as the available Trailer Tow Prep Package) to enable hitching up a trailer. Raptor includes an upgraded tow package standard.

### What is the horsepower for the 2026 Ford Bronco®?

It depends on the model and engine. Models with the 2.3L EcoBoost® I-4 engine are rated at 300 hp. Models with the available 2.7L EcoBoost V6 engine are rated at 330 hp. The Raptor® model, with its standard 3.0L EcoBoost V6 engine, is rated at 418 hp.68

### What is the weight for the 2026 Ford Bronco®?

It depends on the model. 2-door models weigh around 4,500 lbs to 4,700 lbs, while 4-door models weigh around 4,700 lbs to 5,200 lbs.

### What are the G.O.A.T. Modes for the 2026 Ford Bronco®?

There are up to seven available G.O.A.T. Modes® (Goes Over Any Type of Terrain), depending on the Bronco model. The Base, Big Bend®, Outer Banks®, and Heritage Edition models have five modes: Normal, ECO, Sport, Slippery and Sand. Badlands®, Stroppe Edition, and Raptor® have seven modes: Normal, ECO, Slippery, Off-Road, Sport, Rock Crawl and Baja.

### Does the 2026 Ford Bronco® have AWD or 4WD?

Yes, all Bronco® models include 4WD, as well as several selectable G.O.A.T. Modes® (Goes Over Any Type of Terrain). Some models and options offer upgraded capabilities like electronic locking differentials, additional G.O.A.T. Modes, or a higher final drive ratio.

### What are the differences between the trims for the 2026 Ford Bronco®?

Each Bronco model is designed to deliver a tailored experience for a variety of off-road driving scenarios.

- Base: Focused on providing value in the essentials and a great canvas for customization.
- Big Bend®: Balances capability with comfort, with more available off-roading features and the available Mid Package to enhance comfort and style.
- Outer Banks®: Color-matched roof and fender flares, plus larger wheels, add to the look. Includes Mid Package features as standard and offers further upgrades.
- Badlands®: The most factory-customizable model, with a range of available off-roading and interior options so you can get the off-road vehicle you want.
- Heritage Edition: Blends lots of historic design touches with hefty off-road capability, including the standard Sasquatch® Package.
- Stroppe Edition: Stylishly designed for baja (desert or sand dune) racing, in honor of legendary racer and Bronco builder Bill Stroppe.
- Raptor®: Includes the largest amount of trail technology and upgrades, for even the most demanding off-road enthusiasts.

### Is the 2026 Ford Bronco® available with a soft top?

Yes. All Bronco® models are available with a variety of soft and hard top roofs. Which roof is standard depends on the model, but you can select a different or additional roof option when purchasing your vehicle.
