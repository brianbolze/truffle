### Drive Modes

Tailor your driving experience to match the road conditions or your mood. Whisper, Engage, and Unbridle modes each deliver a different combination of performance, lighting\*, and sound. Depending on your model, you are able to select additional options, including 1-Pedal Drive, Unbridle Extended, and RallySport.

#### Engage

This is your go-to mode. Balanced for everyday driving, it includes soft blue ambient lighting.

#### Whisper

Best for slippery conditions, experience a calm, quiet drive with blue ambient lighting for a relaxed feel.

#### Unbridle

Unleash an aggressive throttle response, stronger deceleration, and sportier steering — all lit in orange ambience.

#### 1-Pedal Drive

Maximize regenerative braking. Included with all models, this option lets you speed up or slow down to a full stop using only the accelerator.74

#### Unbridle Extended

This drive mode extension enables more consistent power over longer periods of time by reducing peak power slightly. (Available on Mustang Mach-E® GT model only.)

#### RallySport

When off pavement, add yaw for slides, heighten linear throttle response for control, and step up damping for handling.\* (Available on Mustang Mach-E® Rally model only.)

Blue Zones are pre-qualified sections of divided highway where you can go hands free with BlueCruise.

### Automatic Lane Change

BlueCruise can detect when there is slower traffic ahead of you. It will automatically initiate a lane change, accelerate to pass, then move your Mustang Mach-E® back into the original lane, ahead of the slower vehicle. That means you can spend more uninterrupted time hands free.

### Driver-Facing Camera

To ensure your eyes stay on the road while your hands are off the wheel, a driver-facing camera tracks eye gaze and head position. This driver-monitoring system adds an additional level of confirmation that you’re engaged and ready to resume control at any moment.

This feature can help provide more space by subtly shifting away from vehicles in adjacent lanes, giving you a little extra room when you need it.
