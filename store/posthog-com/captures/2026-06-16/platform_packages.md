# Platform packages

Our platform packages help you manage your teams securely and efficiently on PostHog as you grow. You can [subscribe to packages after signing up for PostHog](https://app.posthog.com/organization/billing/). Need more help getting started? [We also offer service packages.](https://posthog.com/services)

## Available packages

### Boost

Unlimited projects, white labeling, HIPAA BAA, SSO enforcement, and features for collaboration with team members.

**$250**/mo

### Scale

Priority support, SAML, and more features to scale your organization. Includes all features in the Boost package.

**$750**/mo

### Enterprise

RBAC, dedicated support, training, and more. Includes all features in the Scale and Boost packages.

[Contact us](https://posthog.com/talk-to-a-human?edition=enterprise)
