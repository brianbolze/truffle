## A breakthrough in IT infrastructure

With the launch of Amazon Simple Storage Service (S3) in 2006, AWS solved a major problem: how to store data while keeping it highly secure and maintaining privacy and control. A few months later, the launch of Amazon Elastic Compute Cloud (EC2) gave customers instant access to computer processing power. It was a service that would help ensure that anything customers could have done with a massive data center could be done remotely with practically the click of a button. These initial AWS offerings marked the beginning of our commitment to democratizing access to technology, a commitment that continues today as we remove barrier after barrier, freeing our customers to invent and build whatever they can imagine. [Find out who were some of the first customers to use Amazon S3 »](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/aws/the-earliest-aws-customers-who-helped-build-the-cloud)
