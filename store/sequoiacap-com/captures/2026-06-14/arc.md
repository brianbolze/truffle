## WORKSHOPS

We distill 50+ years of company building expertise into 4 days of workshops, focused on building a foundation that endures.

FOUNDER & CULTURE STORY WORKSHOP

What drives you?

Uncover your personal motivations and the scale of your ambition. Understand how your values drive your company's culture and operative advantage.

CUSTOMER STORY WORKSHOP

Who are you building for?

Identify your customer, crisply articulate the problem to be solved and validate that it is a problem worth solving.

PRODUCT STORY WORKSHOP

What are you building?

Set a strong product mission and establish the right metric, strategy and tactics to build winning products with speed.

GTM STORY WORKSHOP

How are you different, not merely better?

Learn the fundamentals of brand, sales and marketing to better position your company in the market.

Business Story WORKSHOP

How will you create value?

Understand what it means to build a business, not just a product. Align all the strategic elements to drive your company forward.

PMF Workshop

Product-Market-Fit

Learn the [Arc Product-Market Fit Framework](https://www.sequoiacap.com/article/pmf-framework/). Demystify PMF and focus on it during 1:1 sessions.

## FREQUENTLY   ASKED QUESTIONS

- 01/













What is Arc?











We find Sequoia founders in many ways,  including cold outreach, chance encounters, data science signals, and more. Arc is our bi-annual open call for pre-seed and seed stage founders. It serves as another pathway for ambitious founders to connect with Sequoia.

- 02/













What can I expect at the seed stage with Sequoia?











When you partner with Sequoia early, you increase your odds of success. We help you gain clarity to move in the right direction with speed and help you build for generational impact.



Sequoia partners with a small number of exceptional companies each year, meaning you get the full support of our team. Our network becomes your network, connecting you to essential talent, customers, and strategic partners that unlock growth.



All early-stage companies we partner with are invited to participate in the Arc Intensive,  our company building program for Sequoia pre-seed and seed stage founders.

- 03/













Tell me more about the Arc Intensive











The Arc Intensive is designed to help Sequoia’s early-stage founders build businesses that endure.  Over 4 days,  founders identify blind spots in customer understanding, competitive positioning, team building, and growth strategies, then rapidly address them with Sequoia's proven frameworks.



The Arc Intensive cohorts are small with ~10 companies. This allows us to dedicate 1:1 time with the Sequoia team and for founders to build deep relationships that last beyond the program.

- 04/













Does Sequoia have dedicated terms for early-stage companies?











No. Every new pre-seed or seed stage partnership with Sequoia is unique. As such any terms are company specific.

- 05/













What other benefits do early-stage Sequoia founders get?











From the Arc Intensive to 1:1s with our partners and operators, you’ll have the full support of Team Sequoia. You’ll also get access to Ampersand, our digital hub for founders with self-serve company building resources, content and Sequoia’s network rolodex, including our Builder community.
