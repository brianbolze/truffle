## Overview

- **Business:** Specialty compounding pharmacy with both patient-specific 503A compounding and 503B outsourcing operations.
- **Stated history:** Homepage says the company was founded in 1997.
- **Facilities:** Site describes a 54,000 sq ft traditional 503A compounding pharmacy in Tampa and a 503B FDA-registered outsourcing facility in Las Vegas.
- **Scope:** Site says AnazaoHealth is licensed in most states, Washington, DC, and select territories; the captured pages did not list each license.
- **Focus areas:** Wellness and pain management, with category pages for hormone replacement, weight management, IV therapy, sexual wellness, vitamin injections, hair care, aesthetics, and related compounding uses.

## What They Offer

- **503A patient-specific compounding:** Customized prescriptions for individual patients, including sterile and non-sterile preparations. `[on-request]`
- **503B office-use outsourcing:** Outsourced office-use preparations from a Las Vegas FDA-registered outsourcing facility, positioned for clinics and providers. `[on-request]`
- **Hormone replacement therapy compounding:** Estrogen, progesterone, testosterone, DHEA, melatonin, pregnenolone, and thyroid-related preparations, with creams, gels, pellets, injectables, and Topi-CLICK applicators referenced. `[on-request]`
- **Weight-management preparations:** Site lists example preparations including compounded T3/T4, MIC injections, low-dose naltrexone, B-vitamin injections, lipoic acid injection, and several capsule or topical combinations. `[on-request]`
- **Other wellness categories:** IV therapy solutions, sexual health, vitamin injections, hair care, aesthetics, pain management, neuro, and related wellness areas. `[on-request]`
