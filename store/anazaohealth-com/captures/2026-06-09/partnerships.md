# PARTNERSHIPS

As part of our Strategic Plan, AnazaoHealth is committed to expanding our intrathecal, 503A, and 503B compounding businesses. We are actively seeking to acquire privately-held compounding pharmacies. Through our acquisition program, we can custom tailor a transition that is beneficial to not only the buyer and the seller, but to our customers as well. We have successfully acquired many pharmacies and are happy to connect you with previous sellers and partners to discuss relationships and transitions.

Our experienced team on staff is ready to speak with you and begin negotiations. As with any transaction of this nature, you can be assured that all information obtained through our due diligence process will be kept strictly confidential.

If you are interested in selling your business, or if you are in search of a joint venture, partnership, or merger, please call 800.995.4363, ext. 1477 to learn about what a business relationship with AnazaoHealth can do for you.
