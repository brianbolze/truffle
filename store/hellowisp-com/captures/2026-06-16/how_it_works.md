## How Does Wisp Pricing Work?

Healthcare costs can feel overwhelming. Here’s what we take into account to help lighten your load.

###### Provider Consult Included

Wisp pricing on prescription care includes your consult fee with a licensed doctor or nurse practitioner.

###### FSA/HSA Eligible

Most of our products can be purchased with FSA or HSA funds.

###### Free Delivery

All products ship free to all 50 states (yep, including Hawaii).

###### Same-Day Pharmacy Pickup

If you have insurance and choose to pick up your meds, you can apply eligible insurance to your pharmacy costs.

###### Wisp x GoodRx

Use our [Wisp GoodRx Discount Card](https://hellowisp.com/prescription-discounts) for lower costs at your pharmacy when you pay out of pocket.
