#### An Easy & Accessible Starting Point for Men and Women

Beginning your fertility journey can feel overwhelming, but getting clarity shouldn’t be. Wisp offers easy, at-home testing for both men and women, helping you understand where your fertility currently stands. Our tests measure key markers like ovulation, hormone levels, sperm quality, and early pregnancy indicators—giving you helpful insights right from home.

Alongside testing, Wisp provides supportive tools like targeted vitamins and insemination kits, so you can take meaningful first steps without the pressure of appointments.

While these tests are a great way to learn more about your reproductive health, some results or symptoms may call for an in-person evaluation. And that’s okay—Wisp helps you start the process with clarity, confidence, and less stress, so you can move forward with the care that’s right for you.
