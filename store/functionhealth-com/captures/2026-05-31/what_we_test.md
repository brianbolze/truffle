#### Biomarkers

[Biomarkers **Omega-3: DHA** \\
\\
Docosahexaenoic acid (DHA) is a long-chain omega-3 fatty acid essential for brain, eye, and nervous system function. It supports cognitive and visual health and helps maintain the structure and flexibility of cell membranes. Measuring DHA offers insight into omega-3 intake and balance, which play an important role in long-term brain and metabolic health.](https://www.functionhealth.com/biomarkers/omega-3-dha)

[Biomarkers **Amylase** \\
\\
Amylase is a digestive enzyme made by the salivary glands and pancreas that helps break down complex carbohydrates from food into usable energy. Abnormal levels can be an early sign of problems in the salivary glands, strain on the digestive system due to celiac disease or IBD, and pancreatic stress.](https://www.functionhealth.com/biomarkers/amylase)

[Biomarkers **LDL Small** \\
\\
LDL particles can be small, medium, or large. Smaller, denser particles are considered more harmful because they more easily enter artery walls, promote inflammation, and raise the risk of plaque buildup and cardiovascular disease. Larger particles are less likely to do this and are generally considered less harmful.](https://www.functionhealth.com/biomarkers/ldl-small)

[Biomarkers **Occult Blood, Urine** \\
\\
Occult blood in urine, means there are traces of blood not visible to the naked eye and may sometimes occur after exercise or minor irritation without indicating disease. Persistent or unexplained occult blood in urine may also be linked to urinary tract infections, kidney stones, and other conditions of the urinary system.](https://www.functionhealth.com/biomarkers/occult-blood-urine)

[Biomarkers **Prolactin** \\
\\
Prolactin is a hormone made by the pituitary gland in the brain. In females, it supports breast development, plays a central role in milk production after childbirth, and also influences the menstrual cycle. Measuring prolactin can help explain irregular periods, infertility, or unexpected milk production. Abnormal levels may point to pituitary gland concerns or other hormone imbalances.](https://www.functionhealth.com/biomarkers/prolactin-female)
