#### Biomarkers

[Biomarkers **Omega-6 Total** \\
\\
Omega-6 Total measures the key omega-6 fatty acids that make up a large portion of the body’s essential fat stores. While omega-6s are important building blocks for cells and play a role in growth, energy, and normal immune function, excess levels can promote inflammation. This marker provides insight into the body’s overall fatty acid balance and inflammatory environment..](https://www.functionhealth.com/biomarkers/omega-6-total)

[Biomarkers **Luteinizing Hormone** \\
\\
Luteinizing hormone (LH) is produced by the pituitary gland in the brain. In females, it helps regulate the menstrual cycle, triggers ovulation (the release of a mature egg), and supports early pregnancy by stimulating the ovaries to produce estrogen and progesterone. Measuring LH can provide insight into fertility, ovarian health, menopause, and conditions like polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS) or irregular cycles. This test is not typically run if someone is using hormonal birth control, since these medications alter natural LH levels and make the results less meaningful.](https://www.functionhealth.com/biomarkers/luteinizing-hormone-female)

[Biomarkers **Zinc** \\
\\
Zinc is an essential mineral that plays a key role in immune strength, wound healing, metabolism, and hormone balance. Testing zinc levels shows whether the body has enough to support these functions. Low levels can be linked to issues such as reduced immune response, slower healing, fatigue, or hormonal changes, while adequate levels help keep many biological processes running smoothly.](https://www.functionhealth.com/biomarkers/zinc)

[Biomarkers **Omega-3 Total** \\
\\
Omega-3 Total reflects major omega-3 fatty acids (including EPA, DHA, ALA) that support anti-inflammatory and resolving pathways. Adequate omega-3s help modulate immune responses, support cell membrane fluidity, and counterbalance excessive omega-6 activity. This marker gives insight into how well the body is poised to resolve inflammation and maintain a balanced fatty acid profile.](https://www.functionhealth.com/biomarkers/omega-3-total)

[Biomarkers **Homocysteine** \\
\\
Homocysteine is a natural amino acid found in the blood that also reflects vitamin B status. High levels can signal low levels of B vitamins (B6, B12, or folate) or problems with a process called methylation. Elevated homocysteine has been linked to greater risk for heart disease, stroke, blood clots, pregnancy complications, memory and thinking problems, and bone loss (osteoporosis). Measuring it can help uncover vitamin deficiencies and highlight potential health risks early.](https://www.functionhealth.com/biomarkers/homocysteine)
