#### Biomarkers

[Biomarkers **White Blood Cell Count** \\
\\
White blood cells are immune cells that help defend the body against infections, inflammation, and other stressors. Maintaining a healthy white blood cell count is essential for immune resilience, as both low and high levels can signal underlying health conditions."](https://www.functionhealth.com/biomarkers/white-blood-cell-count)

[Biomarkers **Prolactin** \\
\\
Prolactin in men plays a supportive role in reproductive and metabolic health, influencing testosterone balance, sexual function, and even mood regulation. When prolactin levels are too high or too low, it can contribute to problems such as infertility, erectile dysfunction, or indicate pituitary dysfunction.](https://www.functionhealth.com/biomarkers/prolactin-male)

[Biomarkers **Lymphocytes** \\
\\
Lymphocytes are specialized white blood cells (WBCs) consisting of T cells, B cells, and natural killer (NK) cells, each orchestrating a different arm of immune defense. Their counts can rise or fall with various conditions, providing clues about immune health. The test includes both percentage and absolute levels.](https://www.functionhealth.com/biomarkers/lymphocytes)

[Biomarkers **Hyaline Casts, Urine** \\
\\
Hyaline casts are the most common type of urinary casts and can appear in small numbers in healthy people. When found in larger amounts, hyaline casts may point to underlying kidney strain or indicate an underlying kidney problem.](https://www.functionhealth.com/biomarkers/hyaline-casts-urine)

[Biomarkers **Red Blood Cell Count** \\
\\
Red blood cell (RBC) count is a key marker of health that reflects how well the body can carry oxygen to tissues, which is essential for energy and overall function. Abnormal RBC levels—too low or too high—can be linked to various conditions, poor quality of life, and increased oxidative stress, making it an important window into both wellness and disease.](https://www.functionhealth.com/biomarkers/red-blood-cell-count)
