#### Biomarkers

[Biomarkers **LDL Particle Number** \\
\\
LDL particle number measures how many LDL particles are circulating in the blood. Standard LDL cholesterol tests estimate the amount of cholesterol by weight, while particle tests count the actual number of cholesterol-carrying particles. A higher particle number means more opportunities for cholesterol to enter artery walls and form plaque, which can raise the risk of heart disease and stroke.](https://www.functionhealth.com/biomarkers/ldl-particle-number)

[Biomarkers **LDL Medium** \\
\\
LDL particles come in different sizes—small, medium, and large. Medium particles tend to stay in circulation longer than large ones, which may increase the chance of cholesterol building up in artery walls and raising the risk of atherosclerosis and cardiovascular disease.](https://www.functionhealth.com/biomarkers/ldl-medium)

[Biomarkers **Sex Hormone Binding Globulin (SHBG)** \\
\\
Sex hormone binding globulin (SHBG) is a protein made by the liver that regulates how much estrogen and testosterone are available for the body to use. In females, SHBG helps maintain hormone balance and can influence fertility, menstrual cycles, and symptoms related to conditions like PCOS or excess hair growth (hirsutism). Measuring SHBG provides useful clues about hormone health, particularly when there are signs of excess androgens, irregular cycles, or unexplained symptoms.](https://www.functionhealth.com/biomarkers/sex-hormone-binding-globulin-female)

[Biomarkers **Testosterone, Free** \\
\\
Free testosterone is the biologically active form of testosterone, influencing libido, muscle mass, energy levels, and overall vitality. Because it measures the unbound testosterone available for the body to use, it provides a clearer view of hormone function and can be especially helpful in evaluating low testosterone or hypogonadism in men.](https://www.functionhealth.com/biomarkers/testosterone-free-male)

[Biomarkers **Antinuclear Antibodies (ANA) Pattern** \\
\\
The antinuclear antibodies (ANA) pattern analyzes the specific patterns of antibody staining on cells, providing detailed information that can help differentiate between various autoimmune diseases. Different patterns, such as homogeneous, speckled, nucleolar, and centromere, are associated with specific autoimmune conditions, helping healthcare providers pinpoint to the exact nature of the autoimmune response. This test is only done if ANA screen is positive.](https://www.functionhealth.com/biomarkers/antinuclear-antibodies-pattern)

#### FAQs

[FAQs **Do vaccines (like for COVID-19) affect results?** \\
\\
Both illnesses and vaccines can temporarily raise inflammation biomarkers like hs-CRP. For the most accurate read, we recommend waiting at least 2 weeks after vaccination before testing.](https://www.functionhealth.com/faqs/do-vaccines-like-for-covid-19-affect-results)

[FAQs **Should I stop taking biotin before testing?** \\
\\
Biotin (Vitamin B7) can interfere with hormone and thyroid markers. Some people choose to pause it 72 hours before testing.](https://www.functionhealth.com/faqs/should-i-stop-taking-biotin-before-testing)

[FAQs **Do I have to use my Mid-Year Test within the year?** \\
\\
Yes. You must use your Mid-Year Test before your annual renewal. It includes 60+ biomarkers to track changes over time and doesn’t roll over.](https://www.functionhealth.com/faqs/do-i-have-to-use-my-mid-year-test-within-the-year)

[FAQs **What is a biomarker?** \\
\\
Biomarkers are measurable signals in your body—like a hormone, protein, or vital sign—that can reflect shifts in your health even before symptoms show up. For example, elevated Apolipoprotein B levels can be linked to increased heart disease risk. Tracking biomarkers helps you spot changes early, so you can take action sooner.](https://www.functionhealth.com/faqs/what-is-a-biomarker)

[FAQs **Is heart health testing included?** \\
\\
Function goes beyond the standard lipid panel and includes advanced biomarkers like Apolipoprotein B, Lipoprotein A, and Lipoprotein Particle Size. These can help you catch red flags early when traditional tests don’t.](https://www.functionhealth.com/faqs/is-heart-health-testing-included)
