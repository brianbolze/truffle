FAQ

## Common questions about GLP-1 care.

How long until I reach my weight goal on a GLP-1?

Most members see noticeable changes within 4–8 weeks and reach their target weight over 6–12 months, depending on starting weight, dose titration, nutrition, and activity. Your clinician will adjust your plan along the way.

How much weight can I expect to lose?

Clinical data on semaglutide and tirzepatide shows average total body weight loss of roughly 10–15% (semaglutide) and 15–22% (tirzepatide) over 12 months when paired with lifestyle changes. Individual results vary.

Can I stay in touch with my provider between visits?

Yes — your care team is one secure message away, 24/7. Ask about side effects, dose changes, nutrition, or anything else, and a licensed clinician will respond, no waiting room needed.

What if I have side effects or want to adjust my dose?

Message your provider directly. They review your response, manage titration, and can pause, lower, or change your dose to keep you comfortable while progressing toward your goal.

What happens after I hit my goal weight?

Your clinician will work with you on a maintenance plan — that may mean a lower dose, less frequent dosing, or transitioning off medication entirely while keeping the lifestyle habits you've built.

Do I need insurance?

No. TeloLife is transparent cash pricing with optional healthcare financing through Cherry. No insurance, no surprise bills, no hidden fees.
