You got questions. We got answers

What is the medical approval process like?

1. Complete an online intake form.
2. A healthcare provider reviews your information.
3. If approved, the medication, instructions, and supplies are shipped to your home along with your Lumen.
4. You’ll have access to both provider and customer support every step of the way.

- GLP-1 (if approved)

- The Brello App — track your progress & connect with your care team
- Brello Rise — hundreds of virtual fitness & nutrition classes
- Syringes with needles & injection instructions
- Private Facebook community

Each GLP-1 plan is a 3-month minimum. Cancel anytime.

What if I am not approved?

You will receive a full refund.

What is the timeline after medical approval?

Your medication and Lumen device ship separately.

How does the Lumen device work?

Each morning, breathe into your Lumen to see whether your body is burning fat or carbs. Your results are shown instantly in the app.

What does the 90-Day GLP-1 Support Program include?

The GLP-1 Support Program lives inside your Lumen app and unlocks the full experience — custom meal plans, carb recommendations, daily macros, in-app meal tracking, and health coaching.

Without the premium GLP-1 Support Program, the Lumen app only shows your metabolic readings (whether you’re burning fat or carbs). That’s why the plan includes 90 days of the GLP-1 Support Program, so you get the most out of your device.

What happens after my 90-day GLP-1 Support Program ends?

After 90 days, the Lumen app renews at $19.90/month, or save with an annual plan at $9.90/month ($119/year, billed annually). Cancel anytime and keep your device for lifetime breath measurements.\*\*

What comes with my Lumen device?

Everything you need to get started:

- Travel case
- Charging dock
- USB-C cable
- Quick start guide
- Instruction manual

Can Lumen help me manage my daily nutrition choices?

Yes. Lumen provides guidance to help simplify your daily food decisions. This includes which macros to eat, how many, and when.

Does Lumen work with high-intensity workouts?

Yes. When you measure your metabolism before a high-intensity workout, Lumen lets you know if you need to fuel up with fast-digesting carbs. It also adjusts your carb intake for the day to account for your training and adequate recovery.

Who is Lumen for?

Lumen is for adults looking to stay consistent and maintain their progress.

The Lumen app is not available for use by individuals under the age of 18.

- Diabetes
- Severe asthma
- COPD
