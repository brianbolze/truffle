## How NAD+ Works

NAD+ is a natural coenzyme found in every cell of your body. 5 It plays an important role in how cells turn nutrients into usable energy. 5

As we age, NAD+ levels naturally decline. 6

Studies examine NAD+'s potential role in:

- **Clearer mental focus** during busy or high-stress periods \*
- **Greater consistency** with movement and wellness routines \*
