# Powering the future of telehealth

## A team of innovators driving meaningful virtual care delivery for the clients and clinicians we support.

## Our   story

OpenLoop was founded with the mission to expand access to care for patients across the country by bringing healthcare into the home. What truly sets our team apart is the level of quality, care and compassion we pour into supporting our digital health clients wherever they need assistance the most. It's safe to say, we are as passionate about providing superior virtual care as they are… and it shows!
