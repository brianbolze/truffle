## Online TRT Clinic Treatment Options

We create an individualized plan based on your goals, comfort level, and blood test results.

### Injectable Testosterone

This is the most common delivery system and allows for careful control and quick adjustment of hormone levels.

### Topical Testosterone

Testosterone can be absorbed through the skin using topical lotions and gels.

### Testosterone Pellets

Pellets are implanted under the skin to deliver a steady dose of hormones over time.

### Testosterone Nasal Gel

This gel delivers Testosterone via the nose and can be combined with other therapies for a Testosterone boost.

### Supplemental Medication

These are prescribed to support a TRT protocol and can help with testicular health, fertility, estrogen levels, sex drive, and more.

### Erectile Dysfunction Treatment

TRT typically relieves ED symptoms, but we also offer access to well-known ED medications like Sildenafil, Tadalafil, and Trimix (if appropriate).
