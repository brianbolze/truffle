## Expanded Options for Trimix Injections

Defy Medical offers an expansive selection of penile injections for more individualized ED treatment.

**Not sure which one is right for you?** Most men start with the original Trimix formula and go from there.

### Trimix

The original formula, Trimix injections work well for most ED patients. They contain Phentolamine, Alprostadil, and Papaverine to encourage strong erections.

### Super Trimix

Similar to the original Trimix formula, but Super Trimix contains Phentolamine at a doubled dosage. This can help men who don’t see results with the original Trimix formula.

### Bimix

This injection contains Papaverine and Phentolamine like Trimix, but it does not contain Prostaglandin. It works for men who are sensitive or allergic to Prostaglandin.

### Super Bimix

This formula contains Papaverine and Phentolamine like Bimix, but offers twice the amount of Phentolamine. It can help men who don’t respond to Bimix.

### Quadmix

Quadmix contains the same ingredients as Trimix, plus Atropine which reportedly increases the positive effects of Trimix. Quadmix is usually prescribed to long-term Trimix patients.

### Super Quadmix

This formula contains Phentolamine at twice the dosage found in Quadmix. This formula is typically reserved for older men with severe erectile dysfunction.
