## What is Tirzepatide?

Tirzepatide functions as an agonist for GIP (glucose-dependent insulinotropic peptide) and GLP-1 (glucagon-like peptide-1). It can:

- Control blood sugar levels
- Reduce appetite through delayed digestion
- promote weight loss

An agonist targets a particular receptor to stimulate a reaction. With GIP and GLP-1 agonists like Tirzepatide, they stimulate the secretion of insulin.

## Tirzepatide Frequently Asked Questions

### How does Tirzepatide Work?

Tirzepatide is a GLP-1 agonist, which means it helps control blood sugar levels by triggering the body to produce more insulin and less glucagon. Insulin takes sugar from the blood and gives it to cells for energy, while glucagon raises blood sugar to keep it from dropping too low.

Tirzepatide also slows down digestion, helping patients to reduce appetite and stick to a low-calorie diet.

Tirzepatide might work better for some patients because it’s a GIP agonist, as well.

GIP stands for Glucose-Dependent Insulinotropic Polypeptide. It’s a hormone produced in the gut that plays a role in regulating insulin secretion in response to glucose (sugar) intake.

### Is Tirzepatide safe?

Tirzepatide is FDA approved to assist in weight loss for obese and overweight patients. It is generally considered safe for most patients. When you consult with Defy Medical, your provider will discuss any potential reasons not to take the medication, along with potential side effects you may experience.
